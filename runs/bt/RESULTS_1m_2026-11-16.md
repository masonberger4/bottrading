## Answer

**1-minute bars do not rescue the entries.** The 5m study was too harsh on the tight-stop setups inside the entry
bar, but once that is measured properly the entries are still a coin flip or slightly worse, and the engine run on
1-minute bars loses more, not less. No switch tested turns it positive in either half.

- Nothing is positive in either half. The pick on 2019-22 is the 3-point minimum stop: it loses least there and
  again in 2023-26, but it is still a loss (-$193k, 2 winning years in 8). It works mostly by dropping tight-stop
  trades (1,565 fewer), not by making the rest profitable: AM -0.09 R, PM SB -0.05 R, lunch -0.04 R.
- Stop size against recent bars (week rule off, 1m): stops under half a typical 5m bar are 27% of trades and lose
  -0.25 R (64% stopped within 5 minutes); 0.75 to 1.5 bars lose -0.04 to -0.07 R; wider than that loses again
  (-0.13 to -0.19 R). Same shape in both halves. So a minimum stop tied to bar size cuts the loss but there is no
  stop-size band with a profit. Not built as an engine switch for that reason; the table is in
  `results_1m_2026-11-16/paths_1m_engine.txt`.
- The two PR #77 switches change which trades are taken, not how they do: same -0.13 R a trade.

## 4. Feature splits on the 1m trades

467 splits tested, 24 found on 2019-22 (21 expected by chance), 2 confirmed on 2023-26 (1.2 expected), and the 2
are one split: AM trades with no SMT divergence -0.11 R vs -0.49 R with one. Still a loss. Nothing to trade on.

## What this means

Bar size was not the problem. On 5m and on 1m, with tight or wide stops, the entries as coded have no directional
information on these three markets. That leaves the question the replication thread is working on: whether the
engine's entries are the ones ICT takes (PR #77: 0 of 41 video trades taken before the two new switches).

Caveats: the 1m engine run uses the same rule settings as 5m (nothing was re-tuned for 1m); NQ / YM use the
runner's price-ratio scaling and $5 a side; `entries.py` part 2 does not apply to 1m trades (it reads 5m bars), use
`entries_1m.py`.
