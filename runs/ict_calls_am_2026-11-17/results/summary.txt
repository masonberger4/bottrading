R = average result a trade in units of its own risk (x $1,000 = $ a trade). t under about 2 cannot be told from zero.
R_range = 95% range for R by resampling the trades. total and maxdd in $ at $1,000 risk a trade, no cap. med_risk = $ risked.
his_side / opposite / no_side: the narrow morning engine allowed to trade only his side / only the other side / either.
..._after_call: same, no entry before the call's time (and none after a later opposite call that morning).
plain_same_stop: his side at the 09:30 open (or the call time if later), stop as far away as the engine's trade that day, 2R, flat 12:00 (only days the engine traded his side).
plain_typical_stop / plain_opposite: the same plain entry on every call day, stop 0.25% (NQ) / 0.21% (ES) away, his side / the other side.
plain_late15 / plain_late30: plain_typical_stop entered 15 / 30 minutes later (call times are estimates).
plain_other_days: chance. The same side, time and stop on every other day within 60 days of the call (not a call day).
plain_half_stop / double_stop / 1R / 3R: plain_typical_stop with one setting changed. plain_hold_to_noon: no stop or target, held to 12:00, in units of the typical stop.
