## Rules in ICT's wording
- Long-legged tails: "inside that area of long legged shadows or tails on the candlesticks ... above the high of the candle that's usually called wick but down below it tails, shadows ... when the market trades lower and I'm bearish I like to know where the consequent encroachment is on the longest of the wicks ... from the open down to the low of it, half of that's consequent encroachment ... that's pretty much as far as the market's going to trade to." [repeats L46 wick CE from the open]
- Gray pool: "the foundation to my gray pool areas where it's a PD [array] that's using the wicks themselves and two or more consequent encroachment levels would constitute a gray pool." [new]
- Premium wick CE sequence: price traded up into the daily VI (the draw) and "the consequent encroachment of this long-legged candlestick doji type formation" on Tue/Wed, then "falls out of bed". "The initial draw was this volume imbalance but because the market is volatile ... it trades to it and to the consequent encroachment" of the wick above. [repeats]
- Call it in advance: "last Saturday I mentioned that we would draw up here and then reject and trade lower to go back below this low." Rejection test: "see the amount of candles it took for it to get there and then how fast it moved away that indicates extreme weakness." [repeats]
- Balanced price range and heavy sell: after the VI, "this is a balanced price range so it's not likely after creating this candle ... to trade back up in this area and treat that as a fair value gap. It's likely it just continues going lower." [refines]
- Weekly structure labels: Mon up into the VI, Tue into the wick CE, Wed lower: "essentially a market maker sell model ... original consolidation, smart money reversal, low risk sell, distribution, redistribution and then the second leg downside." [repeats]
- IFVG on the 15m (validation): the VI "now it's going to act as an inversion fair value gap. Once we leave the upper half and come outside on a closing basis ... we're then going to watch does it return back inside of it and does the lower half maintain price and does it stay outside the upper half." A wick close outside is a "mohawk ... coloring outside the lines" and is permissible; "when it does close outside of it we're then going to watch" the return. [repeats; see IFVG-CONFIRM]
- Entry: "that first fair value gap that forms after breaking away validating the inversion fair value gap ... once that forms getting up into it, perfect ... you place a stop just above consequent encroachment [of the IFVG]. That would be your underlying risk." Micros are fine ("you use the micro to facilitate the trade and you manage risk impeccably"). Add "any opportunity you can". [refines; beginner size note]
- Then price took the first daily relative equal lows, consolidated, broke lower again, "immediate rebound". Target: the old daily low (and the lows on the daily). [repeats]
- Weekend hold: "I have a short position that was only two ticks away from being filled [on Friday]" (limit at the low); "right now I'm over the weekend holding short four contracts ... I don't do a lot of holding over the weekend but in this instance because it was so likely to get down there." Expectation: a gap lower on Sunday's first print; if not, he looks for another attempt below the low. [refines; carries an unfilled limit overnight]
- After 4 p.m.: "regular trading hours closes at 4:14 ... electronic trading hours are still going until 5:00 so you have 45 minutes still trading and then you have that hour break until 6 p.m. ... many times it'll have this little bit of a Judas swing going into the 4:00 hour and then after 4:00 it really starts to accelerate into a target higher or lower. It acts like a market on close algorithm where it knows that the majority of individuals are not participating." He expects it when price is so close to an old daily low in a bearish Friday; prop-firm accounts that must flat by 4:00 "never get these types of movements." [new/refines L72]
- Projection: "a Fibonacci on the highest bodies and lowest body and had projections run" and, later, "the highest wick down to that lowest wick ... 0.5, then -1, then -1.5 ... this is where I believe that we're going to open at or below on Sunday. It's a guess ... not scientific ... experience speaking." Minimum expectation = -1; "perfect scenario for swing trading" further. (Anchor on bodies vs wicks is stated both ways; see RANGE-ANCHOR.) [new; weekly range projection]
