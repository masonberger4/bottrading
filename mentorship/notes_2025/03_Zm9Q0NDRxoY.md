## Rules
- Grade wicks into quadrants: premium wick (high candle: close to high), discount wick; measure PDA effectiveness by premium sensitivity / discount sensitivity (reaction at upper/lower quadrant).
- Weekly/daily volume imbalance (VI) used for the week's bias: defend upper half and never trade back to the lower half = bullish; break low and defend lower half, no higher than halfway = bearish.
- Daily buy side / sell side extremes as the two draws for the day.
- Opening ranges are 30 minutes ("not 15, not 5"): London OR starts 01:30 (example), then use displacement and the first presented FVG (distinct from silver bullet first FVG).
- A first presented FVG that shifts structure after buy-side taken becomes a (bearish) suspension block (inefficiency-to-inefficiency); it reacts at its CE because it is a gap, not a single candle.
- There must be a basis (displacement) for why a given imbalance is used.
