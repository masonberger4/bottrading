# 2025 Lecture 04 (043OW8trIJE): daily-chart PD array grading and 20-day lookback
## Rules
- Look-back for extremes is 20 days (never 40 or 60); in a bullish 20-day lookback do not look at the lower area unless expecting a full reversal / bear market resuming.
- Premium wick: always take it from the CLOSE up to the high (discount wick: close down to the low). Levels written the same way every time.
- Bullish order block must hold from mean threshold up; bearish order block from low up to mean threshold. Do not want to see trade in the lower half of a bullish OB.
- Hovering between quadrants: don't touch it.
- Every meaningful price run starts from a PD array; start on the daily chart and transpose levels to lower timeframes.
- Looking for deep discounts then bullish order flow.
- After a parabolic run, do not expect retracements back to the origin.
