## Rules in ICT's wording
- Weekly plan without the Sunday open: "I don't know what the opening price is going to be on Sunday ... we have to submit to whatever the opening price is on Sunday and then we can start running calculations ... what we think the weekly high is going to be, daily highs and lows." [repeats]
- Daily VI = two candles touching only by wicks: "what makes it a volume imbalance is the fact that there are two candles that are touching one another only by the wicks, the bodies are not touching at all." It was "the only portion of price action that's somewhat broken ... the weak link" and the expected retrace target before lower prices. [repeats]
- Large trading range: "when we're playing inside of a large trading range like this I like to be a little more nimble and not require it to do the extremes ... you can make a whole lot of wonderful setups using 75 to 80% of the trading range high and low and never trade outside the range ... put a fib on that and put in 80 and 20 as the levels ... ideal little intraday turtle soup formations, they could act as targets or they can act as new entries." Sell side chosen = the lower of two nearly equal lows. Unfilled limit orders: "a wonderful testimony that you knew exactly what you're looking for." [new]
- Algorithm test: "if there's an algorithm ... you will see signatures that repeat and they're going to be timed ... price runs ... begin and or end at very specific times ... macros ... the last 10 minutes to the top of the hour and the first 10 minutes after a brand new hour starts, then ... the lunch macro and then the final hour of regular trading hours macro ... four macros inside that last hour." [repeats; MACRO-GRID]
- Kill zone: "The New York kill zone is the same for forex, it's basically 7:00 a.m. Eastern to 9:00 a.m. ... you can extend that one hour before and one hour after and just completely answer all the problems of daylight savings time ... the purist version is 7:00 to 9:00 ... in futures it's pre-market trading." [new definition]
- Pre-market sequence (Fri): relative equal highs taken, then break of a short-term low = shift, "this is model 2022 in and of itself." Institutional order flow entry drill: "this candlestick didn't get completely filled in ... repriced back to the previous candle low ... buy side imbalance sell side inefficiency, then ... sell side imbalance buy side inefficiency creates a balanced price range ... the easiest way to anticipate [it] is if there's a balanced price range in the fair value gap that it's trading up into. Nine times out of 10 if you're bearish it's not going to completely close that in and that means you can do a very shallow run above that high as your entry and not try to use the consequent encroachment or the entire range." [refines; IOFED entry rule]
- Order block change in state: "all of these candles here, the lowest opening price [of the up-candle series] that's change in the state of delivery, trades up into the bearish order block and then it breaks down." [refines; OB = series, open of the first]
- Displacement below a level and stay-open gap: below the low "on an expansion ... a candlestick that creates a fair value gap ... we want to see it stay open and if it stays open it's going to act as a breakaway gap." [refines]
- Implied dealing range and gap grading: "implied dealing range is when the market was up here and you anticipate selling off, where's it going to sell off to ... a price run that has not completed yet ... where your terminus or target is." Measuring gap: "anchor your fib to the high and pull it down ... the 50% level laying over top of the consequent encroachment of this gap that gives me the perfect measurement for measuring gaps." "Where does the breakaway gap form? Around 20 to 30% of the implied dealing range ... and then 50% of it." Verification at the end: bodies stopped at the fib; wick overshot the 18th/19th Mar balanced-range low by one or two ticks. Timing of such gaps is withheld ("volume three of the books"). [new]

- Break of the opening range high: "the market trades above the opening range high, there's your breakout, we don't trade breakouts but that's a break above it. We don't need it to close above it." [refines]

Entry inside it: IFVG, turtle soup, or "anticipate the run back above and trust it will give you a fair value gap ... in this case a beautiful immediate rebalance."
