## Rules in ICT's wording
- Definition: "A troubled market ... where the market just has an unwillingness to go higher or lower and it starts sharing the previous candles or bars ranges." Called "time distortion": "they're holding the market in a holding pattern to afford traders time to build a bias, build a narrative"; "once they rip it out of it higher or lower that will encourage large deeper pockets to participate. Right now they're not participating."
- Keys in a troubled market: "stop thinking classic support and resistance ... you have to think outside the box"; "you have to be very very nimble"; retail patterns fail ("bull flags ... are nice little retail traps when you incorporate them with an algorithmic price level like the consequent encroachment level of that February 24th daily CIBI").
- Use an old daily array graded in quadrants (low, lower quadrant, CE, upper quadrant, high). "If a market's bearish, you're going to see signatures like that" (struggling to get above CE / midpoint into the upper half). Expect reaction at CE, next level the lower quadrant.
- Do not pick tops long term: highs used as relative equal highs draw on liquidity on the daily; still bullish bias long term, gap risk enormous (geopolitics), "not trade advice".
- Short-term bearish plan: when price breaks the swing low (shift in market structure), anything in the premium (anchor the break low to the swing high, "anything at this level or higher is a premium") is a short array. Targets "low hanging fruit": a liquidity void/gap below, the low, or the lower quadrant "if you have the brass to hold".
- Minimum reward filter: "if there is a setup that I can be getting short in I afford myself at least 15 handles in the NASDAQ. If I can't get comfortably 15 handles out of it with spread considered on both sides, then I'm not going to take the trade." (20 handles gross, net 15 after slippage and spread.)
- Entry: retracement up into the 1-minute sell-side imbalance / buy-side inefficiency (a single down-close candle framed high to low) and a volume imbalance; "we want to see it stay below the halfway point" of the 1m imbalance. Stop: "one tick or two above the midpoint"; generally "two ticks above whatever that level is [the level you want not breached] or below if I'm long". Example: stop two ticks above the 30s candle high, which was also the lower quadrant (21,777.00).
- Reaction time: very short time frames (30s) need limit orders; ICT wanted to add two more contracts but price did not give the chance.
- Mentions rejection block (lowest down-close price), volume imbalance CE, bullish FVG trades, minor buy side taken, as the sequence of small objects.
