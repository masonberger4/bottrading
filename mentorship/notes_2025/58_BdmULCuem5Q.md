## Rules in ICT's wording
- Trade: New York lunch macro, 10:50-11:10, aiming for the lunch macro buy-side liquidity (the high that forms after 10:00). After a large down day he avoids the next morning session (he was absent).
- Entry: "immediate rebalance": the candle touched its previous candle and the low of the previous buyside-imbalance sell-side-inefficiency; entered on the candle that came back to the low of the blue FVG. Wants price to stay inside the previous candle's wick (ideal), and not fully overlap it. Bring stop up; position too big to add (volatility).
- Inversion FVG as support: price above an inversion FVG should support price; trade above it, break above, retest, and "stay away from the lower half of it." It may touch the half once; trading to the lower end is the failure. "If it does that your probabilities and odds go astronomically higher" (still not perfect). Reclaimed buyside-imbalance sell-side-inefficiency treated as bullish.
- Why long: morning traded to the low just after 10:00; the first hour (9:30-10:30) is the one-hour dealing range; no real retracement on buy side in it = "a very strong setup for my lunch macro" (retrace into a key higher low).
- Target rule: opening range ends 10:00; the high that forms after 10:00 up to the first high is the target for the lunch macro (when shorting for the day, reverse it).
- Don't trade off his tape reading commentary: "it was violently fast."
