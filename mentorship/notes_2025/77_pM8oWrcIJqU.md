## Rules in ICT's wording
1. **ORG over 120 handles = not likely to fill** (Jan 10 NQ opened RTH over 120 handles below Jan 3-settle; "go back to the logic"). Draw the FIB on the gap (anchor at the 4:14 RTH close, to the 9:30 open); for a premium gap the upper quadrant is 0.75.
2. **Draw on liquidity first.** Before the session he named a level (20983.75, a prior-ORG level) as the draw; "knowing where the market is going to draw to is the most critical thing", gaps and PD arrays do not help without the draw and bias. Bias from a market maker sell model (original consolidation, accumulation, reaccumulation, smart money reversal, redistribution, second stage distribution).
3. **Midnight opening range projection** (12:00-12:30): -0.5 and -1 give "a rough idea" of the day's high; NQ rallied just past -1 then reversed. A fair value gap formed, delivered to its CE and -0.5. **Caveat:** NFP, FOMC and CPI days have heavy manipulation/intervention so these projections are less precise.
4. **First presented fair value gap (FPFVG).** In the 9:30-10:00 opening range, take the first FVG formed (not one formed on the 9:30 candle itself: "can't be the gap if it forms on the 9:30 candle, you have to wait"; that was "the rule for Caleb" (his son's model)). Draw it from the overlap of the candle bodies rather than the wick when that is nearer relative equal lows. Trade it in the direction of the draw; not interested in reversals while the draw (the purple ORG level) remains below. Target = the pre-named draw; example ran about 12,000 dollars of one-lot NQ (mini) points to 20983.75.
5. **Inversion note:** a discount wick becomes an inversion premium when price is below it (small remark).
6. **Fallback.** If premarket is "not paying any mind" to the levels, sit out, let the opening range finish, and trade Silver Bullet at 10:00. ("That's how I'm trading this year.")
7. **Level list (his notepad, not on chart):** midnight opening price, midnight range (high, low, quadrants, CE), 9:30-10:00 opening range, ORG levels (quadrants, CE), previous day high/low, previous week high/low, highest high/lowest low of the last 3 days. Tools: prior videos for draw on liquidity and bias.
8. **Risk:** advises one micro contract (2 dollars per point) while learning; 1,000 dollars minimum in an account; he withdrew 60,000 dollars of AMP account profit and runs a 600 dollar account. NFP at 8:30: he was stopped on a first short at the stop run, re-entered 3 micros and took profit within one minute. Not a rule.
