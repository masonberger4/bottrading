## Rules in ICT's wording
- All-time-high rule (from Tuesday): "you need to be grading all of the premium and discount wicks ... cut through candles, just go over top of this candle like it's not even there and go right to it. It doesn't matter if there's five days, ... four days, if it's one day. You look back during the series of the run in the all-time highs or near all-time highs in close proximity to where you're trading at now. If there's any premium wick or discount wick, you need to grade it and we run the fib on it. So it gives us the 25, the 50, and the 75 level" (fib laid on the candle body to the high; high/low optional). Premium-wick upper quadrant today = 24,619.00 (daily candle high dashed line 24,647.25).
- Keep the chart minimal: only the levels used; annotate printouts/PDFs yourself; "if you're not annotating ... you're wasting your time".
- Thursday: "Thursday is the day right before Friday ... when we have a really bullish week ... the high of the week forms on Thursday or it'll create these fake highs of the week and then it'll come back raging and blow out the high". Priming: retail sees resistance, drop, then run up to take the buy stops left by premature shorts.
- Pre-market (equities pre-market opens 7:00 a.m.): relative equal highs smooth-edged buy-side pool; run above it going into the 8:30 news injection = manipulation accumulating shorts by smart money, distributed lower to the minor sell side / pre-market session sell side. "Right before the 8:30 news, they rally it up, taking out this short-term high and this high here. I want to be selling short right there. Even if it stops me ..." Sequence: time, then price, then liquidity.
- Reclaimed FVG: a bearish FVG that price went above then back below; "it's a reclaim. It's not an inversion fair value gap. It's resuming its original characteristic at the formation of it, which is a CIBI, which is bearish." Time distortion (stalling) inside it. Short entries/adds inside the reclaimed bearish FVG with a limit order.
- Target selection against the daily macro direction: "it's going against the underlying macro direction on the daily chart. So, I want to be nimble and pick a lowhanging fruit objective to get out at" = pre-market session sell side low. "When you're primarily bullish ... have to know exactly where you want to get out at. Otherwise, you'll marry the idea."
- 9:30 bait and switch: first fake drop, drive up into the inversion FVG (trap breakout buyers), then sweep sell side, dig into old daily high / premium wick upper quadrant (24,619), rejection, "sugar rush" back over everything to the buy side. "Avoid trying to pick the top" at ATH. Support/resistance at the wick high ("discount sensitivity") and the first presented FVG (with volume imbalance projected forward) used as a discount array.
- Opening range high used as a reference in the example (OR high touches the lower quadrant of the inversion BISI).
- Lunch macro rule (bullish day): "find 10:00 in the morning and walk forward until you see a prominent swing low or the obvious one since 10:00 a.m. ... lunch macro will seek sellside formed after 10:00 a.m. ... the first prominent sellside liquidity pool after 10 a.m." Around 11:30 the algorithm pulls back into the range for it; bearish FVG CE hit, volume imbalance, then a fractal market maker sell model (low accumulation, reaccumulation, smart reversal, low risk sell, distribution, redistribution, second stage = best run lower).
