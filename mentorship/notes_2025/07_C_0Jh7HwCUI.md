## Rules in ICT's wording
- Suspension block (definition): "a candlestick that has a volume imbalance to the high end of it and a volume imbalance to the low end of it. It need not ... be a inefficiency. In other words, it need not have any one single pass with the candlestick. So it could have a candlestick to the left of it that was a full-bodied candle or a wick that completely overlaps the entire range between the upper and lower volume imbalance." Marked from VI low to VI high (companion L09: "We do not use the wicks in between"). [new]
- Model 2022 appears daily: "the 79% 62% retracement respectively. That's optimal trade entry. Very minimum it's got to go above the low to high. It's got to go to 50% or higher for it to be in a premium market. And that's the ICT model 2022. So for the folks who say it doesn't work anymore, it's there every day." Three model 2022 scenarios on DXY/EURUSD/GBPUSD in the week. [repeats L23]
- Breakaway gap (his classification): "the inefficiency that shows up on your chart that does not get filled back in. If it's between a quadrant level ... and another quadrant, expect that when we are looking for lower prices, when it breaks lower, if it leaves a quadrant ... this is many times going to act as a breakaway gap because it's in no man's land between a quadrant and another quadrant inside the narrative and bias." Same for measuring gaps; applies to wick-gaps and body-to-body gaps. A CIBI left over a lower-quadrant level "we want to see it remain unfilled"; may trade into it a little but "some measure from its halfway point to the high of it ... should likely stay open unfilled." [new]
- Bodies stopping at the CE = heaviness: "the bodies are stopping right at the midpoint, which is exactly what you want to see when the market's bearish. It's showing you premium sensitivity. It's not support and resistance ... This is heaviness algorithmically. It's tipping its hand ... the market will want to seek a deeper discount." [repeats L46/L03]
- Orientation of an array depends on where price is: a wick or imbalance is "a discount array when we were trading lower and now when we were below it, it's going to act ... change its characteristic to a premium array" (EURUSD discount wick upper quadrant 1.16220 hit from below). Which wick to grade: "the lowest reaching wick ... it's lower than this one and it's lower than that one. So it's not ... cherry-picking." [refines; new selection rule, see New conflicts WICK-SELECT]
- Wicks are gaps and are reused after filling: "this level of wicking is treated like a gap and a gap is reused even after the gap has been filled ... The algorithm is not going to just toss the baby out with the bath water. It's going to refer back to that reference point." "We cut through candles because we are not flawed logic of supply and demand." [repeats L46; refines]
- PDA lookback 20/40/60: "How long do I keep PDAs graded on my charts? ... I taught the IPDA data ranges 20, 40, and 60. So you can go back as far as 60 days. And they're just as salient 60 days ago than they are just yesterday ... proves algorithmic sensitivity." [repeats L46; extends to 60]
- Every PDA has an inversion: "Every PDA has an inversion aspect. It's not inverted, okay? It's inversion." Bearish breaker: use "the mean threshold of that last down candle" (low, higher low, higher high, then bullish above it flips it to a bullish "inversion breaker"); candle opening "right at that same price" then dropping back to MT = "discount sensitivity". [refines; BREAKER-ZONE not addressed]
- Breakout inefficiency must stay open for bullish order flow: "If these form after you have a breakout of price action during a consolidation, I never want to see these traded back into ... real order flow, algorithmic order flow is when there's an inefficiency after discovery ... as long as this remains open, we have a bullish order flow. ... if it goes down and closes that in, I am not all that bullish." Break-and-retest is "the trash perspective". A measured move (duplicate the range and project) hits the bulk of the bodies (Larry Williams target-shooter idea). [new]

If you have opening range level quadrants on your chart, go back over the last three days and include them. So ... the trading day that you're trading that opening range gap if there is one in its quadrants and then one, two, three days ago ... four opening range gaps and their respective quadrants."
