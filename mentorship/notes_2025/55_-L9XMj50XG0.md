## Rules in ICT's wording
- Daily holding pattern: relative equal highs (buy side) and the lows were the two levels; "until we got outside of these highs or below these lows the market was going to be held in a holding pattern ... when the market does in fact go after one of these first, be it buy side or sell side, then the market would loosen up and you'd start to see a lot more volatility." Buy side first, then the sell side and the inefficiency below. [new, see RANGE-REGIME]
- Gap/VI hybrid: "I used two of my PD arrays characteristics and blended them together that being the volume imbalance and the actual gap ... this candlestick doesn't have a wick to touch this previous candlestick's wick ... the bodies are not touching ... because [the wick] is absent it's not a clear-cut ICT volume imbalance but I'm viewing it ... as a blend or pseudo." Graded "from the candlestick's close down to the open of the previous candlestick ... using gap theory": high, CE (21,839.50 in the caption), low (three levels, "not a zone"). [new]
- PD array failure: "when my PD arrays fail that shows a significance in directional bias ... when a PD array fails to get to its consequent encroachment if it's a gap or fails to get to its mean threshold [if an] order block ... whatever direction it's moving in it's signaling an excess in terms of bullishness or bearishness." Monday opened and only reached the lower quadrant of the daily gap, never CE, then fell. [new; refines "we want to see PD arrays fail"]
- Inside day: "whenever there's a bias that's strong one-sided ... and then creates a small inside day where the day's high is lower than the previous day's high and its low is higher than the previous day's low ... they are like a spring being twisted and compressed even tighter and eventually when it's let go ... it goes usually in the direction of the bias, which is the draw on liquidity." [new]
- Open that cannot touch the prior day's high: Thursday "open trade up, we don't touch the high on the previous day, we can't get to the high of the inefficiency, and then we roll over" = the largest range of the week; then Friday a "monstrous return back up into the range". [refines]
- Time distortion: "if we were to take all the candlesticks from here up to here and compress all that data into just say three candles ... this would look extremely fluid but it wasn't ... a lot of give and take back and forth ... I'm looking through all of this to the left ... where the algorithm made it very clear [it] left a very clear calling card." [repeats troubled-market rule]
- Notepad of levels: "very specific price levels ... the high of it and the low of it and then the consequent encroachment ... not zones; I'm not trading supply and demand zones." Levels come from the daily; "as new days develop new PD arrays on the daily chart I'm constantly adding them." For the student: dress the chart; journal with annotations. [principle]
