## Rules in ICT's wording
- Framing: "how I look at how framing a setup, looking for the opportunities, the areas at which the setups might form, how the bias, how the price should deliver." The daily range gives the area: Wednesday's daily wick "midpoint or consequent encroachment of it to its close" was "the heart of where the setups ... a silver bullet, a market maker buy model entry, a market maker sell model entry, a unicorn setup" would be found. Limit focus to that segment of price action, then watch it in reference to time.
- Friday before a weekend with open geopolitical risk: "don't expect new buying to send us to new highs"; no one can remove risk, gap risk. "Nothing indicates that it should go higher" -> not bullish, sell the rally; bullish would be "trade above this high and then keep on trading up".
- Wait for 9:30: "I am not interested in doing anything until 9:30 open."
- Market structure (credited to Larry Williams, since 1995): long-term high = swing high with lower swing highs both sides and a higher high further left; intermediate-term similarly. "Intermittent and long-term highs that have imbalances in them are going to be high probability setups" (more meaningful than short-term-high inefficiencies). Used the 1-hour BISI/SIBI at the intermediate high and extended it forward.
- "You need to cast them [inefficiencies, OBs, PD arrays] forward as far as 60 days in advance or 60 days in the past. Look back and that frame of 120 days, the market's always dynamically referring to all of that."
- Entry logic: price Judas-swings up above Wednesday's daily high (Thursday was a holiday) and into the upper quadrant of the 1h imbalance + Feb 24 2025 daily CIBI high (confluence); short using rejection block of an up-close candle, CE of that candle, then order block "changing state of delivery". Built "in a premium ahead of a wartime weekend". Pyramid into the high; "I sold the high. I pyramided into the high" (market maker sell model: distribution, then unicorn at the low, second-stage redistribution = fastest delivery).
- Inversion FVG behavior: "Look at the bodies respecting it"; "If you're bearish, you want to see them respecting the lower half only with the bodies and no trespass in the upper half of it when it's bearish and no trespass with the bodies to the lower half of it when it's bullish." Re-entry/add if price retraces to an inversion FVG; the first presented FVG inside 9:31 to 10:00 is also used, extend it forward.
- Opening range gap: RTH open vs prior close (21,961.75 Jun 19 close) gives a premium gap, likely pull back to close it. "I teach that there's a 70% likelihood that the consequent encroachment midpoint of the opening range gap would be hit by 10:00 a.m. Eastern time"; price slammed into the ORG CE on schedule.
- Target ladder shown: gap closure, minor sellside liquidity, Feb 24 daily CIBI low, Wednesday's area, then the NWOG (scaled most off, kept one contract for NWOG high; NWOG low not expected to be taken). Wicks went below the NWOG low but bodies did not: "the bodies tell you the story. The wicks do the damage." He admits being wrong on the wick.
- Execution notes: 4:50 post, live data not replay; limit order fills specific ("the prices that I'm trying to get are very specific") and why he does not trade live in front of followers.
- Journaling/learning: "explain to yourself in commentary over top the chart with annotations as if you knew it was going to happen"; backtest; find one model that works and "bloom where planted"; don't treat concepts as shiny objects.
