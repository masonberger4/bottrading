## Rules in ICT's wording
- Near all-time highs: "constantly look at volume balance [imbalance], fair value gaps, the opens, closes, and try to avoid trying to pick the top. ... once you're seasoned ... you can look for shorts, but they have to be intraday, but the larger scale price runs are going to be more apt to be in line with going higher".
- Mark yesterday's daily open and close; "very close to running out these relatively highs". Run up at 9:30 then break lower = shift in market structure bearish; relative equal lows = minor sellside; then sellside liquidity pool.
- Model 2022 (not Goldbach, not Enigma): "we run the fib from the high to low. We look for it to trade to a premium. That means it's got to go above 50%." Convergence with optimal trade entry: "a 3/4 pullback in an impulsive price leg"; highlight 62% to 79% (OTE) and the 70.5% sweet spot, overlapping an FVG and the first presented FVG after 9:30.
- Unfinished business: sell side left intact on the rally = false-hope rally for bulls, up into the first presented FVG, sells off through the sellside pool to the daily BISI CE. "this sellside liquidity pool here with this low here is unfinished business."
- Grade every higher-time-frame inefficiency (low, lower quadrant, CE, upper quadrant, high) and transpose to the 1m chart: "more sensitive ... more accurate than things like common floor pivots." Premium sensitivity: reaction at the quadrant levels when price is in premium of the array; discount sensitivity when in discount.
- Always include the volume imbalance with the gap ("the algorithm is going to refer to that as well"); VI vs FVG self-correction.
- Definitions: premium wick = any wick above a candle body; discount wick = any wick below the body; grade wicks too (upper quadrant of a premium wick).
- Venom: FVG with price passing through on the upside and downside isolating the range ("convergence of fair value gap and venom").
- PM: "Price trades up into the first presented fair value gap post 2 p.m."; first stage of FOMC delivery 2:00 (initial sell-off at announcement, press conference delays), second stage from 2:30: "usually there's another wave of price action that begins at 2:30 and whatever the high or the low is, usually it'll run for it." Here: severe discount into a daily volume imbalance, then straight rally to the high of day; consolidation after is untradeable.
- FOMC day: "you want to be very surgical"; surgical = take morning move at the low of the inefficiency, can hold for more but not required.
- Dollar index as barometer, he doesn't trade it: bearish until it builds bullish discount arrays and respects them; euro used opposite. EURUSD weekly relative equal highs 1.19087 hit at 2:00, then rejection; tradable only the FVG scalp with exit on break of the low.
- Journal: multiple time frames at minimum daily + trading TF.
