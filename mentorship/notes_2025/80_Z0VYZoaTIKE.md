# 2025 Lecture 80 (Z0VYZoaTIKE): Midnight Opening Range, Jan 7 2025
Full read (10.2k words). Core teaching: a secret-weapon framework for London session in NQ and forex (GBPUSD). Works in every asset class except crypto (no opinion on synthetic indices or Indian markets).
## Summary
Mark the midnight opening range (12:00-12:30 New York), grade the inefficiency/displacement inside it, project standard deviations from it, and use all of it as reference points for the London session (3:30 macro) and the NY session. About 40% of the video is self-promotion; rules below are the mechanical parts only.
## Rules in ICT's wording
1. **Setup (charting).** Electronic trading hours (Globex) on. Chart in New York local time ("everything runs on New York local time"; anyone using other time is "a fraud"). On a 1-minute chart, mark midnight and 12:30. Three required reference points: the open of the midnight candle (the "midnight opening price", plotted dashed black; it is the Power of Three open for the day), the midnight opening range high, and the low (a fourth: highest high and lowest low between the lines). If the midnight candle is a down-close, its body open is the midnight opening price.
2. **Algorithmic opening range = 30 minutes only.** 9:30-10:00 in the morning, and 12:00-12:30 at midnight. "If it's less than 30 minutes it ain't algorithmic."
3. **Purpose.** Gives rough daily high/low boundaries so setups can be taken "in between". ICT will not teach exact daily high/low; you can only anticipate a rough area "a couple times a week". Related to Power of Three daily range construction.
4. **Inside the range, find PD arrays** (month 4 of the 2016 Private Mentorship): inefficiencies, order blocks, breakers. Grade the inefficiency: the largest displacement inside the midnight range is the "first presented displacement" (a PD array itself; "first presented fair value gap").
5. **Gradient levels of that inefficiency:** low, lower quadrant, consequent encroachment (CE, midpoint), upper quadrant, high. Price returns to these; bodies respect them. Volume imbalance rule (from last night's video): if a candle's close is below the next candle's open (volume imbalance), shade from the wick, not the close; the buy/sell-side imbalance sits around the volume imbalance. Sibi/BISI definition repeated: single candle bordered by candles not sharing its range.
6. **Fib projections.** Fib from the midnight range high and low with negative levels: -0.5, -1 (and -2/-2.5/-4 on forex, "6 and a half"-type values appear in the GBPUSD example, unclear in captions). A -0.5 standard deviation is half the range added to the high; -1 is the full range added. Same on the downside from the low. "How hard and fast it goes to that degree of premium or discount is relative to an economic calendar event during London."
7. **Deviations of the inefficiency itself.** Apply the same 0.5 / 1 / 2-style deviations to the first displacement. Above it: 0.5 and -1 levels are where bodies consolidate then leave. Below: 0.5 and -1 below the low; price probes about one deviation outside and returns ("lures traders to go short then goes right back into the middle").
8. **Time.** London macro at 3:30 (forex sweet spot 3:30; he calls it "exactly 3:30"; a 3:29 reaction is seen in GBPUSD). Targets in the examples: London low at 2:41 (London open), 3:30 run higher to -0.5, 5:00 London end. 8:00 NY open killzone for forex traders. 10:00 news (ISM PMI, JOLTS) wick into a breaker/first-presented gap and breakdown.
9. **NY session link (NQ example).** Pre-9:30 run to the midnight range -1 deviation, turtle soup rally then break. Use the RTH opening gap (RTH open vs prior RTH close, a volume imbalance) as another reference. Bodies stop at that volume imbalance, then an **inversion fair value gap** short: price runs through the gap, comes back up to the bottom, respects the midline, then falls. Target in example 21,640 then 21,630; shorts rode through the midnight range ("leaves it aggressively", meaning heavy market, look for lower prices).
10. **Opening range gap framework** (from yesterday's lecture) is derived by the midnight opening range: before the NY session, refer back to it and carry the levels forward. Every significant run starts from such levels.
11. Broker/data: forex.com feed for forex; TradingView paid plan may be needed for electronic hours.
