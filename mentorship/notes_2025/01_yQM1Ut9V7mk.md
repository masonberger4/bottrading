## Rules
- Include overnight/weekend (Globex) trade in market structure and liquidity, not only regular hours.
- Grade a fair value gap with a Fibonacci-style quadrant split: lowest quadrant, equilibrium (consequent encroachment, CE), upper quadrant; also note the actual opening price.
- PD arrays that fail to be traded to while price is bullish, or a discount that is never touched, are information: price "doesn't need to go there" (strength).
- Preferred FVG is one anchored to another PD array (e.g. FVG sitting on an order block).
- Balance price range (BPR): any portion of price action with delivery in both directions; explained via an inefficiency traversed by one candle.
- Workspaces to keep: new-week opening gap (NWOG), new-day opening gap (NDOG), opening range gap (previous settlement to 09:30 open; Friday settlement to Monday 09:30).
- 09:31 candle: minimum criteria for the "first presented" FVG in the opening range; ICT keeps the son (Caleb) on 09:31 only to avoid ambiguity from extreme rules about the first 1-minute candle. [beginner framing]
- A NWOG that trades down and closes ahead of the morning session; deep retracement into the 10:00 hour may be the criteria for silver bullet (said in passing; silver bullet already settled as off).
- Bias for the day framed by a marker candle: close above its halfway point = bullish, upper limit of expectation = that candle's low-quadrant midpoint ("furthest I'd expect today").
- Scale out: after an OTE entry, ~20% of the position left on late in the move (illustrative).
- Stop so close that being hit "is a complete release from concern" (tight stop after entry).
