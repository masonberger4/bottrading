## Rules
- Look for draws on liquidity on the DAILY chart in the direction the monthly and weekly instigate; with a daily/hourly view "you'll never run out of setups". Do not trade every hour.
- Look for a low-risk entry into a market maker buy model (MMBM) or sell model; model 2022, silver bullet etc. are all "low-risk entry" mechanisms.
- Smart money reversal (SMR) entry = turtle soup of a market maker low/high; entry uses consecutive down-close candles inside an inversion FVG after displacement higher.
- MMBM: find the 70.5 level (OTE) of the previous daily range's high/low range.
- Second-stage reaccumulation in an MMBM "never gets a stop hunt": once it forms, price must not trade back through it; if it does, you are wrong and want to be stopped out. Move the stop up under the new swing low as it rallies.
- Keep a study journal; never public.
- Easy objective for anxious/new traders: take a 5x5 style target (a small fixed handle objective); only criteria needed is time availability.
