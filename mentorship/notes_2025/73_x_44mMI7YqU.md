- Delivery: after a rally and market structure shift, a 5-min buy-side imbalance/sell-side inefficiency acts as an inversion FVG; bodies stay near its midpoint. If market gives up the low after proving the inversion FVG, it trades down into the NWOG remaining portion (Friday candle low to high of the candle inside the gap). "Buy side delivery means the market keeps posting higher prices"; a gap needs delivery to close.

- Qualify the inversion FVG: bodies must stay in the lower half / at CE; wicks above are okay ("that's where the damage can be done" but bodies show the signature). "Mohawk": price colors outside the lines.
