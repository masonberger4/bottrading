## Rules in ICT's wording
- Core principle: "When a market is trading at all-time highs, it is more likely that it will continue to post higher all-time highs." No one can call a bull market complete at ATHs: "it's a guess ... you don't know how far they're going to go ... They keep pressing the boundaries of discovery." [repeats L27/L23; supports ATH-RULE A]
- The ATH checklist (slide), in ICT's words:
  1. "Look for price to trade under down close candles' closing prices. These are rejection blocks and they tend to promote new runs higher in price."
  2. "If possible, look for all BISI [buy side imbalance sell side inefficiency] fair value gaps to offer redelivery prior to ..." (cut off in captions).
  3. "Grade all premium candle wicks and anticipate the gradient levels to offer [discount sensitivity]."
  4. "Look through up close candles and refer to the premium candle wicks. They need not be isolated."
  5. "Look for price to trade under up close candles' closing prices. Near all-time highs, markets tend to overshoot previous close redelivery in the form of bear traps."
  6. "Immediate rebalance is typically very strong and is likely to offer discount sensitivity."
  7. "Avoid predicting the reversal high. In other words, simply stay bullish until the market proves to you that it's completely and utterly broke down and reversed."
  [new as a list; items refine L27/L23/L22; supports ATH-RULE A and RANGE-ANCHOR B only partly]
- Money management: "you either let the market go without you and trade something else or you submit to the idea that you want to keep taking buy signals and let money management and good trade management do its job. If you're not overleveraging ... it won't take you out of the game." [new principle; relates TWO-SIDED]
- Bias scope: "everything here is germane to bias ... narrative." Daily chart: sell-side liquidity raid under a low, then gravitate to the prior high; before it, "the highest probability high it's going to trade to" is the short-term high; "we would anticipate this high being taken out." [repeats]
- Overshoot of the prior close (bear trap): "Next candle we open and we trade down below [the prior up-close candle's close]. ... if there was an opening range gap ... many times at all-time highs, trading right back to the previous day's close, it can overshoot it ... gaps tend to rebalance ... then price goes in the direction of the gap. But near all-time highs it tends to create these little bear traps where it doesn't just simply go back to the previous day's close, it goes beyond that and trades lower." Traders "think the market's now topped"; "they may even look at this as relative equal lows"; "Try to avoid that." [new; refines ORG: at ATH the gap fill overshoots; relates ORG-AFTER-CE]
- Immediate rebalance: big up-close candle, next day opens, trades down to the prior close "and then all the way back down to the day before this up close candle to offer an immediate rebalance ... generally you're going to see a very, very strong reaction ... a lot of discount sensitivity ... a very strong discount PD array, and the market's going to immediately launch higher." [repeats immediate rebalance (L57/L75); definition here is return to the prior candle's level]
- Rejection block: "the next day we open below the previous day's close ... that's a rejection block. That closing price here on a down close candle and it's going to be rejected." [repeats]
- Premium candle wick gradient: lower quadrant, CE, upper quadrant ("we don't need the high and the low annotated"); the market opens, reaches the prior close, trades to the 75 lower quadrant, overshoots a little, then takes out the previous two days' highs; another day goes to the 50% (CE) of the premium wick; "keep them annotated because the market's going to go back into those levels again." [repeats; supports WICK-PRIORITY B, LIQ-LOCATION C]
- Gap as trap: "That gap is a trap set for bears." "Most traders ... see that and think, okay, well, it's gap closure. Now it's going to rally because it's bullish. And the market just goes even lower ... goes right back to where I teach you to watch these premium candle wicks. The CE hits it there beautifully and then trades higher." [new]
