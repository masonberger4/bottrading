Source tier: Charter. 2023 wins on conflicts. FX units.

## Summary
Model 5 recipe with FVGs as the point of origin: weekly bias (power of three on the weekly candle, draw = old low / rejection block), then each day stalk an FVG in the correct half of the PREVIOUS day's range, take the first return to it during a kill zone with the institutional order flow entry drill (near edge), target 40-50 pips. Flout SDs on bodies (and wicks) with previous-day confluences within 5 pips; level calibration to the nearest 10/5 level; big-figure sweeps expand 40 pips.

## Rules
- Weekly bias: anticipate the weekly range expansion (power of three: open, modest move against, most of the range in the bias direction); the close is not forecast ~7:53-12:38
- Draw: old low / rejection block (lowest down-close) / sell-side below it; a down-close candle already consumed is not an OB ~13:00-16:16
- Daily: each day stalk an FVG. High-probability bearish FVGs form in the LOWER 50% of the previous day's range; bullish FVGs in the UPPER 50% of the previous day's range ~17:18-19:53, ~32:10
- Entry: first return to that FVG during a kill zone (London or NY; Asian and London close also usable; NY close kill zone tends to be continuation/position entries) ~20:24-22:07, ~56:30
- Entry price = institutional order flow entry drill: the near edge of the FVG (lowest point of a bearish FVG for shorts, highest point of a bullish FVG for longs), not CE, not a full fill ~22:07-25:45
- Objective 40-50 pips per setup; may leave a runner for a one-shot-one-kill week (50-75 pips/week) ~25:45-28:16
- Days: any day; best Tue/Wed (filter to Tue-Thu if desired) ~31:31-33:58
- SDs: plot flout (and CBDR / Asian per Model 5 rules) on bodies and on wicks, include half levels (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5...); no rule that SD n must match SD m; look for any of today's lines within 5 pips of a previous day's line ~24:57-30:30
- Level calibration: round a target to the nearest 10 or 5 level that does NOT pass the actual low when price is coming down to it (old low 1.36677 -> 1.3670); then expect a 10 pip run below it ~20:40-23:47
- Through a big figure (00), expect a 40 pip sweep (not just 10/20/30); mid figure (50) analogous; if neither is crossed, 10 below the calibrated level is enough ~24:20-25:33, ~43:58-44:28
- Old daily lows attract 10 pip runs below them ~31:36
- Use low-hanging-fruit entries/targets; accept some drawdown; don't demand the extreme ~44:28-45:18
