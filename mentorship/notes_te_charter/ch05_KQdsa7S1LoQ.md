## Summary
Written plan for Model 2 (short-term, 50-100 pips/trade): weekly profile from weekly templates + calendar; Tuesday (else Wednesday) entry filtered by the European (02:00) opening price, 15m OTE / PD array converging with <= 3 Asian-range standard deviations; 25 pip stop (50 pip on the stop-order alternative); targets 50 / 75, close at 100; flat at Thursday NY open. Then a live EURUSD example of the week of Apr 4 2022.

## Rules
### Preparation / discovery
- Match the week to one of the weekly templates using the calendar (e.g. high-impact news Tuesday London -> Tuesday-low / Wednesday-low week profile) ~3:00-4:00
- Dealing range = 20 trading days (no Sundays); if price has already worked both sides of the 20-day range (no PD array left), go to 40 days, then 60; if nothing clear in 60, choose another market or sit out ~5:20-7:10
- Draw = old high/low, FVG or liquidity pool in the direction of the weekly bias ~7:10
- Bullish: identify discount arrays below Tuesday's European opening price inside the 20-day range; bearish: premium arrays above it; if no setup Tuesday, same with Wednesday's European open ~9:00-10:30
### Planning
- Want a Judas swing (snappy move opposite to bias into the entry level) at a calendar volatility time; short above the European open, buy below it ~11:00-12:30
- Bearish: short when price rallies into a 15m premium PD array that converges with no more than +3 standard deviations (Asian range, CBDR or flout - pick one), in London or NY open kill zone ~12:30-13:30
- Time objective: the weekly low (bearish week) likely forms in the Thursday NY session; plan the trade to run until then ~13:30
- Target: sell-side below an old daily low or FVG inside the 20-day range that converges (within 5 pips) with a projected standard deviation and frames 50-100 pips ~15:30-17:00
### Execution
- Bullish: longs only at/below Tuesday's European opening price; 15m OTE in the retracement during London or NY kill zone ~18:00
- Alternative (buy strength): after 02:00 EST, buy stop at Tuesday's Asian range high + 1 pip once price has declined under the Asian low and/or European open (the low of day is assumed in); bearish mirror = sell stop at Asian low - 1 pip after 02:00 once the Asian high was taken ~19:00-22:30, ~55:00-59:33
- Limit entry = SD/PD-array convergence -/+ 5 pips (spread); all orders at the same price; stop = array high + 25 pips (limit entry) or 50 pips (stop-order alternative); TP1 +50, TP2 +75; close all at +100 ~13:00-15:16, ~45:52
- One and done: no re-entry after a stop ~14:15
- If Tuesday gives a trade, don't trade Wednesday; Wednesday only if Tuesday consolidated and price has not yet moved ~37:42-38:23
- Hard time exit: close at Thursday NY open whatever the P&L (he notes you can test holding to Friday NY open as your own tweak) ~31:35-32:38
### Management / money
- Stop protocol 25/50/75: at 25% of expected profit reduce stop risk 25%; at 50% reduce 50%; at 75% stop at breakeven or better ~15:16, ~1:01:58
- Same position-size formula, 50% risk cut after a loss, 50% cut after 5 straight wins (see ch03) ~15:48-19:46
### Std deviation usage
- Measure Asian range SDs both on wicks (tails-to-wicks) and on bodies (highest open/close to lowest open/close); he prefers bodies ~38:58-40:42
- The entry may not exceed SD 3 on either measurement ~40:42-41:09
- Kill zone for London entry: 02:00-05:00 NY ~41:46-42:17
