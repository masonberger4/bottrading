## Summary
"Intraday charts are not noise, they are distorted by time." On a 1m chart the MM sell model is stretched because IPA holds price inside dealing ranges for many candles. Tape reading = tracking the micro dealing ranges (highs/lows/bodies of the buy-side reaccumulations) and watching price move in or out of them, ignoring individual candles. Carry the left-side buy areas (down-close candle bodies) across to the right of the smart money reversal; sell inside them. A break above prior accumulation points on the buy side means there is no sell model.

## Rules
- Distortion, not noise: IPA prices by time and price, not by the chart time frame; compress the elongated period mentally ~1:44-2:12, ~15:20-17:16, ~27:13-27:46
- 15m and 60m charts show MM models more cleanly than 1m ~21:04-21:33
- Sell at the consequent encroachment (midpoint) of the single down-close candle on the left side of the smart money reversal, carried right ~13:22-14:22
- Identify the dealing range (range high to the low); within carried-over prior accumulation ranges you can short anywhere, no need for a FVG or up-close candle ~17:16-18:56, ~22:30-23:00
- The distribution "red boxes" are the carried-over reaccumulation ranges, not visual candle clusters ~18:56-19:33
- Range bottom = body close of the last reaccumulation candle; a break away from it = displacement + FVG; sell short at the FVG, target sell-side below equal lows ~37:10-38:20
- If price breaks above previous points of accumulation on the buy side of the curve, there is no sell model ~23:32-23:55
- Targets graded 10/20 pips below the low (1.2270 -> 1.2260 / 1.2250; lows printed 1.2259 / 1.2251) ~38:20-39:04
- Floor-trader tape reading: track new intraday highs/lows, previous day high/low/open/close, pivot numbers ~30:41-31:52
- Timing is the hardest variable; losses usually come from timing, not direction ~25:49-28:17
- Institutions (90% daily/weekly models) are right in long trends and wrong at turning points ~34:44-35:43

## Definitions
- Tape reading: monitoring the ebb and flow of micro dealing ranges as time passes
- Distortion: elongation of a pattern by time when IPA holds price in a range
