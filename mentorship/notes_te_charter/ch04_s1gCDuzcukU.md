## Summary
Model 2 = short-term trading of the weekly range expansion. Stage = weekly direction; setup = range expansion; pattern = opposing PD arrays (FVG, old high/low, liquidity pool). Buy at/below Tuesday's "04:00 platform" (= 00:00 NY standard time, never DST-shifted) opening price on Tue or Wed, 50 pip stop, hold at least to Thursday NY open. Amplified part: intra-week reversal profile after the weekly target is hit at an HTF array, using the 06:00 European open as the filter, and the "lazy" Asian-range-break stop entry.

## Rules
- Weekly draw: bullish week = buy-stop pool, old high or FVG above the weekly open; bearish = FVG, old low or sell-stop pool below. Order blocks may substitute for the old high/low ~2:11-3:56
- Opening price: Tuesday's 04:00 candle on his (GMT+?) platform = midnight New York in standard time; keep that clock fixed all year, ignore daylight saving ~12:12-13:30, ~40:40
- Bullish: buy at or below Tuesday's opening price (ideally at a discount array below it, e.g. a short-term swing low, limit at that low + 5 pips), 50 pip stop; if no fill Tuesday, try Wednesday; no Monday trading ~14:14-19:10
- Hold minimum to Thursday's New York open (time objective); scale at +50 and +100 pips if reached earlier; at Thursday NY open close everything regardless of opinion ~17:33, ~23:24, ~46:09
- Logic: bullish weeks often make the low Monday/Tuesday with a strong up close Tuesday; the model seeks the Tuesday-open-to-Thursday-NY-open portion, 50-100 pips ~24:24, ~38:24
- Time frames: weekly, daily, 1h (15m for timing) ~25:25
### Reversal market profile (amplified)
- When the prior week reached the weekly objective and it is a monthly/weekly premium array (e.g. bearish breaker), stop buying under the 04:00 open; switch to SELLING ABOVE the 06:00 European open (Tue/Wed); bullish reversal mirrors (buy below 06:00 open). If Tuesday itself is the turning point, the setup is Wed/Thu ~23:33-26:50, ~36:51-37:16, ~42:42
- Supporting context: seasonality, scheduled news (Brexit vote), DXY ~29:41-32:35
- Reversal confirmation: on the lower TF, a short-term low before the run into the key level is broken; a WICK through it counts as a break, no close required ~37:37-38:17 [conflict: rulebook requires a close for structure shifts]
- Lazy entry ("sell on weakness"): extend the Asian range; after 06:00 place a sell stop just below the Asian range low; stop = high of the day while the Judas swing forms (widen and cut size if the swing extends) ~41:41-44:35, ~48:43-49:24
- Selling strength alternative: project Asian range standard deviations; he uses half-deviation midlines (50% of the range projected) to time the Judas high ~45:00-55:50
- Extend the 06:00 open line to 09:00 to filter 08:30 spikes ~40:43
- Kill zones are not shifted for daylight saving; instead one hour is added to the back end of the window ~1:00:28
### Key levels
- Calibrate levels to the nearest 5/0 price: coming down to a level round UP to the nearest 5/0 above it; going up to a level round down (to the nearest 5/0 not past it) ~28:00-30:42, ~36:29
- Balanced price range = an up-then-down (or down-then-up) segment that becomes significant; breaking through it targets the pools beyond ~34:21-35:22
- With cascading discount arrays (pool, void, OB) use the OB first (last sell-side delivery) ~35:52
- Gap/imbalance: price need not fill it; turning at its 50% (consequent encroachment) is a stronger signal than a full fill ~58:03-58:50
- After a CE rejection, target equal lows; 10-20 pip projections below them ~59:17
