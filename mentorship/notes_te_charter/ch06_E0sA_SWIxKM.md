## Summary
Model 3 = swing trading (100-300 pips, 1-2 trades/month) attacking daily liquidity pools. Stage = COT commercial hedging program (12-month range midpoint); setup = daily liquidity pool (equal highs/lows or a clean intermediate-term high/low) as the draw; pattern = OTE (4h, fine-tune on 1h). Expect one or more weeks of the monthly range expansion toward the pool; the month need not close in favour.

## Rules
- COT hedging program: take commercials' net position only; over the last 12 months find the highest and lowest reading; midpoint = new zero line; above = commercial buy program (bullish), below = sell program. If the latest reading broke the 12-month extreme, extend the range to it. Alternative windows: 6 months, 2 years, 4 years by horizon (model rule = 12 months) ~5:00-6:43, ~32:06-36:20
- A crossing of the midpoint is a change of tide, not by itself a turning point/entry ~37:25-38:31
- FX pairs quoted vs USD invert the futures COT signal (e.g. bearish CAD futures = bullish USDCAD) ~9:19, ~29:02
- Bearish program + daily sell-side pool (equal lows / clean low) -> hunt OTE shorts toward it; bullish mirror with equal highs / intermediate high ~6:08-8:47
- Monthly framing: from each new month look back 20/40/60 IPDA days for the pool; cast forward 20 days for completion / next setup ~12:43-13:11, ~24:00
- Month open = opening price of the first trading day's Asian session (alternative: midnight of the first trading day; he uses both). Bullish: buy at or below the monthly open after an initial rally then drop ("pseudo Judas swing" on the monthly) ~13:27-14:24
- Entry: 4h OTE (here price ran an initial swing low at the 70.5% level); never lower than 4h for the model; 1h OTE or a Monday-PM-low-to-Tuesday-high OTE can tighten the stop ~14:24-16:55
- Days: Mon, Tue or Wed; kill zones London open and/or NY open ~15:34
- Fib extension levels (negative numbers): -0.27, -0.68(?), -1.27, -1.68, -2.0, -2.27, -2.68, -3.0, -3.27, -3.68, -4.0, -4.27, -4.68, -5.0 ~17:30-18:19
- Scaling: first partial at Target 2 (-2.0?) of the second (smaller) fib as a "reward"; then at each full integer (-2, -3, -4) or -x.27 levels; prefer to scale where a fib level overlaps a run above an old short-term high; target = overlap of two fibs' extensions with the liquidity pool and an institutional round level ~18:55-21:16
- If you cannot clearly call the next monthly candle's direction, there is no swing trade; drop to the short-term model ~28:10-28:34
- Order block is high probability only with an imbalance (FVG/void/volume imbalance) next to it; consecutive down-close candles form the OB, valid once price trades above it meaningfully ~41:44-42:14
- Volume imbalance (wick-only area) is rebalanced on monthly charts too; "two passes" to fill ~41:10
- Stop for the daily OB swing entry: ~10 pips below the low that guards the equal lows ~52:01
- Liquidity pool = anything above clear equal highs / below equal lows; expansions of 10/20/30 (or 5/10/15) pips beyond them; reversals expected after the sweep ~56:22, ~1:01:48
