## Summary
Refines Model 1: instead of waiting for swing points to form, anticipate them. The highest-probability structure is premium + raided high + break of a short-term low (displacement) + return to the breaker (shorts), mirrored in discount for longs. Frame the dealing range on the daily (fallback 4h, then 1h, never lower), OTE on candle bodies, target previous daily highs/lows inside the 20-day IPDA range (40-day once outside it). Aim: one 25-30 pip scalp per week.

## Rules
- Trading plan should fit on a business card: HTF institutional order flow, trade into inefficiencies, exit at external range liquidity, scalping Mon-Wed with an occasional Thursday, always leave a "leader" (runner) when profitable ~3:30-3:58
- Model 1 foundation (buy after a swing high breaks, sell after a swing low breaks) is training wheels; amplified version enters before the swing point forms ~13:19-15:09
- Bearish high-probability structure: price in a premium of the dealing range; a high is raided (buy stops taken / false breakout); a short-term low is taken out; trade back up into the breaker; sell there without waiting for the swing high; any retracement in a premium will be shallow (a 62-79% OTE of that leg "never works" because a deep retrace means continuation higher) ~12:22-15:44
- Bullish mirror in a discount: a low is violated (sell stops run), displacement breaks a high (MSS), return to the breaker/bullish OB at or below 50% of the dealing range = buy. Called the highest-probability structure, "~90% bracket" of best entries; it is the AMD pattern ~16:16-17:31, ~40:58-41:41
- Dealing range: start on the daily; if the daily has no clear structure, use 4h; if not 4h, 1h; nothing lower than 1h ~21:26
- Ranges include wicks; the OTE fib uses BODIES only (lowest body open/close to highest body close) to get the "core volume" ~18:15, ~23:16
- 20/40/60-day IPDA ranges are dynamic, roll forward each day; Sundays not counted ~17:46
- Targets: previous day highs (longs) inside the 20-day range, one by one, until the highest of the last 20 days; once beyond the 20-day range use the 40-day look-back (e.g. rejection block at a 40-day high) ~25:01, ~28:27, ~35:18
- At previous daily highs expect 10/20/30 pip "grade" sweeps beyond the level ~35:44
- Entry day: Mon, Tue or Wed trading into the OTE/breaker at the New York open ~23:57-24:26
- Stay with the bias until the targeted high is run (or the opposing extreme is taken) ~23:57
- Days that open in a liquidity void into a discount and then rally through a swing high: any of the nearby swing highs can be the one that must break ~18:56
- Model 1 goal: one setup per week; 1.5-2% per week compounded; 6%/month framing ~30:36-31:08
- Breaker-to-breaker trading = the core idea ~40:27

## Definitions
- Leader = runner left from a scalp in case of an unexpected expansion ~3:58
- Energetic swing = swing that creates displacement; with the PD matrix applied the swings that form are energetic ~20:25
- Breaker (here): the up-close candles/zone of the raided high after the short-term low breaks ~15:09

## Open questions
- "Return to breaker" depth: any touch of the breaker vs needing 50% of range; he says it may not come back and that's OK (no trade).
- Index mapping of 25-30 pips/week and 10/20/30 pip sweeps.
