## Summary
"Micro scouting" without a directional bias on a 15s chart: long in a small FVG while price is at a premium to the previous day's settlement, stop beneath the down-close candle, add in a volume imbalance, target buy-side just above; then reverse short at a daily bearish FVG / inverted OB with a limit just below the candle bodies, targeting short-term sell-side. Volume imbalances are the most flexible (weakest) PD array.

## Rules
- Without bias you can trade intraday liquidity on very small time frames inside small ranges; the draw must still make sense ~9:41-10:29
- Long: FVG entry, stop beneath the down-close candle; add in a volume imbalance ~0:00-1:15
- Exit: buy-side just above the nearby high; stop to cover costs near target ~2:01-3:03
- Short: limit just below the candle bodies under a daily FVG high (it may not touch the gap high again) ~5:56-6:42
- Volume imbalances are flexible PD arrays: price can trade back and forth over them; don't expect an exact turn ~6:42-7:21
- First partial in the inefficiency between the candle high and next candle low; costs ~$10/contract round trip incl. fees ~7:21-8:04
- Hard-and-fast scalper: no partials, take the first logical liquidity and exit ~9:12-9:41
- An OB that fails becomes an inversion level ~4:03-4:57
