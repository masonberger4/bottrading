# ch15 ICT Charter Price Action Model 6 - Universal Trading Model (BMYrtYMisnA)
Recorded Jul 22 2018 (uploaded 2024-01-22) / GBPUSD weekly + 1h, DXY 1h, 15m / 32 min. Captions only.
Source tier: Charter. 2023 wins on conflicts. FX units.

## Summary
Model 6 = "universal" model (any time frame / style): buy-side low-resistance liquidity runs with fractals. Stage = liquidity draw; setup = buy side of a market maker model; pattern = FVG. Between the moment a draw is identified and its price lies the "range of opportunity"; buy Point 1 (first retracement after the initial impulse, into an FVG / OB of that leg) and Point 2 (secondary retracement) until the draw is absorbed. Market maker sell model (buy side of the curve) and market maker buy model described; ICT says this is the model he trades most.

## Rules
- Range of opportunity = from where you identify the draw (premium array above: bearish OB, old high, FVG / void) to the draw's price ~3:00-4:07
- MM sell model, buy side: base/consolidation (may not be seen), impulse leg up; Point 1 = first retracement into a discount array of that leg (FVG or OB per the PD array matrix); Point 2 = second retracement; target = the premium array/liquidity; then smart money reversal ~6:18-14:14, ~21:24-25:00
- MM buy model: consolidation leaves equal highs (or triple highs / range); price breaks down to a clear discount array (smart money reversal); Point 1 and Point 2 are the retracements on the way back up; target = equal highs / liquidity above the original consolidation ~25:25-29:14
- Required ingredients for the MM buy model: a consolidation that leaves equal highs + a break down into a clear, obvious discount array ~28:44-29:14
- Fractal: the same picture on weekly (position/swing), daily (one-to-two-day legs within the week), 1h (London point 1, London close point 2), 15m (Asian false break, NY rally) ~14:14-21:24
- Use the time frame's style classification to set hold duration; don't let a lower-TF exit shake you out of a higher-TF trade (take partials instead) ~31:04-31:28
- OB with a propulsion candle (a down-close candle that trades into a prior OB and becomes its own OB): price is expected to stay above the mean threshold; enter at the open/high, stop below the propulsion candle or just below the MT ~23:40-25:02
- Rejection block with bodies aligned near the propulsion candle's open adds confluence ~24:06
