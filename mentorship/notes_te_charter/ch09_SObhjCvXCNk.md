## Summary
Repeat of Model 4 (bearish GBP in May) one year later, confirming the 2018 framework. New: NFP as the monthly Judas swing, refining the 145-pip daily-candle-open entry with 4h breaker / FVG entries (~50 pip risk), pyramiding rules (adds only while price is in the premium of the dealing range, decreasing size 5-3-1), how the dealing range is defined (from the initial sell signal, not the stop run), and a measured move from the fulcrum.

## Rules
- Model 4 requires the market structure (old high run with SMT vs EURUSD or DXY failing a lower low); without it, no model 4 ~6:00-8:31
- COT hedging program: 6-month window here; he also uses 12-month and multi-year by horizon; ignore large and small speculators (large specs mirror commercials) ~15:12-18:34
- Quarterly shift: roughly every 3 months there is a significant price swing; know which quarter you are in and which seasonal tendencies are "hot" now ~15:43-16:47
- Build a monthly watch list of the markets with the strongest seasonal tendency for each month (and the best per quarter) = mega trade list; he uses Model 4 every month with the hot seasonal ~31:50-32:48, ~40:18-41:14
- NFP week: NFP often creates the Judas swing (opposite extreme) of the monthly range; a strong one-sided NFP move against the seasonal bias is a candidate for the monthly extreme ~21:35-23:09
- Entry rule (base model): after the old high (any old high inside the 20-day range) is run, sell stop at the open of the last up-close daily candle; stop at the top of that candle ~44:35-46:24, ~55:55
- Targets: lowest low of last 20 days, then 40, then 60; beyond 60 use the next old low outside it ~46:24-48:48
- Refinements on the 4h (not lower for position trades): (a) breaker after price breaks below the low and returns; (b) the 4h FVG after the stop run (one-pass, "single pass" FVG) ~51:21-52:39, ~1:00:55-1:02:01
- A PD array is only meaningful with context (FVG, seasonal, draw after external range liquidity); an up-close candle traded below is not automatically a bearish OB ~52:39-53:04
- Pyramiding: decreasing size per add (e.g. 5, then 3, then 1); each add's stop above its own FVG high; total risk across adds must not exceed max exposure (his 3.5%; students <= 1% and 2% is too much unless 5 years consistently profitable); plan the first entry below max if you intend to add ~1:03:00-1:06:11, ~1:11:13
- Dealing range for adds = from the INITIAL sell signal swing (where distribution starts) to the low, not including the stop-run high; split at 50%; adds/entries only at the midpoint or higher (premium) for shorts; no adds in discount ~1:07:11-1:08:49
- Take the first partial on all positions at the first liquidity target (FIFO workaround: separate positions per entry) ~1:13:23-1:14:30
- Measured move: the parent swing (low to high) projected down from the fulcrum break gives the terminus ~1:15:26-1:15:58
- Position trades can still be refined on the 4h; 5m/15m entries belong to other models ~1:12:09
- Take profits before FOMC (minutes) - can create NFP-style moves ~56:50-57:19

## Trade example (GBPUSD May 2019)
- Old daily high run (any old high in last 20 days) with DXY higher low (SMT) and EURUSD SMT; sell stop at last up-close daily open ~1.3310 (dictated "1331"); stop 145 pips; targets 20/40/60-day lows, ~430 pips available ~44:35-58:54
- 4h refinement: FVG after the NFP Friday run, entry ~1.3125, 50 pip stop, >10:1; further 4h FVG adds; partials at the first sell-side pool; seasonal window ends ~late May/June ~1:00:55-1:14:54

## Definitions
- Dealing range (for adds) = from the swing where the initial sell signal starts delivering lower, to the current low ~1:07:11-1:08:10
- Market maker sell model profile: consolidation, rally, reaccumulation, smart money reversal = low-risk entry ~59:50
