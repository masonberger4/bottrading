## Summary
Model 1 = intraday scalping of previous daily range raids. Buy (sell) New York session 5m OTEs keyed at the 62% level, anticipating a run to a previous day high (low) inside the 20-day IPDA range. First and simplest of the 12 models; prerequisite is "Mastering High Probability Scalping" vol 1-3.

## Rules
- Trader profile: no overnight holds, wants frequent setups, small trades, a basket of markets reviewed the night before ~1:08
- Pattern: OTE in the New York kill zone, targeting previous daily range highs/lows ~2:43
- "Previous day high/low" = ANY prior day's high/low inside the current 20-day IPDA range, not only yesterday's; key lows = swing lows, the low of a strong up move, relatively equal lows ~3:19-6:46
- Ignore Sunday candles when counting the 20/40/60-day IPDA look-back ~5:44
- Buy program: only after the daily has taken out a swing high in the last 20 days and price is NOT in a premium of that range; buying at equilibrium of the 20-day range is acceptable; parabolic expansion is expected above EQ as price targets a previous day high and the liquidity above it ~12:30
- Sell program mirrors: daily took out a 20-day swing low, price not in discount; shorting at EQ acceptable; expansion expected below EQ toward a previous day low ~17:45
- Days: Mon-Wed ideal; Thursday only if the daily liquidity pool is still untaken (low-resistance run left); if trading Thursday, cut size to 25-40% of normal ~13:41
- Entry window 07:00-11:00 NY, 5m chart. Long: retracement lower against the London session's momentum; fib the NY initial swing; entry at 62% ONLY (not 70.5 / 79), plus 5 pips spread for longs (minus 5 for shorts) ~14:09, ~19:37
- Stop: long = the low between 07:00 and 10:00 NY (the fib anchor) or 5 pips below; short = the high 07:00-10:00 or 5 pips above ~15:50, ~20:40
- Do not move the stop until 20 pips have been scaled out; then lock in 5-10 pips; also move it once price exceeds the NY initial high (long) / low (short) the fib was anchored on ~15:50
- If stopped out: no re-entry that day; wait for another NY session (stop-out suggests momentum weakening) ~16:20, ~22:19
- Targets: scale 1 just before the initial high (low) of the day or at it; scale 2 at fib Target 1; scale 3 at fib Target 2; if news is due late NY (post noon), leave a small runner for the symmetrical price swing, and close everything if it is hit ~16:31
- Symmetrical price swing usually caps the daily range, especially when time overlaps London close or 14:00-15:00 (bond close); only a few times a year it runs past ~17:02
- Following days: keep the same protocol until the daily liquidity pool is reached or swept; don't fight the daily order flow ~22:19
- Swing projection: anchor the fib on the premium build (e.g., Friday low to Monday high, not off equal lows), project the symmetrical swing; grade the swing in four quadrants (start, first grade, EQ, third grade, terminus); scalps between quadrants, better when aligned with NY session ~24:25-26:27

## Trade examples (GBPUSD, week of Feb 26 2018)
- Daily: rejection block run (higher high in bodies, turtle soup), DXY failed to make a comparable lower low (SMT), MSS, retrace into breaker and bearish OB; target relatively equal lows (day 9 / day 13 lows). Sunday-night call: weekly high near the 1h bearish OTE.
- 5m shorts: NY initial high, retrace to 62% minus 5 pips, stop at NY high, hit symmetrical price swing ~23:45.

## Definitions
- PDL/PDH (Charter) = a previous day's low/high inside the 20-day IPDA range ~10:24
- Buy/sell program = the one-sided state that permits only longs / only shorts ~12:30
- Parabolic expansion run = fast, large candles toward the liquidity pool once past 20-day EQ ~19:02
