## Summary
Model 5 = session day trading of intraday volatility expansions. Stage = HTF (daily) liquidity draw for bias; setup = intraday volatility expansions; pattern = session swings with power of three. Trade only the London and/or NY kill-zone move in the direction of the daily draw. Core new content: which range to project standard deviations from (CBDR if 15-plus pips and ranging, else Asian range if 20-40 pips, else flout), measured on 15m, and the "Grail": confluence of today's SD levels with the PREVIOUS days' SD levels (3-5 pip tolerance) at kill-zone times marks the turning points.

## Rules
- Trade the session move (London open or NY open kill zone), not the whole daily or weekly range; Asian session possible but weaker; he prefers Mon/Tue/Wed ~4:25-7:00, ~11:11-12:00
- Bias: the daily chart's most probable draw (equal lows/highs, rejection block, sell/buy-stop pool) incl. intraday/intra-week equal highs/lows; daily ranges are expected to expand toward it; the daily close need not agree ~10:27-14:24
- Mind nearer pools on the way (e.g. an old low before the main target): runs into them can stall or reverse the week ~9:16-9:55
- Range selection for SD projections:
  - CBDR = 16:00-20:00 NY; use only if it is a clear trading range (not trending) and > 15 pips ~16:00-16:33
  - Asian range = 20:00-00:00 NY; use only if ranging and >= 20 pips; avoid if > 40 pips ~16:33-17:59
  - Flout = when neither qualifies: 16:00-00:00 NY range (he uses 20:00-04:00 platform time), projected in 50% increments of the range ~17:59-18:29, ~37:50
- Measure both bodies and wicks (two measurements) and look for confluence between them ~29:48
- Measure on the 15m chart only (bodies are the most consistent there; 1h skews) ~26:31-27:58
- SD expansion levels count more when they overlap previous day high/low, previous intraday highs/lows (e.g. London's high/low while trading NY) ~18:29-19:02
- Key opening prices: the "new day" divider (20:00 NY on his platform) and midnight NY; sell above the opening price when bearish (power of three) ~31:25-31:54
- Turning-point rule ("the Grail"): plot each day's SD levels and KEEP previous days' levels on the chart; a level where today's SD overlaps a previous day's SD (within 3-5 pips), at a kill zone time, and/or 10/20/30 pips above an intraday high = high-probability turning point / profit-taking level ~42:40-44:18, ~1:00:00-55:04
- Session exit: get in during London, out toward London's end / before NY open, because NY often reverses or reloads ~28:50-29:18
- Late-day fade: on a day rallying against bias, a turtle soup sell above an old low/high level in the NY/London close overlap is a scalp into Asia ~39:34-40:45
- HTF caution: DXY reaching a monthly bearish OB / running daily equal highs on a Thursday = reversal risk -> day trade only, don't hold for the weekly range (Thursday reversal profile) ~23:32-26:01
- Entry pattern is plug-and-play (OTE, turtle soup, FVG, breaker, OB, above/below the opening price) ~19:34-21:04
- Discretion (not for the bot): keep hit rate around 65-70% publicly, throw some trades to avoid broker scrutiny ~32:23-36:51 [not a trading rule]
