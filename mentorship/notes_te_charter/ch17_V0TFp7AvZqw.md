## Summary
Worked Model 6 example: seasonal (DXY low late Sep / early Oct; CHF high) + COT hedging program (12-month commercials sell program on CHF futures) + PD arrays top-down on USDCHF (monthly OB mean threshold ~0.9550, weekly bullish OB open 0.9547, low 0.9542). On the buy side of the curve, the up-close candles of the sell side (left of the reversal) become support where shorts are mitigated; runs below short-term lows inside the bullish narrative are turtle-soup buys, not structure breaks.

## Rules
- Seasonals: trust them most when the 15-year and 33-year averages agree on a strong turn; a seasonal gives a window (e.g. Sep 21 to mid-Oct), not a date ~1:44-5:10
- COT: commercials only; 12-month (sometimes 6-month) range midpoint for the near-term quarterly shift; also 2- and 4-year ranges; large specs are right in trends, wrong at tops/bottoms; commercials call the turns ~10:25-14:27, ~20:50-21:51
- Top-down: monthly discount (retrace into the MT of the last down-close OB after equal lows were cleared) -> draw = equal highs above; once sell-side was already cleared, don't expect a deeper retrace into it ~14:31-16:21
- Weekly bullish OB: use the BODY open of the single down-close candle (0.9547), not the wick, when wicks have already traded through ~17:29-18:34
- A market maker sell model doesn't always clear the original consolidation; with bias bullish it can re-accumulate early ~19:04-20:07
- Buy side of the curve: up-close candles on the sell side (left of the low) are where shorts were opened; on the right side they act as support (mitigation) - buy returns into them ~22:19-24:33, ~32:45-33:10
- In a bullish HTF narrative, a run below a short-term low is NOT a bearish MSS - it is a turtle soup long (buying sell stops) as long as the HTF buy-side pools are intact ~25:07-26:06, ~34:18-35:45
- Stop placement: below a low that has been defended twice after a stop run ~26:40-27:06
- Day-trade read: in this phase large-range days open near their low (small lower wick, most of the range up) - power of three buys ~27:06-27:41
- Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday buys; Wednesday stop runs of Tuesday lows are buys; Thursday consolidation ~34:18-35:19
- 4h entry: bullish OB open 0.9575 / high 0.9578; low printed exactly 0.9578 ~30:44-31:49
- Three pillars: smart money (COT), HTF narrative (seasonal + liquidity), institutional order flow (PD arrays) + MM models ~27:41-28:15
