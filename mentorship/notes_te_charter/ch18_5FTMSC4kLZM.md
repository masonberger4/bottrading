## Rules
- MM models are fractal: same picture on weekly for position trades ~2:24-3:05
- Buy-side zones = the sell-side curve's distribution areas (up-close candles) projected across the reversal; not Wyckoff, not supply/demand zones ~3:05-5:04
- Target = buy-side liquidity above the original consolidation's equal highs ~5:04-5:52
- Accumulation after the rebalance can last a long time on the weekly; then large ranges expand quickly ~6:31-8:15
- Daily entry: after a big up candle, return to the bullish OB containing a small FVG; institutional order flow entry drill = buy at the FVG's near edge (candle low 1330.6 vs actual low 1329.6, one point into the FVG) ~8:53-9:55
- Re-accumulate/add at the next bullish OB inside the zone, then expect expansion above the original consolidation ~9:55-10:32
- Gold vs DXY: normally inverse; both can run buy-side at once when DXY stays inside its range; a DXY rally above its weekly SIBI (e.g. 98.40) would be bearish gold ~10:32-13:05, ~15:51-17:22
- Futures contract choice: trade the contract with the largest open interest (gold: roll to October early) ~13:27-14:43
- Futures vs cash charts can differ (volume imbalance on one, FVG on the other); both rebalance precisely ~18:18-20:14
- When uncertain about correlated markets in consolidation, say "I don't know" and wait for specific criteria ~17:22-17:47
