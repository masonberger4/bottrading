## Summary
Model 4 = position trading (weeks to months) on quarterly shifts and seasonal tendencies. Stage = quarterly shift overlapping a seasonal tendency; setup = SMT divergence + COT hedging program (6-month look back here); pattern = external range liquidity (old high run inside the 20/40/60-day range). Entry = sell stop at the OPEN of the last up-close daily candle (bearish OB) after the old high is run; stop at the high; targets old lows (discount arrays).

## Rules
- Bearish version: seasonal tendency bearish for the coming month; COT commercials net short vs their 6-month range (hedging program); daily runs an old high inside the 20/40/60-day IPDA range by at least 1 pip; SMT divergence with DXY or a correlated pair at the same time ~0:38-1:57, ~7:00-7:58
- Bullish mirror: bullish seasonal + commercials net long (6-month range) + old low taken out with SMT; target daily equal highs / buy-stop pool ~2:26-3:06
- Seasonal windows are not locked to calendar days; he uses ~mid-April to 2nd week of June for the GBP May decline ~5:58
- Entry: after the old high is run, place a SELL STOP at the opening price of the last up-close daily candle (the bearish OB open); no kill zones or intraday monitoring needed ~9:05-9:59
- Stop: at the high (here 130 pips) ~10:36
- Targets: old lows (discount arrays) on the daily, with the monthly as the expansion range; first old low gave 5:1, second 8:1 ~11:02-11:40
- Time and price: the move's terminus = the sell-stop pool meeting the end of the seasonal window; if the pool is reached early with seasonal time left, expect more ~12:11-13:07
- The monthly candle of the season is expected to expand (one or more of its four weeks); close direction not required ~8:00-8:31
