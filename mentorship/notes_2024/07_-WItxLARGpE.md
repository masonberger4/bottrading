## Rules
- NFP/CPI/PPI days: don't trade the release itself; wait for the opening bell and usually the 10:00 window (SB is OFF in our rulebook, see conflicts); don't frame initial buy-side/sell-side before the report; frame 07:00-09:30 only after it ~early
- HTF levels first: mark weekly and daily SIBI/BISI, CE, candle lows; the session run often targets them (here one-shot 09:30 → daily → weekly level) ~early
- Don't chase extended ("long in the tooth") moves; buy only in a discount, after a retracement to a predetermined PD array or pool ~early
- Three drives ("three Indians", Street Smarts): measure from the low to the 3rd high; first target = 50% of that range; objective = the low ~early
- Problem days ("kryptonite"): one-sided grinding, compressed, stilted, choppy, back-and-forth (e.g., post-CPI/PPI, election-year supported grind) → stand aside; "this is not a swing trader market" ~1:24:00-1:44:00, ~3:18:00
  - If already in on such a day: move the stop, check back ~15:30, take a large partial or close ~early
  - If you get one win on a hard-read day, take it and leave ~2:42:00
  - Close the stream / stop looking if no setup by ~10:20 or price makes a higher high (for this day) ~early
  - "There has to be something that frames a day you are not allowed to touch; it cannot be open season every day" ~3:30:00
- High probability = very hard to frame the other side; if you can paint both scenarios (smooth highs above AND jagged lows), it's not high probability ~1:24:00
- Minimum range: needs ~20 handles of obvious draw so you can take 10-15; otherwise no trade ~3:10:00, ~2:42:00
- Stop management (short example): if a candle CLOSES above the level used as stop reference, kill the trade before the stop is hit ("save the stop") ~1:52:00
- BREAKAWAY GAP: an FVG formed as price LEAVES a consolidation after a stop run (stops below taken, then trades above the high) tends to stay open → acts as support/resistance ~2:04:00-2:10:00, ~2:26:00
- "I want to see this stay open/unfilled" = the gap should hold; "I want to see it close in" = expected to fail (inversion) ~2:04:00
- IOFED (Institutional Order Flow Entry Drill): a partial entry into an FVG you believe will NOT fully fill; for a long (BISI), limit at the gap's upper edge (the LOW of the third candle) or one tick above it; only one tick of trade into the gap is needed ~2:02:00-2:10:00, ~2:18:00-2:26:00
  - Best fills: CE to the top of the gap; least probable: lower half (spread may not fill you) ~2:24:00
  - Stop: below the gap (beyond the candle on the far side); accept drawdown down to CE or the gap's far edge ~2:22:00
  - IOFED is the go-to entry ONLY when ICT says a gap should stay open (breakaway); otherwise it's an optional personal entry ~2:28:00
  - Example: limit 18,931.00, target daily VI low ~18,956 / CE ~18,960-ish (ICT eyeballs ~15-30 handles); "bread and butter" ~2:42:00
- Position build (pyramid by levels, not funded-account friendly): 6 lots at IOFED, +4 at CE/upper quadrant, +5 one tick below CE (same assumption the lower half stays open) ~2:28:00-2:30:00
  - Funded accounts that forbid averaging-in: single IOFED entry, then add only at higher PD arrays (pyramid) ~2:30:00
- CONSECUTIVE-CANDLE ORDER BLOCK (taught publicly for the first time): with several consecutive down-close candles before the up-move, the OB is all of them; the key level is the OPENING PRICE of the first (highest) one = change in state of delivery ~2:31:00-2:39:00, ~2:52:00-2:55:00
  - Requires a supporting PD array (an FVG below that should stay open); "an order block is never an island unto itself" ~2:32:00, ~2:34:00
  - Validated when that opening price is traded through to the upside ~2:32:00
  - Range for the narrative: from that open to the close of the lowest down-close candle; bodies should stay in the UPPER half; wicks may do the damage ~2:34:00-2:36:00
  - Extend the open forward through the macro (to 11:10); returns to it are entries ~2:52:00
- MACROS (hourly): every hour, xx:50 to xx:10 (20 min), price spools to liquidity (short-term high/low) or to an inefficiency (FVG) ~2:40:00-2:50:00, ~2:58:00-3:08:00
  - Order of preference: if there's no FVG, use the order block; else a short-term high/low; all three never coexist at the same price/time ~2:48:00-2:50:00
  - Macros are a "supercharger"; entries outside them are allowed but need more skill ~2:54:00, ~3:14:00
  - Also: 00:00-01:00 macro and "final hour of the day" macros (detail reserved for his book) ~3:08:00
  - Time is always New York local time; set alarms ~10:45 or vertical lines ~2:58:00
  - No "90-minute cycles"; 90 min is just the minimum window, guaranteeing at least one macro ~3:02:00
- Daily reference levels (algorithm reference points, not S/R): previous day high/low, last 3 days' highs/lows, last week's high/low, and the upper quadrant / midpoint / lower quadrant of those ranges ~2:40:00
- Manual intervention (FOMC 14:00 and 14:30): abrupt, one-sided; trade after it forms ~2:46:00-2:48:00
- Immediate rebalance on daily VI: wick-to-wick separation; ICT wanted the 1m gap to stay open until price reached the daily VI CE ~1:32:00, ~1:54:00

## Definitions
- IOFED: limit order at the near edge of an FVG expected not to fill; one tick of trade into the gap fills it.
- Breakaway gap: FVG created as price leaves a consolidation after a stop run; tends to stay open.
- Consecutive-candle OB: opening price of the first of consecutive opposite-close candles before displacement.
- Macro: 20-minute window xx:50-xx:10 each hour.
