## Rules
- MULTIPLE 08:30 REPORTS [new]: with several reports, give it time to take both sides; staggered rally/decline; with a single high/medium report the first run is usually the Judas swing ~18:00-19:00 (NEWS-WAIT)
- POST-NEWS STALL: price held inside the range made at 08:30 (no higher high, no lower low, lots of wicks) = manual intervention; sit still ~25:00-30:00
- PRE-09:30 POSITION: on high/medium news days ICT normally likes a position on before the 09:30 bell, if something qualifies ~27:00 (PREMARKET-ENTRY)
- FRAMING AN INEFFICIENCY WITH VIs/DOJIS [new]: when an FVG sits next to a volume imbalance or indecisive (open = close) candles, extend the zone to the lowest open/close (bullish case) of those bodies; a plain FVG frame overshoots ~19:00-21:00
  - Always include the adjoining volume imbalance in the FVG box ~14:00
- VOLUME IMBALANCE RULES: most flexible PD array; first expectation is that price trades through it; if one candle lays a body over it up and another over it down, it is efficiently delivered ("paint roller"), treat as balanced; less precision than an FVG's CE/high; the macro timing is what makes it hold ~35:00-40:00
  - Use VIs to start positioning early in your bias, then add or manage the stop on them when the macro starts the run ~39:00 (EXIT-STYLE / pyramiding)
- PROPULSION BLOCK [in rulebook?]: an OB that price retraces into after the first run can be traded again; adds as price dips into it ~16:00
- LONDON FALSE BREAK: false break of a short-term high (breakout longs stopped), then the real move; love seeing it in London ~12:00-13:00
- RELATIVE EQUAL HIGHS: nested REHs are the obvious initial draw until price proves otherwise; price can stop at the CE between REHs and the higher high, leaving the high, then reverse ~10:00, ~22:00-25:00
- ORG SCENARIO PLAN: decide by the 09:30 opening price; premium gap = 70% to mid-gap in 30 minutes; if price rips above a key level before 09:30, treat that run as the Judas, then expect mid-gap / 3/4 / full closure ~44:00-49:00 (ORG-CE-WINDOW)
