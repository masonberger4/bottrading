## Rules
- QUIET-DAY RULE: with no economic news (high/medium impact) that day, demand absolutely clean price action; with a news driver (08:30 or 10:00 "smoke screen"), sloppier action is acceptable; wild back-and-forth after news = no trade either ~28:00-31:00
  - In a PPI/CPI week, trade Monday and Tuesday to avoid the report volatility; after a report, give it 15-20 minutes, then see what's left ~11:00-12:00, ~34:00-35:00
  - Don't rush the first setup of the first 10-20 minutes on a Monday in a big-news week ~15:00
- ORG MID-GAP: 70% of the time the half-gap level is traded to in the first 30 minutes; 70% is not 100%; gaps can stay open for days ~27:00, ~33:00
  - Setup frame: on a premium ORG, any move below a short-term low sets up a trade to half the gap ~27:00
- TOO EXPENSIVE (restated L20): a large premium ORG already prices in an expensive level; don't buy it; wait for discount; "I have to buy discount" ~22:00-24:00, ~45:00-46:00
- ENGINEERING LIQUIDITY at the open: repeated short-term highs each taken by just a little, without speed = smart money selling to the buy stops; wait for speed after the latest swing high fails before selling ~32:00-34:00, ~1:31
  - Don't sell short just because buy stops ran; see the delivery after ~37:00

- SHORT FRAME: bearish PD array (FVG, OB, small breaker, IOFED) forming between the breaker candle's low and the ORG upper quadrant (or 3/4 to half of the distance between its upper and lower quadrants); target the mid-gap; then the lower quadrant ~21:00-22:00, ~49:00, ~1:29-1:32
