## Rules
- DISPLACEMENT OFF THE PD ARRAY: drawing a level is not a setup; wait for displacement away from it (or a stop run into it then a hard drop/rally) before entering ~8:00, ~10:00, ~32:00
- CE TOUCH ONCE: price should hit an FVG's CE once and leave; a second return to CE = likely failure; don't want price sitting inside any gap ~48:00, ~59:00
  - Bearish: once price reaches CE and leaves, the upper half should not trade again, and CE should not be revisited until the low is taken; "could be a model in and of itself" ~1:00
  - Bullish mirror: may trade into the upper half, touch CE, wick through it, but better if the lower portion holds ~51:00
  - A 1m body closing below the gap invalidates it even if 15s bodies held ~43:00
- CALEB'S STRONGEST MODEL: a short-term high taken, then a short-term low taken (both sides), then a rally using an FVG or inversion FVG ~16:00 [new]
