## Summary
After the 08:30 CPI spike, NQ ran into the July 28 NWOG (the upside objective named the day before), then the change in state of delivery at the opening price of a consecutive-candle bearish order block flipped it to a sell program. ICT teaches: (1) measure every swing-point wick and use its CE (midpoint) like a gap; (2) when the 17:00 settlement equals the 18:00 open, mark the single "6 PM opening price" line; (3) the two forms of turtle soup: a reversal/change in state of delivery, and an entry technique inside an existing run (every FVG entry is a turtle soup); (4) baseline FVG entry and stop, plus an advanced tight stop at the candle-1 wick CE + 2 ticks; (5) a quarter-range projection target beyond an old low.

## Rules
- WICK CE: on every swing high/low with a wick, measure the wick (body edge to extreme) and mark its CE; treat the wick as a gap ~5:00-6:00, ~14:00-16:00, ~1:16:00
  - If price can't reach a wick's CE (like an FVG that can't reach its CE), it likely wants the other direction ~6:00
  - Wick quadrants (upper/lower quarter) are targets; e.g., after the 08:30 news wick, the lower quadrant was the next target ~12:00, ~42:00
  - Bodies should not close beyond a premium wick CE; if a candle backs off there, expect the next candle to make a lower high ~30:00
  - Applies on all timeframes ~50:00
- 6 PM OPENING PRICE: if the 17:00 close/settlement equals the 18:00 first print (no NDOG), draw that single price; it acts like a NDOG reference (traded through and revisited). Use the 18:00 price, not 17:00 ~3:00, ~9:00, ~1:44:00, ~1:52:00, ~2:32:00
- Most likely retracement level when several layers converge (FVG high + 6 PM price + wick CE): err on the side of the single price level before the FVG ~1:52:00
- CONSECUTIVE-CANDLE ORDER BLOCK (bearish): the opening price of the first of consecutive up-close candles = change in state of delivery from buy-side to sell-side delivery; crossing it is enough: "I don't need to see it close below it" ~1:18:00-1:30:00
  - Bodies tell the narrative; wicks may "color outside the lines"; returns stay below the OB mean threshold ~1:28:00-1:30:00
  - After the change in state of delivery, every premium array is a short candidate; arrays from the buy side of the curve that converge with new sell-side arrays carry the most weight (inversion) ~1:38:00-1:40:00, ~2:10:00
- If an FVG lies inside an order block's range, use the ORDER BLOCK range, not the gap ~1:56:00
  - First FVG of the new sell program that is still inside that OB range: ignore it ~1:58:00
- BALANCED PRICE RANGE INSIDE AN FVG (new): when candle 3's body overlaps candle 1's body flush (no wick between), that overlap is a BPR inside the FVG and is the real level ~2:28:00-2:31:00
  - "Mohawk": a wick poking just past a PD array because of a BPR; most are anticipatable (detail withheld) ~2:02:00
- TURTLE SOUP, two forms only (not the Street Smarts 20-day pattern) ~1:10:00, ~2:22:00-2:28:00, ~3:32:00
  1. Reversal / change in state of delivery: runs stops above highs into a known premium objective (e.g., NWOG) and breaks down; breakers are usually this kind ~2:54:00
  2. Entry technique inside an existing sell/buy program: every FVG entry is a turtle soup, since you sell above an old candle high (candle 3's high in a SIBI) ~2:28:00, ~2:46:00, ~2:54:00
  - To know how far a stop run can go: keep NWOGs (5 weeks) and NDOGs (5 days) on the chart as the baseline ~2:22:00
- FVG ENTRY (short in SIBI): limit at the gap's near edge (candle 3's high) or one tick above = first 6 contracts; +4 at CE; add again on a later CE test ~2:12:00, ~2:50:00
  - Allowed: price may fill to the top of the gap ("permissible"); better if half or less ~2:52:00
  - Baseline stop: above candle 1's high ~2:56:00-3:00:00
  - Advanced tight stop: if candle 1 has a wick above the gap (a premium array), stop = CE of that wick + 2 ticks ~3:02:00-3:06:00
- IMMEDIATE RESPONSE: if the far half of the gap stays open, price must accelerate away; if the gap fills completely, it must immediately give speed, distance, and large-range candles, or the idea is suspect ~3:02:00, ~3:06:00
- BEARISH OB (single up-close candle in a sell program, e.g., at the wick CE): stop above the OB's high (or the wick above it) + 1 tick; if a spike runs into the CE of that wick and you're stopped, re-enter there ~3:08:00-3:12:00
- TARGETS: rejection block (lowest body close) as a partial; wick CE/quadrants; quarter-range projection: take the range (swing high to the low) and project 25% beyond the low = "low hanging fruit" target below old lows ~1:46:00, ~2:40:00-2:46:00
  - Standard deviations (2017 mentorship) give tighter exits ~2:44:00
- WICK FORECAST WHEN A RANGE IS BEING HELD: if you believe highs are being held (liquidity engineered for later, e.g., 09:30/10:00), the next candle's high tends to stop at the CE of the prior candle's new wick ~3:16:00-3:20:00
- NEWS DAYS (CPI/PPI/FOMC/NFP): manually intervened; price may fall short of, or overshoot, the objective; don't trade at the release; trading 15-30 minutes after (even NFP) is fine; afternoon is fine ~3:12:00-3:16:00
  - On these days relative equal highs/lows aren't required; target the liquidity of traders who profited on the news run ~3:38:00
- NO BIAS PRE-SESSION: wait for the 09:30-10:00 opening range; use jagged vs smooth to decide ~3:38:00
- Going in without a known draw and forcing a trade: ~80% chance of a losing day ~3:34:00
- An open trade can retrace ~50-60% of unrealized profit (back toward entry) as normal; not a reason to exit ~4:06:00
- Opening range = 30 minutes after 09:30 (to 10:00); watch which of two FVGs is used ~6:00

## Definitions
- Wick CE: midpoint of a candle's wick (body edge to extreme).
- 6 PM opening price: first print at 18:00 ET when it equals the 17:00 close.
- Sell-side delivery / buy-side delivery: algorithm offering lower/higher prices (not liquidity).
- Price delivery continuum: drop to a lower timeframe to find later entries in a run already underway.
