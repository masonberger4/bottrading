## Rules
- NFP WEEK: trade Monday and Tuesday (cleanest), Wednesday 50/50, Thursday potluck; new students: Wednesday only before 09:00 (if no trade by 09:00, do nothing), Wednesday after 11:00 very challenging, Thursday and Friday no touch ~3:00-4:00, ~6:00-7:00, ~1:50 (NFP-THU-FRI, MONDAY)
  - In NFP weeks don't expect a trending day; expect range-bound, stop-runs both ways ~22:00
  - Also less active in FOMC, CPI and PPI weeks ~34:00
- HIGH-IMPACT NEWS PROTOCOL: FOMC (14:00/14:30 two-stage), CPI, PPI, NFP (08:30) are the "Destroyers"; never trade ahead; after the release wait minimum 15 minutes, then study 1m and sub-1m; trade only when PD arrays (FVG CEs) are respected again; FVGs traded straight through = still manipulated, stay out; medium-impact news can be traded ~38:00-44:00, ~1:50-1:51 (NEWS-WAIT)
- CALENDAR PLANNING: report on Thu/Fri = best trading Mon/Tue; report on Mon/Tue = do nothing early in the week, easier later; combine with the 2017 weekly profiles ~1:51-1:52 (CPI-WEEK)
- LIQUIDITY FIRST: before a significant run (30-50+ handles, not 10-20) expect a liquidity grab (relative equal lows) first; trust discount arrays more after it ~19:00-20:00
- OB MEAN THRESHOLD: if a candle on the OB's own timeframe closes a body below the bullish OB's mean threshold, it is less likely to hold; ideal = price stays in the upper half ~27:00 (OB-VALID)
- STOP OPTIONS: enter on the first FVG with a wider stop (above the higher FVG), or wait for price to stab into the higher FVG and enter there; new students need wider stops ~58:00 (STOP-PLACE)
  - Model 2022 stacked FVGs: allow price to stab into the higher one; a third gap that is breakaway need not be reached; bodies should stay in the lower half ~1:38-1:39
- INVERSION FVG: a bullish FVG traded below becomes bearish inversion; want price to stay heavy at its CE or low, not explore the upper half; it is not meant to hold first time, it should stop price then let it run, and the later FVG is the entry ~1:03, ~1:10
- ORG BIAS [beginner]: the opening gap is the initial bias for the first 30 minutes; gap down = look up to the mid-gap; mid-gap reached ~70% ~1:24-1:25, ~1:37 (ORG-CE-WINDOW)
- ORG SIZE: at least 40 handles = ICT's minimum interest in the first 30 minutes; larger = more interest and most leverage; 75 handles "really good" ~1:26 (ORG-SIZE)
- ORG TURTLE SOUP ENTRY [new]: gap down, price breaks a minor low; take the down-close candle that pierced the low (an OB), place a buy stop at its opening price; target the ORG mid-gap CE; not filled if price keeps falling; "mechanical, back test it" ~1:26-1:28
