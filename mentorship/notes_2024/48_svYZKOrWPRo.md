The core lesson: define the dealing range (high to low of the swing), grade it with quadrants; when bullish, only FVGs/OBs with a portion at or below the lower quadrant / equilibrium are proper candidates; pyramiding is limited to at or below equilibrium; arrays above the upper quadrant are for management only. In low resistance an FVG should only be partly filled (IOFED); stops can sit just below the entry candle.

## Rules
- LARGE RANGE DAY [new definition]: range larger than the average of the last three days; ~90% of large range days are low resistance liquidity runs; small days are mostly high resistance ~8:00-9:00, ~1:39 (AFTER-BIG-DAY)
  - New students avoid the AM session after a large range day; ICT lost twice doing it ~7:00-8:00, ~44:00
- INSIDE DAY / NR7 [new]: an inside day, or the smallest range of the last seven days, is a precursor to expansion; gives no direction, needs HTF order flow ~9:00-12:00
- SPLIT NEWS (08:30 and 10:00): can create a whipsaw where the first run is fake (like FOMC 14:00 vs 14:30); wait a few minutes (worst case 15) after the 10:00 release before a 10:00 SB / FVG entry; filtered out when the bias is clear ~4:00-5:00, ~28:00-29:00, ~37:00 (NEWS-WAIT)
- 08:30 ALGORITHM: at 08:30 the algorithm starts whether there's news or not; if nothing runs, wait for 09:30 ~1:12-1:13
  - 08:30 is the only 30-minute macro; others :50-:10 each hour; last hour 15:15-15:45 ~1:12
- MIDNIGHT OPEN: when bullish, entries below the 00:00 opening price are where smart money accumulates ~28:00
- DEALING RANGE PICKER [new]: draw the dealing range high to low, mark equilibrium and quadrants (OTE: 0.62 / 0.705 / 0.79 levels) ~1:08-1:09
  - Bullish: a candidate FVG or OB must have a portion at or below the lower quadrant (and equilibrium); lowest risk, highest R entries are at or below equilibrium; pyramid only at or below it ~1:08, ~1:12, ~1:14
  - Arrays above the upper quadrant are management only, not entries ~1:24
  - Bearish mirror (at or above the upper quadrant / equilibrium)
- IOFED [in rulebook]: when an FVG's CE coincides with dealing-range equilibrium in low resistance conditions, expect only a partial fill: limit at its CE, or at the upper quadrant of the FVG + 1 tick ~1:21-1:23
  - In low resistance a valid FVG should not fully close; the lower gap stays open (breakaway) ~1:24-1:25
  - Stop can be just below the entry candle's high/low (ultra tight) because the lower part of the FVG won't trade ~1:26
- OB DEFINITION: bullish OB = the down-close candle(s) with the lowest body; use the body, not the wick; mean threshold = half of the OB (CE is for gaps); want price to use the mean threshold or higher and repel; ICT enters on the lowest candle, or one before / after it ~1:14, ~1:28 (OB-CANDLE)
- ORG ON A RUN DAY: in low resistance with unfinished liquidity above, throw out the 70% ORG mid-gap; expect a dash to the draw ~1:00-1:01, ~1:23 (ORG-CE-WINDOW)
- LOW RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT: don't rush partials; manage by the stop, set limit orders at targets, walk away; high resistance: don't trail (you'll be stopped), low resistance: a relaxed stop isn't hit ~1:39-1:43 (STOP-MGMT, EXIT-STYLE)
- Model 2022 stacked FVGs: trade the lower one but the stop must respect the second FVG ~40:00 (STOP-PLACE)
- SESSION RANGES as draws: Asia, London, pre-market 07:00-09:00, NY AM, lunch 11:30-13:30, PM; the lunch macro attacks the AM range ~32:00, ~1:35
- PRE-09:30 HOLD: ICT may be positioned between 08:00 and 09:00 with partials, hold through the 09:30 blowoff, and with a 10:00 SB hold through lunch and pyramid in the lunch macro ~1:00 (PREMARKET-ENTRY, OVERNIGHT-HOLD n/a)
- SB = any hour: a Silver Bullet exists every hour; 10:00 was a mold for Cameron ~55:00 (SB stays off)
