## Summary
Clarifies Lecture 01: beginners look for relative equal highs/lows that form AT/AFTER 07:00 only; anything before 07:00 is London (advanced traders may use it). Then the annotation routine: mark 07:00/08:00/09:00, relative equal pools, every FVG (with volume imbalance), and once direction is set extend ONLY the FVGs in that direction (SIBIs when bearish). Key PD-array rule: the first FVG prior to the stop run (inside the leg that runs the stops) is the preferred entry over the breaker; it becomes the inversion FVG. 15-second chart for missed entries and small pyramids.
