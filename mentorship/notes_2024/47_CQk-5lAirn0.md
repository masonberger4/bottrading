## Summary
No medium or high-impact news Monday or Tuesday. ICT strips the chart to opening range gaps and session liquidity pools. How to mark the ORG: on a 1m RTH chart, the previous settlement is the 16:14 candle's close (no 16:15 candle); for Monday use Friday's RTH settlement; label it as today's (Monday's) ORG high/low depending on where the 09:30 print lands. The NWOG is Friday's 17:00 ETH close to Sunday's 18:00 open (small today, 20,484.25-20,496.25, CE ~20,490.25). Price is well below Friday's settlement, so a discount gap is likely: bias bullish from 09:30 to 10:00 until at least half the gap fills (70%); never short just because it opened lower. Morning draw: London's high (02:00-05:00, smooth relative equal highs) rather than a nearby single short-term high; the ORG mid-gap and the London highs line up. Caleb's plan: use the first presented FVG (or any FVG) toward those levels; win and be done, lose and stop.

## Rules
- ORG MARKING: previous RTH settlement = close of the 16:14 candle on a 1m RTH chart (5m charts mislead); Monday uses Friday's RTH settlement; the 09:30 first print is the other side; label it for today's date ~5:00-8:00, ~11:00-12:00, ~25:00
  - If price traverses back above the settlement before 09:30, swap the labels (settlement becomes the ORG low) ~12:00
- NWOG [in rulebook]: Friday's 17:00 ETH close to Sunday's 18:00 open; mark its CE ~9:00-10:00
- DISCOUNT ORG BIAS: open below the previous settlement = bullish 09:30-10:00 until 50% of the gap fills (70%); if not reached by 10:00 still look for it; never short simply because it opened lower ~20:00-27:00, ~31:00-32:00 (ORG-CE-WINDOW)
  - "That could be your entire model": gap lower, look for it to go up to half the gap ~32:00
- LONDON SESSION RANGE [new]: London high and low (02:00-05:00 ET) are the morning session's draws, 09:30-11:00/noon; prefer the smooth London relative equal highs over a single nearby short-term high; frame it every day ("you'll never run out of setups"), also on high resistance days ~14:00-19:00, ~22:00
- CONFLUENCE OF DRAWS: blend session highs/lows with the ORG mid-gap; several finite reference points, not one ~26:00-27:00
- Before 09:30 in a range: the only question is whether price takes the near buy-side or sell-side first ~35:00-36:00
- DAILY HANDLE GOAL [beginner]: first rung 10 handles NQ / 5 ES a day; grow to 15-20 NQ / 10-15 ES; once hit, stop and tape-read the rest of the day; look for one good setup ~15:00-16:00 (MIN-ROOM)
- CALEB'S PLAN [beginner]: first presented FVG (or any FVG) toward the drawn levels; if it wins, done; if it loses, don't do it again that day ~33:00-34:00
- Retail compares yesterday's close to today's open; professionals compare today's open to today's close ~33:00
- No-news days: the protocol still works; delivery is slower without the volatility injection ~29:00-30:00 (CPI-WEEK / no-news)
