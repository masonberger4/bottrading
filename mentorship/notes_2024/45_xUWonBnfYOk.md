## Rules
- DECOUPLING [beginner filter]: for NQ, ES and YM should move in the same direction; mixed direction = lower probability, no low-risk entry to frame; new students don't trade it ~7:00-8:00, ~19:00-21:00, ~38:00 (SMT-USE)
  - Options: wait for agreement in the morning; if not, the PM is usually better after lunch sorts it out; worst case decoupled all day ~12:00
  - Often happens on no-news days ~7:00
- FORECAST THE RE-SYNC SMT [new]: when decoupled, expect a later SMT; e.g. NQ makes a lower low after taking sell-side while ES fails to, then NQ returns into its range and to the first presented FVG / opening range ~25:00-26:00, ~56:00, ~1:06-1:08 (SMT-USE)
- DOW IS NOT SMT: YM diverging from ES is not an SMT for ICT; only NQ vs ES; the Dow "bends to the will" of the other two when they really move ~1:07-1:08
- FPFVG AS DRAW: the first presented FVG is "coded" as a draw; price returned to it before 10:30 after the SMT ~56:00, ~1:00, ~1:10 (FPFVG-DEF)
- INVERSION FVG EARLY ENTRY: when convicted, enter inside the inversion FVG as price touches the high of the candle that enters it, or at its CE (first 6 contracts); a strong market may never return to prove it and you may miss the 4-contract add; no rule for when it runs away: "experience" ~40:00-46:00
- BREAKAWAY GAP: an FVG he wanted to stay open = breakaway, price doesn't come back to it ~1:00
- BODY SIGNATURES: bodies stopping at an FVG's CE, at the high of a VI, at the open = algorithmic delivery; wicks do the damage ~13:00, ~18:00, ~37:00, ~46:00
- MARKET MAKER BUY MODEL: need not come back up to clear the original consolidation; just know where it draws to ~1:10
- Time-based candles only (no range bars / Renko / Kagi) ~47:00
- RISK [beginner]: fixed 1% per trade; contracts grow with equity, risk % never grows; ICT doesn't pyramid new equity into long-term positions ~1:21-1:22
