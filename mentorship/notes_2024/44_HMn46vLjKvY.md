## Rules
- OVERNIGHT RANGE [new]: high and low of 00:01-04:59 ET (exclude the 00:00 and 05:00 candles); fib with 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75; use only when overnight is a consolidation inside a larger range, never when Asia-London is a one-sided run ~9:00-13:00, ~58:00
  - Bullish: FVGs (and other PD arrays) forming below a graded level are discount = high probability; bearish: arrays just above the levels ~23:00, ~1:00, ~1:30
  - Extensions for targets/partials: -0.5 first, then -1 (one standard deviation) ~1:05
  - Price in the upper half of the range when bullish should not come back toward its low ~29:00
- MACROS [in rulebook]: every hour from :50 to :10 (05:50-06:10, 06:50-07:10, 07:50-08:10 pre-market); 08:30 news is the outlier where price runs on its own ~16:00, ~23:00, ~30:00, ~1:30
- PRE-MARKET 06:00-09:30 is enough on its own for those who can't trade the open ~21:00-22:00 (PREMARKET-ENTRY)
- REBALANCE vs REDELIVERY: just trading back to an old SIBI is redelivery; rebalanced only once price leaves the range ~20:00
- Time must be right for price to move; held price = manual intervention, deferred to later ~28:00
- ORG SIZE: 45-50 handles is ICT's minimum threshold, prefers 75; at 75+ he enters at the first print without waiting for the first FVG (Caleb's FVG model is for Caleb), trading the 70% mid-gap odds ~40:00-41:00 (ORG-SIZE, OPEN-RANGE-TRADE)
- SMALL ORG: an anemic gap (here ~12 handles) = 50/50 morning; sit still, wait for 10:00-11:00 FVG + liquidity, or skip the AM and trade lunch, PM (watch 13:30-14:00 first) or the last hour (from 14:45) ~42:00-44:00, ~47:00 (ORG-SIZE position D)
- PM OPENING RANGE: 13:30-14:00 sets up the PM's initial liquidity (relative equal lows here) ~47:00
- SIZE + ADD: 6 contracts on a qualified FVG, add 4 when price returns to its top ~1:00 (EXIT-STYLE / pyramiding)
- LAST 5 DAYS: know every daily high and low of the last five days and last week's high/low: "an endless supply of setups" ~1:10
- INVERSION AFTER BREAKOUT: after price breaks above an old high (Oct 10), don't expect a retest of that high as support; look for an inefficiency on the way down to act as an inversion FVG; if strong it won't even touch it ~1:13
- VOLUME IMBALANCE: most flexible PD array; usable for entry, partials or stop management ~1:13-1:14
- ENTRY TIMING: enter while the candle trades against your direction (buy as price drops into the array), never chase a candle already moving your way; limits would refine it ~1:19-1:20
- EXIT ON LOW-HANGING FRUIT: on an abbreviated (holiday) day, limit exit just above the sell-side target, don't ride the return into the range ~1:26 (EXIT-STYLE)
