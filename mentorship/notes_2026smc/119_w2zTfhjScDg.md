## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Event horizon (EH) between two lows**: "whenever we have two of them like that ... You go to the low to the other low ... That midpoint, that's event horizon ... you can use it for an entry technique. You can use it for a partial, which is many times what I do." [refines B6 event horizon]
- **Partial ladder in a long-running move**: "take something below this low, something below this low. And ... take about 25% of the remaining portion off when you're at event horizon ... let the runner reach for the rest." Or "take everything but the last 20% off below here ... leave that remaining 20% ... running." Used when "I'm near the end of a run"; otherwise EH is an add level. [new; see conflict 66]
- **The range resets after a low breaks**: "Because the range resets. Because we broke this low here ... the only thing we did was went down into discount and then right back to equilibrium ... if we trade back to equilibrium, that's a short-term premium." "Don't ... say I don't want to sell short in here because we're too far in a discount" when lows of a larger run are being taken. [new; see conflict 62]
- **Close below a wick CE, gap left open = continuation**: "Below consequent encroachment that wick. That to me indicates that we have a high probability of continuation ... It didn't close that gap in ... We want to see the gap stay open." A body back above the CE "weakens the strength signature." [repeats 115/116]
- **IFVG validation sequence**: close below the BISI by body -> "it has to be given the permission ... to get up into this and try to touch the midpoint. If it wicks there and then the bodies close below that midpoint, that's a validation ... and when ... it ever trades back out below the low again, the very first fair value gap or touch of that is a wonderful little entry." "Just simply because I draw a rectangle ... doesn't mean that it's going to go straight down from there. There's things that have to happen." [refines B6 IFVG entry]
- **A failed IFVG = no trade**: Wednesday's gap "didn't give any kind of validation of an inversion fair value gap. So, it ... just used it back and forth." [repeats]
- **Candle open as a target**: the DXY target was the open of the discount-wick candle; "open, high, low, and close. That's predominantly all you need." [repeats]
- **1m suspension block at 10:13**: "a bearish close. I said, 'Note that.' ... the bodies were kept below halfway point. And then it was a short targeting that low." Later used again as "a reclaimed sell-side imbalance." [refines B6 A2 (to 1m)]
- **Two-stage delivery on CPI + FOMC day**: expected "take out the CPI 8:30 high ... during FOMC ... and then rip it all the way back down." "Nobody in their right mind trades ahead of FOMC." [repeats B6 event days]
- **Near the VI without entering = strength** (DXY): "getting very close to this volume imbalance but not getting into it, I'm going to ... associate that to underlying strength." [repeats B6 reluctance]
- **Selectivity**: "just because I see them doesn't mean I'm willing to take every single one of them." [repeats]
