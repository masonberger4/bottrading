## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Immediate rebalance (IR)**: open, trade back to the previous candle's high (no gap left), then run: "When the market's in a hurry ... it's going to be an immediate rebalance. So there's going to be no waiting around deferring the price run to get just to a fair value gap." "One of the strongest algorithmic price delivery PD arrays I've discovered." [new; see conflict 67]
- **IR timing and failure**: "the day it forms, it can do it, or it's ... the next day ... if it doesn't deliver a strong protection, the next day it will." "If day two fails, and it trades below immediate rebalance, that's a complex reversal. It will not run and protect the upside. It'll go the other direction." [new]
- **IR on DXY = trade every USD pair**: "if it's immediate rebalance, that means it's going to go higher sharply yesterday and today ... an easy shorting opportunity on Euro dollar, on pound dollar, on Aussie dollar." [new; FX]
- **Grade the more extreme wick**: "this wick went higher than that one, so this is the one you're going to grade." [refines 113]
- **Close beyond a wick CE = green light**: crude "needs to close above that. If it closes above this, that's the green light go that it is okay ... to make a new higher high." ES/MNQ: "already have a close below the midpoint of this discount wick. That implies that there's likely continuation lower." [repeats 115/119]
- **A wick above price is a premium array**: "this is a premium array even though it's a discount wick cuz it's the wick underneath the candlestick's body ... It's going to trade up into that." [new]
- **Body swing high then displacement**: "Look at the bodies, not the wicks ... High. Higher high. Lower high. There's your swing high. And then displacement lower. After the third candle closes, immediately takes off." Called the "institutional order flow entry drill." [refines 114 body swings]
- **Short entry after the FPFVG high holds + MSS**: "The bodies can't overtake the high of Thursday's first presented fair value gap ... this shift in market structure right there. Once that happens, I'm going to trade inside of these two price points here. And the first one that gives me, I'm going to take it." "Kind of like a pseudo model 2022." [refines 2022 model]
- **Stop above the OB at the IFVG base**: "this is the order block at the bottom of an inversion fair value gap. That's kind of like a big brick wall ... If you're strangling it, you're not going to give the trade enough room to breathe." [new; see conflict 53]
- **Nested opposite gaps**: an FPFVG with a BISI right after it = "two of them conflicting and they're nested together"; short from above, target the lower one, accept some heat. [new]
- **Failure to touch gradient levels = continuation**: on the way down, "We want to start seeing it fail to touch these gradient levels." [repeats B4 octants]
- **Runner in clean markets only**: "if we were in a market that was not seeing so much manipulation, this is where you could just let it stay with the runner ... for the rest of the day." [repeats B6 management]
- **Sunday gap plan**: expect a lower open, stay heavy to the low; "best case scenario would be to open up simply just below it ... then I would expect some measure of a retracement to try to close the gap halfway minimum. And then if it rolls back lower ... keep going lower." [repeats B5 NWOG]
