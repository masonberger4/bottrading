## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Back-adjust toggle = volume imbalances only**: "The only time I've ever utilized this function here is when I'm looking for volume imbalances ... They're not to find a fair value gap." Also check the settlement-price toggle: "if you toggle that on and off here, that's changing things too ... that's why yours doesn't exist." [refines B5 A2 back-adjust toggle; narrows conflict 41]
- **Pick the version the bodies respect**: "I may start with this one, but let's say I'm noticing that it's not really utilizing these levels ... and say the other one's doing it. I will abandon this one and adopt the one that was the other one." First preference = the volume imbalance that is part of the breaker (the down-close candle). [new; backtestable as "score both series' VI levels by body respect, keep the better"]
- **Continuous contract for old data**: "If you're looking for something prior to that low ... you can't trust any of this data here. So, you have to look at a continuous contract like every other market analyst." [repeats]
- **Low-hanging fruit = the nearer series' level**: from 25,333, continuous high 25,495 (~160 handles) vs June high 25,715 (~400): "Which one do you think is more prone to get traded to?" The gap between the two also measures "real buying strength." [repeats 105; see conflict 41]
- **Suspension block measured with VI bracket**: measure from the close to the open across the volume imbalances; "you have to include that when you're doing your measurements"; grade with quadrants + octants ("octane levels"). [refines B1/B3 suspension block]
- **Discount retest of a gap-up VI**: "I would expect the upper half of this to show discount sensitivity keeping itself out of the lower half by the bodies." [repeats]
- **ORG CE = first draw for the first HOUR**: "70% of the time ... If you're trading at any time during that first hour of trading, if you do not know [the half-gap level] you are absolutely guessing." Discount ORG (CE above the open) = look for buy-side; premium ORG = sell-side. "Session bias. It means the first hour trading." A run away from the CE at the open "is a Judas swing ... opening up more potential profit." Sometimes the first candle hits the CE: "Done. It's over ... just relax." [refines B1/B5 A1; extends the 30-minute window to the first hour; see conflict 50]
- **Wick = first presented FVG**: a small FVG with a wick to its left: "It is a fair value gap, but what's to the left of it? The wick ... wicks take precedence." [repeats B5 (104)]
- **Heaviness**: price "can't even accomplish getting to the high" of the range (wick short of the candle open) and the body closes below the wick CE = bearish. Retracement bodies "can't even stay inside of that wick" = bearish. [refines B4/B5 body reads]
- **Confluence low**: relative equal lows + the CPI low + the daily suspension block low + the ORG CE = the draw. [refines]
- **Event horizon in any wide range**: "Whenever you have a wide enough range separation between a PD array, an old low, an imbalance, a gradient level, always look for that gray area in the middle ... It's the event horizon." Bearish read there: wick past it, bodies below it. [refines B5 A2 event horizon]
- **Manual intervention still hits algorithmic levels**: "The manual intervention still always goes to a level that's algorithmic ... The problem is it's going to go to one that you didn't personally expect." Normally 2-3 times a month, mostly scheduled news; more often now = use a stop, don't over-leverage. [context; refines risk]
- **Consolidation with no FVG = use the last wick's CE**: "That wick right there is the last one that was going up in price before it dove down. When we trade back up into this, this acts just like an old high ... We only want the half measurement ... it cannot breach it by body." Close above it allows continuation. [refines B5 BOLO/wick CE]
- **Micro structure inversion**: slightly higher high at a time it should sell off; "this little separation right there, that's inversion. Carry that over." [repeats B4 IFVG]
- **TGIF trigger**: had price reached the old RTH ORG high, "start looking for TGIF 20 to the 30% of the weekly range." [repeats B5 A3]
- **Continuation easier than contrarian for new students**: "They're always easier when you're looking for continuations expansion runs to target buy side once it's already been proving it's bullish"; turtle soup/false breakouts are harder. [repeats; supports experienced-trader default only as context]

Beginners: first 30 minutes. "Carve out the first 35 to 40 minutes of the day." Intraday 20-40 handle runs vs 200-500 home runs. [refines B5 lunch macro from 10:30 first-hour range]
