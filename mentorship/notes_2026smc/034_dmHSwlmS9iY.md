# 2026 SMC 034: "A week in the life cycle of price": weekly bias, draws, TGIF Friday short (Sat Aug 15 2026)
Full read (14.5k words, ~40% monologue). Clean caption. Frames laptop-only. Core weekly-prep lecture.
## Summary
Weekly workflow: continuous contract first; PMH/PML and PWH/PWL; draw = nearest higher-timeframe pool (path of least resistance) in the primary trend; daily BISI/SIBI and wick grading; daily "gladhand" test; large-range-day calls on news; 15-minute "bellwether" chart for session pools; weekly profile (consolidation, midweek rally) matched to the economic calendar; TGIF Friday retrace to 20-30% of the weekly range; guarded relative equal highs; Silver Bullet short with IFVG + rejection block.
## Rules in ICT's wording
- "My analysis always begins with a continuous contract" (monthly/weekly) for key highs, lows, inefficiencies that the front month may not show (rollover); CFD traders use the continuous contract and blend the CFD's own highs/lows.
- **Draw selection**: from the week's open, which is closer: prior week/month high or prior week/month low? "Clearly, it's easier to get to it here. So, this is the path of least resistance. It's also part of a primary bullish market" -> look for PWH and PMH.
- Smooth relative equal highs on the daily = target ("market doesn't like to keep those"); before the next high, price cuts through candles = inefficiency; daily premium wick "treated just like a gap" (graded to CE).
- Retail bull flag valid when it agrees with the draw (PWH/PMH proximity).
- **Gladhanding**: "pairing of orders" at a level. Bullish BISI: if the retracement "failed to gladhand with the consequent encroachment", i.e. stayed in the upper half, "it really is bullish"; Tuesday's low failing to even touch the BISI high = second confirmation. "A bullish market should have inefficiencies left open."
- Daily rejection block (Friday's up-close body highs) capping Monday.
- Large range day call: Thursday = PPI, end of big news, before Friday, after small-range days, not yet closed above the rejection block -> "it's going to be a large range day"; expected a drop then a run above Wednesday's high.
- **Pools list**: "session highs and lows ... last three days high and low (a dynamic range) ... previous day ... previous week ... previous month." Intraday: Asian, London, AM session, NY lunch (2-hour window) highs/lows and their midpoints. "The higher the time frame, the more likely it's going to draw to it ... institutional sponsorship." Use the 15-minute chart as the "bellwether" for the week.
- **Weekly profiles** (2017 mentorship ~month 6-7): map the expected weekly delivery with the economic calendar (here CPI Wed 08:30, PPI Thu 08:30, medium Fri) -> "consolidation midweek rally" profile: Mon/Tue tradable swings, Wednesday important low, rally into the end of week. "Categorize ... the week that just closed" every week.
- **TGIF**: "when we have bullish weeks usually the market trades off from the high intra-week and settles somewhere around the 20 to 30% of the weekly range"; he aims for half of 20-30% (25%); "in extremes it can go down to 40%" (rarely, and then probably deeper). Use 15-minute to measure.
- Power of 3 for Friday: opening range 09:30-10:00, "the high is going to form somewhere in between there" on a bearish TGIF.
- **Guarded buy side**: relative equal highs not taken when: the narrative is TGIF retrace, a rejection block holds (bodies can't close above it), the BISI just below becomes an IFVG and price fails at it. Macro 09:50-10:10 ("a big one ... during the first hour ... and the close of the opening range, which is 30 minutes long"); watch whether they guard the highs during the macro.
- Entry: close below the 09:30 opening price after lower highs; IFVG confirm with two candles failing; short at a suspension block FVG near the CE of the longest wick; 2 contracts because the stop (wick CE) was wide. Bearish: no bodies in the upper half of the wick; order block open (change in state of delivery) as retrace limit.
- Targets: 20% of weekly range, prior month high, 25%, 30% (low-hanging fruit first); "the meat" analogy (premium arrays: lower half).
- "Model 2022": high, low, taken low (reversal) mentioned; "certain models are just not going to [form]" in continuation environments.
- Trade outside the macro is fine when the macro already started the spool and price is at a PD array (entry 10:11).
