## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Settlement-price toggle: use the setting that shows the widest VI / longest wick**: "I'm only interested in seeing the one setting that gives me the widest volume imbalance." If there is no visible difference, measure both, "watch it for a few hours ... you'll see which one the market's reacting and keying off of ... dismiss" the other. [refines B6 A3 (107); conflict check vs 41]
- **Daily swing low = key level traded to, lower low, then a higher low**: "a candle that creates a low, a lower low ... the next candlestick ... has a slightly higher low ... candlesticks four and or five should be bullish candles." If 4/5 don't confirm, "you're probably misreading the underlying bias." Mirror for highs. [new as a confirmation rule]
- **Which old low is the target**: coming down, "the swing low prior to that low ... is the first one you're going to come to"; after three drives lower fail, the next draw is that swing low. [refines]
- **Double wick CE**: grade the discount wick (low to body) and premium wick (high to body) of the turning-point daily candle; the midpoint of the two CEs is a level ("new PD array"). [new]
- **NDOG = 18:00 Monday open vs 16:59 settle; carry it all week; keep the last 2 weeks**: "keep them for at least 2 weeks rotating in your chart ... used many times for trade setups, targets, stop management." Same for Friday NDOGs and Monday/Friday pre-RTH opening range gaps of the last two weeks. [refines B4 last ORGs; new for NDOG two-week retention; see conflict 89]
- **07:00-09:00 = pre-market dealing range; consolidation there = purest SD projection**: "treat this entire range just like an Asian range or flout or Central Bank Dealers range ... the dealing range is defined by time." Measured move from the range; "many times you can nail down the high or low of the day." [new; see conflict 73]
- **Too-fast run leaving sell-side intact = expect a return**: "they left sell-side just too intact and they took out the relative equal highs first ... it's likely that they're going to come back and clear the board." [refines B1 both sides]
- **Turning-point wick: expect a retrace up to its CE**; stop just above the wick; if the next candle opens, tags it and slides, move stop to "break even ... give yourself a pizza dinner" (a little profit, not exact BE). [repeats B7 wick dominance; refines BE rule]
- **Counter-bias trade exits in full above the pool**: "I want to get out at the low of the sell side plus a tick ... because I'm shorting a primarily bullish market." Limit not filled and you expect reversal = close at market. [new; see conflict 74]
- **Down-close SIBIs in the down-leg become an IFVG ladder after a suspected low**: "go through the range ... every CB turn it into an inversion fair value gap," include adjoining VIs; bodies not touching the IFVG CE = strength; octants of the dealing range hold to a tick (0.625, 0.875) with bodies off them. [refines B3/B5 IFVG]
- **Midterm/election weeks: modest bullish tendency** ("not a panacea"). [new; seasonal filter]
