## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Wick near a gap is the more salient PD array**: "when the area in close proximity the wick is going to be the more salient PD array ... It's going to try to work inside the wick more than it will this buy sell side efficiency." Stops below the FVG's candle 2 must "really consider that wick more because it can trade down into that upper half." On YM: "that wick takes precedence over that fair value gap." - **A wick is a range like a gap**: "having that wick highlighted, it helps you formulate a range the same way inefficiency like a SIBI or a BISI ... or an opening range gap." Bodies not reaching the wick CE = barrier ("This is not going to be easy to get through"). [refines B5 wick CE]
- **Three PD arrays giving way = exit or reduce**: "if three of them give up that strength that would support the underlying narrative ... that's usually warning signs that you're probably offside ... you either reduce the trade significantly or close it. That's the protocol I teach." Holding = price stays below OB mean threshold and gap CE. [new; see conflict 57]
- **Failing to reach a BISI midpoint = heaviness** (GBPUSD): "not seeing it trade to midpoint ... indicates usual heaviness ... higher probability ... continue moving lower." [repeats B6 heaviness]
- **Bodies not touching a quadrant after wicks = signature**: "Even though we whipped to it here and here, this is a signature ... The bodies fail to touch that. So that indicates it's probably going to make a run up the very minimum into this wick." [repeats B6 109]
- **Turtle soup signature**: after a high is taken "the bodies are staying under the wick high. That is turtle soup signature." Bodies gravitate to the event horizon between the pool and the next level. [refines B6 event horizon]
- **Premium ORG: first draw = CE in the first 30 minutes**: "When we have a premium gap, I want to see price trade down ... That's the first order of business all the time." "For the first 30 minutes of trading 9:30 to 10:00 ... a 70% chance by 10:00." "That's a SOP ... I'm looking for that every single day until proven it's not going to do it ... - **Low-hanging fruit = 09:30 open / first-minute low**: "low hanging fruit objective would be obviously the opening price or the lowest of that first minute candle ... that's where engineered liquidity is ... regular trading hours has to book that price ... That's the efficiency model." Full gap closure = best case, don't count on it in this climate. [new]
- **First FVG toward the ORG CE** frames the first trade (09:35 FPFVG used as the marker). [repeats B5 FPFVG]
- **Time distortion rule**: "if you're seeing shared ranges in the candlesticks on the time frame you're looking at, go up a time frame and you'll see it's just simply utilizing a higher time frame inefficiency."
