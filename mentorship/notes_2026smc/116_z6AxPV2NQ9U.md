## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Friday one-sided at the low: don't chase**: "If it weren't ... Friday ... I'd be all over this shorting it. But, because it's Friday, I'm a little reluctant." "We opened right up at the high of the regular trading hours session ... if it spiked above it and got that high taken out just by one tick ... then I would look for ... continuation lower. But, because it hadn't done that, it makes me reluctant ... on a Friday." [refines B4/B6 TGIF; see conflict 62]
- **Deep in the RTH range = poor short location**: "We're in a deep deep deep discount. And when you're short or trying to get short, you don't want to be getting short like this." Lunch approaching adds to it. [new]
- **Too many candles inside an FVG = not valid**: "We don't want to see time distortion inside of inefficiencies." "Too many candlesticks inside a fair value gap. It needs to be one, maybe two at most, and leave." Compare: "when the market's really wanting to move, it'll go in there one candle and then it's gone." [new]
- **A broken low must accelerate**: "A drop below this should not see it return back inside the range ... it should break below that low and accelerate down with a lot of energy." A small break and return = weakness (the dog back on the porch). [repeats 115]
- **Gap left open below the low = breakaway**: "I would like to see that stay open. If it can stay open and trade below that low ... it's showing potentially a breakaway. But if it comes back up and closes that in, then I don't like to see that" (runs below get "stunted"). [new]
- **Lethargic price when it should deliver = step aside or reduce**: "whenever I'm seeing a lethargic price action when it should be delivering ... that to me is problematic for being short ... should I be taking off partials?" [repeats B6 reluctance]
- **Can't reach the CE of a daily wick = distribution**: "The fact that it can't get up to its halfway point on that daily wick ... this is distribution. This is not a bull flag." Grade daily wicks in quadrants/octants on both the continuous and the delivery contract ("You want to do this on both"). [refines B6 A3]
- **Immediate feedback after a CE miss**: "It can't get the consequent encroachment. So, the next candle should give you ... immediate feedback." [repeats]
- **Lunch macro = back to the first short-term high after 10:00**: "the time of day, 10:30 going to 11:00, that's usually the macro during lunch hour ... where is liquidity going back to 10:00? What's the first short-term high? ... retrace back to that. That's it." On a Friday it's an observation, not a trade. [repeats 115; conflict 59]
- **Balanced price range**: a candle's gap traded above and below "takes on the characteristic of a balanced price range"; for it to act as IFVG it "has to deal with it on a body basis ... close outside of it." [repeats]
- **Partials on a warning candle**: long two contracts in the IFVG, stop below the low: "Because it did that right there [body in the lower half], I would have peeled off one ... managing the position when it gives you indications that everything isn't as it should be." "If you're just simply going to say, 'I'm going to take my full target or nothing.' Well, that's too myopic." [new; see conflict 63]
- **Lower exit threshold in the current climate**: "By having a lower threshold of exit, not demanding perfection, that's what I'm finding is working right now in this harder climate." In such conditions, once the near high is taken, "trail below. Just strangle it all the way up on a 15-second chart." [refines 115 three-candle trail; conflict 61]
- **Third PD array lost = walk away**: "That's your third PD array. If it's going to go for this, it needs to be defended by this inversion fair value gap. If it loses this gap, then I would walk ... move to the sidelines and wait for the PM session." [repeats 113; conflict 57]
- **Target the level that unseats both sides**: the level that "stops out people that are short from up here and it runs against the idea of anyone that went short in here ... it's a more probable level to trade to" than the lower low. Stop "one tick above" the level that proves you wrong. [new]
- **Wednesday short (Mar 25)**: entry at the CE of the discount wick / "small separation" inside the FPFVG in a consolidation; target the nearest REQ lows, "the easiest low-hanging fruit," not the lower low (24,351.25). [repeats B5 low-hanging fruit]
- **Sunday 18:00 open is the weekly key**: "once I get that price, then right away ... I can see where we're going to make the weekly high, where we're going to make the weekly low, what's Monday's high and low." [repeats B6]
