## Summary
Explains the Friday short of the daily high: the 07:00-09:00 "time-based range" is the data set; its -0.5 projection gave the projected daily high (28,723, off by 2.75 handles); every PD array after 09:00 must be tethered to that range's levels; last Friday's NDOG confluence; then the daily low projected from the prior day's range and a 3-day-old wick (cluster).
## Rules in ICT's wording
- "You can make the entirety of your career just focus on that little time window of 2 hours [07:00-09:00] ... You don't need to do anything else." Or use it as information for the AM session.
- "Any old range is ... not important. But when we look at very specific time-sensitive ranges, then it means a whole lot." The range is finite at 09:00 (no hindsight).
- Fib on the 07-09 lowest low to highest high: "you should have a -0.5 ... That's a projected daily high" (expected rally since 07-09 drifted lower; relative equal highs above). Levels: 0, octants, quadrants, CE, -0.5.
- **Tethering rule**: "at 9:01 any PD array that forms ... at one of these levels ... the candlestick has to touch it, lay across it, or touch it." Example: BISI/suspension block tethered to the lower quadrant (28,400.75); run begins at 09:01, "1 minute after close of that time-based range."
- "Whenever there's a volume imbalance, you have to include the volume imbalance candle where it has to be tethered to it."
- 07-09 range CE as a level; down-close FVGs at CE act as IFVGs on the way up; bullish = bodies avoid the lower half, "can't even touch consequent encroachment", only the upper quadrant.
- Short at the projected high; also at last Friday's NDOG CE (also a daily VI): "You got to have Monday and Friday" new day opening gaps / ORGs from last week.
- Before 09:30, minimum expectation was a run to the 07-09 relative equal highs before the 28,400 target. Target hit ~1h45m later. Then "market maker buy model" structure on the sell side of the curve.
- **Daily-low projection**: once a called level breaks, (1) project yesterday's range (low to high) [-x SD, 28,111.5], (2) grade the wick 3 days back as a gap (28,097.25 / 28,070.25 / 28,124.25); take the CE of the two projections; "a high probability draw ... it's going to be inside of a clustering of PD arrays, not just a single PD array."
- "Your entire universe of liquidity and inefficiencies can be summed up in the last 3 days. Once you breach the last 3 days range, then you got to look back further." Intraday traders need only 3 days of data.
- Ranges like 12:45-13:15 carry nothing (except a macro); the quality is in time-defined sources.
