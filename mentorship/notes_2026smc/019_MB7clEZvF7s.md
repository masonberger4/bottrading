## Summary
Layouts: (1) Monthly macro keys: OHLC of the last three months and the prior week's high/low only; where is price in the 3-month range (equilibrium on Sep 2), the month, the prior week (opened in its premium, now in the discount). (2) Daily: a big open daily VI/BISI with no bodies inside = little opposition; price cascaded through it overnight Tuesday at 03:00 and then respected its octants/quadrants on the 1m, never returning to its CE once weak, down to the gap low and its midpoint. (3) NWOG matrix: last five NWOGs (Fri 16:59 close to Sun 18:00 open; Thursday close if Friday is a holiday), labeled and colored consistently. (4) FPFVG matrix (first FVG after 09:30, or the first with displacement). (5) RTH ORG matrix: when ORGs nest, use the widest; an overlap ("shadow") of two ORGs anchored on a down-close candle validated an order block. Post-11:00 Wednesday of NFP week = limbo between arrays: new students stop.
## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Monthly macro keys**: last three months' open/high/low/close plus last week's high/low; "Are we in a discount or a premium relative to that range?" Weekly open inside the prior week's premium/discount. [refines B1 dealing range]
- **Seasonals (rule of thumb)**: "September are usually bearish months ... we usually trade down into some low in September or some low in October. Rare ... seasonal low in November. And then ... trading up into the first week of February." Quarterly shifts link to seasonals (2016-17 Mentorship). [new; see conflict 82]
- **Open-air daily gap = fast, unstaggered travel**: no bodies inside = "there isn't a whole lot of opposition"; on the way, 1m respects its octants; "if it's weak, it won't return back to consequent encroachment." [refines B6 A2 block gradients]
- **NWOG = Friday 16:59 close to Sunday 18:00 open; keep the last five; Thursday close if Friday is a holiday.** [repeats B4; 001 says NDOG two weeks]
- **FPFVG = first FVG after 09:30; or use the first with displacement.** [repeats 013]
- **Nested ORGs: use the widest one**: "When there's nested PDAs like [NWOGs] or regular trading hours opening range gaps ... the largest one that we're inside of, the widest one." [new; see conflict 83]
- **Shadow = overlap of two gaps; an order block inside a shadow is valid**: "That makes a very strong support or resistance level." [new]
- **Retracement only to the CE of the day's ORG in a bearish open-air context = bearish**. [repeats B7]
- **Macro 06:50-07:10 broke the previous week's low.** [repeats 008 macro definition]
- **Between two distant arrays with nothing near = limbo; don't trade** (new students: stop by Wed 11:00 of NFP week). [repeats 017]
