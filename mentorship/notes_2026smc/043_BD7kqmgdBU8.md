## Summary
Study how session highs/lows form (daily chart is the source); ORG graded in octants drives every PD array that day; first-hour dealing range; breakaway gap above ORG CE; IFVG "prior to relative equal highs being taken"; immediate rebalance; ORG -0.2/-0.5/-1 projections; fulcrum; and a simple, explicit "shortcut" ORG short plan with partials.
## Rules in ICT's wording
- Study how highs/lows form in sessions (AM session, 07-09, daily, weekly): "it's always inside of the daily chart ... daily ranges are defined by a daily chart."
- Cluster of levels: measure from the highest to the lowest; body closing back at the midpoint after a wick through = defines the range; bodies can't re-enter the lower area -> bullish.
- **ORG grading**: RTH ORG = 09:30 open vs prior 16:14 settlement (28,576.75 / 28,324.75 here); levels: upper octant, upper quadrant, octant, CE, octant, lower quadrant, octant, low. "Half gap 70% of time gets traded to by 10:00" (hit at 09:43). PD arrays tether to these levels ("price relative to time").
- First hour dealing range 09:30-10:30: mark its high/low.
- 07-09 FVGs projected forward; BISI's first utilization = buy-side delivery; when bearish it acts as IFVG.
- **Breakaway gap**: a gap that forms at the upper quadrant (above ORG CE, in premium) after 1-2 candles consolidate between two PD arrays, followed by displacement: "nothing gets traded back up into there."
- Up-close candle on the lower octant = bearish order block on the retrace.
- Beyond full gap fill: project 09:30 open to prior settlement: -0.5 and -1 as extension targets (low came "real close" to -1).
- PM session: after relative equal highs taken, the FVG "prior to relative equal highs being taken out" becomes the bearish IFVG = the real FVG; include the candle with the VI; bodies stop at the ORG upper quadrant.
- **Immediate rebalance**: the very next candle returns to the prior candle's level (no gap); "both of them actually do it here".
- Fulcrum: climax reversal high to low = hinge; equal swing projection beyond the low.
- **Shortcut ORG plan (gap up, bearish)**: targets = (1) "low-hanging fruit": if the 16:00 candle made a short-term high above the 16:14 close, use the 16:00-candle high (he did this trading S&P in the 90s), and (2) ORG CE (with 16:14 settlement). Entry: retest of the 09:30 opening price from below, short the small FVG / wick: 1 contract when price breaches the wick midpoint, add at the upper quadrant and at the 09:30 open, pyramid in drawdown up to the VI/wick low ("some prop firms" forbid adding to losers). Stop above the wick CE ("trade micros" if too wide); trail to the FVG CE then above the last high.
- **Partial plan**: first partial at target 1; at ORG CE "80% to 75% of your position should be off"; aim to hold the rest for full gap closure + "a little bit for spread"; beyond: -0.2 (partial), -0.5 (partial), -1.0 (close). Then "walk away."
- Comfort: "If you're a 100 handle trader and you want to get to 250 to 500 handle runs ... take the majority ... at your 100 and leave a runner and have no concern with what it does."
