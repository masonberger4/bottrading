## Rules in ICT's wording
- **FHDR defined**: "Here's 9:30 and then 10:30 ... just snap it down to the lowest low ... It's not the opening range, which is 30 minutes, but this is the first hour's range or first hour's dealing range." "What the algorithm refers to." [new as a named range; refines B5 first-hour SD (101)]
- **10:00 high = lunch macro target**: "after 10:00 what high forms? This. This is the high that the lunch macro will target." Its EQ also "qualifies the ... lunch macro run to that for a trade." Lunch "is 11:30 to 1:30. Yes. But because things are so skewed, it can happen a little bit earlier ... The bleed over from London close"; "10:30 to 11:30, that little sweet spot ... it can start the lunch macro." [new; see conflict 59]
- **Close below a wick CE inside the FHDR = PM continuation**: "as soon as that candlestick closed right there below that midpoint of this wick ... this is in many ways a confirmation if you're going to trade the PM session ... the day is going to continue going lower." "If we can close below this wick that was inside of the first hour's dealing range ... that sets a stage for lower prices in the afternoon." [new]
- **Shallow run below the FHDR low after a one-sided morning = turtle soup**: "this low being taken out here on a shallow basis is because we have already established the first hour's dealing range. And it's been one-sided right from the opening bell ... That's going to entice people to ... chase it on a breakout ... This is how you find wonderful turtle soup setups." Trailed short stops sit above the post-10:00 high / FHDR EQ. [new]
- **Trending day: trade the first FVG after the FHDR break**: "First presented fair value gap after first hour's dealing range is broken, that's the one that ... you sell on." "First gap after blowing through it, that's the one you want to trade. You don't want to expect it to go back up into this one." [new; see conflict 60]
- **FVG right before the stop hunt** is the short array; bodies in its lower half = "No passy." [repeats 113]
- **FHDR projection**: drag the FHDR so its 50% sits on the low (or project 1 SD lower). "This is like a standard deviation of the range established in the first hour, then projected lower. Think like ... flout and the Asian range projections." Add octants: bodies use the quadrants/octants of the projected range ("a range that didn't exist yet"). [refines B5 101]
- **75% take-profit in a bad climate**: "borrow the 75 level. So, 75% of the range that could be delivered if it's 100% run ... anywhere in here is an ideal area to take a close and be content. Or as soon as we get into this, be aggressive about trailing your stop loss." Avoid holding overnight/weekend risk. [new; see conflict 61]
- **Three-candle trailing stop**: "Every three candles ... trail the stop loss down. You don't need swing highs ... Your stop loss would be above the last three candles' high ... if you get stopped out, you're done, just walk away." "You're not doing that in every trade. You're doing that when there's a condition like this," where the target may not be reached. Also his logic for partials on up-close candles. [new; see conflict 53]
- **Off-level runs = manipulation**: "If you're seeing things happen in price action and it runs where it doesn't make sense and it's not on or trading to an octant or a quadrant level, it's manipulation." [new; hard to define]
- **Wick next to an FVG is the dominant PDA**: "the wicks are going to be the dominant PD array ... if it's next to or just to the left of a fair value gap." Daily close below a wick's CE "is indicative of a continuation." [repeats 113]
