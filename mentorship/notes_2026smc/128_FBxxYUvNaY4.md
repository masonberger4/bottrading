## Rules in ICT's wording
- **HRLR defined**: 15m block where "every candlestick or range shares the previous range of the candle before it ... high resistance means. It's resisting your ability to find efficient low-risk trades ... doesn't imply at all support or resistance." "Treat it like a rattlesnake." LRLR = "small consolidation, then an inefficiency forms, then ... a trade back down into that inefficiency, and then a higher high ... porous price action, little gaps." [refines B4/B5; see conflict 96]
- **Scale-in on a wick poke only in LRLR**: "In low resistance liquidity run conditions, I am doing that 90% of the time. I'm scaling in ... add one more if it poke its head outside the bottom end ... In high resistance ... don't do that." [new; conflict 96]
- **Range contraction -> expansion (Larry Williams)**: "goes from large ranges to small ranges ... That is a precursor to large ranges." In LRLR bearish: small candles inside a SIBI, then "stretched-out black limousines." [repeats B8 small/large ranges]
- **Daily VI neutralized by a spanning body**: "I would not use this any longer ... if the daily chart lays a body down over top of the entire range ... I may use it for stop management ... but I'm not going to take it as a new entry or a terminus." [new]
- **Low participation mutes red-folder news**: PPI Friday "just didn't really do it"; "low participation does not inspire them to spool price too far." [new; see conflict 98]
- **Yearly participation cycle**: first month or two = measure risk, not participate; "Second Tuesday or first Tuesday of February ... slowly start dabbling"; March picks up; summer mostly HRLR; LRLR "the last 2 weeks at the earliest of August or at the beginning of September all the way through till ... the first week of December, second week"; holidays 50/50. [new; see conflict 98]
- **TGIF in a consolidation week**: normal 20-30%; "What if ... it's a consolidation week? ... Go back to the middle of the range. 40% ... That's the minimum ... What's the extreme ... 60% of the range." [refines B8 A6; conflict 75]
- **FPFVG time**: "forms at 9:32. It can't be 9:30. 9:31, there is no fair value gap." [repeats]
- **FPFVG is not a long while REQ lows sit below in the first 30 minutes**: "you can't take that as a long if these relative equal lows are there and we're in the first 30 minutes of trading. Because the opening range is not 15 minutes ... 30 minutes ... The first 60 minutes ... much more information." If the lows had been taken first, a retrace into the FPFVG is a viable long. [new; see conflict 97]
- **Reclaimed vs inverted FVG**: up-close BISI traded through and back above = reclaimed; a down-close SIBI traded back above = inversion; "up closed candles ... we have to rely on time and patience to take us up above it. And then ... respect the upper half." [new; refines B3 IFVG]

- **ORG anchors**: RTH 16:14 settle to 09:30 open; "You can use the settlement price at 5:00 p.m. Eastern time ... create a opening range gap with that price, too." Before 09:30, label the settle as the ORG high (discount gap) or low (premium gap) from where price sits around 09:00. [refines B7]
