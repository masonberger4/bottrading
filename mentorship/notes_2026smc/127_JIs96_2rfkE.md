## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Line-chart SMT**: "when I'm doing comparative analysis or relative strength analysis between correlated market ... I'm plotting them on a line basis and if I'm comparing their highs ... plot all of them on a line basis on the high ... If I'm looking for ... bullish scenarios I would reverse this. I would plot it on the low." [new; see conflict 94]
- **Weakest index gets the short**: NQ "was the unwilling indices to go higher ... heavy distribution ... any sell-off ... should be seen more aggressively and travel probably more in magnitude lower in the Nasdaq." [repeats SMT/relative strength]
- **Rollover: use the next contract's gaps as targets**: "this time of the month when we have rollover, I start looking at both contracts ... the next month out ... will build in by default more volume imbalances or common gaps ... it will work towards giving me targets that would otherwise be obscure and not noticeable in the nearby." "all trading should be done on the March contract." Daily: nearby + continuous for history; next month too spotty on the daily, useful on 15m/5m. [refines B7 rollover; see conflict 95]
- **NWOG on a gap up = CE resistance**: "if we open a little bit higher ... I would look for an open overlap that ... new week opening gap ... and then treat the midpoint as kind of like the resistance point of that and not try to see it go above that and go lower." Preferred: gap lower, trade up into the gap, "preferably fill it before we get into tomorrow's regular trading hours." [refines B4 NWOG]
- **VI as inversion**: a bullish-delivery daily volume imbalance; "If we go below it and trade back up into the lower half of it and keep the upper half clean without having any bodies ... it should be a formidable resistance ... keep the midpoint or consequent encroachment in control." [refines B3 IFVG to VIs]
- **Suspension block**: BISI whose low end has a volume imbalance ("the bodies don't touch") = suspension block; retrace "bodies ... permitted to get into the lighter area, not in the darker area ... Leaving a small portion down here open would be wonderful." [repeats B8 pre-market suspension]
- **Toggle settings to find the uniform VI**: gold: the plain FVG "doesn't do anything"; toggled setting shows "a very, very nice volume imbalance that's much more uniform ... it jumps off the chart"; bodies open/close on its edges and CE. "a knack, an experienced thing." [repeats B6/B8 settlement toggle]
- **Breakaway and measuring gap**: after a balanced price range, the next FVG "should act as ... a breakaway gap, and then next fair value gap ... could be used as a measuring gap. And then half of that range, measured from the low ... would project some measure of a upside objective." [new]
- **Bullish SIBI-as-IFVG holds the upper half**: "We went through it, but we didn't stop there. We closed in the upper half ... It did not lay the body down on the halfway point." "bullish PD arrays are going to be utilizing the upper half. Bearish PD arrays use the lower half." [repeats]
- **Nested PD arrays: keep the higher one open**: an FVG nested on top of a potential IFVG; "whenever there's a nesting area of two PD arrays ... anything higher than that ... I want to see that stay open." Wick through to its low/lower quadrant allowed in volatile markets. [new]
- **Balanced price range wick takes precedence**: "I'm looking at none of the candlesticks. I'm not looking at the order block. I'm looking at that wick cuz it takes precedence. Why? Because it's a balanced price range ... two deliveries." Bearish unless a close above the wick CE. [refines B7 wick dominance]
- **Invalidation stated in advance**: "If we trade below this low here, everything I'm saying here goes to the wayside and we have to wait for more information." [repeats]
- **Spring seasonal**: "springtime where usually there's a tendency for the markets to try to draw down a little while." [see conflict 82]
- **Prefer bias-direction setups**: "That doesn't mean I can't scalp longs intraday ... I prefer to take that setup over something that would be maybe short-lived on the upside." [repeats]
