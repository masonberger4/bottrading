## Rules in ICT's wording
- **20/40/60-day lookback; last 20 is best**: "I use the frame of reference of a look-back period of 20, 40, and 60 days, your highest probability, precision, high-frequency setups are going to form in the last 20 days ... trading days, not calendar days." [refines GAP-LIFE]
- **Opening gaps kept at least 5 days, filled or not**: "by having them on for at least 5 days ... whether they fill in or stay open, they're important. They're not just discarded." Notepad = the last full week (five days back) of NWOG, FPFVG and RTH ORG levels. [repeats B9 notepad; GAP-LIFE]
- **Old FPFVGs stay live**: FPFVG of Jan 2 ("two Fridays ago") graded in eighths still drew reactions; its CE marked as a draw; Jan 7 FPFVG run through. [refines 138 magnet]
- **Trend lines only as targets**: "I don't use that information except for targeting ... when it's clean ... and it's diametrically opposed to what I think the market's most likely going to do next." Phantom trend line midpoint low = the liquidity. [repeats]
- **Session ≠ killzone**: "Asian session kill zone is 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. [sic; 2-hour window per 139] ... Asian session ends and closes around 5:00 a.m."; Asia session "7:00 p.m. Eastern time ... to 5:00 a.m."; "London kill zone ... 2:00 in the morning to 4:00 a.m. That's your sweet spot, but it goes all the way to 5:00 a.m. ... 3-hour window"; "4:00 a.m. is the sweet spot ... purest little runs"; "London session goes till 10:00 to 11:00 in the morning." [refines; see LONDON-MACRO]
- **Look for smooth levels across the whole session, not only the killzone**: "Do not limit your perspective to only that of during the kill zones ... what happens if you already went outside that range ... it's useless to you." [new]
- **London 10:00-11:00 profit taking**: "if you're trading London, the extreme of the range is usually defined by 10:00 to 11:00 in the morning. So, that's profit taking." [repeats 139]
- **05:00-07:00 dead time**: "there's a gap of dead time ... avoid this area ... Wait until 7:00 in the morning. Then the price action cleans up." [repeats PREMARKET-RANGE F]
- **07:00-09:00 runs every day to overnight smooth liquidity**: "You can do all of your setups between 7:00 in the morning and 9:00 in the morning and never ... engage the 9:30 opening ... It's going to do this every single day ... after a holiday ... partial day." 07:00 FPFVG to the Asian REQ highs = "bread and butter set up in pre-market session time." - **PM-session reference = 02:00-11:00**: "If I'm looking at PM session trading ... beginning 2:00 a.m. ... [to] 11:00 a.m. ... between this vertical line at 2:00 a.m. and this vertical line at 11:00 a.m., I'm looking for the highest high and the lowest low and inside that range ... smooth highs and smooth lows." In a premium (pushed-up) market look for relative equal lows: "knock out weak bulls." "So, it's first time, then it's price." [new]
- **No smooth levels = single high/low**: "Say it was just this high ... that would be a single high liquidity where that would be overnight highs. That's where I would aim for." Odds rise "exponentially if it's part of or around a quadrant level of the entirety of the range." [new]
- **REQ anchor and the trap**: "the one to the right is lower than that one. That's the trap"; "Anchor point is point number one, the higher of the relative equal highs, and anchor point to the relative equal lows is the lower one." [repeats]
- **Grade the anchor's wick**: "If that low or high has a wick ... you have to grade that ... it's only going to go down into upper quadrant, consequent encroachment or lower quadrant and then reject"; "You have to take some kind of a partial. I like to use anywhere between the lower quadrant and consequent encroachment [of the wick] ... if it stops short of consequent encroachment ... that's indicating ... Weakness." [new]
- **FPFVG size filter**: "if it's less than 10 handles in NQ, chances are you're already 80% likely that that's not the first presented fair value gap ... I'm waiting for another fair value gap to form." [new; FPFVG-DEF]
- **Displacement defined by time**: "An interesting gap is one that has displacement ... It's a run through a high or low at a very specific time. What's the time? 7:00 in the morning." Common gaps ignored. Not an engulfing pattern. [repeats 139]
- **Counter-bias SIBI = wait for IFVG**: "you're bullish ... What do you do with that ... Do you try to short it there? No. You look for it to act as ... Inversion fair value gap ... If it's in a consolidation like this, it's less likely ... we're in a holding pattern." [repeats 139]
- **Scenario if pre-market had not run the highs**: "any drop down would be viewed as a Judas swing during the 9:30 opening to 10:00 ... look for displacement to the upside ... first presented fair value gap ... and then run up to that." [repeats]
