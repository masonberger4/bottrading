On a one-sided bull morning, the high came exactly at the 8.0 SD of the ORG inside the first hour of the 2-hour New York lunch (11:30-12:30). ## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Time first, then price**: "it's always time first then price." "Always have your chart set to New York local time."
- **ORG definition**: "regular trading hours opening range gap ... between that candlestick's close and that candlestick's opening price"; "discount opening range gap ... we opened lower than where we settled at the previous day at quarter after 4:00." [B1 ORG]
- **ORG as discount array once overcome (bull market)**: "if it can overcome that gap ... The very next candle we open, we trade back down to it there. So, now it's going to act as ... a discount array"; "the first time it trades down to it ... That's the original first run into that range. If it's a bull market, it should act as discount sensitivity ... like a trampoline." Fading the gap fill "can be a trade, but not if it's against the higher time frame."
- **ORG standard deviations**: Fib from the 09:30 opening price to the prior close; "how many replications or standard deviations"; eyeball range "anywhere between 6 1/2 to 7 1/2", then check 6.5, 7, 7.5, 8: "Watch what happens when we do eight. There we go." Consolidation days: "measure that opening range gap and get a measurement of 0.25 and 0.50." "It unlocks like the tumblers in a lock": go up the vertical (SD levels) until time (11:30) arrives, then move horizontally. Levels that price already exceeded before 11:30 are ruled out.
- **Lunch window**: "11:30 starts the New York lunch hour technically it's a 2-hour period ... it ends at 1:30." "At 11:30, that begins that 2-hour window where whatever took place in the morning session, it will roll against that. That's the lunch macro. That's them simply just going back for a low that formed after 10:00." [refines B2/B3 lunch]
- **Setup forms in the first lunch hour when one-sided**: "because it's gone higher, we limit our interest in this to the first hour of the lunch macro" (11:30-12:30); "the delivery can afford itself in the second hour. But the setup optimally forms in the first hour."
- **Lunch target selection**: "This low here [just before 10:00] ... We don't want to use that." "You want to use the very first one that you can come to when probabilities are shifted against you ... if you at 11:30 go to the left from there, towards 10:00, where's the first low you come to? This one. That just happens to have ... relatively equal lows." The deeper post-10:00 low is the "best case", not used against the HTF.
- **Minor vs primary sell-side**: "I have minor sell-side on that low ... What's a minor mean? It's not the real sell side that he's going to go for. This is the primary sell side." Partial at the minor, partial at the "event horizon" (50% between the two lows), final "right below the relative equal lows".
- **Exit placement**: a low swept by one tick is "too shallow ... it never would have afforded me the exit"; place the limit "a tick or two below that" low.
- **IFVG entry read**: "how did you know it's going to be inversion fair value gap? Because it was immediately on the next candle unwilling to go to the previous candle's high"; fill "at the upper portion of that inversion fair value gap"; next candle "goes right to the consequent [encroachment] of that level" and then sells off. PD array named: BISI with a volume imbalance at the top.
- **PM opening range**: "at 1:30 to 2:00 in the afternoon ... that's your opening range ... all those PD arrays are going to be salient for the PM session, the final hour trading, and ... when we restart at 6:00 p.m." The return to the IFVG/breaker came "during the opening range for 2:00".
- **8:30 micro vs mini spike**: when the micro spikes on 08:30 news and the mini doesn't, "it wants to redeliver that ... It goes to it through it and operates as a stop hunt."
- **Slop = no trade**: after the targets were hit, "It's in slop. I don't want to trade this ... go in tomorrow morning pre-market session. 7:00 in the morning to 9:00-ish."
- **Counter-trend shorts at ATH are for the experienced only**: "you should be afraid to take the short because you don't have the experience"; take low-hanging fruit exits.
