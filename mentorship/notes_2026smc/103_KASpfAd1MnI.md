## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Drill spec (beginner, paper only)**: "Whatever I'm looking for in terms of the run or the target off of the fair value gap. 10 minimum, no more than 15" handles, one micro, 1:1; record time in trade, drawdown, max favourable excursion even when stopped. "At least 15 minutes a day." [new; beginner switch only]
- **No FVG = sit still**: "So far no gaps to work with. So if you have nothing to work on, you sit still ... If there's nothing in the chart, don't force it." [repeats 101]
- **High-resistance signature**: "the market's sharing a lot of the previous ranges. And it's very infrequent when it creates the imbalances"; bodies that look like "the barcode thing" = "more likely that they'll run through that just to get the liquidity that's on the other side." Clean = "fast and loose. Lots of inefficiencies." Wants clean price "on the 1-minute and in your executable time frame." [refines B2/B3 HRLR vs LRLR; candidate filter]
- **Time distortion**: fuzzy consolidations "inside of a wick, you enter time distortion. So, you have to wait for that to be resolved. That means it's got to leave it." [repeats 100]
- **Higher high whose prior high is a wick**: "I want to see the bodies ... inside the range of that wick's high. The consequent encroachment ... a body needs to be protected to this midpoint or higher. As long as it's doing that, continuation is likely. If it puts a body below this, that's problematic." [refines B3 wick CE body filter]
- **After a run above highs, no give-back**: "now we have a higher high, there should be no give back at all now ... when the market creates that problematic stutter ... that to me is a little bit suspect" = take partials. [refines B4 stuttering]
- **"To it and through it" at relative equal highs**: "we don't want to see it just do a small little run above it in shallow format and then go lower ... We want to see it go above them and accelerate"; a shallow peek and return "is more indicative of a intraday reversal or a deeper retracement ... the false breakout for the day." If it then takes the low, "I'm not interested at all for the rest of the day." [new; backtestable]
- **Half-the-stop-hunt projection**: after a drop that ran stops, "if it does take out these highs, it should have at least half of this range ... equilibrium ... from that high down to that low ... add it to that high"; "it'll give you algorithmic price levels to reach for. They're new thresholds." "If the market fails to go here and reverses, that's indicative of weakness ... lower prices going all the way into 1:30." It hit it. [new; backtestable]
- **Candle-after-wick-CE test**: "when this happens, you want to see the very next candle show a willingness to get down to its midpoint and close below it ... otherwise this is problematic for continuation"; roll the stop to cover commissions. [refines B4 IFVG next-candle tests]
- **Sick sister (SMT)**: ES lagging NQ's higher high; "if it doesn't do that, that shows a break in correlation"; once the sick sister makes its own higher high (any size), "any sell-side can be engaged ... The decoupling goes away." Lunch 11:30-13:30 retracements "healthy" if ES first takes its high. [refines B2/B3 SMT]
- **Four market maker models per hour on 15s**: "1 hour, you have four 15-minute intervals. That means you have four market maker sell or buy models ... generally four of them per hour." [new claim; test on data]
- **Need a draw**: "If nothing is the target, you'll have a 100% strike rate hitting that. Nothing." [repeats]
- **Lunch macro**: runs nearby sell-side without going back to the morning low when price books higher all morning. [refines B4 lunch]
- **Practice outside the live account** (profitable traders still do drills); record yourself narrating. [study advice]
