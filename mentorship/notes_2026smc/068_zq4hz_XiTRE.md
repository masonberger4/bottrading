## Rules in ICT's wording
- **15-minute chart is a "bellwether"**: "you can utilize [it] in and of itself to frame all day trading, all of your scalping ... and get in sync with a move that's already underway on a higher time frame."
- **Daily boundaries**: the open of the last up-close candle (OB) and a discount wick. While above the wick CE, "we have to at least be aware of the upper half because it could come down just to that level and turn bullish ... Only until we close below consequent encroachment, do we then refer back to this area and only use the lower half."
- A larger-bodied candle's open becomes the change in state "if we ever trade below it, but we're inside this range. So, we're content with using this."
- **Up to go down**: from 09:30, run buy side into a gap and its CE, then roll over to sell side.
- Low-hanging-fruit objective first; a runner can target the deeper gap/sell side.
- After the daily wick sweep: "look how fast and sharply we returned back inside that range."
- **Daily framing or sit**: "That's the easiest strongest delivered price runs ... If you can't structure or frame an idea from the daily chart, then you need to probably sit on your hands." Forcing the model when the market won't yield "does make you a bad trader."
- **Model 2022 = OTE** ("my oldest model ... the ICT optimal trade entry ... Same criteria").
- **Lunch macro setup**: lunch 11:30-13:30; "The setup usually forms in the first hour"; buy side taken "during the 11:50 a.m. to 12:10 macro. That's when the algorithm will start to do its work"; target the 10:00 low. If it doesn't deliver in the second hour, hold into the PM session.
- Bodies respecting the FPFVG CE = failure swing.
- New relative equal lows framed by an old liquidity pool = "suspect" (likely drawn to).
- Bearish suspension block: VI at the high and the low.
- Correct drawing: include the one-tick VI between a close and next open.
- **IFVG volume**: "all of the volume that's represented by the bodies" sat in the IFVG's lower half = bearish; no footprint/volume profile needed.
- Retracement into a range is likely after a precise target is hit; "do it in the first half of the week, be done."
- Event horizon between two FVGs (midpoint between gaps); gyrating around it at a dull time = stand aside.
- **Holding period**: short-term = held past the 18:00 restart (2-5 days; "one shot one kill"); day trading = out before the 17:00 settlement. "I would not hold anything over the weekend now. The gap risk is absolutely ridiculous."
