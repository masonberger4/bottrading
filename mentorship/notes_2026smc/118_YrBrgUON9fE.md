## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Time distortion: go up in timeframe**: "Time distortion requires you to go up in time frame. Ranges that spend a lot of high resistance on the higher time frame, the way you can combat that is drop down to lower time frames." 1m chop -> 5m -> 15m ("It's already jumped off the chart"); if nothing on 15m, "I would go to the 1 hour or the 4 hour, and I would look for the inefficiency it's trying to get to." HTF version: a weekly sideways range "is waiting for the fulfillment of a higher time frame ... PD array." [refines 113; see conflict 65]
- **Bearish FVG rule (restated)**: "It can wick above midpoint, but it cannot lay down a body in the upper half if you're looking for it to go lower." Bullish mirror: "If it does leave a body [in the lower half], it needs to leave the range entirely, then come back, re-enter it, use it as support ... then stay out of the lower half." [repeats B6]
- **"As soon as it touches [the CE] ... watch what the body does. Does the body close below it? ... that's real institutional order flow."** [repeats]
- **Continuous contract for HTF levels**: "whenever you're looking at higher time frame analysis, you want to be using a continuous contract because it's always factoring in front month." "If you're just simply looking at the contract month of delivery ... you're not getting the full picture." The 23,999 sell-side existed only on the continuous chart; carry those levels onto the front month. [refines B6 A3; conflict 41]
- **SMT = distribution, not a trigger**: NQ lower high vs ES higher high "is indicative of heavy distribution and no new buying ... It's not the catalyst that's driving it down. It's just ... showing you that there isn't new buying." "That's what I use SMT for." S&P > Dow for credibility (500 vs 30 stocks). [refines B6 conflict 51]
- **Two PD arrays in conflict = low probability**: "We have the arm wrestling match between two PD arrays. So, right away, does this indicate high probability? No. Because both sides could be justified. When it is one-sided ... There's no alternative view that would make sense. That's high probability." [repeats 120; conflict 64]
- **Uncertainty protocol**: "whenever I have been confronted with uncertainty, my experience has taught me to dial back frequency ... and then lower leverage ... go down to the smallest leverage you can." [repeats B4/B5]
- **Every bias has a trigger line**: DXY suspension block; "this would be the trigger mechanism for me to go bearish ... I'm not playing both sides." [repeats 117]
