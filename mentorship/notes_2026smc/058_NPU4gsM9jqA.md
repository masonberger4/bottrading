## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Weekly premium draw = NWOG** inside the premium half of a daily suspension block: "if we're bearish, this is going to act like an inversion fair value gap ... this was the draw for the week." That "limits the upside" and points to lower prices.
- Suspension block definition: "a bullish buy side inefficiency, but specifically a suspension block because a volume imbalance at the high and a volume imbalance at the low." Split it in half; premium half = sell zone if bearish.
- Repeated failed rallies (4 attempts) before a premium open = weakness.
- **NWOG rejection**: Wednesday wicked into the NWOG, "can't overcome the new week opening gap and it leaves the body lower than where the open was" = go lower.
- **Premium wick CE**: a daily close below a wick's CE makes it a premium array: "We don't want any bodies above that wick's halfway point ... It delineates where price is not allowed to go." Failing to even touch it = "weak. It's bearish."
- Successive daily wick CEs traded through = continuation.
- **Day-of-week profile on CPI/PPI week**: "Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday is going to be a little bit more difficult ... after Wednesday's PM session, things should get really easy, which means it's low resistance liquidity run conditions" (Thu/Fri expansion to the weekly draw).
- **Weekly range**: premium draw (NWOG) and discount draw (daily relative equal lows) bracket the week.
- **TGIF**: from the week's high to Friday's low, after Friday pierces and works back above the low, "how far can it retrace? 20 to the 30% of the weekly range." "You're forecasting where the close of the week is going to be."
- 1H/5m: market maker sell model ("smart money reversal, low-risk sell, first stage distribution, second stage distribution, biggest part of the run ... to weekly target").
- **ETH fills RTH gaps**: compare RTH gaps from the 16:14 settlement to the 09:30 open each week. "Many times electronic trading hours goes into these gaps before regular trading hours does ... whatever electronic trading hours does, regular trading hours will tend to overlap that and redeliver those price runs." Hence overnight highs/lows and relative equal highs/lows get run in RTH.
- Liquidity runs also "turn on buy programs" / "pursuit of yield" for algorithms, not only stops.
- **Friday time rule**: with relative equal highs nearby but the weekly draw unmet, "don't think that it needs to go up there first. It only has today, Friday." The 07:00 pre-market start and 09:30 Judas swing are the "rocket fuel" toward the weekly draw.
- After the weekly draw is hit on Friday: "I would personally be done with the week."
