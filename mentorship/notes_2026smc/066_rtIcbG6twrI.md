# 2026 SMC 066: Monday before CPI/PPI with Caleb: exploring with one contract, ORG CE fib routine, five-candle rule inside an inefficiency (Monday live session)

## Summary
Week plan: Tue/Wed morning sessions are news-driven and should be avoided; trade PM sessions; cleanest price Wed PM to Fri close. Daily: price dropped into the gap between two wicks (a daily suspension block, VI at the low) = discount; prior Friday's discount wick CE is the first premium array; a daily close above it = higher. The NWOG did not fully close. Mild long bias toward Friday's settlement (30,048.5) and the NWOG, because RTH has a liquidity void (no RTH prints) between settlement and the open. ICT probed with one paper contract: long held above a wick CE, stopped; short at a wick CE toward sell side, then looked to reverse. Key lesson: a bearish FVG that holds price for too many candles (five or more) is failing; also don't sell in a deep discount.
## Rules in ICT's wording
- **News-week plan**: "absolutely insane volatility in the morning session" on CPI/PPI + Fed days; "PM session probably going to be the better ... more discernible price run." "Once Wednesday's done, then we'll probably see the cleanest price action Wednesday p.m. to Friday's close."
- No-news Monday before big news: "it's a 50/50 whether or not we're going to have a nice price run."
- **Daily wicks**: always grade the wick to the left (show the 50%). Prior Friday's discount wick CE "could act simply as a premium array. Any movement above this on a closing basis on this daily chart would warrant likely higher prices." Next target: the next wick's CE.
- Daily gap between two wicks ("separation between the two wicks") is not the FVG itself; it's the imbalance part. The suspension block "has not fully closed in."
- Clean slate: look at the chart fresh, no preconceived idea, find what is "too smooth, too clean" (liquidity).
- **Minor vs prominent buy side**: nearby relative equal highs are minor; the NWOG is more prominent. A minor pool can still be traded but price "could potentially keep going."
- Daily VI should act "like a breakaway gap": if price is not interested in returning, the swept sell side is sufficient to send price higher.
- **ORG CE fib routine (prep before 09:30)**: anchor the fib at the prior settlement (e.g. 30,048.5), drag it to ~09:32 on the time axis, show all levels, label "RTH ORG CE"; at 09:30 move the low anchor to the opening price. He prefers manual over scripts ("I don't trust the data").
- 70% to the ORG CE "about 10:00. That's usually the statistics."
- Bullish OB after a short-term low is taken: change in state of delivery; watch for a 09:30 head fake.
- **Long hold rule**: "As long as we stay in the upper half of the wick, I'm okay ... as long as the close stays above the halfway point"; stop below the wick CE.
- One contract = no partials; manage through the stop. New traders: micros, not minis.
- Short at a wick CE toward sell side after a "failure to launch"; then look to reverse long at the sell side.
- A body below a level "is much more significant than just doing wicks."
- **RTH liquidity void**: the gap from settlement to the open with no RTH prints is a "liquidity void"; "regular trading hours has to balance out electronic trading hours"; "I place more emphasis on regular trading hours than electronic trading hours." (Matches 058.)
- Time stop: "If it can't do it by 10:10, then I'll bail." Event horizon between two lows; a close below it means a run to the relative equal lows (~29,395).
- **Five-candle rule**: "Inefficiencies, you want to have one, two at most. Three, the probability start to shift lower ... three to five candles ... No more than five candles. This is the last ditch." "A market that's in a hurry to get somewhere doesn't want to go in the gap ... one candle and not leave a body there." "The only time I'm counting candles is when inside inefficiencies."
- **Don't sell in a discount**: an FVG short at the daily low after a straight drop "goes against what I taught in the very first month of ... 2016 private mentorship." "You want to buy in a discount."
- Failed bearish FVG = draw back up to the old low it broke; trailed stops get wiped.
- **Mondays**: new traders "try not to trade on Mondays ... the manipulation and accumulation part of the week," except NFP week ("the cleanest price action tends to be on the Monday of non-farm payroll week ... always trading Monday"). Mondays with no news before big Tue/Wed news are where new traders "fail most."
