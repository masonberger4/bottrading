So, yes, the wicks are allowed to do the damage." "when the market's compression builds up over days ... it creates a release where it's just springs ahead." - **Discount wick CE = continuation switch**: "I would like to see it trade down below that and close below that ... generally, not all the time ... indicative of a continuation ... If we fail to get below the midpoint of the wick on a closing basis ... we're going to stay in high resistance liquidity run conditions." [refines B7 wick CE]
