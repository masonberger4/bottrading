## Rules in ICT's wording (as far as the translation allows)
- Calendar: Forex Factory, USD only, keep red/orange (high/medium); grey/yellow low-impact are mostly noise. Do it on the weekend.
- NFP week: "Monday before Non-Farm Payrolls is the day you must be focused and looking for a setup." Any other Monday "is a lottery". New traders avoid Thursday and Friday of NFP week; "to 11:00 a.m. Wednesday is your safe zone." "It is not true" that ICT says never trade Mondays.
- 10:00 news day (ISM/JOLTS): wait for the first 30 minutes of trading to form liquidity/inefficiency before the 10:00 news, then work with what's left after it.
- Monthly chart: mark prior month high, low and close (monthly open "not important", because the new week opening gap carries it; "every 4 weeks or so" the monthly opening gap is embedded in a NWOG). Weekly: same. Levels bold/black, right-aligned, shown on all timeframes.
- Buy-side above the monthly high needs no extra label: liquidity rests above it by definition.
- Daily: locate where price is within the prior month's range (equilibrium). Mark the daily SIBI; a SIBI with a volume imbalance at both its high and low = a "stop block"; shade the 0/25/50/75/100 inside it; key level line thickness by timeframe.
- Octant = midway between the 0% (top) and first quadrant (12.5%). Upper octant used as a level.
- Large wick: split at 50%; bodies should stay below the midpoint when short ("premium sensitivity"); a body close above the wick midpoint = concern.
- RTH ORG: put a line at Friday's 16:14/16:15 RTH close labeled "RTH ORG calculation"; after 09:30 relabel low (if open above) or high (if open below). Discount gap = open below prior close.
- "Half of the gap closes by 10:00 in 70% of cases" (first 30 minutes). Gap down -> price usually tries to climb to the gap. Two competing targets for the 09:30-10:00 opening range: nearest minor sell side vs the ORG high (prior close). 09:30 drove straight to the ORG close, then on to the daily SIBI high.
- Macro: "the last 10 minutes of an hour and first 10 minutes of the new hour"; "macro gives you no direction, only the time price should become active." Consolidation through a macro = concern; then expect the move at 10 minutes past the hour ("recent market activity" observation, not decades old).
- Partial profit at the "event horizon": halfway between the entry-side low and the target line (e.g. lower quadrant of the daily array); close 1 of 3 micro contracts there; move stop just above the last short-term high; "funded the trade".
- Two PD arrays nested (FVG CE overlapping a wick CE) give strong feedback.
- After the open filled the gap and ran stops: bearish FVG -> inversion check; big up-close candle after the stop run, next candle opens with immediate rebalance (no gap, no VI) = "one of my strongest PD arrays"; short with stop one tick above the up-close candle's 50% (mean threshold); take 50-65% off below the next low, rest to the sell side.
