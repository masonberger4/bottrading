## Summary
How ICT engages price at or near all-time highs. On the continuous MNQ daily chart he toggles back-adjustment (B-ADJ) on, which brings an old high (Oct 30 2025, longest wick of the cluster) back into range; that wick's CE (26,745) becomes the HTF premium array. Add the weekly TGIF level (20% of last week's range from the high) and Friday's RTH ORG CE as draws. Today's RTH ORG was a discount gap near the ATH: price went to the ORG CE, closed below the lowest octant, and never left a body near the wick CE, so he shorted the wick (CE to candle close) and targeted Friday's ORG CE, which traded "perfectly". He then picks the market to trade by which index is still near its old high (NQ) vs far above it (ES).
## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Continuous contract + back-adjust toggle at ATHs**: without B-ADJ "we're really far away still from these levels. So right away I'm not interested"; "When we toggle it on notice what happens. It brings price back down into a range where we can use it." "The secret is that continuous contract toggling it on and off and where we [are] in deference to previous all-time highs." [new; data question: back-adjusted vs unadjusted continuous series]
- **Longest wick**: the Oct 30 2025 high "has a wick ... longer than the previous and the two subsequent candlestick highs. So ... the longest wick is why I'm utilizing that." [refines B4 longest wick (091)]
- **Premium vs discount wick (terminology)**: a wick at the candle high = premium wick; below the body = discount wick; in context, "if we're below it then it'll be a premium wick". Grade it at CE. [new terminology]
- **Wick = gap, read like an IFVG**: "This is how we interpret the wick. It's a premium gap. So think of this as a inversion fair value gap"; "best case scenario for entry is going to be at the midpoint ... to the low of that wick"; on the return "it needs to show no willingness to get up into consequent encroachment. Not even for a wick now." Short "at consequent encroachment of the wick ... to the close of that candlestick. So anything in here ... is fair game." [refines B3 wick CE body filter]
- **TGIF**: "if it's a up close candle forming or a bullish week, I look for measurements to 20 30% of the week range" from the weekly high (up to 40% sometimes); measure from Thursday 13:00 and keep re-reading; "If it doesn't form on Friday ... I'm always looking for the potential for it to form on Monday. If it doesn't form by Tuesday, I'm abandoning it." [refines older TGIF; new Monday/Tuesday carry-over rule]
- **Weekend sentiment flips**: "whatever the sentiment is going into the weekend, it's probably going to be the opposite." [opinion; not for the engine]
- **Discount ORG near the ATH**: allow price to go to the ORG CE, and if still bullish allow a full close, a trade above it, then the ORG used as a discount array. "When this candlestick went to consequent encroachment and then closed down here below its lowest octant ... I interpreted that as it's probably not going to run for full gap closure. And this could be the very extreme high." [refines B4 ORG grading]
- **Prior Friday's ORG CE as the draw**: "50% of last Friday's regular trading hours opening range gap" plus TGIF 20% were "the two levels that were important for me in terms of deriving a bias". Target hit: "the gap midpoint was traded to here perfectly and then we came all the way back up." [refines B4 prior ORGs stay live]
- **First presented FVG after displacement** that takes a short-term low; old FVGs "don't go stale ... They're not supply and demand zones"; extend them into the next session. [refines B1/B3]
- **Common gaps** (tiny separations) are watched but carry little detail; "the gap needs to be a little bit more significant." [new]
- **Removing the TP to run the trade**: he removed his TP and left stacked buy limits, then took the majority off anyway because of the news climate. [noted; discretionary]
- **No re-adding near the exit**: "because it's not so far away from where I exited ... I don't feel comfortable re-entering the partials ... If this would have been say another 20 to 30 handles lower and then we retrace ... I would have added them back." [new; refines B4 re-entry]
- **Bolo (defensive array) = stop location, not entry**: "as long as my stop is outside of that I'm completely comfortable"; if it trades there "it tells me I'm wrong ... I'm not looking to get back in with the same trade idea." Some stop management "I'm just never going to teach." [refines B4 defensive arrays; conflict check: B4 #36 re-entry]
- **15-second chart detail**: first 15s candle that closes below the IFVG, retrace fails the CE = heaviness; minor then significant sell-side. Links to last week's entry drills (drills are for overcoming fear, not profit). [refines B3 sub-minute reads]
