## Summary
Teaching theme: cast a vision (the draw) first, then build a narrative candle by candle. Dec 23 at 09:30: run up, false break above a short-term high (turtle soup), displacement back below a swing low; the first FVG after the turtle soup is kept on the chart and projected forward up to 20 days. On the later day, pre-market rallied straight into that Dec 23 FVG, rejected, and the draw became the overnight relative equal lows. The 09:30 FPFVG repelled price at its low during the 09:50-10:10 macro; a SIBI formed and its CE was retested; a consolidation range (FPFVG to the new minor low) had a SIBI at its equilibrium that capped the retrace; the 10:50-11:10 macro displaced into the overnight lows. PM: after 13:30, a rally to the FPFVG and the top of the daily suspension block, an IFVG short to the AM-session low and relative equal lows, then lower into the close.
## Rules in ICT's wording
- **First FVG after a turtle soup, carried forward**: "a short-term high, rallied above it, false break above that, and turtle souped it right back down ... that displacement is significant ... Notice there's no fair value gap here until that after it took that high. So this PD array is first presented fair value gap after turtle soup ... The first fair value gap that forms after that, keep that on your chart. Project that forward ... What's the lowest one? 20 days. Keep it on your chart for 20 days." Works on the buy side too. "You don't need this to be a market reversal." [new; see 142]
- **Zoom out to find standout runs**: "By zooming out ... your eye will jump right to these types of swing highs and swing lows." [repeats]
- **Overnight low as the routine draw**: "mark off midnight Eastern time to 7:00 in the morning. Whatever the lowest low there is, if it's relative equal lows, that's a loaded deal ... It's going to want to seek that if it's bearish. Not all the time, not that very day." [new; PREMARKET-RANGE]
- **REQ test**: "What makes them relative equal? The one to the right is slightly higher than the one to the left. So this is a strong candidate for a raid"; if not, "it can eventually trade through it, but not without at least retracing back for an inefficiency above the low ... or a short-term high." [repeats 140]
- **Macro candles are the "leading actors"**: "950 to 1010 macro ... return back into the first presented value gap ... should repel price or keep it from going in the upper half ... just breezes to the low of it ... That's a SIBI ... we want a supporting actor ... to come up ... to half of its range." [repeats]
- **Common gap until the low breaks**: "this little tiny piece right there ... Why is this not so important? Because we haven't even broken this low ... If this would have broken down below that, then that would have been much more salient ... that fair value gap is just a common gap." [refines FVG-VALID]
- **Time-distortion range, equilibrium inefficiency**: range = "from the premium array it reacted to to the new sell-side low"; "Look at the midpoint ... That equilibrium price point has to have an inefficiency inside that range ... That's a SIBI ... it's only going to go up to where I say it's going to go with this fair value gap ... during time distortion." [new]
- **Bodies in the lower quadrant = about to fall**: "The wick goes almost to the top but the bodies stay in the lower half. That's the signature." [repeats]
- **10:50-11:10 macro displaces**: "until we get to a macro time ... 10:50 to 11:10 it's going to displace." [repeats MACRO-GRID]
- **Lunch and PM**: "don't trade between 11:30 in the morning to 1:30 in the afternoon if you're brand new ... Wait until 1:30"; PM draws: relative equal lows plus the "regular trading hours AM session low"; "you want to see speed ... elongated candlesticks." Partials at those lows: "Are you rich? Are you retired? Because if you're not, don't give advice." [repeats]
- **IFVG = last displacement before a premium array**: "an inversion fair value gap because it's the last major displacement on the upside before it hits a premium array and then it folds over ... your model 2022 just on a very small time frame." [repeats]
- **Daily suspension block**: "the real inefficiency is framed by the volume imbalance down here and that volume imbalance there ... It is not ... a liquidity void ... a liquidity void is when there's no trading at all." PM rally went "up to go down" at its high; upper quadrant sliced, so "likely going to reach for half of that range." [repeats B9]
- **Narrative over structure; time distortion**: "Narrative trumps market structure ... time distortion can screw up current market structure." [repeats]
- **Pre-market rhythm test**: "I use the pre-market hours ... 7 o'clock to 9:30 ... if I'm really dialed in and I'm calling every candlestick's fluctuation ... I know I'm going to destroy the opening range ... But if I'm not able to do that in pre-market hours, then I'm going to wait probably till 10:00. So, I'll let the first 30 minutes trade." [new; OPEN-RANGE-TRADE]
- **Holidays thin; Swiss precision or nothing**: "I stopped trading around the holidays because it gets very thin ... I demand the market to be Swiss precision level delivery or I'm not engaging." [repeats SEASON-ACTIVITY]
- **No setup = do nothing; no narrative = forcing a trade.** [repeats]
