## Rules in ICT's wording
- **Wick CE as target**: "take this wick ... Drop your fib on the open of that candle down to its low. Only highlight the 50% level ... that is your consequent encroachment ... your next downside target. If we close below this, then we'll likely take out that swing low."
- **Event horizon (definition)**: anchor low to the next lower low (or two old highs), only the 0.5 level. "This is a very, very strong candidate for price reaching to. If it goes lower, it could just just touch that level and then turn around." Use = "predetermined partials and also weighing the magnitude and velocity of a price run."
- **Nested event horizons**: two adjacent event horizons treated as a gap; "if ... it closes below, then my confidence level increases ... both event horizons would be closed below."
- At event horizon: "an event on the horizon that you must make a decision about where your stop loss is now going to be adjusted to, and are you going to take partials? And you should take partials."
- **Rollover toggle for targets**: VIs shift with adjustment on/off; "where there is a plethora of those volume imbalances, I want to first look at those"; then old highs/lows and inefficiencies; needs a bias first.
- **Refused draw -> sell stop**: "If it fails to get there ... you can put a sell stop order ... right below that low ... it's putting you in net short." Measure from the failed high (below event horizon); "when it breaks below that low, you want to see it then become very, very, very aggressive."
- "Time first, then price"; post-macro (after 10:10) run lower; graded levels between the old high and event horizon get used by bodies; "coloring outside the line" (small wick overshoots) is permissible.
- **Daily HTF context for intraday**: "every day until those targets on the daily chart are met, strive for entries that put you in sync with that run based on the daily chart."
- **Previous day's range retracement**: "when the market is indeed bearish, the market's going to be only retracing in the lower half of the previous day's range. If it does retrace up to half of the previous day's range, most likely it's going to be an afternoon or late session continuation or the next day." Failing to reach half = "really bearish."
- **Daily wick + London**: price below a discount wick turns it premium; London open kill zone (03:00) traded up into "50% of the previous day's wick" and that made the daily high; "The bodies are supporting the logic."
- **Herding**: public stop levels create liquidity pools; ICT is dialing back public participation.
- New traders may exit early before the target ("It's okay to say ... I'm done"); the draw "does not need to" be reached; "it just needs to be a reasonable expectation."
- Study regime: max two videos a day, log questions, "at least 60 minutes a day in tape reading"; record the screen if you can't watch live; no market replay.
- Money management: 2-6% compounding a month; don't share results.
