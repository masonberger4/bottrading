# ICT Silver Bullet (SB): everything in the notes

Sources: every notes file that matches "silver bullet" (36 files; one, cc11_003, uses the name for
something else), plus digests/models.md 1.4 and section 6, digests/times.md E, and mentorship_rules.md
T6, L14, L15, M3, D2 and P6. Wording was checked against data/mentorship/captions at 044 ~0:49:58,
~0:54:53; 047 ~08:00; 048 ~57:36, ~1:03:12, ~1:34:31, ~1:44:30; 054 ~08:21-09:52, ~15:00-15:56,
~18:01-18:40; 058 ~44:20-45:23; 059 ~08:56; 060 ~56:50; 065 ~4:48:20-4:49:40, ~5:15:00; 066
~0:42:10, ~0:44:20; 068 ~0:16:00-0:18:00; 072 ~35:05.

Short answer: the SB is a separate, named setup that ICT says repeats daily. It is not a one-off.
Structurally it is the same displacement -> FVG entry as his 2022 model and the entry legs of
the market maker model. What makes it its own setup is the time box (one-hour windows) and the
"10 handles of room, take 5" framework. Section 5 has the details.

## 2. The rule set as ICT states it

### 2.1 Time windows (New York local time, DST observed; 054 ~08:51)
- **AM 10:00-11:00.** Named in every SB video (044 ~0:50:05 onward).
- **PM 14:00-15:00.** First taught in 054 (~12:41). 044 ~0:54:53 says "an afternoon session I'll
  teach you later". Also 056, 058 ~55:21, 060, 066 ~0:42:23, 068, 072, 080, 081, 092, 099.
- **London 03:00-04:00.** 054 ~08:51, 060, 092 ~20:04, 099. This one is for futures and FX. SPY
  does not trade then.
- Extra windows that appear later: **15:00-16:00** (092 ~20:04; 099 ~53:30). An optional
  **07:00-08:30** (099). For FX only, 03:00-05:00 and 07:00-09:00 (065A ~55:55).
- **The entry must be inside the window.** "If I get an entry at 11:01 ... at 9:57 you're not
  following the rules" (058 ~44:37). The exit can come after the window, e.g. a London trade held
  to 05:00 or the 07:00 open (054 ~11:27), or a target printed after 11:00 (047 ~08:00; 059: low
  just after 11:00).

### 2.2 What must happen first
- **A draw on liquidity is the main input.** "The factor you lean heavily on is knowing where
  price is going to go ... not necessarily the bias" (054 ~09:31-09:52). Direction comes from the
  next draw.
  - Draw list from 054 ~04:02-06:40: previous day H/L; previous session H/L (London H/L for the
    AM); previous week H/L; a return to or expansion from the current/old NWOG; a classic OTE; a
    2022-model FVG/BISI/SIBI.
  - If a draw was already raided that morning, use an HTF pool or inefficiency (054 ~06:50).
- **Displacement toward the draw** that leaves an FVG facing the trade. 044 ~0:50:05 (the
  original definition): "between 10 and 11 there will be a displacement in a direction you should
  be anticipating; once it occurs you wait for an FVG to form ... traded into and then repriced to
  a pool of liquidity opposing your entry".
- **MSS.** It is shown in almost every example: 054 London and PM, 055, 058, 059 ("after a shift
  in market structure", ~08:56), 060, 069, 093. It is stated as a precondition in 059 and 093
  ("after SMT + MSS"). 048 ~1:34:31: the FVG must sit beyond the level that needed breaking. An FVG
  under that level "is not even a fair value gap, it's a balanced price range".
- **A liquidity sweep before the MSS is not required in any definition.** It is present in most
  examples: 055 sell-side at the open, 056 lunch lows, 058 the 09:00 lows, 060 4139.5, 062 the
  weekly gap high, 063, 069 the London/Friday highs, 072 sell-side at 09:30, 093. 060 ~46:40 calls
  that version the "AM blend with the 2022 model".
- Day context makes it better or kills it. Skip it on "spotty" days with no displacement or
  inefficiency (045 ~31:22-36:27). It is weak when ES/NQ/YM/DXY are out of sync (045; 048). An
  immediate rebalance in 10-11 means expect more than 5 handles (048 ~39:02). To "supercharge" it,
  align it with the weekly profile and seasonals (048 ~2:02:12).

### 2.3 Which FVG qualifies
- **Type**: a "classic ICT FVG" (054 ~09:31) that faces the trade: a SIBI for shorts, a BISI for
  longs (044 ~1:06:34). It must fit the narrative; "not every 3-candle imbalance" counts (048
  ~28:06).
- **Where it formed: this changed over time** (section 4.1).
  - Early wording: it forms in the window (044; 047 ~08:00 "the setup forms between 10 and 11";
    054 ~09:31 "a classic FVG that forms between 3:00 and 4:00"; 054 ~15:26 "the first FVG that
    forms inside of and reprices to between 2:00 and 3:00").
  - From 058 on: "the FVG could actually occur before 10 o'clock; the entry must be taken between
    10 and 11" (058 ~44:37). Also 059 ~08:56 (both a pre-10:00 FVG and an in-window FVG are "good").
    Also 060 ~56:50: "it does not need to be an FVG that forms between 10 and 11". 065B ~5:15:20:
    it can be the FVG in the 09:30->10:00 leg ("the run-up if bullish ... the move from 09:30 down to
    10:00's low if bearish").
- **Which one**: the *first* FVG in the bias direction during the hour (048 ~1:03; 054 ~15:26). If
  price leaves it without a retrace, move to the newer FVG in the window ("evolving SB", 057
  ~00:02:16; 058 ~46:43). "Several FVGs in the leg are each valid entries" (093 ~00:09:17).
- **Timeframe**: any. Start on the 15m and step down 5-4-3-2-1m, then seconds charts. "If you're
  adamant about finding one it will be there" (065B ~4:48:40). His own examples use 5m (047, 054),
  3m (055), 2m (063), 1m (048, 057, 066, 068, 069, 072), 15s (059) and 30s (092). The 054 boxes
  begin on the 09:55 / 13:55 bar.

### 2.4 Entry
- Use a limit order inside the FVG on the retrace, for full size. Don't chase. "Better to miss it
  than chase" (057 ~00:01:16-00:02:45).
- Where in the FVG:
  - near edge (044 "at the low of that FVG"; 057 at the evolved FVG low 4206.25; 054 quotes sit at
    the near edge or in the lower half);
  - CE as the "worst case" for the room math (048 ~1:03:12);
  - 066: a limit a little above the FVG candle's high so it fills.
- Bodies should stay inside the gap. A wick through it ("mohawk") does not invalidate it (054
  ~10:33, ~14:50).
- **Cancel** if price trades through the target before the fill (057 ~00:03:35).
- Don't enter early at market when that puts the stop too far away (057 ~00:02:54).

### 2.5 Stop
It is never stated in 054. The other sources give:
- 2 pts above entry, about the top of the evolved FVG (057 ~00:06:41; risk $1,000 on 10 ES).
- Below the candle whose low forms the FVG's low end, which is also below the bullish OB mean
  threshold (090 ~2:14:00).
- Below the entry candle (086 ~11:35).
- Just above the CE of the higher of two FVGs (069 ~13:46, the "SB logic" short).
- 054 ~10:33: a wick a tick above the FVG "would not have stopped you with the rules I teach". So
  the stop is not at the FVG edge itself.
- Abort early when a body closes outside the FVG and the next candle exceeds it (045 ~01:19:58).
  This is general FVG management, applied to an SB-time short.

### 2.6 Target and minimum room
- **Target**: the opposing untapped liquidity pool (044; 054). In practice he takes **~5 ES handles**
  or covers at the old low/high (044 ~0:57:16 "goal is 5 handles, hitting the pool is optional";
  047 ~08:00 "minimum five handles"; 054 ~12:00, ~13:35; 068 ~0:17:55 "we just need five handles").
  Optional extended target: the next imbalance beyond the pool (047 ~08:26). Runner to the next
  pool after the first partial (058 ~48:13). Layered exits at NWOG high / 75% / CE (057).
- **Room ("framework")**: the best-case expected delivery, measured from the entry and allowed to
  run "to it and through it" (054 ~00:56, ~12:00):
  - >= **10 handles** (40 ticks) for index futures, >= **15 pips** for FX (054 ~00:36; 058 ~37:16;
    059 ~00:10:36; 060 ~50:00). Below that it is "not a classic SB, scalp only" (058 ~54:40).
  - Looser versions: > 5 handles (044 ~0:57:16); 10 for new traders, 5 for experienced (046
    ~0:34:00); "4-5 handles minimum" (072 ~35:11).
  - Conversions: 10 handles = 20 pips (054 ~00:50); 5 ES handles = 20 NQ handles (049). For SPY,
    5 ES pts is about $0.50 and 10 about $1.00.

### 2.7 Trades per window, sessions, instruments
- "One of these will form every single day" in any one market across the three windows. "If you
  don't get it in London, wait for the AM; if not there, wait for the PM" (054 ~18:01-18:33).
- If it doesn't form in your market, do not trade another market out of frustration. Wait for the
  next window (046 ~0:55:21).
- "If I was only going to trade one time a day in the morning ... between 10 and 11" (047 ~08:00).
  The rules file reads this as "one AM SB a day". The caption makes it a preference, not a cap.
- He does take more than one entry in a window: 049 (two shorts), 060 (two longs), 063 (an add),
  069 (an optional pyramid). After the AM, he also trades the PM SB of the same day (068, 072,
  074: AM and PM both shown).
- Instruments: "all asset classes" (054 ~00:13). Shown on ES, NQ, EURUSD and GBPUSD. Trade one
  market (054 ~18:01).

### 2.8 How it differs from the 2022 model and the market maker model
- **2022 model** (M2 in mentorship_rules.md):
  - a run on liquidity is required (buy-side for a short), then displacement that breaks a
    short-term level, then an FVG in the premium half of the leg, at any time of day.
  - The SB requires the time window plus the draw and the 10/5 framework. It does not require the
    sweep, and any qualifying FVG in the window may be used.
  - ICT blends them often ("AM blend with the 2022 model", 060 ~46:40; 062; 069; 058 EURUSD
    pyramid onto a 2022-model long). 081 lists them side by side as three "simple rule-based
    models": SB, 2022 model, OTE.
- **Market maker buy/sell model** (104):
  - a whole swing framework: original consolidation, accumulation stages, smart money reversal,
    distribution stages, target below the consolidation.
  - It gives draws and stages, not one entry rule.
  - The SB is an entry that can sit inside it: 104 ~55:24 spots "an NY open Silver Bullet" inside
    one MM sell model. 072 shows an AM SB long, then a "market maker sell model" in the PM.
- **One Trading Setup For Life** (067) makes it explicit: time window, then the pool raid against
  the bias, then MSS + FVG, then "enter on any PD array you like". The SB is that template with
  the PD array fixed to an FVG and the time fixed to one hour.

## 4. Contradictions and how they changed over time

1. **Must the FVG form inside the window?**
   - Inside: 044 ~0:50 (displacement "between 10 and 11", then the FVG); 047 ~08:00; 054 ~09:31
     and ~15:26 (Apr-May).
   - Before is fine: 058 ~44:37 (May 23) says the FVG "could actually occur before 10 o'clock; the
     entry must be taken between 10 and 11". Same in 059 ~08:56 (both fine), 060 ~56:50 (May 25,
     "does not need to be an FVG that forms between 10 and 11") and 065B ~5:15:20 (Jun 15, the
     FVG from the 09:30-10:00 leg).
   - Even in 047 and 054 the charted gaps start at ~09:40-09:55 / 13:55.
   - Reading: he loosened it within weeks of the lesson. From late May the rule is about the
     entry time only.
   - Correction to models.md section 6 / times.md D3: 066 does **not** say "FVG formed in that
     hour". The caption (~0:44:20) says only "the entries must be taken between 2 and 3".
2. **Minimum room.**
   - > 5 handles (044, March).
   - 10 for new traders, 5 for experienced (046, April).
   - 10 handles / 15 pips as the rule (054, 058, 059, 060, May).
   - "4-5 handles minimum" (072, July).
   - The take-profit is 5 handles throughout. The 10 is room to the draw, not the target.
3. **Windows grew.**
   - AM only (044, "PM later").
   - Then London + AM + PM (054).
   - Then FX 03-05 / 07-09 (065A), 15:00-16:00 (092, 099) and optional 07:00-08:30 (099).
4. **Late fill.** The rules file (D3 in times.md) says 068 accepts a fill a few minutes after
   11:00. The caption (068 ~0:17:35) says price went "a little bit higher" a few minutes after 11:00
   while the fill came inside the window with some drawdown. The rule is restated there: "you have
   to enter between 10 and 11". **No contradiction with 058.**
5. **Which FVG.**
   - The first FVG in the hour (048 ~1:03; 054 ~15:26).
   - Evolve to a newer one if the first is left unfilled (057; 058 ~46:43).
   - Any FVG in the leg is valid (093).
   - Sub-minute FVGs are allowed (059 15s; 065B; 092 30s).
6. **Sweep / MSS.** The 044 definition needs only displacement. MSS is required in 059 and 093
   wording and present in nearly every chart. A sweep is present in most examples but never
   required. 065B says "be adamant, it will be there", which drops conditions entirely.
7. **Frequency vs selectivity.**
   - "Forms every single day" (044 ~0:49:58; 048 ~1:44:30; 054 ~08:21, ~18:18; 090 ~2:00:22).
   - Against that: skip spotty days (045), skip when the room isn't there (046, 058), only ~3
     trades a week (099 ~47:36) and one good setup a week for new students (065B).
   - The 047 "one AM trade a day" is conditional ("if I was only going to trade one time a day").
8. **Stop.** No stop in the main lesson (054). Later sources give 2 pts above (057), below the
   FVG candle-1 / OB MT (090), below the entry candle (086) and above the CE of the higher FVG
   (069).
9. **Name reuse.** In 2017 Core Content (cc11_003) "my silver bullet" meant stock relative-strength
   divergence. It has no link to the 2023 setup.

## 5. Verdict and what the bot needs

### Separate and repeatable?
**Yes.**
- It has its own lesson (054, "time-based algorithmic trading model for all asset classes") and
  its own follow-up (057, "Evolving ICT Silver Bullet").
- It appears in 30+ videos from Mar 30 to Sep 27 2023. It is the label on most of his live
  2023 intraday fills (049, 057, 063, 066, 069, 076).
- He says it forms "every single trading day" in some market in one of the three windows. One
  market is enough: London, else AM, else PM. He admits it is not always in *your* market.
- With the 10-handle room filter applied, his own reviews reject some days (045, 046, 058 AM).
  The "every day" claim leans on dropping to seconds charts (065B).

It is **not a different price engine.** It is the 2022-model style displacement -> FVG retrace
toward a draw, restricted to fixed one-hour windows, plus the 10/5 framework. The market maker
model is a bigger swing context, and an SB can be one entry inside it (104; 072).

### What the bot does today (mentorship/strategy.py)
- `_in_sb(t, p)` returns True for minutes in `sb_windows=((600,660),(840,900))`, i.e. 10:00-11:00
  and 14:00-15:00.
- `Trade.sb` is True when the fill is inside a window. A PM order armed at 14:00-15:00 gets the
  setup tag `pm_sb`.
- `sb_only=True` (D2 test): the session's single sweep -> MSS -> FVG order may fill only inside
  that session's window. An order armed earlier waits for the window. A pending order is cancelled
  at the window end. `pending_minutes=30` still applies, so an order armed at 09:35 is gone by
  ~10:05.
- `sb_setup=True`: one *extra* trade per session inside the window (`done_key="SB_AM"/"SB_PM"`,
  setup `sb_am`/`sb_pm`), but only after the session's normal trade has filled. Its pending order
  is cut at the window end.
- Shared engine in both modes (`_scan`):
  - a sweep of a listed session pool (PDH/PDL, OR, London, lunch, prior PM, REL/REH) in the same
    session, then an MSS on a close through the swing that led into the sweep, then an FVG formed
    after the sweep extreme and still open;
  - `fvg_pick="last"`, entry at the near edge (`entry="edge"`), stop at the FVG bottom or the OB
    MT (`stop_mode="ob_mt"`);
  - T1 = the nearest target, T2 >= `min_draw_pts=10` ES pts; cancel if T1 trades before the fill.

### Gaps vs ICT's rules
1. **A sweep is mandatory** in the bot. For the SB, ICT requires displacement/MSS toward a draw;
   the sweep is optional.
2. **Direction comes from the sweep side**, not from choosing the draw first (054 ~09:52).
3. **No evolving SB.** Once the sweep is consumed by the first FVG, a newer FVG in the window
   can't re-arm the order (057).
4. **First vs last FVG.** ICT says the first FVG in the hour (048, 054). The bot picks the last
   open one.
5. **Target.** ICT takes a fixed ~5 ES pts (or the pool) as the main exit. The bot's T1 is the
   nearest level, which may sit below 5 pts or far beyond it.
6. **`sb_setup` runs only after a normal session trade.** On a day with no `am_mm`/`pm` fill the
   SB trade is just the regular trade, tagged `am_mm`.
7. **`pending_minutes=30` can expire a valid pre-10:00 FVG** before or early in the window under
   `sb_only`. ICT keeps the order working to the window end.
8. **Missing windows.** London 03:00-04:00 (no SPY data) and 15:00-16:00 are not covered.

The standalone `silver_bullet/strategy.py` is closer to 054. It has 3 windows, draw-first,
MSS -> FVG, framework >= 10 / target 5, and a near-edge limit valid to the window end. But it
requires the FVG to form *inside* the window (the early rule, dropped from 058 on), uses a stop
at the swing extreme and has no evolving re-arm. Per memory, that bot showed no edge.

### A faithful implementation needs
1. **Windows**: fills only 10:00-11:00 and 14:00-15:00 (03:00-04:00 on futures data); exits may
   run past; cancel unfilled orders at the window end; flat by 16:00.
2. **Draw first**: the nearest untaken pool from the 054 list (PDH/PDL, London H/L for AM, lunch
   or AM H/L for PM, PWH/PWL, NWOG, HTF FVG). Use an HTF pool if it is already raided. The trade
   direction is toward it.
3. **Room**: draw >= 10 ES pts (SPY ~$1.00) from the entry. Parameter 5 vs 10.
4. **Trigger**: displacement toward the draw that closes beyond the last opposing swing (MSS).
   Make the sweep an optional filter or tag (`sb_need_sweep`).
5. **FVG**: faces the trade, sits beyond the broken level, still open. It may come from the
   09:30-10:00 (13:30-14:00) leg (`sb_fvg_in_window` False/True). Take the first such FVG.
   **Evolving**: if price leaves without a retrace and a newer FVG forms in the window, move the
   limit there.
6. **Entry**: limit at the near edge (variant: CE). Cancel if the draw or +5 pts trades before
   the fill. Keep the order alive for the whole window.
7. **Stop**: beyond FVG candle 1 / OB MT (090). Variant: a fixed 2 ES pts beyond the FVG (057).
   Optional abort on a body close back through the FVG (045).
8. **Exit**: most at +5 ES pts (SPY ~$0.50) or the pool, whichever is first. Optional runner to
   the draw with the stop at breakeven.
9. **Count**: one SB per window, independent of the session's other trades. Optionally stop for
   the day after the first win (bread and butter, 044/046).
10. **Data limits**: the sub-minute FVGs he uses (15s/30s) can only be approximated on 1m bars.
    Expect fewer qualifying setups than his "every day".
