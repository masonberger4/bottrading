## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. RTH opening range gap: first hour, octants, prior day's gap
- DRAFT: **ORG CE is the first-hour draw**: "If you're trading at any time during that first hour of trading, if you do not know [the half-gap level] you are absolutely guessing" (107). Discount ORG = look for buy-side to the CE; premium = sell-side. A run away from the CE at the open "is a Judas swing ... opening up more potential profit" (107). [refines B1/B5 A1; see conflict 50]
- DRAFT: **CE hit in one candle**: "Boom. Went right at the consequent encroachment. Done. It's over ... just relax" (107). If it then fails the next octant by body = "not bullish," expect the other side (112). [new; see conflict 54]
- DRAFT: **Through the CE = full gap closure and beyond** on a bullish plan: "if we get to that consequent encroachment level ... and go through it, full gap closure obviously would be the next expectation ... it has to get to this level first" (106). [refines B5 A1]
- DRAFT: **Yesterday's ORG gradients** act as today's levels (bodies hold its low, bounce off its lower quadrant) (112). [repeats B4 last three ORGs]
- DRAFT: **Big gap needs commitment**: with an enormous premium ORG "you want to see it ... accelerate with range expansion," not "fishing below the old lows" (110). [new]
- DRAFT: **NWOG + RTH ORG only** on a Monday; event horizon between the NWOG high and the old intraday high (106). [refines B5 A2]

### A2. HTF anchor: the daily suspension block and its quadrants
- DRAFT: **Measure the block across its volume imbalances** (close to open) and grade it in quadrants and octants (107). [refines B5 A2 (105)]
- DRAFT: **Working only in the lower half = bearish** (109). Draw = the block low (109, 110).
- DRAFT: **Retracement cap after leaving the block**: "any retracement back in needs to be at this level [the lower quadrant, 25,155.75] or less. Anything climbing back above ... sets the stage for a run for intraday high"; bodies not touching it = "the signature that the algorithm lays down for smart money. This is going to go lower" (109, 110). [new]
- DRAFT: **Failure to reach the next octant on re-entry = weakness** (109). [refines B4 octants]
- DRAFT: **"Real" horizontal support/resistance = HTF block gradients that bodies respect** (109). [refines]

### A3. Continuous vs delivery contract (the lecture in 107)
- DRAFT: **Toggle back-adjustment only to find volume imbalances / suspension blocks**: "The only time I've ever utilized this function here is when I'm looking for volume imbalances ... They're not to find a fair value gap." The settlement-price toggle also changes what shows (107). [refines B5 A2; narrows conflict 41]
- DRAFT: **Keep both, adopt the one the bodies respect**; first preference = the VI inside the breaker's down-close candle (107). [new]
- DRAFT: **Continuous data for history**: the front month gets "spotty" before ~October; use continuous for older levels (107). [repeats]
- DRAFT: **Low-hanging fruit = the nearer series' level** (25,495 continuous vs 25,715 June from 25,333); the spread between them gauges "real buying strength" (107, as 105). [repeats; conflict 41]

### A4. Reading the tape: reluctance, timing, reclaimed gaps
- DRAFT: **Reluctance = warning ("canary in the coal mine")**: "When it should be ripping, tearing off, going lower, and it's not doing it, that's a big warning sign that ... we're probably going to go the other direction" (106). [new]
- DRAFT: **Should -> must**: on a second test of the same array the expectation "shifts from ... it should do it to it must do it. It needs to leave this energetically" (106); "it needs to do it in the next two candles" (112); the first FVG's high as "the last line in the sand ... on this candle or the next" (108). [new]
- DRAFT: **Reclaimed FVG / VI**: a gap used as an IFVG that price closes back through becomes a reclaimed gap in the new direction; bodies must stay out of the half nearest price (106, 111). Applies to wick-separated volume imbalances too (111). [refines B1/B4]
- DRAFT: **Heaviness**: wick can't reach the range high and the body closes below the wick CE = bearish; bodies can't stay inside the wick on the retrace (107, 108). [refines B5]
- DRAFT: **Consolidation without an FVG**: the last up-wick before the drop = an old high; use only its CE, no body above it (107). [refines B5 BOLO/wick CE]
- DRAFT: **The gap right before the low/high is taken** is the one that matters (109). [repeats B5 (104)]
- DRAFT: **Engineered sentiment**: on a headline-bearish day buy-side taken first (shorts out, breakout buyers trapped), then sell-side; "they cleared the board on both sides ... more prone to show you the real move" (112). Fast stop hunt below a low -> go for the opposing liquidity; after a stop-out, same array, same model (106). [refines B5 re-entry (099)]
- DRAFT: **Confluence low**: REQ lows + news-candle low + HTF block low + ORG CE (107); CPI low = "big bullseye" (108). [refines B5 A4 news magnets]

### A5. Entries
- DRAFT: **Bread-and-butter IFVG entry (106, 09:54)**: down-close candle's gap; wick may pierce ("It's the wick. It's not the body"), body closes beyond the CE; next candle can't reach the wick CE; enter on the retest ("When it hammered that right there, that's an entry"), stop beyond the gap, target minor pool then event horizon. [refines B4/B5]
- DRAFT: **LTF confirmation = the very next LTF FVG**: price at a 1m array; on 15s the move away should create an FVG, "the next one you could take as an entry"; no such gap = no trade (106). [refines B2/B4]
- DRAFT: **09:30-09:31 first presented FVG**: once left, only a wick may come back across the separation; "It's stronger if there's absolutely no candlestick portion at all" (109). [refines B1]
- DRAFT: **Hard-to-frame stop = no trade** (106, 112). [repeats]

### A6. Conditions filter: decoupling, Fridays, event risk
- DRAFT: **Decoupled averages lower precision**: "when they're all three doing whatever they want to do, that drops your probability"; YM gets little weight ("only 30 stocks") (108). SMT down-weighted on headline days (112). [refines B2 SMT; see conflict 51]
- DRAFT: **No Friday continuation against a divergence**: "if this was Thursday ... I would have entertained ... longs ... but not on a Friday. Not against the divergence" (108). [new]
- DRAFT: **Event-driven days**: one HTF target only, smaller size, "be content with enough"; 1m candles of 200-400 handles at the 18:32 Asian open (110, 111); danger days = observe only, profits there are "lottery" (112). [refines B5 stop after target]
- DRAFT: **Asian/London analysis waits for the open price** (18:00 / midnight) (109). [refines B1 midnight open]
- DRAFT: **Morning fallback**: if the morning loses interest, lunch 11:30-13:30 or PM from 13:30 (108). [refines; see B5 conflict 42]
- DRAFT: **First hour 09:30-10:30 = "an entire universe"**; beginners focus on the first 30-40 minutes (107). [refines B5 A4]

### A7. Management
- DRAFT: **Stepped partials**: half off by the second target, "one contract running and the majority of the position closed" (110). [refines B5 A7]
- DRAFT: **Protect at the opposing wick CE**: "A run into this and above would collapse my position. I would not let the stop loss at that high be challenged"; giveback to the wick CE "is not healthy" (110). [new; see conflict 53]
- DRAFT: **No re-entry after protecting** in an event climate (110). [refines B5 re-entry]
- DRAFT: **Withdraw rule**: a closing body through the key inefficiency/rejection block ends the morning ("show no more interest") (106, 108). [refines B5 BOLO]
- DRAFT: **Scaling into an IFVG**: "accumulated that, added to it, added to it" on an earlier ~200-handle trade (108). [noted; see conflict 55]
