## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. RTH opening range gap: bias, CE and projections beyond the gap (the batch's main theme)
- DRAFT: **ORG direction sets the morning bias.** "If we open up higher at 9:30 than where we settled at 4:14 p.m. ... that sets the bias bearish. We only want to look for shorts" (101). Beginner model after ~4 weeks: "only look for shorts in premium gaps in the first 30 minutes ... aim for the half gap, and best-case scenario would be 3/4 gap closure or full gap closure" (101). [refines B1 ORG; see conflict 44]
- DRAFT: **ORG CE 70% in the first 30 minutes**, higher when an ETH pool (the 08:23 low) sits near it (099, 100, 101). [repeats B1]
- DRAFT: **Projection beyond the gap = -0.5 SD of the ORG.** Bearish: once price trades "to it and through it" (the ORG low), the next target is the -0.5 SD of the 09:30-open-to-settlement range; "the body will be right on that level or just above it, and the wick might go just a tad bit below" (101). Bullish mirror: in a bull trend the gap can be explored below its low to -0.5 SD, then back to the ORG low and CE (100). [new; see conflict 43]
- DRAFT: **Back at the ORG CE after the projection = 50/50; done for the session** (101). [new]
- DRAFT: **Discount ORG near an ATH**: allow CE, then a full fill and use as a discount array if still bullish; but a candle that reaches the CE and closes below the lowest octant marks the ORG extreme, fade it (098). Premium ORG: a body that can't lay on the upper quadrant = continuation the other way (099). [refines B4 A1 octants]
- DRAFT: **Prior Friday's ORG CE as the Monday draw** (098), hit "perfectly". [refines B4 prior ORGs stay live]
- DRAFT: **Opening range = 30 minutes**, "not 15-minute ... not the 5-minute orb" (101). [refines B1]

### A2. All-time highs, contract choice and HTF anchors
- DRAFT: **Back-adjust toggle at ATHs.** On the continuous contract, toggle back-adjustment to find old highs that sit near current price; use the one in range (098, 099, 104). Cycle delivery vs continuous and "side with the one that's closest to market price" (105). [new; see conflict 41]
- DRAFT: **Premium/discount wicks.** A wick at the candle high = premium wick, below the body = discount wick; read a wick as a gap (CE and low only), "think of this as a inversion fair value gap"; short from the wick CE to the candle close when bodies can't reach the CE (098, 100, 102). [refines B4 longest wick]
- DRAFT: **Event horizon between two highs** (prior day high and the ATH draw) = partial and retrace level (099, 100). [refines B4]
- DRAFT: **Daily suspension block + bearish breaker** (high, low, higher high, fold below the low) as the anchor for where the big pool is (105). [refines B1/B3 suspension block]
- DRAFT: **SMT by distance from the old ATH**: trade the index still near its old high (NQ) when ES is far above its own (098); short the weakest ("sick sister") index for shallower retracements; a lagging index that later prints its own higher high resolves the decoupling (103, 105). [new; see conflict 47]

### A5. PD arrays: FVG selection, IFVG grade, OB, BOLO
- DRAFT: **Wick to the left of an FVG**: the wick's lower half (low to CE) is the refined first presentation; the FVG's far edge is the worst case (104). [new; see conflict 48]
- DRAFT: **Not every FVG becomes an IFVG**: the obvious one is "the only inefficiency right before this high is taken" (104). Old FVGs that worked "don't go stale"; extend them (098). Tiny "common gaps" carry little detail (098). [refines B4 A2]
- DRAFT: **AAA inversion FVG**: price can't touch the CE and bodies don't reach the edge; "That's the one" (099). [new grade]
- DRAFT: **OB / propulsion block mean threshold**: a wick may reach the OB's quadrant ("rare ... but it can happen"); bodies must stay beyond the mean threshold; a propulsion block's bodies may not pass the parent OB's mean threshold (099). Trading back through an up-close candle's open activates it as a bearish OB (104). [refines 2016-17/2023 OB; see conflict 49]
- DRAFT: **BOLO = defensive array**: rejection block + the adjacent wick's CE; "No wicks, no bodies" between them; "you're not actually trading in it ... You do not want to see it touched"; put the stop beyond it; if it trades there, the idea is wrong, no re-entry on the same idea (098, 099). [refines B4 A3 defensive arrays (096)]
- DRAFT: **Time distortion**: wicks do damage but bodies stay inside an array (or a consolidation sits inside a wick); go up a time frame or wait for price to leave it (100, 103). [new term]

### A6. Conditions filter and drills
- DRAFT: **High-resistance signature**: candles sharing the prior range, few imbalances, "barcode" bodies = expect a run through the consolidation for liquidity; no FVG after the bell = sit still or wait until 10:00 (101, 103). [refines B2/B3 HRLR]
- DRAFT: **"To it and through it" at relative equal highs**: a shallow peek and return = false breakout/reversal and no more longs that day (103). [new]
- DRAFT: **Half-the-stop-hunt projection**: target = old high + 0.5 x the stop-hunt leg; failure there = weakness into 13:30 (103). [new]
- DRAFT: **Judas swing measure**: on a bullish day, open + (open - early low) bounds the accumulation; don't chase longs above it (105). [new]
- DRAFT: **Drills (beginner, paper only)**: every FVG, 1 micro, 10-15 handle target = stop (1:1), 15s chart with 1m levels, log drawdown and MFE; "four market maker models per hour" on 15s (101, 103). [beginner; see conflict 45]

### A7. Management
- DRAFT: **Stop after the target**: "once it hits your targets, why rush back in?" (105). [refines B4]
- DRAFT: **Don't re-add near the exit**: re-add only after a retrace well beyond the partial (~20-30 NQ handles) (098); don't add above the average (099). [new; see conflict 36]
- DRAFT: **Re-entry after a one-candle stop raid** on the same narrative, at the next FVG (099), versus no re-entry once a BOLO is hit (098). [see conflict 36]
- DRAFT: **"My stop will rest here"** = fixed stop for the rest of the trade (099). [new]
- DRAFT: **Scale-out**: partial at the target FVG's low and high, at smooth minor pools, dump into the event horizon; partials at gap lows; nearer target in high volatility (099, 102, 104). [refines B4 A7]
- DRAFT: **Removing the TP** to let stacked limits run (098) is discretionary. [noted]
