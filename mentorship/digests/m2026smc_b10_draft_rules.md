## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. The year and the day
- DRAFT: **January is the "Asian session" of the year: no real trading until February** (first Tuesday, 138/140; second week, 141; streams resume Feb 10, 143). Holidays thin, avoid (143). [refines SEASON-ACTIVITY; conflict 106]
- DRAFT: **No-news day before CPI/PPI = holding pattern**; expect no big expansion (140). After the AM run, a protective low taken before the target = done for the morning, no PM either (139, 140). [new; conflict 119]
- DRAFT: **"Fuzzy" price (candles sharing prior ranges) = intervention, not algorithmic**: lighter hand (138). [repeats B9 HRLR]

### A2. Sessions, killzones and overnight liquidity
- DRAFT: **Session ≠ killzone**: Asia session 19:00-05:00, Asian KZ a 2-hour window (139 correction); London KZ 02:00-05:00 with 02:00-04:00 sweet spot and 04:00 the purest; London session to 10:00-11:00; 05:00-07:00 dead time (140). [refines; LONDON-MACRO B]
- DRAFT: **Search for smooth highs/lows over the whole session range, not only the killzone** (140). [new]
- DRAFT: **No smooth level = the single overnight high/low; odds rise when the level sits on a quadrant of the overnight range** (140). [new]
- DRAFT: **Overnight low 00:00-07:00 (REQ preferred) is the routine bearish draw** (143). [new; conflict 109]
- DRAFT: **PM-session reference range = 02:00-11:00 high/low and the smooth levels inside it** (140). [new; conflict 108]
- DRAFT: **REQ: anchor = the higher high / lower low on the left; the right one must be lower/higher (the trap).** If the anchor has a wick, grade it: price may only reach its lower quadrant/CE and turn; take a partial there (140). Non-REQ = deeper retrace first (143). [new; conflict 114]

### A3. Pre-market 07:00-09:30
- DRAFT: **07:00-09:00 runs to overnight smooth liquidity every day, holidays included** (140); "bread and butter" FPFVG-to-REQ setup (139, 140). Owner rule stands: pre-09:30 entries are test-only switches. [refines PREMARKET-ENTRY B]
- DRAFT: **Pre-session dealing range from the nearer reference (the lower of two REQ highs)**; jagged side vs smooth side; watch whether levels are used defensively or run (138). [refines B9 134]
- DRAFT: **A pre-market IFVG inside a holding pattern is not a fade**: wait for the 09:30-10:00 release (139, 140). [new; conflict 116]
- DRAFT: **8:30 and 9:30 are run times with or without news**; 08:30 A+ setup: sweep before news, fail to reach the lower eighth, reclaim the breaker low, IFVG on an eighth, run to minor buy side then the inefficiency low at 3/4 of the range (138). [refines]
- DRAFT: **Pre-market rhythm test**: if the pre-market read was clean, trade the open; if not, let 09:30-10:00 trade and wait for 10:00 (143). Stopped out pre-open = wait for 10:00 (141). [new; conflict 112]

### A4. First FVGs and how long they last
- DRAFT: **Pre-market FPFVG anchors at 07:00 and must displace through a short-term high/low**; tiny gaps without displacement are common gaps; engulfing ≠ MSS (139, 140). [refines FPFVG-DEF D]
- DRAFT: **FPFVG size filter: under 10 NQ handles is ~80% not the FPFVG; wait for the next** (140); skip a small first gap that displaced into an RTH inefficiency (138). [new; conflict 105]
- DRAFT: **An FVG before the minor low breaks is a common gap** (143). [refines FVG-VALID]
- DRAFT: **First FVG after a turtle soup (false break of a short-term high/low) is kept and projected forward up to 20 trading days** (142, 143). Unfilled FPFVGs are weekly magnets like NWOG/NDOG (138); a Jan 2 FPFVG still worked on Jan 12 (140). [new; conflict 107]
- DRAFT: **20/40/60-day lookback; last 20 trading days are the highest probability** (140; same in 2025 L46). Opening gaps kept at least 5 days, filled or not (140). [refines GAP-LIFE]

### A5. PD arrays
- DRAFT: **Two SIBIs joined by a VI = one PD array; CE = midpoint of the whole** (138; IFVG of 139 is such a zone). [new; conflict 117]
- DRAFT: **At a daily swing high, the inefficiency on the buy side of the curve (VI + wick high left of the high) beats the right-side FVG**; caveat: may not fill (142). [new; conflict 115]
- DRAFT: **Grade a daily wick that reaches beyond the candle's open; its quadrants/eighths are turns** (142; L46 measures open to extreme). [refines B9 131]
- DRAFT: **Time-distortion range (from the reacting PDA to the new low): the FVG at its equilibrium caps the retrace** (143). [new]
- DRAFT: **Daily suspension block = VI at both ends; not a liquidity void; in bias it should give no resistance** (141, 142, 143). [repeats B9]
- DRAFT: **Model ladder**: inefficiency to liquidity (beginner) > liquidity to inefficiency > liquidity to liquidity (false break high to false break low, "highest form") (138). [new]

### A6. Opening range gap
- DRAFT: **Copy the prior settlement, drop the 09:30 open, grade it; CE ~70% by 10:00; last quarter need not fill** (140). [repeats B9]
- DRAFT: **After a ~3/4 partial fill and a rip the other way, expect the gap to stay open; a close through it ends AM gap-fill targets** (138). [refines ORG-AFTER-CE]
- DRAFT: **Beginner target = half gap only; still valid after 10:00 when the daily array points there** (142). [refines; conflict 113]
- DRAFT: **Bank something when 3/4 of the ORG fills within minutes** (140). [new]

### A7. Entries
- DRAFT: **Enter at the FVG's near edge/upper quadrant, not a deep CE limit** (139) vs **short entries above the CE of a bearish FVG** (142). [conflict 104]
- DRAFT: **Wicks through the entry gap are fine if bodies stay inside ("mohawk")**; the opening-range FPFVG must not resist in bias; add on a return to its edge (142). [repeats]
- DRAFT: **Core tenet**: HTF draw (old high/low, REQ, imbalance), recent stop hunt, LTF FPFVG at the taught times that displaces, candle-1 stop, partials preset (139). [repeats]
- DRAFT: **10:50-11:10 macro displacement into the draw; 09:50-10:10 retest of the FPFVG low** (143). [repeats]

### A8. Stops, exits, holding
- DRAFT: **Candle-1 stop for FVG entries** (139). [repeats STOP-PLACE C]
- DRAFT: **Never let a stop go stale: after each new swing/equity high move the stop a bit closer (~3+ handles), more liberal with partials** (142) vs **don't jam to breakeven** (139). [conflict 118]
- DRAFT: **Close above a minor high before rolling; no stop roll in the last minute before 09:30** (141). [new]
- DRAFT: **Hold-for-the-close bias, but offload part 10:00-11:00 (London close) on 07:00-10:00 longs** (139, 140). [new; conflict 110]
- DRAFT: **Partial when target anxiety hits, then roll the stop** (142); two-stage beginner exit (138). [repeats]
- DRAFT: **No overnight holds now (gap risk)** (139). [repeats OVERNIGHT-HOLD A]

### A9. News
- DRAFT: **CPI/PPI 08:30: no position before the release; look again from 09:00; no FOMC afternoon** (139). [refines; conflict 111]
