## 2. What ICT says about trading before 09:30

**A. Default for indices.**
- "No 08:30 news, wait for 09:30" (soft-bias day): 007 ~11:14 "there really wasn't any news driver today so you have to wait until 9 30" (cap); 019 ~7:57 (cap).
- The NY AM session for indices "starts at 8 30 and goes to 11" (065 ~4:16:00 (cap), said to new students). Also 001 ~48:20 "between 8 30 and 11" (cap) and 073 ~14:24 "8 30 in the morning to noon" (cap).
- cc10_011 / cc10_010 put the index "true day" at 09:30-16:00.
- His own futures kill zone was "8 20 in the morning to 11 o'clock" (cc10_007 ~4:35, cap).

**B. 07:00-09:00 / 07:00-08:30.**
- 022 ~2:14:00 (cap): "There's nothing saying you can't trade 7:00 to 9:00 in the morning" if the framework offers a setup. But expect 09:30 to be messy afterwards, and if the draw was delivered before the open, trade the PM.
- 032 ~5:46-7:05 (cap): when he expects a hard-to-read or range-bound day (news later in the day or week, e.g. FOMC), he will "adopt the seven o'clock to 8 30 time window". He gets in before 08:30 and uses "the 8 30 hour to roll right into my targets", then is done for the day.
- 099 ~53:30 (cap): a Silver Bullet "if you really really want it you can do seven o'clock in the morning to 8 30".
- 065A ~55:55 (cap): SB in the 07:00-09:00 NY kill zone. He says "the really good ones" are 07:00-09:00 and 03:00-05:00 **for Forex**. Index SB windows stay 10-11 and 14-15.
- 073 ~14:10 (cap): he keeps the 07:00-09:00 kill zone in mind for FX, commodities and index futures alike.

**C. London 02:00-05:00 and the London Silver Bullet 03:00-04:00.**
- London kill zone 02:00-05:00 (he personally uses 01:00-05:00) (065A ~1:51:40; cc08_001; cc08_002). FX-framed.
- London SB 03:00-04:00, "first setup time is 3:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m." (054 ~8:51, cap), shown on ES; and NQ (092 ~20:00, cap: "every single day between three o'clock and four o'clock").
- If London gives no setup, wait for AM, then PM (054 ~18:18).
- The exit may run past the window, into 05:00 or the 07:00-08:00 NY open (054 ~11:27).
- For ES the London 02:00-05:00 high/low is mainly a **liquidity pool** for the AM (067; times.md Conflict 8). 018 used a 03:00 FVG as the 09:30 draw.

**D. 08:30 news.**
- No position into or at CPI: "don't stand ahead or get in front of the CPI number" (064 ~4:30, cap). Stops do not protect because liquidity is pulled. New traders should "not trade before the CPI number" (030 ~7:22, cap). He would not trade ahead of CPI (092 ~13:54).
- After high/medium-impact news "wait 30 minutes minimum", then trade only if there is ~10 handles of room (065 ~5:46, cap). "You can trade after non-farm payroll about 30 minutes after" (065 ~4:12:54, cap). 006 ~8:23 (cap): "a way to trade the market after that first 30 minutes", but "not something that I would advise".
- First liquidity run on the release is the losing side; bodies retrace to the 08:30 opening price (064 ~52:12, ~55:00). The NFP profile: the first side taken after 08:30 is usually false (073; 078).
- Day-trade stop below the 08:30 opening price / below the CE of the 08:30 1m wick (064 ~1:38:40, cap).
- 08:30 is the "trigger time" (013 ~4:52). Three opens: midnight, 08:30, 09:30 (010 ~38:31).
- Two releases in one session (08:30 + 10:00): stand aside; no early entry before or at 09:30 (100 ~1:26:20, cap: "sometimes you'll see me enter before or right at 9:30").

## 4. What coding each pre-open model on ES 1m (Databento, all hours) would need

Shared plumbing (all models):
- `strategy.run_day` only scans from 09:30. `Params.am = (570, 660)`.
- A pre-open model needs its own session key (e.g. `"pre"` / `"london"`) with its own `done[...]` flag, so it does not consume the AM trade. It also needs its own `blocked()` rules and a `flat_at` minute.
- `sb_model.py` already has `sb_london` (window 180-240, leg from 120, off by default). It is the template.
- `levels.DayCtx` already builds the London 02:00-05:00 pool and the overnight H/L. The Asian, NDOG and 07:00-08:30 pools would be added the same way.
- Execution as futures (no SPY): use ES/NQ 1m from `ES_1min_db.parquet` / `NQ_1min_db.parquet`, MES/MNQ `lot` sizing, 1-tick fills.
- Overnight books are thin. Add a slippage parameter (+1 tick per side pre-09:30) and treat limit fills as filled only on a trade-through. 092 and 096 used 30s/15s FVGs, which 1m bars cannot reproduce.
- News: `calendar.py` has CPI, jobs, PPI and FOMC for 2019-2025 only.
  - Weekly jobless claims can be derived: every Thursday, Wednesday in holiday weeks.
  - Retail sales, PCE, GDP etc. before 2026 are missing. 032 (retail sales) and 100 (PCE) need them.
  - A `pre_news_flat` switch (flatten at 08:29) vs `hold_through_0830` covers contradiction 2.
