## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. RTH opening range gaps as the main framework (the batch's main theme)
- DRAFT: **The last three ORGs are enough.** "You only need three of these. And you have all the information you'll ever need to do intraday trading ... without a hard line higher time frame bias" (096). Friday's ORG = Thursday 16:14 to Friday 09:30 (096). Prior days' ORGs stay live and are graded in quadrants/octants: Tuesday's ORG high hit to the tick; turns at Friday's highest octant and CE (096); Monday's ORG high/CE/low as defense lines and targets (095, 097); Friday's ORG octant and upper quadrant on the next Tuesday/Wednesday (091). [refines B1 ORG ledger; core, backtestable]
- DRAFT: **ORG standard-deviation projection x time.** Fib from the 09:30 open to the prior close; on trend days look for 6.5/7/7.5/8 SD (8 SD = the daily high inside the first lunch hour), on consolidation days 0.25/0.5; a level already exceeded before the time window opens is ruled out: "go up the vertical axis ... once I get up to the time, then ... move over horizontally" (088). [new; conflict check: B1 ORG, 2023 SD projections]
- DRAFT: **ORG 75% entry.** Short from "3/4 of the opening range gap" toward the 09:30 opening price and the NDOG (092). [refines B1 ORG grading]
- DRAFT: **Extreme gap = gap and go.** "Chances are it's going to ... trade up to about a quarter which would be the lower quadrant of the gap ... then ... go in the direction of the gap" (091). [new; conflict check: B1 ORG CE by 10:00 70%]
- DRAFT: **Overcome discount ORG in a bull market** = the first trade back into it is "a discount array ... like a trampoline"; fading the fill against the HTF is not for beginners (088). [new]
- DRAFT: **One body beyond an ORG edge** (today's low or yesterday's) = "a catalyst ... to anticipate lower prices" later in the day (096, 097); weaker on Fed days (097). [new]

### A2. FVG utilization, inversion and validated levels
- DRAFT: **Carry forward only FVGs that proved their first utilization** (097); a gap that acted in both roles (original, then inverted, then original) is "a really good level to be treated in the future ... keep that projected" (096). [refines B3 A2 first utilization]
- DRAFT: **IFVG confirmation, three readings:** the next candle refuses the prior candle's high (088); "It's got to leave a body outside of it" and come back (097); SIBIs that "failed in their initial role of first utilization" become inversions (089). A failed inversion returns to its first utilization (097). [see conflict 32]
- DRAFT: **Premium/discount sensitivity** named: bearish = bodies only in the lower half, wicks may enter the upper half; bullish mirror (097, 089, 091). After leaving a reclaimed bullish gap, "the lower half should not see any candlestick bodies" (089). Mohawks permissible (089, 091, 092, 097); leaving part of the gap open = possible breakaway gap (097). [refines B3 A1/A2]
- DRAFT: **Measuring gap** left open on the way, without requiring the measured move (092). [new]

### A3. Wicks, the gray pool and defensive arrays
- DRAFT: **ICT gray pool.** The zone between the CEs of two adjacent wicks; "split that in half"; "No bodies are allowed to be in there" (092). Resolves the unclear "gray pole" in batch 1 (038). [new]
- DRAFT: **Longest wick in a cluster** is the one to measure "as a gap" (091); a wick beside a small SIBI with a break to the left: "the wick is what you want to use" (093). Stops under wick CEs (089). [refines B3 A1]
- DRAFT: **Defensive arrays.** "Bolo's not part of the 81. 81 are entry models ... balanced price ranges, their utilization are defensive"; put the stop behind them; when they fail, "something on the other direction is underway ... take the next PD array" (096). [new; conflict check: 2023 BPR]
- DRAFT: **Three PD arrays supporting a run; stop below the lowest** (rejection block) (091). Footholds/handholds analogy (091, 097). [refines B3 footholds]

### A4. Order blocks
- DRAFT: **OB candle-selection "book definition."** In a series of same-direction candles, use the open of the last candle if it is the largest or the smallest of the series; if they are uniform, use the first (lowest) candle; with two equal bodies, the earlier one (097). Earlier: the larger, "more protracted" candle's open = the CISD (091). [see conflict 31]
- DRAFT: **Short inside the last suspension block** (a bullish up-close candle) after a failed ATH run; collapse on a body outside it (095). [refines B1/B3 suspension block]

### A5. Draws, targets and daily context
- DRAFT: **Previous day's high/low framework.** "A limitless supply of opportunities. You don't need anything else" (093). [new; backtestable]
- DRAFT: **08:30 news candle extreme as the draw.** When 08:30 news builds a narrative the other way, fade it and target the 08:30 candle's high/low (089). Micro-only news spike gets redelivered and swept (088). [new]
- DRAFT: **Bodies are the real pool.** "See all those bodies? That's the real liquidity pool. It's not so much the wicks"; target below the bodies (096, 097). [see conflict 35]
- DRAFT: **Minor vs primary pools.** Minor = nearer smooth pool before the real target; first partial there (088, 089, 092). ETH/RTH confluence of the same pool raises its odds (091). Low-hanging fruit in volatile 2026 conditions (091, 088). [refines B3 nearest draw first]
- DRAFT: **Failed ATH run.** A run that stops short of the ATH with untaken sell-side below = short to that sell-side (095). Shorts at the PDH at ATH: three drives above the first hour's high (09:30-10:30), FVG just before the high read as bearish above the PDH (093). [new; refines B3 climax reversal]
- DRAFT: **Thursday intermediate high** via weekly profiles; can be taken from Thursday lunch into the PM (093). [see conflict 39]
- DRAFT: **Up-close streak.** After 11 straight up-close days, allow for a retracement; daily wick CE (Oct 30 2025) as the magnet (096). [new]
- DRAFT: **Engineered liquidity.** Retail-looking support wicks and relative equal highs left by bodies that can't re-enter an IFVG are "engineering ... liquidity" (093, 096). [refines B2]

### A6. Time windows and day filters
- DRAFT: **Time first, then price.** Vertical lines at 09:30, 10:00, 11:30, 13:30; New York local time only (088). [refines B1 A6]
- DRAFT: **Lunch (one-sided morning).** 11:30-13:30 rolls against the morning; setup "optimally forms in the first hour" (11:30-12:30); target the first post-10:00 low reached going left from 11:30 when against the HTF; the deeper low is the best case (088). [see conflict 33]
- DRAFT: **PM opening range 13:30-14:00**: its PD arrays govern the PM session, the last hour and the 18:00 restart (088). [new]
- DRAFT: **FOMC day.** "Trading by 10:30 or sooner. And then let the rest of the day cook"; no bias; beginners don't trade; FOMC mornings are skittish every time (091). **Fed speech** = low probability; 09:45-10:15 chaos window (096, 097). [see conflict 37]
- DRAFT: **07:00 pre-session push = lethargic morning**; wait for lunch/PM, or skip a Friday (093). London clean highs + 07:00 high run = buy-side taken for a short day (096). [see conflict 40]
- DRAFT: **Slop after targets = stop**; next opportunity is the 07:00-09:00 pre-market (088). [refines B3 two-sided = no trade]

### A7. Management
- DRAFT: **Scale-out ladder.** Partial at the minor pool, partial each new high/low ("running down equity", 089, 093), partial at the event horizon with the limit a little short for spread (089, 092), exit 1-2 ticks beyond the target pool (088). [refines B1/B3]
- DRAFT: **Speed and time tells.** Second leg should take fewer candles than the first (089); after a long consolidation, move within 3 candles (089); a 2-3 candle time stop when price lingers (092); overlapping candles near the target (093) or many candles inside a SIBI (095) = scale out; "stuttering" slightly lower lows defending a level = expect a stop (095). [new; backtestable]
- DRAFT: **Limit touched but not filled near the target = take it at market** (095). [new; also a fill-model question]
- DRAFT: **Stops by conditions.** HRLR: give room; LRLR: trail aggressively (089); high volatility: don't tighten (092); deep retracements before the run are "the flavor of the month", keep the stop high (093). [refines B2 HRLR/LRLR]
- DRAFT: **Re-entry on the same narrative** after a stop, unless "too far up on the curve" (089); beginners: no second trade after a stop. [see conflict 36]
- DRAFT: **No holds into lunch** (089) or over the weekend (093). [refines B2/B3]
