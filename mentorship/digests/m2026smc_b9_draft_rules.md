## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. Market regime: HRLR, ranges, the year
- DRAFT: **HRLR = each candle inside the prior range ("barcode", "picket fence"); LRLR = small consolidation, a gap, a retrace into it, a new extreme** (128 on 15m; 133 on 1m). Skip HRLR; scale-ins on a wick poke only in LRLR (128). [refines B4/B5; conflict 96]
- DRAFT: **Range-bound daily: runs beyond the prior day's high/low are turtle soup back in** (125); **target the FVG just inside the old extreme, not the extreme** (132). Leaving the range = shift to LRLR. [new; conflict 91]
- DRAFT: **Yearly calendar**: Jan-Feb measure risk / dabble from early Feb; cleaner late March-May; summer HRLR; LRLR late Aug/Sep to mid Dec; holidays 50/50 (128, 131, 132, 135). [new; conflict 98]
- DRAFT: **Low participation mutes red-folder news** (PPI Friday, 128). [new]
- DRAFT: **Both sides plausible on the daily = no hard bias; lower TFs won't help** (132); after time away, watch only (131). [repeats B7]
- DRAFT: **Uncertain day**: no pre-market trades; FPFVG, else the 10:00 hour, else nothing until PM (125) / wait for the first hour and trade its graded range (136). [new; conflict 93]

### A2. Daily context
- DRAFT: **Inside day by bodies**: body inside the prior body (129) or inside the prior range (131); compression releases Tue/Wed. [refines B8; conflict 79]
- DRAFT: **Discount/premium wick CE = continuation switch** on a closing basis; fail = stay HRLR (131). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Daily suspension block CE = bull/bear fulcrum**; bodies in its upper half = bullish IFVG, wick only = bearish (135, 136). [new]
- DRAFT: **Daily VI is "neutralized" for entries/targets once a later body spans it**; stop management only (128). [new]
- DRAFT: **Settlement toggle: pick the setting with the most exaggerated/uniform VIs** (127, 133, 136). [repeats B8]
- DRAFT: **Breakaway / measuring gap**: FVG after a balanced range = breakaway; next = measuring gap, project from its midpoint (127); breakaway confirmed if it stays open and the low goes (132). [new]
- DRAFT: **Balanced price range: the wick takes precedence over candles/OBs** (127); **wick immediately left of a SIBI replaces it with its gradient levels; one candle between them keeps the SIBI** (129). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Event horizon between two static pools**: fib two old daily lows; a close beyond the midpoint = sustained run (129). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Spring draw-down tendency** (127). [conflict 82]

### A3. Contracts and SMT

- DRAFT: **SMT on line charts of the highs (bearish) or lows (bullish)** (127, 135); **qualifier, not a timing tool** (135); **use it only at liquidity events: 09:30-10:00, news, a raid** (131); short the weakest index (127). [refines; conflict 94]

### A4. Carried levels: the notepad
- DRAFT: **A week of RTH ORG highs/lows + NDOG CE + NWOG gradients is the notepad** (131); last week's same-day ORG reused (133, 134 "like new week opening gaps"). [refines B4/B8]
- DRAFT: **No man's land: a PDA counts only when it sits on one of these gradient levels at the right time** (128, 131, 133, 136). [refines B7; conflict 102]
- DRAFT: **ORG anchors**: 16:14 settle to 09:30; label settle = ORG high on a discount open, low on a premium open; a 17:00-settle variant exists (128). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Big figures (100-point NQ) get swept by ~2 ticks** (132). [new]

### A5. Pre-market and the opening range
- DRAFT: **07:00-09:30 session bias**: mark the smoothest overnight/London REQ high and low; the first raided is a false break, the other is the draw (134). [new; conflict 73]
- DRAFT: **Opening range = 30 minutes; it runs nearby pools first** (128, 131, 133). **FPFVG 09:31-10:00 with displacement** (131, 133); **not a long while REQ lows against it remain in the first 30 minutes** (128). [refines; conflicts 80, 97]
- DRAFT: **ORG CE ~70% by 10:00** (128, 133); "too clean" high one tick above CE (128). **Half-gap model wants a >=100-handle gap** (133). [repeats; conflicts 84, 85]
- DRAFT: **First hour 09:30-10:30 graded on no-bias days; bias half sensitive** (136). [new; conflict 93]

### A6. Times: macros, lunch, last hour
- DRAFT: **Macros = last 10 + first 10 minutes of the hour** (125). [repeats B8; conflict 78]
- DRAFT: **Last hour: 14:50-15:10, 15:15-15:45 (fallback 15:30), then MOC** (125). [refines; conflict 90]
- DRAFT: **Lunch 11:30-13:30; 11:30-12:00 = lunch opening range; bullish AM -> first low after 10:00 (consolidation day) or the most prominent low (trending day); 13:30-14:00 = PM OR** (128). [refines B7/B8; conflict 87]
- DRAFT: **Every hour's range carries the same setups** (134 book chapter). [new]

### A7. News
- DRAFT: **PPI/CPI: no position; target the liquidity left in the wake (FOMC-like)** (129). **NFP on any weekday: wait for 09:30, explore, else PM** (133). [refines B8; conflict 76]
- DRAFT: **Don't add bias trades in the low half of the 5-day PD matrix before a red-folder event** (129). [new]

### A8. Entries
- DRAFT: **Propulsion block**: second down-close candle inside the first above its 50%; active once its open is breached (no close needed) (125). [new]
- DRAFT: **Reclaimed FVG** (up-close BISI traded through and back above) vs inversion (down-close SIBI) (128). [new]
- DRAFT: **Rejection block / sharp reversal: market order when price trades back through the up-close candle's open; stop mid-NWOG** (131). [new; conflict 101]
- DRAFT: **Sub-1m: enter in the far half of a 1m gap; 1m parent, 15s/5s only after 1m displacement** (129, 133). [new]
- DRAFT: **Tiny "nub" bodies outside a gap don't invalidate it** (125). [new; conflict 92]
- DRAFT: **Bearish gap weakness: bodies miss CE, wicks to 0.625 not 0.75** (131); wick CE rejection = immediate rebalance (133, 134). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Nested PD arrays: the upper one should stay open** (127); first inefficiency above a consolidation = first consideration (133). [new]
- DRAFT: **Eighths and sixteenths on every grid** (135). [refines]

### A9. Stops, targets, size
- DRAFT: **Stop**: 1 tick beyond FVG candle 1, re-enter with the same stop (134); in high volatility use the 1m setup swing, not sub-minute (129, 136). [conflict 99]
- DRAFT: **Trail**: after each short-term high taken (126); one contract = trail after displacement through a key low (129). [conflict 77]
- DRAFT: **Targets**: 15-handle minimum (129) vs 15-30 handle cookie cutter without a pool (131, 134); exit one tick before the pool (134); implied dealing range with two-stage offset partials (133). [conflict 100]
- DRAFT: **Size**: 0.25-0.5% risk (max 1%) for two years; +1 micro per $2,500 gained, -1 per $2,500 lost (134). [new; conflict 103; owner reporting stays $1k risk]
