## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. Pre-market dealing range 07:00-09:00 (08:30 on news days)
- DRAFT: **Treat 07:00-09:00 like an Asian range / CBDR**: highest high, lowest low, SD projections; "When we're in consolidation makes the purest, the most reliable" (001). On 08:30 news days the range ends at 08:30 (004). [new; see conflict 73]
- DRAFT: **Relative equal highs/lows inside it are draws**: "the algorithm will absolutely refer back to that" (012, 016). Sweep one end, target the other = bread and butter (016). [new]
- DRAFT: **Phase predicts the AM**: pre-market (or overnight) trend = AM consolidation; consolidation = expansion after 09:30 (016, 020). "Not a panacea." [new; conflict 73]
- DRAFT: **Pre-market grades carried into the day**: stop the lines at 09:00, look for agreement with the 09:30-10:00 opening-range grades (012); pre-market octants and its suspension block stop bodies later (013). [new]

### A2. RTH opening range gap (ORG)
- DRAFT: **ORG CE ~70% by 10:00** (016); if not, "deferred delivery"; still open at 10:30 = likely stays open for a while (020). [repeats B7; see conflict 85]
- DRAFT: **Gap-and-go weakness test**: discount ORG whose first rally high misses the lower quadrant and whose bodies close below the lowest octant = go for the far pool (008). [new; see conflict 84]
- DRAFT: **ORG SD -1 often marks the AM high/low** (012); cycle -1, -1.5, -2, -2.5 until one meets a daily pool or event horizon (004, 008). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Large ORG (~400 handles)**: try the gap small, favour trades toward it; first stop-out is acceptable (020). [new; conflict 84]
- DRAFT: **Nested ORGs: use the widest**; a "shadow" (overlap of two gaps) validates an order block inside it (019). [new; conflict 83]
- DRAFT: **09:30 opening price is a magnet**; failing to reach it = weakness (007, 008, 013). [refines B7]
- DRAFT: **Prior day's RTH relative highs/lows from the last 15-20 min (MOC macro)** are referred to again; view on the RTH chart (012). ETH prints count only once confirmed in RTH (020). [new]

### A3. Carried-forward matrices (separate layouts)
- DRAFT: **Monthly macro keys**: last three months' OHLC + last week's high/low; trade premium/discount relative to them (019). [refines B1]
- DRAFT: **NWOG matrix: last five** (Fri 16:59 to Sun 18:00; Thursday if Friday is a holiday) (019). **NDOG (Mon 18:00) and Friday NDOG: keep two weeks; Mon/Fri ORGs of the last two weeks too** (001). Lows formed inside a prior NDOG are targets (015, 017). [refines B4; see conflict 89]
- DRAFT: **FPFVG matrix**: Monday and Friday FPFVGs of the last two weeks "many times create significant intraday or daily highs ... a monthly high ... a weekly high" (015); prior days' FPFVGs used as entry/target ladders (013, 014, 017, 018). [new]

### A4. FPFVG family and the reflection gap
- DRAFT: **Three arrays**: FPFVG (first FVG after 09:30), FPFVG with displacement (first that takes out a high/low; "take greater command"), and **first presented reflection FVG** = the first opposite-direction FVG after a bullish FPFVG (and mirror); stronger when formed in a macro (012, 013). [new; see conflict 80]
- DRAFT: **Wick left of a gap takes precedence** as the target; its CE caps the move (002, 008). [repeats B7]

### A5. Daily context
- DRAFT: **Daily swing at a key level**: low, lower low, higher low; candle 4 or 5 must be bullish or the read is wrong (001). [new]
- DRAFT: **Daily double wick CE**: grade both wicks of the turning-point candle; the midpoint of the two CEs is a level (001). [new]
- DRAFT: **Open-air daily gap (no bodies inside) = fast travel**; 1m respects its octants on the way; weak = no return to its CE (019). [refines B6 A2]
- DRAFT: **Back-to-back inside days = range expansion incoming** (011). [new; conflict 79]
- DRAFT: **Settlement-price toggle: choose the setting with the widest VI / longest wick**; if no difference, track both and keep the one bodies respect (001). [refines B6 A3]
- DRAFT: **Seasonals**: September bearish, low in Sep/Oct (rarely Nov), rally into the first week of February; midterm/election weeks a modest bullish tilt (001, 019, 020). [new; conflict 82]

### A6. Weekly profile: TGIF
- DRAFT: **One-directional week retraces 20-30%** (max 40%) from Thu 13:00 into Friday (015); **if Friday doesn't deliver, Monday does** (Tuesday after a Monday holiday) (002). Monday Oct 19 topped at exactly 0.35 (002). [refines B5; see conflict 75]

### A7. Times: macros and lunch
- DRAFT: **Macros = last 10 min of an hour + first 10 of the next** (:50-:10, incl. 06:50-07:10); "17 minutes after the hour ... They're not macros." If no spooling in the first half, the second half spools; give up the extreme entry (008, 019). [refines B7; see conflict 78]
- DRAFT: **Lunch macro latest start 11:30; target the first 10:00-hour high (low) looking left from 11:30** (015); don't short into the 10:00-hour low before it's drawn (012). [refines B7; conflict 87]
- DRAFT: **10:30 = one-hour dealing range in place; algorithms speed up** (020). [new]
- DRAFT: **Asia: slow; take only an easy nearby pool** (006, 014 sync trades). [conflict 86]

### A8. News, holidays and NFP week
- DRAFT: **Never position before NFP/CPI/PPI; wait 15-20 min (NFP Friday before a long weekend: the first hour), then fade the first leg back into the news range at a convergence level** (004, 017, 018). "Two-stage delivery"; FOMC likewise; **FOMC sweet-spot entry 14:40-14:50** (011). [refines B7; see conflict 76]
- DRAFT: **NFP Thursday/Friday: ICT "abstains"** (015) yet trades NFP Friday live (004, 017, 018) after the first leg. **New students: done by Wednesday 11:00 of NFP week** (017-019); Wednesday morning lock-out (005); engage NFP-week Monday (008). [see conflicts 76, 81]
- DRAFT: **Day after a holiday-shortened session: one contract (or paper), low-hanging-fruit exit** (013, 014). [refines B7]

### A9. Entries and order flow
- DRAFT: **IFVG validation**: body close beyond the gap and the nearest octant, then no bodies back in the upper (lower) half (018); pre-validation retraces go to logical levels (quadrant) (018). [refines B3/B5]
- DRAFT: **After a high is run, the inefficiency formed before it becomes the IFVG**; no VI between those bodies = use the wick (011). Down-close SIBIs in the down-leg become an IFVG ladder after a suspected low (001). [refines]
- DRAFT: **First candle wicks through a carried array and closes back = one of the strongest reversal patterns**; enter at that wick's CE (013). [new]
- DRAFT: **Body-based liquidity run**: an old high counts as run once bodies close well beyond its candle close (015); flush body lows are engineered and get broken (018). [new; conflict 88]
- DRAFT: **Convergence required**: "Isolated levels are useless" (004); daily-gradient = ORG-gradient level is the target (020); daily highs/lows form at liquidity + SD agreement or a key inefficiency (008). [new]
- DRAFT: **Small ranges below a level, large ranges once above it** (005); stop entry above the wick high = market entry (005). [new]
- DRAFT: **Backtest convention**: entry at FVG CE or CE -1 tick; stop = the last candle that opened outside the gap +1 tick; target 1 tick before the pool; record drawdown and time (012). [new]
- DRAFT: **Market maker sell/buy model, second-stage redistribution/reaccumulation** = ICT's preferred leg (017, 018). [repeats older model]

### A10. Management
- DRAFT: **Exits**: counter-bias trade = one full exit at the pool +1 tick (001); "double tap" half at the chest, rest for the head (006); news trades get an extreme final limit plus partials (004); pay yourself at each new low (014). [see conflict 74]
- DRAFT: **Stops**: "steak dinner" stop (small locked profit) after the first target (001, 006); trail under three defensive arrays (006); MM model stop anchored at the first-stage distribution, not trailed tight (018); don't give back more than ~half the open profit (017); range-redelivery trail (020). [see conflict 77]
