## Part A. Rules by topic (DRAFT)

### A1. RTH opening range gap: first 30 minutes, prior ORGs
- DRAFT: **ORG CE by 10:00 is the SOP first draw**: "That's a SOP ... I'm looking for that every single day until proven it's not going to do it ... Whether it hits that level or not is irrelevant" (113); 70% "between 9:31 and 10:00" (115); watch "until we get to 10:00" (114). [repeats B1; conflict 50 leans to the 30-minute side]
- DRAFT: **Low-hanging fruit = 09:30 open / first-minute extreme**: "that's where engineered liquidity is ... regular trading hours has to book that price" (113). After a premium ORG, a body back below the 09:30 open = "all but guaranteed as a run on sell side," acceleration below the CE = full gap closure (114). [new; see conflict 54]
- DRAFT: **Failure to reach the ORG midpoint = order flow has turned** (113). [refines B5 A1]
- DRAFT: **Last five RTH ORGs stay live** (124); a prior ORG that price traded above becomes a discount array down to its CE, bodies on its octants (124); yesterday's ORG alone can be enough (123). [refines B4/B6; conflict 69]
- DRAFT: **Small ORG = harder, two-sided morning** (124). [new; conflict 64]
- DRAFT: **FPFVG = first displacement out of the opening price in the bias direction** (124); the 09:31 FPFVG by time still used (116); FPFVG upper half = discount sensitivity, a body in the lower half flips it to IFVG (120). [refines B5; conflict 70]

### A2. First Hour's Dealing Range (FHDR, lecture 115)
- DRAFT: **FHDR = 09:30-10:30 high/low**, not the 30-minute opening range; EQ and octants; project one range beyond the broken side (50% on the extreme) and watch bodies on its quadrants (115). [new; refines B5 first-hour SD (101)]
- DRAFT: **Close beyond a wick CE inside the FHDR sets the PM direction** (115). [new]
- DRAFT: **Shallow break of the FHDR extreme after a one-sided morning = turtle soup back into the range**; trailed stops sit above the post-10:00 high / FHDR EQ (115, 116). [new]
- DRAFT: **Trending day: only the first FVG after the FHDR break** (115). [new; conflict 60]
- DRAFT: **Lunch**: lunch is 11:30-13:30 (113, 123) but the lunch macro can start 10:30-11:30 (115, 116); its target = the first short-term high (low) formed after 10:00 (115, 116). PM opening range = 13:30-14:00 (123). [refines B6; conflict 59]

### A3. Wicks, VIs and suspension blocks
- DRAFT: **A wick next to an FVG/VI is the more salient array**; grade the more extreme wick; stops must allow for its upper half (113, 115, 117, 121). [new; conflict 56]
- DRAFT: **A wick is a range like a gap**: CE, quadrants; bodies not reaching its CE = barrier/distribution ("not a bull flag") (113, 114, 116). A wick above price is a premium array even if it is a "discount wick" (121). [refines B5/B6]
- DRAFT: **Close beyond a wick CE = continuation / green light**, gap left open (115, 119, 121). [new as a named rule]
- DRAFT: **VIs are the most-used array; read flexibly**; annotate the series (continuous or delivery) that shows the VI (117, 120). [refines B6 A3]
- DRAFT: **Suspension blocks on 1m too** (10:13 candle, 119); DXY daily block as the line in the sand (114, 118). [refines B6 A2]

### A4. PD-array rules new in this batch
- DRAFT: **Immediate rebalance (IR)**: a bar trades straight back to the prior bar's high/low with no gap = "one of the strongest" arrays; delivers that day or the next; day-2 failure through it = complex reversal (121, 122). On DXY it sets every XXX/USD pair (121). [new; conflict 67 not scored]
- DRAFT: **Lunch reversal gap**: if NY lunch runs a high/low and reverses, the FVG formed right before the sweep is carried into the next day; first trade into it = the fade, "many times the high or low of the day" (123). [new]
- DRAFT: **OB must sit on an ORG gradient level**; a propulsion block inherits from its parent OB; a bullish OB that fed a run into buy-side under a bearish bias becomes a mitigation block once its FVG inverts (122). [new; conflict 68]
- DRAFT: **Body-based swings**: swing highs/lows from bodies; the gap before the run through the body high is a sensitive IFVG; three-body swing then displacement = "institutional order flow entry drill" (114, 121). [new; conflict 58]
- DRAFT: **Gap-fill gate**: "If it wants the sell side ... this SIBI will remain unfilled ... If it doesn't take sell side ... This will completely fill in" (124). [new; conflict 72 not scored]
- DRAFT: **Event horizon** = midpoint between two pools: entry, add or partial level (119, 122, 123). [refines B6]
- DRAFT: **Range reset**: once a low of the larger run breaks, measure from the break; a return to its EQ is a short-term premium (119). [new; conflict 62]

### A5. Reading the tape
- DRAFT: **Time distortion: go up a timeframe** (1m -> 5m -> 15m -> 1h/4h) to see the array a chop is reaching for; bodies not touching that array's far edge = the signature (113, 118). [new; conflict 65]
- DRAFT: **Too many candles inside an FVG = invalid**; a valid gap is left after "one, maybe two at most" (116). [new; conflict 60]
- DRAFT: **A broken low must accelerate**; a small break and return = weakness; the gap left just above it should stay open (breakaway) (116). [refines B6]
- DRAFT: **IFVG validation**: body close through, a wick to the CE with the body back, then the first FVG after leaving again (119); a failed IFVG = no trade (119). [refines B6; IFVG-CONFIRM]
- DRAFT: **Two-sided read = low probability** (118, 120, 124). [new; conflict 64]
- DRAFT: **Must-move-now** ("this candle or the next") (113, 114, 120). [repeats B6]
- DRAFT: **Gap-fill and "Phil"**: off-level spikes (not on a quadrant/octant) = manipulation, fade (113, 115). [new; hard to define]

### A6. Conditions filter
- DRAFT: **Friday one-sided at the RTH extreme: no continuation entries**; opened at the RTH high without taking it = reluctance (116). [refines TGIF; conflict 62]
- DRAFT: **Rollover week: light size, carry the old contract's levels; continuous contract for HTF levels** (118, 120). [refines; CONTRACT card]
- DRAFT: **The day before FOMC/CPI: no trades**; CPI: fade the first leg after ~10:00 only when range-bound (113, 119, 120, 123). [refines B6; conflict 71]
- DRAFT: **After time away (vacation/illness): watch only for a couple of days** (114, 120). [new; operator]
- DRAFT: **Holiday weeks (Passion Week) skipped** (117); spring = cleaner ranges, more loosening after triple witching (122, 123). [new; calendar]
- DRAFT: **SMT (ES vs NQ) = distribution filter, not the trigger** (118). [refines; conflict 51]

### A7. Entries and management
- DRAFT: **Short after the FPFVG high holds + MSS, enter in the first array between two points** (pseudo 2022 model) (121); short above a wick CE after the close below it (122); IFVG/VI entry on the next candle (113). [refines]
- DRAFT: **Stops**: beyond the wick (113); above the OB at the IFVG base, don't strangle (121); "one tick above" the proving level (116); a scratch is not a loss (124). [conflict 53]
- DRAFT: **Three PD arrays lost = reduce or close** (113, 116). [new; conflict 57]
- DRAFT: **Three-candle trailing stop / 15s strangle** in conditions where the target may fail (115, 116). [new; conflict 53]
- DRAFT: **Exit at 75% of the FHDR projection or trail from there; lower exit threshold** (115, 116). [new; conflict 61]
- DRAFT: **Partials**: peel one of two when a body closes in the wrong half of the entry array (116); ladder below each intermediate low, 25% at the EH, 20% runner (119); partial at the FPFVG extreme (120). [new; conflicts 63, 66]
- DRAFT: **Adding at each wick CE** (4 micros) (122), pyramiding the CPI short (123). [refines; conflict 55]
- DRAFT: **Target the level that unseats both sides** before the farther extreme (116). [new]
