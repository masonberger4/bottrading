## 1. Rules

### 1.1 What bias is and the top-down order

- **B1 Bias = direction toward the next draw.** Price moves only to run liquidity (old/relative-equal highs and lows) or to reprice an inefficiency (FVG, VI, gap, wick CE); otherwise it consolidates or is manually manipulated (news). (038 ~47:04; 050 ~8:01; 061 ~41:29; 065A ~46:30; 067 ~1:29:00; 098 ~39:50; 102 ~2:05:44; 104 ~14:19) [2023] {1m}
- **B2 Search order:** inefficiency first; if none, liquidity: in premium the sell-side below an old low, in discount the buy-side above a short-term high (065A ~50:00). Beyond the obvious pool, the nearest inefficiency sets how far the run goes (104 ~16:18). [2023]
- **B3 Timeframe chain:** weekly direction first, daily is where most analysis is done; intraday dips in a bullish week are buys (064 ~1:44:34; 084 ~0:18; 095 ~12:52; 075 ~31:00; 065A ~46:30, weekend work). Monthly drives, daily changes direction most often (cc04_003 ~13:40). For day trades the floor is the daily, minimum 4h: ideas not aligned with D/W/M arrays are not high probability (cc07_007 ~26:34; cc09_004 ~0:00:37 "4H last line of defense"; cc12_003 ~12:43; cc08_001 ~7:41 says weekly/monthly not required for day trades). [both]
- **B4 Bellwether:** 15m chart once the daily draw and weekly expansion direction are known (013 ~2:36; 065B ~05:01; 092 ~2:30); the 1h in the 2016 course (cc01_003 ~19:10). [both]
- **B5 Monthly checklist** (run at the close of the last trading day of the month, valid 1-3(4) months): seasonal -> quarterly shift (9-18 monthly candles) -> rate differentials (stocks: bonds) -> market profile (consolidating/trending/retracing) -> intermarket -> market structure + SMT -> PD array matrix -> key levels -> monthly bias (cc12_001 ~1:45, ~11:20 [chart], ~16:18-36:00). [CC]
- **B6 Weekly checklist:** relative strength -> COT -> sentiment (headlines, weekly Williams %R) -> market profile -> intermarket -> structure with IOF -> PD matrix -> key levels -> weekly bias, transposed to daily (cc12_002 ~2:42-20:30). [CC]
- **B7 Daily checklist:** carry M/W in; if conflicted run from the daily alone. COT 12-month midpoint -> OI +/-15% at HTF array -> daily institutional order flow ("most important step") -> weekly profile -> weekly opening prices -> intermarket/SMT -> structure (breakers first) -> PD matrix -> key levels -> daily bias = buyer or seller (cc12_003 ~5:12-26:24). [CC]
- **B8 Intraday checklist / daily routine:** day of week -> IPDA true day -> kill zones -> CBDR/Asian/Flout SDs -> intraday profile -> PD levels -> 5-day ADR (cc12_004 ~3:20-18:54); 17-step routine incl. calendar, reference-market order flow, 20-day P/D (cc09_008 ~41:20 [chart]). [CC] [fx-only for CBDR/Asian/Flout]
- **B9 High-reward filter ("seven things"):** 2 of 4 big picture (macro, rates, intermarket CRB vs USDX, seasonals) + 2 of 3 intermediate (top-down chart, COT, sentiment) + one from each short-term group (correlation/SMT, time & price, IPDA). A filter, not an entry; set at the start of the week, may change Tue/Wed (cc02_006 ~22:40-54:36). [CC]
- **B10 Swing gates:** seasonal -> trending majors (one of rates/DXY and one of commodities/stocks) -> COT -> DXY -> commodities -> OI -> top-down + IPDA ranges; any "no" = no swing, day trades only (cc06_008 ~2:53-13:49; cc06_007 ~0:53). [CC]
- **B11 Bias is a condition, not a trade:** needs displacement in the bias direction, an FVG, a retrace into it and a valid time of day; no setup = no loss (063 ~12:22-15:47; cc12_003 ~25:07). Bias = the direction of the day's largest move toward the draw, not the first move (068 ~16:30); expect the first delivery against the bias (082 ~8:02). [both]
- **B12 Workflow:** decide the draw, then only one parameter (long or short), then read the tape (019 ~13:57); narrative (how the day trades) is separate from bias (001 ~17:01); daily decision tree = bull/bear, then inefficiency or liquidity (015 ~8:07). [2023]

### 1.2 Institutional order flow (candle respect) [mostly computable {1m}]

- **B13 Bullish IOF:** down-close candles (bullish OBs) support price, up-close candles get run through, any short-term low taken is a stop run that reverses at once; bearish = mirror. Score it on the daily (cc12_003 ~11:41; 075 ~1:21:40; 099 ~14:56, ~50:15; 102 ~1:42:05; 058 ~12:00; 085 ~10:27; cc06_002 ~4:53; cc10_001 ~14:19; cc09_006 ~08:17; 028 ~21:12; cc05_015 ~16:25). [both] {1m}
- **B14 OB validity:** only after a change in the state of delivery = first trade through the open of the last opposite candle before the MSS; its low must then hold (075 ~1:16:40; 032 ~52:45; 078 ~53:53; 092 ~2:02:31). Daily/monthly versions: valid once a later candle trades beyond the OB candle's high/low (cc04_003 ~0:35; cc03_004 ~4:50). An OB needs a narrative (059 ~18:58; 065A ~1:12:00; 085 ~40:02). [both] {1m}
- **B15 Daily OB mean-threshold close rule:** bullish order flow is intact while no daily close is below the MT of the last daily bullish OB; a close through it is "definitive failure"; carry an untouched level into next week (058 ~6:01-7:41). A daily close below 50% of the last down-close daily candle = high of the week probably in, not a short signal; deepest acceptable spike = the 1h OB with an FVG (101 ~32:58-38:57 [chart]). Propulsion block holds until a daily close below its MT (075 ~28:00; 079 ~10:27). Daily bullish OB MT = "last line in the sand" (007 ~2:15 [chart]). [2023] {1m}
- **B16 Three-PD-array invalidation:** list three arrays against the position; the first may be breached, the second is the line of defense, bodies/closes beyond all three = idea wrong; daily closes for bias (098 ~47:00, ~77:20), body closes intraday (078 ~1:02:38; 026 ~28:02; 027 ~38:26; 045 ~1:49:28; 105 ~1:21:06; 044 ~1:52:49 stop for the session). [2023] {1m}
- **B17 Bodies tell the story, wicks do the damage;** a 1-2 tick "mohawk" beyond a level is allowed (032 ~9:00; 047 ~8:51; 074 ~26:05; 082 ~3:44; 038 ~46:16; 065B ~03:59; cc03_002 ~6:12). [both]
- **B18 Strength / heaviness tells:** each untouched premium array (VI, SIBI CE, SIBI high) adds to the bearish case (102 ~1:26:55); failing to reach a nearby gap = heavy (091 ~2:21); price allowed to reach a level but refusing = strong bias (098 ~66:40); bearish retrace that can't reach the upper-wick CE = very bearish, stalling at 25% even weaker (028 ~0:15:48; 025 ~25:37); with a large up wick, a next-candle close above its CE = follow-through, close below = revisit idea (100 ~38:13; 065B ~04:18). [2023] {1m}
- **B19 Open-float tug of war:** bearish when runs above short-term highs gain no ground (rejected) while each break of a short-term low extends; intermediate highs keep falling; mirror (cc05_002 ~14:28-18:50). Count which 20/40/60-day pools are being taken: highs repeatedly taken = bullish, do not short (cc05_004 ~8:51). [CC] {1m}
- **B20 Range-sweep profile:** a sweep below a consolidation followed by a rally reveals a bullish profile; treat later drops below ranges as buys until the pattern changes (cc02_008 ~14:28-16:20); with HTF bullish, a decline below a short-term low is a buying opportunity (cc04_002 ~24:03). [CC]
- **B21 Buy/sell program:** after an MSS the algorithm buys every short-term discount array until the draw; never pick tops in a buy program (061 ~45:25; 076 ~5:21; cc06_003 ~5:25). Monthly regimes: shade each leg from OB to target liquidity, trade only in-regime arrays (cc03_002 ~10:12). [both]

### 1.3 Higher-timeframe market structure

- **B22 Swing hierarchy:** STL = plain swing; ITL = low with a higher STL each side; LTL = ITL below the ITLs either side (014 ~0:12; cc05_002 ~20:14). [both] {1m}
- **B23 Intermediate-term high:** a weekly close below the low of the most recent down-close weekly candle -> favor sells until bullish again; close, not wick (077 ~9:19). [2023] {1m}
- **B24 Close-based daily invalidations:** bearish retrace idea holds unless a daily close above Friday's high (077 ~10:53); ES bearish while no daily close above the CE of last week's upper wick, otherwise stand aside (102 ~1:26:30); daily close above a wick CE kills the bearish view (098 ~67:30); a whole week can be gated on a daily close above a weekly wick's upper quadrant (102 ~6:45; 103 ~2:29). [2023] {1m}
- **B25 MWD sequential:** a TF is bullish when a discount array there has induced buying (displacement). Case A M+W+D bullish: buy every daily and 4h discount array. Case B M+W bullish, D correcting: buy daily arrays only at/nested in weekly arrays. Case C M bullish, W+D correcting: only at monthly arrays. A correction does not cancel the bias; only a bearish breaker (higher high rejected, structure broken) on that TF does. Mirror for bears (cc06_004 ~1:02-7:36, ~14:47; cc06_005 ~0:46-3:40). [CC] {1m}
- **B26 Market profile questions** per TF: consolidating? trending (continuation only, no top picking)? retracing (find depth via PD matrix)? First expectation is a retracement, not a reversal (cc12_001 ~29:26; cc12_002 ~14:24-15:47). Swing markets must trend on M/W or have left a consolidation (cc06_001 ~2:21-6:19). [CC]
- **B27 Quarterly shift:** a major turn or consolidation every 3-4 months; in a strong trend it shows as consolidation or a run below a daily STL (cc05_001 ~5:05-7:26). 9-18 month trend bullish -> treat the next shift as a buying opportunity; unclear trend -> expect the last 3-4 month direction to retrace (cc12_001 ~18:43-20:16). [CC] {1m}
- **B28 Candle-pattern day filters:** inside day -> large range next (012 ~16:17); inside week -> expansion next candle or the one after, magnitude not direction (103 ~12:53-14:21); down-close outside day -> lower prices, cancelled only by trading above that day's high (081 ~1:31); after 3 consecutive up (down) closes, no longs (shorts) on day 4 (cc08_006 ~2:07); day after a range > 2x ADR5 = chop (cc08_006 ~1:22); Mon/Tue range far above ADR5 into an HTF array -> midweek reversal candidate (cc07_007 ~20:45); inside bar or smallest range of 3/7 days -> expansion, no direction (cc06_007 ~5:26). [both] {1m}
- **B29 Daily immediate rebalance** (open trades exactly to the prior day's high/low, no gap) = strong continuation (059 ~4:18; 060 ~7:20). [2023] {1m}
- **B30 Direction principle:** the side of the matrix price most recently reacted from (with displacement) implies the opposite side is the next draw (cc06_003 ~0:50, ~4:39); a daily BISI/SIBI must be re-delivered the other way (010 ~23:44; 091 ~4:09). [both]

## 3. Bot implications

**What strategy.py does now (bias-related):**
