Stop above the high of the candle that created the FVG; never move it to breakeven or trail on a daily swing; risk max 1%, low leverage ~19:00-24:25 [new].
- Swing scaling: partial at first target, re-add the same size when price retraces into a newly formed FVG, keeping the original stop ~24:25-26:10 [new].
- Partials between two targets: when a low is expected to break toward a lower target (implied dealing range), split the range into quarters (25/50/75%) and take partials at each; ~75% booked by the 3rd quarter. Not "quarters theory" ~26:20-29:55 [chart] [new].
- Fib settings: 0, 1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 plus negative standard deviations; for TGIF change 0.25 to 0.20 and use 0.3 (20-30% of weekly range retracement) ~27:45 [chart] [new].
- Midpoint naming: in a gap/inefficiency, 50% = consequent encroachment; in a range filled with bodies (order block or balanced trading range) 50% = equilibrium ~34:50 [new].

- A PD array price is allowed to reach but refuses to trade to (e.g. CE of a wick, unfilled breakaway gap) signals strong bias ~66:40, ~78:40 [new].

## Definitions
- Mitigation block: a down-close candle that fails to make a higher high; price trading back up into it is a sell zone; stop above its mean threshold ~46:00 [new].
- Implied dealing range: range between a current low and a lower target price has not reached yet ~26:40 [new].
- Balanced price range: range traded through both ways with bodies; its 50% is equilibrium ~34:20.
- SIBI = down-close weekly/daily FVG; BISI = up-close FVG ~75:15.
- Quarterly shift: 2-3 month directional change in the higher-TF draw ~58:30 [new].
- TGIF: 20-30% retracement of the weekly range on Friday ~28:15.
