## Definitions
- IOFED (institutional order flow entry drill): partial entry into an FVG (BISI or SIBI) that leaves part of it open ~0:05:44
- BISI is named for the up-close middle candle: it "offers" only buy side. The inefficiency is the missing sell side ~0:06:08
- Breaker (bearish): a low with a high to its left and a higher high to its right; the breaker forms once that low breaks ~0:12:35 [chart]. Mean threshold = middle of the big down-close candle ~0:13:27
- Judas swing = fake move at the start of the session ~0:07:56
- ORG: previous day's RTH close to the 09:30 open ~0:17:10
