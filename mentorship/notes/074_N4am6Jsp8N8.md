## Rules
- Liquidity is a draw, not support/resistance: buy-side above old/relative equal/single highs and sell-side below lows get run "to it and through it"; never buy at an old low because it held before. ~4:20, ~50:30
- Gaps are not "filled and done": price keeps referring back to an old gap's high, low or CE even after trading through it. ~4:56, ~38:24 [chart]
- Transpose weekly gaps/inefficiencies onto the daily chart; they can be invisible on the daily. ~7:00 [new]
- Inversion FVG: a gap price traded through is expected to act as support from the other side (bullish IFVG), or resistance (bearish). Wicks poking out ("mohawks") are fine if bodies respect it. ~9:17, ~38:44 [new][chart]
- Bodies tell the story, wicks do the damage: judge PD-array respect by candle bodies. ~26:05, ~39:26
- When a gap sits inside a larger gap/liquidity void, use the CE of that inner gap as the target (ES weekly: 4559.50). ~22:24-23:47 [new][chart]
- TGIF: after a big one-directional week, expect a Friday retracement of 20-30% of the weekly range (fib low-to-high of the week). Use only when the week ran hard. ~24:04, ~28:24-29:21 [new][chart]
- Breaker range projection: after a stop run below a low, measure the low to the high before the lower low (bullish breaker); -1 and -2 SD extensions of that range project the high. Use only with strong bias and clean technicals. ~29:57-31:30 [new][chart]
- IOFED variant: when two gaps sit close together, a retrace only needs to reach the nearer one; without the second gap expect deeper retrace into the first. ~35:47-36:16 [refines Ep 03/04]
- Don't pick weekly/daily tops or bottoms; wait for a clear reversal, then trade with the weekly-to-daily bias. ~37:16-37:52 [new]
- "Model 2022" (as he calls it): run on a level, sharp displacement that takes a short-term level, short the FVG retrace, target the low below. ~26:53-27:10 [chart]
- Three-drives pattern into liquidity that fails, with a breaker or model 2022 inside, is a strong reversal setup. ~32:49-33:14 [new]
- Seasonals (ES): weakness late April into May is typical; a strong low tends to form around the 3rd week of May to 3rd week of June. A seasonal that fails to appear signals a strong move the opposite way. ~16:54-19:23, ~49:22 [new]
- Fib with 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 laid on a weekly volume imbalance; study LTF reactions at those quadrants (homework). ~16:03-16:43 [new]

## Definitions
- Mean threshold = midpoint of an order block; consequent encroachment = midpoint of a gap. ~2:28
- Liquidity void (real): a range with no trading at all between two weekly candles (a week's low vs another week's high), not just a one-sided move; ICT says the common usage was a misnomer. ~20:51-21:33 [conflict with Ep 01 wording, which is close but not identical]
- Inversion FVG (IFVG): an FVG that was traded through and now acts in the opposite role. ~9:30
- Measuring gap: gap mid-move expected to start a new leg toward the next draw (EURUSD). ~12:45
- Bullish order block: consecutive down-close candles (two shown). ~13:47, ~24:14
- Mohawk: wick-only poke beyond a PD array, no body; acceptable. ~26:08, ~38:46
- TGIF: Friday pullback to 20-30% of the weekly range. ~29:02
- Offset distribution: smart money selling above a high to take profit on longs; not resistance. ~33:44
