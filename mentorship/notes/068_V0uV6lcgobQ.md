## Definitions
- Opening range gap: prior day's RTH settlement to 09:30 open; 50% of it is a reaction level; expires in 5 days ~00:08:31, ~00:10:00.
- Liquidity void (real): range with no trading at all, e.g. a gap between 1h candles ~00:08:24.
- Breakaway gap: SIBI/BISI near a swing high/low that price leaves aggressively and does not refill ~00:06:11.
- Rejection block (DXY): referenced as the highest up-close price of the wick (level at the up-close body) and its wick CE ~00:01:08.
