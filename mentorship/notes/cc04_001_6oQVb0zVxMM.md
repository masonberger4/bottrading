## Summary
Interest rates are presented as the main driver of currencies. Smart money accumulation shows as a
correlated asset making a higher low while the benchmark makes a lower low (distribution: lower high
vs. benchmark's higher high). The "interest rate triad" (30y, 10y, 5y futures) is compared at the
moment DXY reaches a PD array; a failure swing in one of the three validates the DXY order block.
Without such a divergence, pass on the idea.

## Rules
- Divergence = smart money footprint: benchmark makes a higher high while correlated assets make
  lower highs -> distribution (bearish); benchmark lower low while correlated assets make higher
  lows -> accumulation (bullish) ~2:52-5:47 [new as a general rule; extends the ES/NQ SMT rule]
- Index example: Dow making higher highs while S&P 500 and/or Nasdaq fail to = warning of a
  distribution cycle ~6:13-6:36 [new]
- Benchmarks by market: Dow (stocks), DXY (currencies), CRB (commodities) ~3:06-3:24 (forex/other
  items marked; Dow vs S&P/NQ is the index-futures use)
- Interest rate triad: the three rate futures should confirm each other's highs/lows when DXY is at a
  significant level; one failing to confirm (failure swing) validates the trade ~7:19-7:38 [chart] [new, forex]
- Only one of the three needs to fail; it signals an interest-rate shift ~7:51-8:40 [chart] [new, forex]
- DXY long at a bullish OB is confirmed by bonds making a lower high in one contract while others
  make higher highs; DXY short at a bearish OB / raided old high / FVG confirmed by one bond failing
  to make a lower low ~8:56-9:31, ~19:09-19:56 [new, forex]
- Workflow: first have a predetermined bias for the traded market; then, when price reaches a focus
  point (OB, liquidity pool, liquidity void/FVG), check the correlated markets for divergence; if none,
  pass on the trade ~13:14-13:25, ~18:41-19:08 [new]
- Divergence can be read on a closing basis ~13:45 [new]
- Bond prices up = rates down = dollar bearish (and the reverse) ~17:37-17:50, ~20:07 (forex)
- Blend several assets: e.g. EURUSD made a higher high while GBPUSD failed to (and DXY made a
  lower low) ~16:51-17:07 [chart]
- Short-term disagreement can occur when a longer-term shift is underway; accept it ~18:00-18:15

## Definitions
- Interest rate triad: 30-year T-bond (long-term benchmark), 10-year note (intermediate), 5-year note
  (short-term), all futures ~1:22-1:41 [chart].
- Failure swing: one market of a correlated group fails to make the new high/low the others make
  ~7:33-8:36 [chart].
- Smart money accumulation / distribution as defined by the divergences above ~2:52-6:36.
