- Liquidity void = real gap between candle highs/lows; once passed through both ways it becomes a balanced price range price should not re-enter ~13:02-15:01 [chart]

## Definitions
- Buy-side liquidity: buy stops above old highs; sell-side: sell stops below old lows ~13:02
- BISI ("buy side imbalance sell side inefficiency"): one candle passes between candle 1 high and candle 3 low ~13:02, ~21:00
- Liquidity void: actual gap with no overlap ~13:02
- Consequent encroachment: halfway into the gap ~15:01
- Balanced price range ~15:01, ~42:02; Breakaway gap ~40:03, ~44:00; Breaker ~45:02, ~56:01
- "Rebalance" vs "balance": price entering the gap balances it; it is rebalanced once price leaves ~1:01:00
