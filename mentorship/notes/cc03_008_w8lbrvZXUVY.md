# cc03_008 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 03 - Market Maker Trap Head Shoulders Pattern (w8lbrvZXUVY)
Teaching 8 of 8, November 2016 mentorship (Core Content month 3). Market: GBPUSD (forex).
Timeframes: daily, 1h, 30m. Duration ~17 min. Examples: Aug 10-12 2015, Oct 9-20 2015.

## Summary
Classic head-and-shoulders (H&S) and inverse H&S are genuine only at intermediate/long-term
highs/lows; on lower timeframes they are retail traps. When the higher-TF order flow (daily OB /
breaker) points the other way, ICT fades the neckline break: buy the sell-stop run below a H&S
neckline (target above the head) or sell the buy-stop run above an inverse H&S neckline (target
below the head). Concept transfers directly to index-futures intraday charts.

## Rules
- Lower-TF H&S / inverse H&S patterns are treated as liquidity traps, not signals; only act when the
  higher-TF bias (daily institutional order flow) is opposite to the pattern ~1:48-2:12, ~4:57-5:24 [new]
- H&S in a bullish higher-TF context: the neckline (two clean equal lows) break is a turtle soup long;
  buy the sell stops below the equal lows / neckline ~6:22-6:43 [new]
- Its first objective: buy stops above the head (highest peak); first profit can be at the right
  shoulder ~6:43-6:56, ~7:34 [new]
- Inverse H&S in a bearish higher-TF context: sell the break above the neckline (equal highs) as a
  buy-stop run; target sell stops below the head (lowest low); first partial at right shoulder
  ~7:01-7:38 [new]
- Entry level for the fade can use the highest candle body instead of the wick ("where the most
  volume is") ~15:40-15:50 [chart] [new]
- Do not wait for the classic pullback to the neckline; the break itself is the fade entry ~16:02
- Daily bullish OB: the down candle before the up move becomes a bullish OB once price trades through
  its high; the zone from its high down to its open is where to buy ~8:30-8:42 [chart]
- Daily bearish breaker: a down candle (bullish OB) used to run buy stops above equal highs; when price
  later trades below it and returns up into it, that zone is a sell area ~12:15-13:00 [chart]
- Avoid picking long-term tops and bottoms; aim for intermediate and short-term swings ~4:20-4:46

## Trade examples
1. GBPUSD, Aug 12 2015 (daily + 1h), long. Daily bullish OB validated Aug 10 (chart lines ~1.5468 and
   ~1.5420; captions say "1 55 46", likely misread for the OB high) and price trades into its body
   on Aug 12. 1h: high, higher high, lower high with two equal lows = H&S; retail measured target
   ~1.5495. ICT: buy 1.5550 at the sell stops below the equal lows, target 1.5620 above the head
   (~70 pips) [chart: labels 1.5620, 1.5550 "Sell Stops", 1.5495]. Price swept the lows ~4 times,
   then expanded above 1.5620.
2. GBPUSD, Oct 9-13 2015 (daily + 30m), short. Daily: down candle that ran buy stops above equal highs
   is a bearish breaker; return into it at 1.5345-1.5350 (daily line 1.5346) = bearish context.
   30m: inverse H&S (low, lower low, higher low), neckline sloping slightly down ~1.5365-1.5385
   [chart]. Sell the break above the neckline into buy stops; price dropped ~80+ pips to ~1.520 on
   Oct 13 [chart].
3. GBPUSD, Oct 15-20 2015 (30m), short. Daily: price trades up into the low/body of the last up
   candle (bearish OB). Inverse H&S around Oct 15; sell above 1.5502 (highest candle body), cover
   below the head ~1.5418 [chart: lines 1.5502 and 1.5418]. Price ran above 1.5502 again ~Oct 20 for
   buy stops and then back toward the head.

## Definitions
- Head and shoulders: peak, higher peak (head), lower peak; neckline joins the lows between them;
  classic target = head-to-neckline height projected below the neckline ~0:44-1:34 [chart].
- Inverse H&S: low, lower low (head), higher low; neckline joins the highs; target = height added above
  the neckline ~2:38-3:14.
- In this video ICT uses "bullish order block" for the down candle that preceded the up move and
  "breaker" for that OB once price trades back below it ~12:20-12:56.
