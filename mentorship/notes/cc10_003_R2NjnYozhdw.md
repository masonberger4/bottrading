## Summary
A tour of ICT's favourite commodity seasonal tendencies. Each slide shows a 40-year (solid black) and a 15-year (dashed red) seasonal line, plus an unmarked 5-year weekly chart. He calls seasonals a road map, not a signal. A market that does not follow its seasonal tendency is itself information: it is either consolidating or showing strength or weakness against the seasonal.

## Rules
- Use a seasonal only when the 40-year and 15-year lines agree on the turn ~6:41 [chart] [new]
- Seasonals are context. Blend them with PD arrays and COT commercial hedging, and do not force a trade from them ~40:19 [new]
- Failure signal: if the market goes against its seasonal (rallies when it should fall), read that as strong underlying demand. If it goes sideways instead, it is a consolidation to avoid ~3:03, ~14:33, ~41:02 [new]
- Trade the contract month with the highest open interest and volume (normally the nearby). Gold sometimes skips months ~5:21-5:51 [new]; this matters for picking the ES/NQ front month at a roll
- Keep a monthly checklist of which seasonals are active ~40:31 [new]
- Open interest belongs to its own market only. Do not infer one market's direction from another market's open interest (he retracts his old oats/canola/rice idea) ~39:38 [new]

## Trade examples
No trades. The commodity seasonal windows he favours (weekly, paper only):
- Soybeans: long Feb to Jun/Jul, short Jun/Jul to Sep/Oct, long again from Sep/Oct ~9:27

- Cocoa: low late May/early June, high late Aug/Sep ~23:47. Orange juice: low Sep/Oct, rally into Dec/Jan ~25:43
- Coffee: buy Jun/Jul, sell Apr/May ~26:41. Cotton: sell from April/May ~28:29
- Crude oil: rally from the start of the year into a Sep/Oct high ~30:35 [chart: the 33-year line bottoms in Feb and peaks in Oct]. He read 2017's failure to rally as heavy supply, so bearish
- Copper: buy June and November, sell April and September ~32:24
- Gold: buyer's market, look for a July/August low ~34:30 [chart: 40-year low in July]. Silver: same, low in Jul/Aug ~36:00

## Definitions
- Seasonal tendency: the average yearly path over 40 and 15 years, scaled 0-100 ~6:00 [chart]
- Nearby contract: the first (front) delivery month, where the open interest is ~5:21
- Contract roll sequence for soybeans: Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Sep, Nov, Jan ~4:53
