## Definitions
- Immediate rebalance: open trades directly to the prior candle's high/low (here Monday's high on DXY daily, Monday's low on EURUSD), with no gap left ~00:04:36 [chart]
- Inversion FVG: an FVG price closed through that now acts as opposite support/resistance ~00:03:47
- Rejection block (refined): among a cluster of down-close candles with long lower wicks at lows, the lowest close is the rejection block level ~00:09:20-00:10:20
- Opening range gap: prior regular-session close to the next regular-session open on ES ~00:13:52
- Silver bullet (AM): 10:00-11:00 NY FVG entry after MSS toward the draw ~00:08:56
- Short-term premium: price retracing up into a sell-side inefficiency before the drop ~00:19:12
