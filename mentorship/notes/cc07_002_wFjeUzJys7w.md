# cc07_002 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 07 - Short Term Trading Defining Weekly Range Profiles (wFjeUzJys7w)
Lesson 2 of the March 2017 short-term trading module (~0:10) / generic (FX sessions mentioned; no live chart) / weekly shape across Mon-Fri, study on 1h charts / 11m. Slides only: each profile has a schematic Mon-Fri line drawing plus text.

## Summary
Twelve weekly range templates (six bullish/bearish pairs) describe which day forms the week's low or high and what the earlier days look like before it. Every one depends on knowing the higher-timeframe (HTF) premium/discount arrays. The setup is that price "hovers" near an array without reaching it, and the profile says on which day and session the run into that array (the low or high of the week) is likely. Two "Seek & Destroy Friday" profiles are low probability and best avoided.

## Rules
- Use the weekly profile only after the weekly bias is set; each profile assumes you are already bullish or bearish ~1:00-1:08, ~1:43-1:52 [new]
- Classic Tuesday low of week (bullish): Monday hovers above an HTF discount array without reaching it, then Tuesday drops into it and forms the week's low, in the London open and/or the NY session. The chart shows the week's high on Thursday and a Friday pullback ~1:00-1:34 [chart] [new]
- Classic Tuesday high of week (bearish): the mirror, with Monday below an HTF premium array and Tuesday rising into it. The chart shows the week's low on Thursday and a Friday bounce ~1:41-2:16 [chart] [new]
- Caveat for both Tuesday profiles: the London high (or low) can be run once more in Tuesday's NY session and still be the extreme of the week ~2:18-2:44 [new]
- Wednesday low of week (bullish): price hovers above the array on Mon-Tue, then Wednesday drops into it in London and/or NY. Mon and Tue may both be down days. The Wednesday London low can be run in NY and still form the week's low. The chart shows a rally into Friday's close ~2:50-3:42 [chart] [new]
- Wednesday high of week (bearish): the mirror. Mon and Tue may be up days, and the chart shows a decline into Friday's close ~3:46-4:35 [chart] [new]
- Consolidation Thursday reversal (bullish): Mon-Wed consolidate, then Thursday runs the intra-week low and reverses. The driver is news or a rate release late in the NY session, around 14:00 ET (FOMC/rate decisions). The chart shows a Thursday low below Tuesday's low and Friday breaking above Wednesday's high ~4:40-5:28 [chart] [new]
- Consolidation Thursday reversal (bearish): the mirror. Thursday runs the intra-week high around 14:00 ET on a rate release, then reverses ~5:34-6:20 [new]
- Consolidation midweek rally (bullish): Mon-Wed consolidate, then price runs the intra-week high and expands higher into Friday. It is expected when price recently rallied off a discount array, has not yet reached the HTF premium array, and the pause shows no bearish reversal. The chart shows Mon up, Tue high, a Wed pullback, then a run to Friday ~6:22-6:53 [chart] [new]
- Consolidation midweek decline (bearish): the mirror. It is expected when price recently fell from a premium array, has not yet reached the HTF discount array, and the pause shows no bullish reversal ~7:00-7:34 [new]
- Seek & Destroy Friday (bullish or bearish): consolidation Mon-Thu, with shallow stop runs above and below the intra-week highs and lows, then Friday runs one side and expands. This is neutral / low probability. It shows up while markets wait for a rate announcement or NFP, and in July-August. Avoid trading these conditions ~7:42-8:56 [chart] [new]
- Wednesday weekly reversal (bullish): Mon-Tue consolidate, then a drive lower into an HTF discount array takes the sell stops and reverses strongly. It is expected when price trades at a long-term or intermediate-term low, so institutional buying is paired with resting sell stops (a sell-stop raid). The chart shows a Tue high, a Wed low well below Monday's, and a rally into Friday ~9:00-9:28 [chart] [new]
- Wednesday weekly reversal (bearish): the mirror at a long-term or intermediate-term high, with a buy-stop raid ~9:34-10:04 [new]
- Study method: on 1h charts of any market, label past weeks by profile and note what came before each one ~10:16-10:43 [new]

## Definitions
- Weekly range profile: a template for when the week's low or high forms and how the days before it look ~0:16
- Hovering: price sits near an HTF array on the days before without trading into it. That failure to reach the array is the tell that the run comes on the profile's day ~1:10-1:31
- Intra-week high/low: the highest/lowest price so far in the current week ~4:54
- Seek & Destroy: a range-bound week of shallow stop runs on both sides before a late Friday expansion ~7:52-8:06
