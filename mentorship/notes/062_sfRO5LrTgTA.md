## Rules
- Gap hierarchy, like a PD-array matrix: breakaway gap, measuring gap, common gap; classify every FVG in a swing before using it ~43:58-45:03 [new]
- Common gap = ordinary FVG; can be revisited several times and reclaimed as support/resistance; tradable only with context (2022 model: higher-TF level, buy side taken, short-term low broken, gap left) ~34:53-35:24, ~41:09, ~45:36 [chart] [new]
- Breakaway gap needs context: it breaks away from a level you already expected to hold (here the weekly gap high, already respected Monday) with a market structure shift; here the second MSS ~35:59-36:30 [chart] [new]
- Breakaway gap qualifies when price returns into it, leaves part unfilled, and then makes a new low; then trail the stop to that gap level ~37:02-37:21, ~38:59-39:20 [chart] (refines rules line 23/72)
- Stop on a short into the breakaway gap: above the high of the candle that retraced into it ~37:28 [chart]
- If the gap is fully filled it is not a breakaway: if stopped, wait for price to tag the higher-TF level / prior FVG, break lower again, form a new FVG, and treat that as the candidate breakaway gap ~37:57-38:31 [new]
- Measuring gap also tends to leave a portion open; if price does not reach the far edge, it qualifies ~44:13-44:30 [chart]
- Swing projection: fib from the swing high (bearish) to the near edge of the measuring gap (the low edge of a SIBI, i.e. candle-3 high); the gap is about half of the "implied dealing range"; target = -1 standard deviation ~42:19-43:09 [chart] [new]
- The -1 SD target must agree with a run below an old liquidity pool (here sell side at the weekly gap low); the projection alone is not enough ~44:36-44:46 [new]
- Read candle bodies: bodies holding the edge of a gap (opens/closes not through it) confirm it is respected; wicks are noise ~41:21-42:02 [chart]
- 1m two-candle FVG = one 2m candle: CE of that 2m candle's range is where the retrace stops ~39:51-40:30 [new]
- Balanced price range (gap offered both sides, BISI then SIBI over same prices): price tends to stop at its high, low or CE; once left, unlikely to trade back through its far edge ~03:44-04:33 [chart]
- Neutral stance when several markets reached their higher-TF objectives: expect consolidation (stage 1 of consolidation/expansion/retracement/reversal); don't trade ~11:18-11:45, ~19:44-21:07 [new]
- High- vs low-resistance liquidity run: back-and-forth range (many swing lows) = high resistance, filter out; single clean V low/high = low resistance, trade those ~06:33-12:41 [new]
- Bullish thesis invalidation: trading (no close needed) below the stated candle low ~22:39-23:07

## Definitions
- Breakaway gap: FVG leaving a level that had context, part left unfilled, followed by a new extreme ~36:01, ~39:01
- Measuring gap: FVG roughly at the midpoint of the implied dealing range, used for projection ~40:39, ~42:38
- Common gap: plain FVG that can be retested repeatedly ~44:09
- Implied dealing range: the range you expect to complete but price has not yet reached ~42:41
- Swing projection theory: high to measuring-gap edge, -1 SD ~43:56
- Low/high resistance liquidity run ~06:41-08:03
- Wick CE / "Wick discount CE": 50% of a wick, used as a level (ES ~4264) ~24:07 [chart]
