# cc05_007 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 05 - Qualifying Trade Conditions With 10 Year Yields (N8_8tEw2_44)
2016-17 Private Mentorship, "lesson 2.2 of January 2017". Markets: 10-year T-Note futures (ZNU15, ZNH17), Dollar Index (DXU15, DXU16, DXH17), US 10Y yield (investing.com line chart). Daily charts. Duration ~9 min. Forex/position-trading context (quarterly shifts); concept transfers as SMT between inversely correlated markets.

## Summary
To qualify a seasonal or quarterly-shift trade, compare swings of the 10Y note with the Dollar Index. They should mirror each other (note lower low <-> dollar higher high). When the mirror breaks (a "crack in correlation", i.e. SMT divergence), an underlying trend or manipulation is under way; confirm with the yield chart and open interest. Use this to align all shorter-term trading with the higher-timeframe order flow.

## Rules
- Qualify seasonal/quarterly ideas with SMT between the 10Y note and DXY: perfect symmetry = note lower low with DXY higher high (and vice versa). If one side fails to make its mirrored extreme, it is a crack in correlation and qualifies the trade. ~00:52-02:13, ~05:46-05:58 [chart] [new; extends the SMT rule (ES vs NQ / ES vs MES) to inversely correlated markets]
- Example logic: notes making lower lows (early June) while DXY makes lower highs instead of higher highs -> bullish notes / bearish dollar idea confirmed. ~01:05-02:19 [chart] [new]
- Confirm with the yield chart: yields declining while note futures rally. ~02:24-02:40, ~03:58-04:08 [chart] [new]
- Equal lows in notes should map to equal highs in DXY; DXY making higher highs while notes did not make lower lows = qualifying divergence. ~03:14-03:51 [chart] [new]
- Ignore an outlier event wick when reading structure (e.g. election-night spike); judge highs/lows without it. ~04:42-04:54 [chart] [new]
- Declining open interest during a dollar rally read as smart-money short covering. ~05:58-06:07 [chart] [new]
- Trending note market (more range to go) supports a trending dollar; blend with the quarterly shift concept. ~06:11-06:55 [new]
- Time horizon for these position trades ~3 months; setups usually complete in about half that. ~06:55-08:03 [new] (position trading; not for intraday bot)
- Alternative qualifiers (either is enough): interest-rate triad, or SMT of a forex pair vs DXY. ~08:22-08:44 [new] (forex)
- Use the HTF alignment as a filter for swing, short-term, day trades and scalps too. ~09:02-09:19 [new]

## Definitions
- Crack in correlation: one of two correlated (here inversely) markets fails to confirm the other's new swing extreme; same idea as SMT divergence. ~02:08, ~08:12 [chart]
- Market symmetry: inverse markets should print mirror-image swings. ~01:38-01:46, ~05:46
