Recorded Jan 2017 (lesson "2.3 of the January 2017 mentorship", ~0:13) / forex only: AUD futures (A6Y00 cash, A6H17 March contract) daily with volume + open interest, DXY daily, JPY futures daily and weekly / timeframes: weekly, daily / 18m. Slides plus static charts; no intraday content.

## Summary
Long-term macro pair selection for forex: pair a high-interest-rate currency against a low-rate one, buy the strong-yield side at higher-timeframe support (or sell the weak side at resistance), and confirm with smart-money clues (open interest drop, seasonals) and the dollar index. Two worked examples: AUDUSD long off 0.7150 (Dec 2016) and USDJPY long / yen futures short off a weekly bearish order block. ICT says outright this is not for day trading. For our index-futures bot it is at most background; nothing here maps directly to ES/NQ.

## Rules
- Forex-only, HTF: pick a country with a high central-bank rate and one with a low rate; the strength of the difference matters, not extremes; couple them as a pair ~4:16-4:52 [chart] [new]
- Buy the high-yield currency against the low-yield one ("money seeks yield"); funds buy strong and sell weak ~2:32-4:09 [new]
- Long checklist (8 steps on the slide): high-rate country, low-rate country, pair coupling, strong support on HTF charts, wait for smart-money clues it is being bought, seasonal tendency and/or open interest confirm, USDX directional confirmation qualifies ~4:16-7:10 [chart] [new]
- Only one or two smart-money confirmations are needed, not every tool ~6:59 [new]
- Short mirror: strong resistance on HTF charts, smart-money clues it is being sold ~12:25-12:51 [chart] [new]
- Sharp drop in futures open interest at HTF support = commercials short covering, expecting higher prices ~7:59-8:27 [chart] [new]
- HTF support alone is not a buy signal; it needs the OI clue and the rate differential together ~9:27-9:45 [new]
- These differentials are for HTF / swing direction, not to time day trades; trading in their direction raises odds ~15:34-15:52 [new]
- Avoid exotic pairs ~17:39-17:53 (forex-only)

## Definitions
- Interest rate differential: gap between two central banks' policy rates; the pair formed from them is "fundamentally" poised for strength (high vs low) or weakness (low vs high) relative to others ~0:40 [chart]
- Short covering via OI: a large fall in open interest at support signals commercials closing shorts ~8:02 [chart]
