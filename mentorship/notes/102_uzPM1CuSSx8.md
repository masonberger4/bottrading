## Definitions
- Inversion FVG: an FVG that failed its bullish/bearish function. When price returns, it acts the opposite way. Shaded orange (white when he wants to avoid hinting) ~1:02:03-1:03:00 [chart]
- Color scheme: blue/green = discount PD array expected to support; pink/red = premium/resistance; orange = inversion ~1:02:05
- Mohawk: a small deviation beyond a level that is still treated as respected ~1:11:17
- High- vs low-resistance liquidity run: whether consolidation/balanced ranges lie between entry and target ~2:16:05
- Balanced price range: sell-side, buy-side, sell-side, buy-side offered back and forth; hard to get through in one pass ~2:19:30
- Upper/lower quadrant: 25%/75% of a wick or range (fib 0/0.25/0.5/1) [chart]
