## Rules
- CPI day: no position into the 08:30 release; stops do not protect (price gaps, brokers pull liquidity). Trade only after the release, like NFP. ~0:04:35, ~0:28:55 [new]
- Any profit taken through the release itself is luck; treat the first spike as untradeable. ~0:12:10, ~0:23:50 [new]
- Before the release, mark: buy side (relative equal highs), sell side (relative equal lows), the nearest FVG. Smooth equal highs/lows are both targeted on high-impact news. ~0:20:50, ~0:30:16 [chart]
- On medium/high-impact news, the first liquidity pool run is the losing side: sell side swept first -> expect the larger, prolonged move to buy side (and vice versa). ~0:52:12, ~0:54:02, ~1:09:53 [new]
- Best time to buy is after sell stops have been taken. ~1:44:03 [new]
- After both pools are taken, price retraces into the range between them, to its CE (50%) and stops there. ~1:10:43-1:11:26 [chart] [new]
- Retracement bodies stop at the 08:30 opening price; wicks can go past it. ~0:55:13 [chart] [new]
- Order block = change in the state of delivery, not every down-close candle. Prefer the down-close candle with the larger body. A high-probability OB has an FVG next to it. ~0:50:06-0:51:52 [new]
- After the MSS, the OB candle high to low is the dealing range; ignore FVGs/OBs inside it until price trades out of it. Valid while price holds above the OB's mean threshold. ~0:55:46-0:56:24 [new]
- Wick CE (50% of a long wick) is the first objective, e.g. the 15s release wick at 4405, YM daily wick. ~0:22:18, ~0:34:02 [chart]
- FVG holding test: step through 1m-4m; if only wicks enter the FVG and bodies stay outside it, expect continuation. ~1:30:49-1:32:07 [chart] [new]
- Partial exits: when price makes successive short-term highs, sell part above each one (smart money distributes into buy stops; three-drives shape). ~1:03:42, ~1:29:17 [new]
- Projection: anchor a fib low-to-high on the impulse swing; -0.5 SD is always a first partial (even if under 5 handles); -1 SD = measured move. ~1:33:48-1:35:28 [chart] [new]
- Day trade (hold for the daily range) vs session trade (AM or PM scalp): day trade keeps the initial stop below the 08:30 opening price / below the CE of the 08:30 1m wick, does not trail it, and pays itself with partials. Session trades trail stops, and trailed stops eventually get hit. ~1:38:44-1:43:40 [new]
- Lunch: between 12:00 and 13:30 price often runs stops below the low formed before 11:00/noon. Trailed stops in the AM get hit there. ~1:45:41-1:46:17 [new] (refines the Ep 03 lunch note)
- Bias order: weekly direction first, then daily; intraday dips in a bullish week are buying opportunities. ~1:44:34 [new]
- Look for one good setup per week (repeats Ep 03). ~1:37:34
- Index futures delivery months: H, M, U, Z only. ~0:16:22

## Definitions
- SIBI = sellside imbalance, buyside inefficiency: delivered only downward, so it should be repriced on a rally. BISI is the mirror. After price has traded through both ways it becomes a balanced price range. ~1:27:05, ~1:31:19
- Propulsion block: the consolidation of candles inside a bullish OB's range before expansion. Named only; he points to month 4 content. ~0:57:57
- Day trading = trying to capture most of the daily range, open to close. Intraday scalping = session trading (AM before NY lunch, or PM), in and out within an hour or two. ~1:41:00
- Manual intervention: the release spike where liquidity is pulled. ~1:06:15
