## Definitions
- Fair value gap explained with a paint-roller analogy. Price delivered in a single pass leaves a pocket that was not offered both ways, between candle 1's high and candle 3's low. Price must come back to rebalance it. He coined the name because the gap offers "fair value" to the side of the bias (~2:43-5:51).
- Quadrants of a wick: fib 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 on a candle wick; 0.5 = CE. He says it is not "quarters theory" and has nothing to do with Goldbach levels (~6:40-7:00) [chart].
- "On side": price will run for the liquidity you are targeting (~1:16:19).
- Funded position: enough partials banked that being stopped on the rest still leaves the day profitable (~24:41, ~37:55).
