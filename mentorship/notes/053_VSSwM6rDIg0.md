# 053 NQ Futures Review & The ICT Sick Sister Consolidation Model (VSSwM6rDIg0)
Date discussed: Mon May 8 2023 (recorded ~17:40 ET same day). Market: NQ (NQM2023) traded; ES (ESM2023) and YM (YMM2023) as comparison. Timeframe shown: 15m only. Duration ~11 min.

## Summary
Quiet Monday (no medium/high-impact news, heavy calendar Wed-Fri) plus an old NWOG close to the current NWOG -> expect consolidation. YM took both reference highs, ES took one of two, NQ took neither. "Sick Sister": in a consolidation, the weakest index is expected to catch up later, so wait for it to drop into a deep discount, buy, and target the buy-side the others already ran. He bought NQ ~09:45 and sold 5 at 13327.

## Rules
- Day filter: Mon/Tue with no medium/high-impact news while the week's news is Wed-Fri -> expect a consolidation day, not a trending one; trade smaller, more "nimble" ~00:38-01:22 [new]
- Converging NWOGs (an old NWOG close to the current week's NWOG) -> market likely to consolidate / go sideways ~01:31, ~08:50 [new]
- Sick Sister sequence (bullish form) [new]:
  1. Compare NQ, ES, YM on the same timeframe (15m) at the same reference highs ~01:56-04:22 [chart]
  2. Classify each: how many reference highs it exceeded at the key time (here ~08:00 ET). YM 2 of 2, ES 1 of 2, NQ 0 of 2 ~06:57-07:16 [chart]
  3. The one that failed most (NQ) is the "sick sister"; focus only on it ~07:41-07:57
  4. Do NOT buy it while it is up; wait for it to drop into a deep discount of the dealing range ~05:44, ~09:45
  5. Entry where these overlap: below equilibrium (50%), in OTE 62-79%, inside the bullish order block (consecutive down-close candles) and inside the FVG ~04:47-05:25 [chart]
  6. Target: the buy-side liquidity above the highs it failed to take ~05:52 [chart]

- Partial/exit before the final high: once the intermediate buy-side is taken, exit, since the sick sister may "rejoin" the weakness of the others; he placed an exit between the two highs ~06:02-06:31 [new] [chart]
- In a trending (non-consolidation) market the same divergence means the opposite: expect the lagging market to resume in trend direction rather than discount-then-catch-up ~08:20-08:43 [new]

## Definitions
- Sick Sister (~02:16, ~07:16): among correlated indices in a consolidation, the one that failed to make the higher high (or lower low) the others made; expected to make it later after first offering a discount (premium for shorts).
- Order block (bullish), restated: consecutive down-close candles ~05:00.
- Discount here = below equilibrium of the dealing range, ideally at OTE 62-79% ~04:53.
