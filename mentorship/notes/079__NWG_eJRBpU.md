- Propulsion block: a down-close candle (OB) followed by another down-close candle that trades into it only to about its half. A close below half of the propulsion block turns the bullish view off and makes it a reclaimed level to sell from ~10:27-11:06 [new] [chart]
- A propulsion block whose second candle goes past the midpoint of the first, with an FVG nearby, is more prone to fail (NQ weekly) ~11:39-12:10 [new]
- A volume imbalance can act like a breakaway gap (should not be refilled) ~14:20 [chart]
- Timeframe-relative OB: a daily down-close candle can be a bearish OB when the lower timeframe shows its last up-close candle there; anchor to the lower TF ~15:06-15:33 [new]
- An up-close candle inside a sell program is very influential: its mean threshold, its low and its open are sensitive levels ~15:53-16:04
- Wicks are treated like gaps: CE of a wick = its midpoint ("premium wick CE" / "discount wick CE") are reaction levels ~0:44, ~16:55-17:05 [chart]
- Transpose daily and weekly annotations onto the lower timeframes so the HTF levels stay visible ~20:12
