You will take notes on several videos, one after another. For EACH video in your list,
follow mentorship/notes/PROMPT.md exactly, substituting that video's IDX, ID and TITLE.
Finish and write one notes file before starting the next. Keep each video's frame budget.

Per-video adjustments:
- If the caption file just says there are no captions, work from the frames only (view most
  of them in order) and say clearly at the top of the notes that they are chart-only.
- IDX values starting with "cc" are ICT's 2016-17 Private Mentorship Core Content (older,
  partly forex). Focus on concepts/definitions/rules that apply to index-futures day trading,
  mark forex-only items, and tag [new]/[conflict] against mentorship_rules.md as usual.
- Videos longer than ~90 minutes: read captions in chunks, frame budget ~60-80, notes up to
  ~1400 words.

When all are done, reply with one short paragraph per video: notes path, frames viewed, and
the 3 most important [new]/[conflict] items.
