- Generalised: close of the prior trading day vs open of the next trading day ~10:46 [new]
- Gap lower (open below prior close) = "discount ORG"; gap higher = "premium ORG" ~10:53-11:16 [new]
- The ORG is a PD array with three levels: high, low and its midpoint = consequent encroachment ~11:19 [new]
- Midpoint naming: CE for gaps and wicks; mean threshold (50%) for order blocks, breakers, propulsion blocks ~11:30-11:56 [new]
- Repricing macro: after the 09:30 Judas swing away from the ORG, expect the algorithm to reprice back into the ORG; target the ORG (he exited at its upper edge) ~14:50 [chart] [new]
- Midnight opening price line runs from 00:00 to 11:00 ~4:37 [chart] [new]
