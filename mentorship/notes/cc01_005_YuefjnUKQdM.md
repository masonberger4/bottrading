## Summary
Define a dealing range from a clear swing high to swing low with a fib. Equilibrium = 50%.
Shorts only in premium (above 50%), ideally at 62-79% (OTE 70.5%), best when the
retracement also runs an old high (turtle soup sell). Targets = below old swing lows. You
do not need a directional bias: ranges exist in trends, consolidations and reversals.

## Rules
- Range anchor: fib from a clear, impulsive swing high down to the lowest low of that swing (use the extreme low, not an intermediate one); skip sloppy/questionable swings ~2:07, ~9:33 [chart]
- Price must first trade through equilibrium (50%); only above it is the market at a premium ~3:47-4:31 [chart] [new as explicit precondition; Ep 08 has "FVG at/above 50% to sell"]
- Do not short at 50% itself; ICT rejects the 50% level as an entry. If price tags 50% and drops, it is a missed trade, let it go ~3:22, ~5:09 [new]
- Short zone: 62%-79% retracement; OTE sweet spot 70.5% ~5:31, ~11:33, ~15:43 [chart] [new]
- Premium + old high taken out = turtle soup sell, high-probability short ~7:03-7:27, ~8:53-9:10, ~14:46 [chart] (Ep 08 turtle soup plan; premium filter is [new])
- If stopped out and price keeps running old highs while still above 50% (deep premium), keep taking turtle-soup sells; losses like this are normal ~12:36-13:20 [new]
- Re-anchor: a stop-out at 62-79% of a small swing may be the OTE of a larger swing; always check the larger high-to-low range too ~8:28-8:50 [chart] [new]
- Nested ranges: a big swing can contain smaller swings, each its own range ~1:55, ~4:57 [chart]
- Swing high choice: the most recent one before the down move, preferring the one with more price action around it ~19:20 [chart] [new]
- New range starts as soon as a swing low forms (watch the 4th candle for willingness to go higher) ~7:58-8:14, ~13:30 [new]
- Swing high/low = 3 candles (middle extreme), not the 5-candle Williams fractal ~11:49 [new]
- Targets: first partial below the nearest old swing low, rest below the next low; can also target a bullish order block below ~4:07, ~10:05, ~10:37, ~13:25 [chart]
- Up-close candle at the 62% where bodies stop = bearish order block, a sell by itself ~13:59 [chart] (OB already in Ep 02)
- Consolidation regime: range trading (premium/discount + turtle soup) is the go-to method ~11:01 [new]
- No daily bias required; trade inside the current range in any regime ~18:51-19:17 [conflict] (2023 rules lean on draw on liquidity/bias; treat as a fallback mode)
- Concepts are timeframe-independent ("universal"), daily or intraday ~18:14 [new]
- Target buffer beyond the low plus spread: 10 pips below the low, ~4 pips spread allowance ~17:49, ~20:47 [forex-only]

## Definitions
- Premium: price above 50% of the current defined range; "overbought" relative to the range, no oscillator needed ~4:07-4:33.
- Equilibrium: 50% of the range (fib) ~3:57.
- OTE: 62%-79% retracement, sweet spot 70.5% ~2:32, ~11:33.
- Impulse price swing: a clear directional leg; several small ones make a larger impulse leg ~1:32-2:03.
- Turtle soup sell: run of buy stops above an old high, then sell ~7:22.

## Seen only on the charts
- Fib template levels: 0, Equilibrium (50), 62%, OTE (70.5), 79%, 100. No extensions drawn.
- Anchors are on wicks (high wick to low wick).
- In the 1h example price first stalled near 50% (Sep 8-9), then a second push reached 79% and ran a minor high; the later Sep 16-21 rally went past 100% (range invalid).
