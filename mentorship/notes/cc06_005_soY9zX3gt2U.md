# cc06_005 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 06 - High Probability Swing Trade Setups In Bear Markets (soY9zX3gt2U)
Lesson 5 of the February 2017 mentorship (~0:10) / EURUSD (forex) 2011-2012 / monthly, weekly, daily, 4h / 31m. Slides (bearish MWD sequential) for 3.5 min, then one long EURUSD walkthrough with levels carried from monthly down to 4h.

## Summary
Mirror of cc06_004 for shorts. Mark the low and open of every up-close candle (bearish OB) plus the mean threshold on the monthly and weekly charts, carry them down to the daily and 4h, and sell each rally into them while the bearish sequential holds. Most 4h shorts at these levels paid; a few stopped out. Monthly/weekly levels give fewer but stronger reactions and can be hit several times. He says next lessons build on this, ending with the "million dollar swing model" in lesson 8.

## Rules
- A timeframe counts as bearish when a premium array has "induced selling": price displaced lower off it ~0:46-1:28 [new]
- In a bear sequential, every up candle becomes new resistance and little strength shows ~1:28-1:34, ~24:08-24:37 [chart] [new]
- Case A, monthly + weekly + daily all bearish: sell every daily bearish premium array (old high or low, rejection block above the bodies, bearish OB, FVG, liquidity void, bearish breaker, bearish mitigation block) and every 4h one ~1:34-2:20 [new]
- Case B, monthly + weekly bearish, daily bullish (correcting): sell daily premium arrays at or nested in weekly arrays; sell 4h arrays at daily and/or weekly arrays. Avoid it if the daily just made a lower low and rejected (bullish breaker) ~2:20-3:04 [chart] [new]
- Case C, monthly bearish, weekly + daily bullish: sell daily premium arrays at or nested in monthly arrays; sell 4h arrays at weekly and/or monthly arrays. Avoid it if the weekly made a lower low and rejected (bullish breaker) ~3:04-3:40 [chart] [new]
- Levels to mark on each timeframe: the low and the open of every up-close candle before a down move (bearish OB) and its mean threshold ~4:06-5:29 [chart] [new]
- Several consecutive up candles form one OB. Measure the range with the bodies only (no wicks) and take its mean threshold ~5:45-6:24, ~14:13-14:24 [new] [conflict] with Ep 02 (MT measured high to low, wicks included)
- A small up candle whose body sits inside a larger up candle is not its own OB; use the bigger-bodied candle ~19:02-19:28 [new]
- Mitigation block (bearish): the last down candle before a rally that later fails. Price returns to it so trapped longs can exit, then drops ~11:39-12:46 [chart] [new]
- In the premium half of the range, an old-high false break is a sell point ~16:19-16:57 [new]
- In a consolidation the stops sit outside the range; expect raids beyond it ~16:03-16:19
- Each leg down seeks the next discount array (bullish OB, void/gap below, rejection block, old low) as the objective ~10:05-10:29 [new]
- Take profits at a logical point (e.g. 4h equal lows) even inside a swing ~21:30-21:53 [new]
- Holding time: two weeks to a month. A 3-day winner "didn't fit the time criteria" ~21:21, ~22:24-22:30 [new]
- Higher-timeframe levels can be traded to several times because big positions are built there. Allow some trading through them, e.g. to the mean threshold ~29:36-30:30 [new]
- After a stop-out at a valid monthly/weekly layered level, take the next setup; don't drop the idea ~30:42-31:07 [new]
- The more monthly/weekly/daily/4h levels converge at one price, the more sensitive it is. Weight monthly and weekly most ~28:42-29:11 [new]
- 4h entries cut risk versus daily; daily setups are fewer but stronger ~26:47-28:28 [new]

## Definitions
- Bearish OB: up-close candle(s) right before a down move; reference its low and open, plus MT ~4:06, ~5:15
- Bearish mitigation block: the down candle before a failed rally; price returns there for trapped longs to get out ~11:39-12:46
- Premium PD arrays here: old high/low, rejection block above the bodies, bearish OB, FVG, liquidity void, bearish breaker, mitigation block ~1:52
