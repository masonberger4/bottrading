## Summary
Swing longs are only taken in a "bullish monthly-weekly-daily (MWD) sequential": the higher timeframes must already show displacement up off a discount array. Which timeframe supplies the buy level depends on which timeframes are correcting. The daily and 4h discount arrays (mainly the high and open of down-close candles) are the entry points; 4h is the "executable" timeframe that cuts risk. A fib "expansion" of the dealing range marks premium/discount; buy only in discount. Holding time 2 weeks to about a month.

## Rules
- A timeframe counts as bullish when a discount array there "has shown to induce buying": price displaced higher off it (OB, old high/low, FVG/void, breaker) ~1:02-2:14 [chart] [new]
- In a bull sequential, on every timeframe down candles become new support and little weakness shows ~2:19 [chart] [new]
- Case A, monthly + weekly + daily all bullish (higher highs and higher lows): buy every daily bullish discount array and every 4h one, each time price trades down to it ~2:31-3:34 [chart] [new]
  - Daily discount arrays listed: bullish mitigation block, bullish breaker, liquidity void, FVG, bullish OB, rejection block, old low or high ~2:51-3:02 [chart]
- Case B, monthly + weekly bullish, daily correcting: buy daily discount arrays only at or "nested" inside weekly discount arrays; buy 4h discount arrays at daily and/or weekly arrays. The weekly level is the requirement; a daily array there is optional ~3:41-5:43 [chart] [new]
  - Avoid (case B): the daily made a higher high, was rejected and broke structure, i.e. a bearish breaker is forming ~5:51-6:11 [chart] [new]
- Case C, monthly bullish, weekly + daily correcting: buy daily discount arrays only at or nested in monthly discount arrays; buy 4h arrays at weekly and/or monthly arrays. Wait for price to reach the monthly level ~6:20-7:18, ~15:22-17:10 [chart] [new]
  - Avoid (case C): the weekly made a higher high, was rejected, bearish breaker ~7:23-7:36 [chart] [new]
- A correcting monthly does not cancel its bullishness; only a bearish breaker does ~14:47-14:53, ~28:30-29:06 [new]
- Entry levels are exact prices, not zones: the high and the open of each down-close candle (bullish OB) on daily and 4h; for multi-candle OBs use the bodies, not the wicks ~18:12-19:28, ~20:43 [chart] [new]
- Use the OB high as the "fair" level; the open is the conservative one ~13:07-13:17 [new]
- With price above a level, don't expect the exact level; a nearby round number may do (15.56 high -> 15.60) ~11:28-11:50 [new]
- Confirmation of real buying on the 4h: a new higher high after the bounce. If the bounce fails to make one, wait for the next lower (daily) level ~21:34-22:32 [chart] [new]
- Buying below an old low is also a discount array: the sell stops there are the counterparty ~26:41-27:03 [new]
- Stops: put them beyond the sell-side liquidity (the prior swing low), not right under the level; it's swing trading ~21:17-21:28 [new]
- Targets: monthly/weekly premium arrays above (liquidity voids, bearish breakers). First objective was a daily liquidity void ~27:43-28:19 [chart] [new]
- Premium/discount: draw a fib expansion from the dealing-range low to the high (0 = low, 100 = high), levels 0, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 (equilibrium), 0.8, 0.9, 1. Below 0.5 is discount, above is premium ~31:39-34:00 [chart] [new]
- 0.3 is deep discount, 0.2 the deepest; 0.8 deep premium, 0.9 very expensive. No new longs up there and no new shorts down there ~34:27-34:53 [chart] [new]
- Bullish OBs in the premium half give only small pops and no new high; in discount they drive real expansion. Bearish OBs hold in premium and fail in discount ~35:06-38:15 [chart] [new]
- Holding time: two weeks or more, preferably two weeks to a month ~30:07-30:15 [new]
- Part-time execution: check the 4h chart a couple of times a day, about an hour before the kill zones ~31:13-31:21 [new]
- Swing setups must be rule-based; an OB or old low alone does not make a swing trade without the sequential ~38:37-38:56 [new]
- Monthly/weekly/daily levels are few, so institutions all wait for them, which is why they are sensitive ~39:06-39:20

## Trade examples
1. Silver monthly (~9:00-12:20) [chart]: 2015-16 rally broke swing highs (Apr 2016 broke a key high), so the monthly is bullish. Dealing range low 14.00 (the level it keyed off) to high ~21.1 (Jul 2016). Monthly bullish OB = last big down candle, high 15.56, rounded to 15.60.
2. Weekly (~12:36-16:16) [chart]: weekly OB = last two down candles before the surge (around Mar 2016), line ~15.78. From Jul 2016 the weekly and daily were bearish (case C). Price fell into the 15.60 monthly level in Dec 2016 (low ~15.6, below an old low and rejection block, sweeping a short-term low) and rallied away.
3. Daily and 4h (~16:30-23:35) [chart]: after the Dec low, short black lines mark each daily down candle's high and open (~16.2 up to ~17.8). Blue 4h lines read 15.60, 15.78, 16.31, 16.55, 16.76, 17.18, 17.37. In late Jan 2017 price broke through a 4h OB but held the daily OB (~16.6), then made a new high. The final daily-OB retest (~17.5, early Feb) led to the push to ~17.99 on Fri 10 Feb (futures ~18.02).
4. Target logic (~28:07-29:20) [chart]: daily bearish breaker from Nov 2016, low ~17.97-17.98, upper line ~18.98. 1798/1800 was the first objective (a liquidity void closed). Only if the daily keeps rising would he look higher (19.20s, 22s) off monthly/weekly premium arrays.
No entry/stop/size was given. The "1600 to 1800" move is described as about $10k profit potential (per contract, presumably) ~25:33-25:40.

## Definitions
- MWD sequential: the bullish/bearish state of the monthly, weekly and daily charts together, which decides which timeframe supplies tradable levels ~1:02, ~4:44
- "Nested": a lower-timeframe discount array sitting at or inside a higher-timeframe one ~4:01-4:42
- Executable timeframe: the 4h for swing trades ~19:43
- Fib expansion as a PD-array map: anchor low to high, drag the second point back to the low ~31:39-32:35

## Open questions
- "Bullish timeframe" has no mechanical test. Candidate: the last structure break on that timeframe was upward with displacement, and no bearish breaker since.
- Correcting timeframe: a lower high/lower low on that timeframe, or a close below the last daily up-move's OB? Not defined.
- "No new longs in these levels": above 0.5, or only above 0.8/0.9? The words point at 0.8-0.9; the coloring says above 0.5.
- Which dealing range: the monthly swing low to high was used for all timeframes here. A rule for when to re-anchor is not given.
- Entry: limit at the OB high, or at the open? Stop distance and position size are not stated in this lesson.
- Exit is vague: the "next premium array"; no partial-profit rule here (see the cc05 position-management notes for the 20/40-day trailing stop).
- Asset is silver (FX/CFD feed). Applying it to ES/SPY needs the same monthly/weekly/daily levels on index data.
