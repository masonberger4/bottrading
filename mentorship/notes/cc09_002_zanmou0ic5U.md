## Summary
IPDA tends to deliver the daily range across about four "numbers" from the entry. Four interchangeable measuring tools: 0 GMT floor-trader pivots (with midpoints), Central Bank Dealers Range (CBDR) standard deviations, Asian range standard deviations, and the Flout (15:00-00:00 NY range, projected in half-range steps). Previous-day high/low is always a candidate. Which tool IPDA is using becomes clear during London/NY; the daily high/low is where projections converge with a HTF PD array and time of day.

## Rules
- Previous day's high or low: expect one of them to be traded to most days (not guaranteed when the day's range is small); also watch the last 3 days' highs/lows ~0:54-1:40 [chart] (matches Ep 08 liquidity matrix)
- Pivot ladder (0 GMT pivots): S3, M0, S2, M1, S1, M2, central pivot, M3, R1, M4, R2, M5, R3; each M = 50% between adjacent pivots ~1:59-4:36 [chart] [new]
- Pivots are "staged orders"; IPDA trades to and through them. Retail buys S1/S2; once the range expands down, a retrace to S1/S2 can be a continuation short. Used for range extent, not entries ~3:02-3:44, ~27:06-27:21 [new]
- Count from YOUR entry: long -> the next four sequential levels above; short -> four below. Example: short near R2 -> M4, R1, M3, CP ~5:40-6:20 [chart] [new]
- Four is a rule of thumb; large-range days fill more ~6:23-6:39 [chart] [new]
- Exits: take ~75-80% off once four levels fill; keep 25-30% for a possible large-range day (e.g. London already filled four, NY may extend), if time allows ~6:39-7:24 [chart] [new]
- CBDR (short): sell above the CBDR low; CBDR low = level 1; each further CBDR-range standard deviation below = next level; expect four. Long mirror: buy below the CBDR, CBDR high = level 1 ~7:30-10:22 [chart] [new]
- Range edges may be taken on wicks or on bodies (highest/lowest open-close); compute both because retail feeds differ ~8:03-8:11, ~12:31-12:44 [new]
- Asian range (long): buy below the Asian range, Asian high = level 1, stack Asian-range-sized projections above, expect four. Short mirror ~10:48-11:33 [chart] [new]
- Flout: range from 15:00 to 00:00 NY; split at its equilibrium into two half ranges. Short only above flout equilibrium; flout low = level 1; each further half-range below = next level. Long mirror (buy below EQ, flout high = level 1). Project the HALF range, never the full range ~11:50-15:10, ~22:24-23:09 [chart] [new]
- Choosing a tool: unknown before the day; London into NY reveals which measurements IPDA is using; check all four, don't keep a favourite ~16:08-16:40, ~21:13-21:30 [chart] [new]
- Daily high/low = confluence of one or more projections + time of day (time left to get there) + direction + a HTF (daily/4h) PD array ~17:01-17:18, ~20:20-20:46, ~23:27-23:48 [chart] [new]
- Bearish day: expect the Judas rally at/after the midnight NY candle, then measure down; bullish mirror, ideally buying after a protraction below the Asian low ~19:01-19:19, ~23:15-23:26
- Delivery timing varies: London may do ~80% of the range, or the whole range may come 07:00 to London close (~11:00 ET) ~19:39-19:59 [new]
- Get out ahead of a projected level: take the nearest round number before it ~26:36-26:43 [new] (consistent with Ep 08 "exit before the level")
- Precision needs volatility/displacement; quiet markets do not fill the numbers cleanly ~28:36-28:50 [new]
- Specialise in one market plus one correlated secondary ~21:47-22:08

## Definitions
- Filling the numbers: IPDA's tendency to trade through ~4 measured levels per day from the entry ~0:30-0:54 [chart]
- Staged orders: resting buy/sell orders at popular pivot levels ~2:47-3:17
- Flout: the 15:00-00:00 NY range, measured in half-range (equilibrium) steps; more to come later in the month ~12:04-13:41 [chart]
