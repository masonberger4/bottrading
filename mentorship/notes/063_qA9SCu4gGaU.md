## Rules
- A bias is only a condition; no trade unless the framework appears in price: displacement in
  the bias direction, an FVG left behind, a retrace into it, at a valid time of day. No setup =
  no loss; being "wrong" in opinion without a trade costs nothing ~12:22-15:47 [new]
- Neutral stance = take no new trades and wait for price to show its hand ~5:19, ~10:24
  (consistent with the Ep 08 neutral-bias rule)
- If-then planning: e.g. "if DXY trades below this low and back up, treat the inefficiency as
  resistance and target the lower relative equal lows"; the plan only activates on that condition
  ~7:50-8:22 [new]
- FX trade windows for this: London open, NY open, London close kill zones ~13:22-13:35
- DXY is a risk-on/off barometer; index futures can ignore it, FX nearly cannot ~10:53-11:15
- Breaker on the daily: range uses the lowest close of the down-close candles (bodies), not
  the wick; bodies carry the narrative, wicks do the damage ~3:49-4:06 [new] [chart]
- Wick CE as a PD array: 50% of a large single candle's wick (the "wick discount CE" on the 1h)
  is an algorithmic reference even with no FVG, old high or S/R there; price can sweep relative
  equal lows down into it and then run buy-side ~21:57-24:21 [new] [chart]
- Which wick: the one below a consolidation whose top is relative equal highs (buy-side) and that
  sits under the weekly gap; he does not give a general selection rule ~23:00-23:14 [chart]
- Smooth relative equal highs that retail sees as resistance are a target ("smooth edges get
  made jagged") ~23:14-23:31, ~25:14-25:43 (matches Ep 04)
- Fast immediate rebalances of FVGs signal price wants to reach the next liquidity/inefficiency
  quickly ~20:22-20:35 [new]
- Running down equity: once ~3/4 of the way to the objective, every new short-term high that is
  run (when long) is a partial; take partials there ~26:23-26:52 [new detail vs Ep 03]
- Exit limit orders a couple of ticks before the target level to allow for the spread (target
  4290.50, exit order at 4290) ~29:41-30:01 (matches Ep 08 "0.5-1 point before")
- Pyramiding on a bullish breaker: high, low, higher high; when price trades above the high and
  retraces into it, add ~28:37-28:59 [chart]
