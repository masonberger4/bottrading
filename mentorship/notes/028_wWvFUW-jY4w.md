- NWOG: measure Friday close to Sunday open, mark the high, low and CE (midpoint) and carry all three until Friday's close; price keeps referring back to them ~00:11:19-00:12:09 [new]
- ES regular-hours gap: previous session's close to the 09:30 open is also a liquidity void and a PD array ~00:35:46-00:36:09 [new]

- Short stubby wick (small vs the candle's high-low range): price often probes only to its CE, retraces, and goes through later. A long wick in the bias direction often stops dead at its CE and reverses ~00:14:19-00:16:05 [chart] [new]

## Definitions
- Short stubby wick: wick that is small relative to the candle's full range ~00:14:41-00:15:17.

- True liquidity void = a session/week opening gap (no trading at all). The large one-sided candle often called a "liquidity void" is really a sell-side imbalance (SIBI) because trades did occur in it ~00:36:17-00:37:30. [conflict] with Ep 01's "liquidity void" wording.
