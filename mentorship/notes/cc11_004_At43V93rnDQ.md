## Summary
Bonds tend to put in a seasonal low in May-June and rally into Sep-Oct "fall highs". A long "mega-trade" is qualified by interest-rate SMT, meaning a divergence at the low between the 5-yr, 10-yr and 30-yr. The entry comes from a higher-timeframe bullish PD array in discount after a sell-stop run. He then walks through 2008-2017 Sept/Dec contracts: most years paid out, but 2012 and 2013 were duds.

## Rules
- Seasonal: 30-yr bonds are weak early in the year into a May-June low, which is the main yearly long setup. Not every year; do not force it. ~00:00:54-00:02:19 [chart] [new]
- Secondary seasonal: Sep-Oct-Nov short-term rallies (short-term or swing trades only). ~00:03:00 [new]
- Mega-trade process (slide): 1) wait for the May-June bond low, 2) identify institutional order flow / HTF PDA, 3) let bonds move to discount, 4) scan daily for interest-rate SMT, 5) if long, target the fall highs, 6) do not over-trade bonds because they trend. ~00:05:42-00:07:29 [chart] [new]
- Interest-rate SMT: the 5-, 10- and 30-yr should move together. If any one fails to make the lower low that the others make, that divergence shows smart money accumulating. One diverging is enough. ~00:11:38-00:12:41, 00:13:33 [chart] [new]. This is the same idea as the NQ/ES SMT in the rules file, applied to three correlated instruments.
- SMT can be read on 3-point swing lows (lowest in the middle) rather than single lows. Sometimes price must trade through a short-term low before the divergence shows up (2011). ~00:27:30-00:29:11 [new]
- Entry framing: price must be in discount of the HTF range, have run sell stops below short-term lows, and be trading into a bullish order block or FVG. That is the discount array where the SMT forms. ~00:18:54-00:19:30, 00:24:42-00:25:34 [chart]
- Roll from the Sept to the Dec contract to capture more of the move. ~00:20:52
- Bond trend direction also sets the bias for day trades in the same direction. ~00:07:47 [new, general]
- Threshold: a mega-trade is a move of at least 10 handles (about $10k per ZB contract, $1k per full point). Less than that is just a good trade. ~00:40:23, 00:42:41

## Definitions
- Interest-rate SMT: divergence at swing lows (or highs) among the 5-yr (short-term yield), 10-yr (intermediate) and 30-yr (long-term) treasuries. ~00:12:58
- Mega-trade (bonds): an annual seasonal swing of at least $10k per contract. ~00:42:41
