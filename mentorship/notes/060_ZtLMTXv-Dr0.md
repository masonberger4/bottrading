# 060 ICT Mentorship 2023 - Immediate Rebalance & Institutional Order Flow (ZtLMTXv-Dr0)
Thursday May 25 2023 (recorded that evening, before Memorial Day weekend). DXY weekly/daily,
EURUSD daily/5m/1m, ES (ESM2023) 15m/5m/1m/15s/10s. Duration ~1:25.

## Summary
Defines the "immediate rebalance" (a daily candle opens and trades back exactly to the prior
high/low, leaving no gap) as a strong continuation signature (DXY up, EURUSD down). Introduces
RTH opening range gaps (kept 3 days) and the new day opening gap, then dissects the ES AM and PM
Silver Bullet trades on 1m and sub-minute charts as "institutional order flow": entries at
FVG/OB pockets visible only below 1m, and balanced vs unbalanced ranges deciding where price returns.

## Rules
- Immediate rebalance (bullish): new daily candle opens, trades down to a previous day's high to the tick and no further, leaving no FVG between them; then expect fast continuation up. Bearish mirror: opens, trades up to a prior day's low, then down (EURUSD) ~07:20-08:40, ~10:20 [new] [chart]
- If the retrace stops short of the prior high, the FVG left is a normal deferred inefficiency / breakaway gap; an immediate rebalance is the stronger signal ~07:50 [new]
- Bias read from immediate rebalance on DXY carries inversely to EURUSD (risk-off: dollar up, FX down) ~09:30 [new]
- Bearish day: extend the NY midnight open line to ~11:00; any return to it before the objective is met is a short (best shorts at/above midnight open) ~15:20, ~17:30 [chart] (extends Ep04/06)
- Opening range gap (ORG): on an RTH-only chart, the gap between the prior session settlement (16:15 close) and the 09:30 open. Track each ORG for 3 days, then remove it ~30:20, ~32:40, ~50:20 [new] [chart]
- ORG levels (high, low, CE) act as support/resistance; bodies respect them while wicks pierce ~31:00, ~38:40, ~46:00 [new] [chart]
- If price trades above a bearish-viewed gap and holds, treat it as an inversion FVG (bullish) ~06:10, ~28:10 [new]
- New day opening gap (NDOG): 17:00 close to 18:00 open on ES; its CE and edges are reaction levels ~37:50 [new] [chart]
- CE = half of any gap/inefficiency; mean threshold = half of an order block; keep them distinct ~38:10 (consistent)
- Silver Bullet: entry inside 10:00-11:00 (AM), 14:00-15:00 (PM), also 03:00-04:00 (London); the FVG may have formed before the window, only the entry must be inside it ~56:50, ~1:21:10 [new]
- Silver Bullet filter (index futures): the entry must offer at least 10 handles to the next draw; fill assumed when price prints 1 tick through the level ~50:00, ~53:00, ~1:12:30 [new]
- AM blend with the 2022 model: sell-side swept, quick MSS (short-term high taken), buy the FVG in the window ~46:40 [chart]
- PM short: after the day's buy-side objective is hit during 14:00, sell the first SIBI on the break lower; target the nearest real liquidity void/FVG, not the low ~1:09:00-1:15:00 [new] [chart]
- Partials: book 5 handles, then 10, then aim at the void; trail the stop under the latest swing low once price reaches a key level ~54:10, ~1:15:00
- Pyramid 6, 3, 1 (10-lot model) on sub-1m FVG/OB retests ~1:03:00 (Ep04 used 6,3,2,1)
- Too-close second gap: skip an FVG that sits right above a liquidity pool (price can run that pool without reaching it); look lower TF instead ~1:10:30 [new]
- Balanced price range: once an inefficiency gets two-way delivery (up and down candles) and price leaves it, price need not return below its high; unbalanced single-pass pockets are where it returns, often to the tick ~1:05:30-1:08:40 [chart] (refines Ep01)
- Re-entry: if stopped and the setup still stands, re-enter on the next sub-1m signal ~1:22:30
- Short entries only above equilibrium of the leg (inception to terminus); sub-1m OB entry with stop above the FVG candle ~1:23:00-1:24:20 [new]
- Thursday often makes the high (or low) of the week after a one-sided week ~1:11:50 [new]

## Definitions
- Immediate rebalance: open-to-prior-high (or low) retrace that leaves zero gap, ~08:00.
- SIBI = sell-side imbalance, buy-side inefficiency (needs up-delivery); BISI the mirror ~05:50.
- Real liquidity void: open space between two candles with no prints at all; distinct from an FVG ~1:09:40.
- Mitigation block: down-close candle between a high and a lower high (breaker variant with no new high) ~59:30.
- Breaker range: use bodies; "wicks do the damage" so discard the wick ~26:40.
- IOFED: partial entry into a gap that does not completely fill ~25:30 (consistent).
- Institutional order flow: reading FVG/OB/balance on sub-1m charts, not volume/DOM ~58:00.
- Three drives pattern: three pushes into a 1m FVG then break ~1:20:00.
