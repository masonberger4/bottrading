# cc09_005 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 09 - Trading Market Reversals (KrGTimL3YRQ)
Lesson 5 of the May 2017 "ICT Amplified Day Trading & Scalping" month / FX-centred (pips, EURUSD) but generic / slides with daily and intraday line/candle charts, no prices or instruments legible / 37 min. Slide lecture only, no live trade.

## Summary
ICT lists eight intraday reversal setups: raids on the previous day's high/low, intra-week high/low, intermediate-term high/low, plus New York session reversals and London close reversals. None is traded on the raid alone: each needs higher-timeframe (HTF) order flow and a HTF PD array at the raided level. New York is a continuation of London by default unless NY opens into an unmet HTF PD array.

## Rules
- The eight tradable reversals: PDH raid then short; PDL raid then long; intra-week high/low raid; intermediate-term high/low raid; NY session reversal; London close reversal ~00:29, ~12:00 [chart] [new]
- Never fade an old high/low just because price traded beyond it. It needs confluence: a HTF PD array (daily FVG, weekly OB, etc.) at or just beyond the level, plus HTF institutional order flow (weekly/daily/4h) the same way ~03:12, ~17:15, ~18:14 [chart]. The general idea is already in the rules (Ep 08: an old daily level alone is not an entry). The multi-tier version is [new].
- PDL long (or mirror PDH short): only inside a larger expansion swing in your bias direction. During a normal retracement against it, price trades under the PDL into a discount array (example: a FVG) and then gets bought. Bearish mirror: in a down expansion, a retracement fills a FVG/premium array, then trades above the PDH and finds sellers ~15:10-18:56 [chart] [new]
- The candle that dips below the prior candle's low into the FVG can be the buy. Think "buy below that candle's low" when a FVG sits just below ~16:15 [chart] [new]
- Every day after the close, check how today traded against the last 3 days' highs/lows (today counts as day 1) ~14:22-14:50. This is already in the rules as the Ep 08 liquidity matrix.
- Intra-week high short: the week is already trending up, and a HTF premium array not yet reached is hit on Tue/Wed/Thu. The same move runs the earlier intra-week high (e.g. Tuesday's high taken Wednesday, and that becomes the high of the week) ~04:04-05:20 [new]. Mirror for intra-week low longs (low on Mon-Thu run into a HTF discount array) ~05:22-06:55 [new]
- Equal highs/lows inside the week are prime targets for the raid; ICT says a run of 10-20 pips beyond collects the stops ~19:36-19:52 [chart]. FX units; for ES we would need our own buffer.
- Equal highs can simply be a target for a larger move higher. Only fade them when the market is neutral or consolidating, or due to turn, AND a premium array sits just above ~20:00-20:27 [new]
- Overlapping reversal types (e.g. intra-week high raid that happens at the NY session) make the setup stronger ~20:32-21:01 [new]
- Intermediate-term high/low = older than yesterday: intra-week, previous week, up to 1-2 months back ~07:00, ~23:11 [chart]. Fade them best in range-bound/consolidating markets, which ICT says occur more often than trends ~22:54, ~25:46 [chart] [new]. Ask whether the raid is for exiting positions (profit taking after a long trend) or for trapping traders before a reversal ~07:55-09:44.
- New York session: by default it continues London's direction ~09:47-10:19 [new]. A NY reversal is expected only when price reaches an unmet HTF discount (or premium) array during London's move and at the NY open; then NY makes the day's low and closes above all of London's range (mirror for bearish) ~10:36-11:05, ~29:08-31:08 [chart] [new]
- London close (10:00-12:00 NY) scalp: only on large-range days. Today's range must be at least about 125-130% of the 5-day ADR (e.g. ADR 100 pips, today 125-160) ~11:16-12:22 [new]. Expect a retrace of about 20% of the day's total range between 10:00 and noon NY ~32:26-32:37 [chart] [new]. Skip it if the range is below the 5-day ADR or the move was not explosive and one-sided ~12:41-12:55.
- London close can also start a multi-day reversal at a HTF PD array, read with the weekly templates ~32:38-32:51 [chart]
- After a NY-session reversal, London close may come back and run the NY low or high once more (a "second swipe" of stops). ICT re-enters there, buying below the NY low for a bullish NY reversal ~33:11-34:24 [new]
- Day trading needs displacement: you are fighting time to get paid before the close ~24:34-24:47
- Judas references named: London open, the CME open (NY Judas), and Asia at 20:00 NY (00:00 GMT); London close on reversal days ~28:03-28:25

## Definitions
- Intra-week high/low: the highest high or lowest low made so far in the current week (Mon-Thu) ~03:28
- Intermediate-term high/low: a swing older than yesterday, from earlier in the week up to 1-2 months back ~23:11
- Large range day: today's range exceeds the 5-day average daily range (ADR). ICT prefers at least 1.25-1.33x ~11:19
- "Candyland": equal highs/lows, an obvious pool of retail stops ~19:37
- Turtle soup: a false break above an old high or below an old low. These reversals are turtle soup plus context ~15:30
