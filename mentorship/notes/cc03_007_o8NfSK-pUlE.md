# cc03_007 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 03 - Market Maker Trap Trendline Phantoms (o8NfSK-pUlE)
Teaching 7 of 8, November 2016 mentorship (Core Content month 3). Market: GBPUSD (forex; captions
say "52 34", "150 60", "143.85" = 1.5234, 1.5060, 1.4385). Timeframes: daily with 15m and 30m.
Duration ~24 min. Examples: Dec 11-18 2015 and May 9-18 2016.

## Summary
Diagonal trendlines (support through higher lows, resistance through lower highs) have no edge;
price only seeks liquidity. ICT fades the obvious third touch: when the higher timeframe disagrees
with the trendline, he looks for an order block or turtle soup at the swing between touches 2 and 3
and targets the stops beyond touch-point 2. Concepts apply to index futures; examples are forex.

## Rules
- Diagonal trendline support/resistance is not used for entries or bias; treat a very obvious
  trendline as a trap (liquidity for the market maker) ~2:06, ~9:20. [new]
- Up-trendline trap (sell scenario): with higher TF not bullish, at the third touch look to sell at
  the swing high formed between touch 2 and touch 3, via a bearish order block, a lower-TF bearish
  breaker, or a turtle soup (brief poke above that high) ~8:01-9:20 [chart] [new]
- Its target: below touch-point 2 (the second trendline low), where long stops sit ~11:34-11:46 [new]
- Down-trendline trap (buy scenario), mirror: with higher TF bullish, buy at a bullish order block at
  the low between highs 2 and 3; accept a small dip into that low or a break just below it (turtle
  soup / sell-stop run). Target the buy stops above high #2 ~10:08-11:32 [new]
- Context comes from higher-TF "institutional reference points" on the daily chart (daily OB,
  daily FVG/liquidity void), framed like a bank would ~14:48-15:40, ~24:15 [chart]
- Daily efficiency: a range delivered only one way (all down candles / one pass) must be
  rebalanced in the other direction; price returns to the last opposite candle (daily OB) ~24:00 [chart]
- Bullish OB validated when the following candle trades above the down candle's high; later
  retrace into its body near mean threshold is the buy ~22:13-22:36. Consistent with existing OB/MT rules.
- Stop for the trap short: above the old high behind the setup (higher-TF swing) ~19:00
- Limit entry at/just around the OB level (e.g. buy at the level or a few pips above/below) ~20:42

## Trade examples
1. GBPUSD, Dec 11 2015. Daily: bearish swing from a high leaves an FVG; reference level 1.5234 (low of
   the candle above the gap). Price rallies into 1.5234 on Dec 11 (15m) [chart, red line 1.5234,
   cyan box on daily]. Daily target below: 1.5060, the high of a down candle with only one upside
   pass (second cyan box). On 15m/30m, retail trendlines through higher lows; each third touch only
   retraces into the last up candle (bearish OB) or its mean threshold, then a turtle soup above an
   old high, then sell-off through the trendline to ~1.5060 by Dec 15-17 [chart; 30m shows a drop to
   ~1.487]. Stop above the old high.
2. GBPUSD, May 9-11 2016 (30m). Daily level 1.4385 (open of down candles, rounded from 1.4384).
   Lower highs form a down-trendline; third touch at 1.4385 = buy (turtle soup / bullish OB) at
   1.4385-1.4390 limit or 1.4380 or lower. Target buy stops above high #2 (label on chart "Buy Stops
   Taken For Long Sale Exit" ~1.4478) [chart]. After the buy-stop raid, lower prices allowed.
3. GBPUSD, May 18 2016 (15m). Daily bullish OB: down candle open 1.4446, mean threshold ~1.4400.
   Descending triangle / down-trendline (blue shaded) with false breakdown, then rally to the 1.4600
   big figure = daily last up candle (OB) above [chart: lines 1.4600, 1.4446, 1.4400, 1.4406].

## Definitions
- Trendline phantom: an imaginary diagonal line through higher lows / lower highs that retail
  extends forward and treats as support/resistance ~0:45-2:00.
- Big figure: the round 100-pip level (e.g. 1.4600) ~22:31, ~23:26 (forex).
- Market efficiency paradigm: delivery must be offered both on the buy side and the sell side; a
  one-sided move gets rebalanced ~16:24, ~24:00.
