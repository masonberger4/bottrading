# cc09_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 09 - The Sentiment Effect (_TNhWRPa6GA)
May 2017 content, lesson 1 of "ICT Amplified Day Trading & Scalping" (~0:11) / forex: EURUSD, USDCHF / daily and 15m (M15) with a 10-period Williams %R pane / 12m. Slides plus annotated charts from the week ending Fri May 12 2017 (examples Thu May 11 and Fri May 12).

## Summary
Day-trade filter: with daily/4h institutional order flow and a daily/4h PD array already in play, wait for the intraday Judas move against that bias, i.e. a run below the midnight NY opening price and the Asian range low (longs) or above the opening price and Asian range high (shorts), into a 15m PD array, ideally while short-term sentiment (Williams %R) is at its most opposite extreme. Not every day qualifies.

## Rules
- Bias source: daily and/or 4h institutional order flow plus the PD array matrix for next objectives; enter only against the intraday move ("opposing market direction") ~1:26-1:47 [chart]
- Longs: buy below the opening price and/or the Asian range low; shorts: sell above the opening price and/or the Asian range high ~0:31-1:04, ~2:00-2:22 [chart] [new] (Asian range not in rules; opening-price part matches Ep 04/08)
- Opening price = midnight NY candle open in this lesson; the 0 GMT open can be used if the range from 0 GMT has been small/consolidating ~2:24-2:50 [chart]
- Long checklist ~3:22-5:52 [chart] [new]:
  1. Daily or minimum 4h discount array is in play: price recently traded into it and showed a reaction (support).
  2. Sufficient room to the opposing daily/4h premium array: 50-60 pips ideal for a day trade, under ~40 pips is only a scalp.
  3. Price declines under the midnight opening price AND the Asian range low.
  4. Ideally that decline lands in a logical 15m discount array.
  5. Price should leave the 15m array sharply; the longer it hovers there, the lower the odds.
  6. Short-term sentiment is most bearish at the moment of the long entry.
- Short checklist: exact mirror (premium array in play, room to the opposing discount array, rally above opening price and Asian high into a 15m premium array, sharp rejection, sentiment most bullish at entry) ~7:57-9:00 [chart] [new]
- Sentiment gauge: 10-period Williams %R on the 15m chart, used only as a confluence with price, not as an overbought/oversold signal ~5:55-6:30 [chart] [new]
- Selectivity: the setup does not occur every day; day trading is not every-day trading. More trades = losses arrive sooner ~10:12-12:02 [new]
- Weekly template: weekly low expected to form on Thursday's NY session; sell stops taken around the 08:30 data (before the release) was read as pricing in the week's low ~6:33-7:01 [new]

## Definitions
- Sentiment effect: a short-term sentiment shift where less-informed traders chase the initial intraday swing (Judas swing) against the day's eventual direction; breakouts beyond the Asian range trap "street money" on the wrong side ~1:04-1:26, ~2:53-3:17 [chart]
- Asian range: high/low drawn between the 0 GMT (Forex Ltd) line and the midnight NY line on his chart ~5:40 [chart]
