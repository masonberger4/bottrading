## Summary
The "setup" is not a pattern: it is a fixed list of time windows whose highs and lows are the liquidity pools price runs to. Bias comes from the weekly draw. Wait for one pool to be raided against the bias, then enter with whatever PD array you prefer, and target the next pool on the other side. Shown with a 30-lot ES long on Jun 21 PM (lunch sell-side swept, target the opening range gap and lunch buy-side), +$9,362.50.

## Definitions
- PM session range: 13:30-16:00 high/low of the previous day, RTH ~0:13:00
- London session range: 02:00-05:00 high/low ~0:22:00
- NY lunch range: 12:00-13:30 high/low ~0:42:30
- AM session range: 9:30-12:00 high/low ~0:51:40
- Opening range gap: space between the RTH close and the next 9:30 open. The opening range is 9:30-10:00 ~0:30:00
- Rejection block: the lowest closing price of the swing ~1:05:30
- Inversion FVG (IFVG): an FVG price closes through, which then acts as support/resistance in the other direction (shown as support) ~1:18:00 [chart]
