## Rules
- FVG high/low edges are boundaries: candle bodies respecting an FVG's edge signals precision and raises the odds the level holds again when other factors agree ~3:02 [chart]
- A balanced price range (price passed both ways) can be revisited in full but is generally not traded through, especially with an FVG next to it ~6:18 [chart]
- Do not trade micro structure (1m and sub-1m) inside a small consolidation; micro structure is only usable when price is moving fast/displacing. Inside chop, wait for a specific time of day ~9:52-10:51 [new]
- Seek and Destroy day profile: morning consolidation that runs short-term highs and lows alternately, then a big run that fully reverses ("murder"), then climbs again. Do not hold trades long on such days ~13:57, ~19:27 [new]
- In consolidation (not trending), the NWOG's three levels (Sunday open, Friday close, and their midpoint CE) act as exact reaction levels; treat them as levels, not a zone ~18:37-19:11 [chart] [new]
- Reference levels are tagged by the candle time on a given timeframe, with charts in New York time; a level can be widened by recalibrating it from the 5m to the 15m FVG of the same move ~13:10, ~15:36, ~21:39 [chart]
- Entry on an FVG uses only two prices: CE (highest-probability) or the institutional order flow entry drill (at the gap's opening candle extreme or 1 tick beyond, "guarantees" a fill). No demand/supply zones ~23:19-24:11
- Trade filter: if aiming for 5 handles, the setup must offer the potential for ~10 handles in your favor (CE to the target PD array) ~25:26-25:54 [new]
- Study the last hour (15:00-16:00) in detail every day ~20:55
- The draw can be the NWOG low (Sunday opening price) ~17:47
