## Definitions
- New Week Opening Gap: Friday close to Sunday's first-tick open; the difference is the gap ~00:04:00.
- Consequent encroachment (C.E.): the midpoint of any gap/inefficiency ~00:08:21.
- Speed bump: a zone price will run through, not a breaker (no reversal meaning) ~00:00:30.
- Running down equity: taking partials into an extended move instead of holding bulk ~00:11:05.
