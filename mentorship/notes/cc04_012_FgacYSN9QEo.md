# cc04_012 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 04 - ICT Fair Value Gaps FVG (FgacYSN9QEo)
December 2016 teaching 6 of 8 ("Reinforcing Fair Value Gaps - Trading Inside the Range"). Market:
EURUSD (forex only). Timeframes: daily, 4h, 15m, 5m. Duration 18 min. Dates on chart: Dec 14-19 2016.

## Summary
Formal FVG definition, then a daily EURUSD bearish FVG (1.0475-1.0500) that is filled after a turtle
soup below an old low in a holiday range. Lower-TF section shows the FVG overlapping a run on buy
stops above an old high, and refining entries to body gaps (close-to-open) for a limit sell.

## Rules
- FVG: range where only one side of liquidity was offered; usually a liquidity void on lower TFs;
  price can literally gap ~0:35-0:53 [chart] [new wording; consistent with Ep 01]
- Bearish FVG bounds: candle 1 low to candle 3 high around the big down candle; the part of the down
  candle already traded up through by candle 1 (low to close) or candle 3 (open to high) is excluded
  ~1:52-4:46 [chart] (same as Ep 01 SIBI)
- An FVG exists on the timeframe you study; on lower TFs it shows as a multi-candle liquidity void ~5:07-5:30 [new]
- Expect price to come back to fill an open FVG; reversed for bullish gaps below price ~6:15-6:25, ~8:50-8:57
- Range-bound conditions (e.g. late December holidays): trade turtle soups beyond old lows/highs
  toward FVGs and equal highs/lows above/below ~7:01-8:07 [new]
- Confluence: run on an old high (buy stops) that also trades into a higher-TF FVG = sell ~10:32-10:50 [chart] [new]
- Entry refinement: a gap between one candle's close and the next one's open (volume imbalance)
  inside the FVG gives an exact limit price; sell on a limit there, or at market as it trades there ~12:27-13:08 [chart] [new]
- Bodies, not wicks, define the fill: the up candle's body perfectly closed the body gap between two
  down candles = "efficiency" ~13:08-13:36
- Once a range has been delivered both ways (up and down), it is efficient; the last point where
  buy-side was offered (a low-to-close or open-to-high) is the next sell level; sell there and target
  sell stops below lows ~15:09-17:34 [chart] [new]

## Trade examples
- EURUSD daily, Dec 2016: down candle FVG from 1.0500 down to 1.0475 (~25 pips); 4h turtle soup
  below an old low, equal highs just below the FVG; filled in about two days, >100 pips ~4:40-8:12 [chart].
- EURUSD 15m/5m, Dec 19 2016: second run into 1.0475; old high 1.0477, candle high 1.0478 (one pip
  through). Body gap 1.0472 close to 1.0470 open = limit sell at 1.0470 ~12:02-12:53.
- Later sell at 1.0455 (also 1.0450), target below 1.0415-1.0410 sell stops; price hit 1.0455 twice then
  ran the lows ~15:37-17:31 [chart: 15m lines at 1.0475 and 1.0455].

## Definitions
- Liquidity void: several candles creating open space; the lower-TF view of a higher-TF FVG ~5:22
- Efficiency: both buy-side and sell-side delivered across a range ("full block of delivery") ~16:46
