## Summary
Open float = all resting orders (buy stops above, sell stops below) around current price. The
liquidity pools to track are above/below short-term (weekly/monthly) highs/lows, the 3-month
high/low (quarterly shift), and the 6- and 12-month highs/lows. Which side price is seeking is
read from the "tug of war": if each run above a short-term high gains no new ground while each
break of a short-term low extends lower (lower intermediate-term highs), the market is drawing
to sell-side, giving daily bias.

## Rules
- Track liquidity pools for open float: above/below short-term highs/lows (weekly, monthly),
  the highest high/lowest low of the last 3 months, the current 6-month and 12-month high/low
  (~2:17-5:12) [chart: slide] [new].
- Shorts' protective buy stops sit above the last bearish shift; longs' sell stops below the
  last bullish shift (~2:24, ~4:42) [chart: slide] [new].
- Quarterly shift: every 3 months note the 3-month high/low; expect a significant run on
  liquidity roughly each quarter; a market structure shift points at the 3-month extreme
  (~3:52-4:23, ~9:28-9:38) [chart] [new].
- After a bearish daily structure shift, look straight at the high it came from: that high
  holds a buy-stop pool (funds' protective stops) (~7:58-8:12) [new].
- Directional bias test [new]: compare each stop run. Bearish if rallies take short-term highs
  but fail to make new ground (rejected immediately) while each break of a short-term low gains
  distance; intermediate-term highs keep getting lower. Mirror for bullish (~14:28-18:50).
- While price trends to one side's liquidity, the other side's pools are left alone (no sell
  stops taken on the whole rally 1.0534 to 1.15) (~13:28-13:44).
- First clue of the flip: after the big buy-stop raid, the first short-term low with sell
  stops gets violated (~13:47-14:33) [chart].
- Price at equilibrium (50%) of the big range after the flip is unlikely to make a new high
  (~15:30-16:06).
- Long-term draws on liquidity tend to take ~3-6 months on the daily (~22:12-22:44).
- Use this daily-chart bias for day trading/scalping too (~19:14-19:26).

## Definitions
- Open float: current open interest above and below price, i.e. entry stops and protective
  stops on both sides (~1:09-2:09) [new].
- Short-term high/low: a swing point. Intermediate-term high: a high with a lower short-term
  high on each side. Long-term high: an intermediate-term high with lower intermediate-term
  highs on each side (head-and-shoulders shape); invert for lows (~20:14-20:46) [new].
- Quarterly shift: the roughly 3-month cycle of a major liquidity run (slide title).
