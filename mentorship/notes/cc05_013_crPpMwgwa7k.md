Slide lecture on money management for long-term (position) trading; two slides, one chart.

## Summary
Money-management mindset for higher-timeframe position trades: risk is sized on only 30% of
account equity, 1% (at most 2%) of that per trade, setups must offer 3:1 or better, and stops
are proportional to the timeframe. Expect only 2-3 position setups a year; scale out at
logical targets and re-add on retracements. Most of the rest is managed-funds career talk.
Little of it is directly intraday-index; the sizing rules are the bot-relevant part.

## Rules
- Allocate only 30% of total equity to trading; compute all risk % on that 30%, not the full
  balance (e.g. $100k -> $30k base; 2% risk = 2% of $30k = $600) ~4:13-4:46 [chart] [new]
- Ideal max risk per trade: 1% of the 30% base ($10k account -> $3k base -> $30 risk) ~6:27-7:01 [chart] [new] [conflict] (mentorship_rules.md Ep 03/07 use ~1-2.1% of the whole account)
- With more skill, up to 2% of the 30% base per trade; two concurrent trades = 2% max exposure ~26:39, ~27:23 [new]
- Only take setups offering 3:1 reward-to-risk or better; 3:1 is the minimum ~8:21, ~17:36 [chart] [new]
- Low risk + high reward tolerates low win rate; the remaining 70% equity keeps margin free for other setups ~8:40-9:08 [chart] [new]
- Target drawdown: ~15% max per year is excellent, 20s acceptable; target return ~18-25%/yr ~1:52-2:29, ~9:28 [new]
- Stop size must match the timeframe: no fixed pip stop. Intraday FX he uses ~30-35 pips (about one third of a ~100-pip ADR) ~15:23-16:02 [new] (forex)
- Position trades on the daily chart: do not move the stop to breakeven or reduce risk early; wait until price has moved a significant distance. Lower-timeframe trades may move to breakeven sooner ~17:36-18:40 [chart] [new]
- Exit part (1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 3/4) at logical resistance/targets, then re-add the same or slightly larger size when the retracement completes at a level where the next leg is "logically timed" ~18:56-20:31 [chart] [new]
- Expect 2, maybe 3 good position setups a year; rarely 4 (quarterly shifts) ~24:12-25:18 [new]
- Homework: hold at least one long-term trade for at least 3 months ~27:10 [new]
- While a long-term position retraces, trade short-term in correlated/inverse markets (e.g. short EURUSD or GBPUSD while long-term short USDJPY retraces up) to offset open-profit drawdown ~12:00-14:32 (forex) [new]

## Definitions
- Velocity: how fast capital is put to work and returns profit; day trading has it, position trading does not ~21:10 [new]
- Allocation base: the 30% slice of equity used for margin and risk calculation ~5:50 [new]

## Seen only on the charts
- Slide 1 "Money Management & HTF Analysis" lists 8 points: account balance not important;
  not required to use majority of equity; limit allocation to 30%; max risk % on 30%; ideally
  1% max risk per trade; target 3:1 or higher; low risk/high reward permits low accuracy; low
  risk allows equity for more setups ~3:00-8:40 [chart]
- Slide 2 "Money Management & Expectations" adds: few long-term setups annually; allow short
  term drawdown in profits; stops not a measure of ability; don't move stop too soon; exit at
  logical targets and re-enter later; not get rich quick but steady ~11:00-20:40 [chart]
- On the chart the risk box is ~1/3 the height of the gain boxes, consistent with 3:1.
