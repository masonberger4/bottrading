## Rules
- NDOG = 17:00 ET close to 18:00 ET open; extend through the day, price reprices to it repeatedly. [new] ~00:03:24
- NWOG = Friday close to Sunday open; CE = its midpoint; track both this week's and last week's NWOG. [new] ~00:04:35, ~00:06:50 [chart]
- Opening range gap (ORG) = previous RTH close to the 09:30 open; distinct from NDOG/NWOG; expect intraday price to reach into it unless it acts as a breakaway gap (no traction, keeps falling). [new] ~00:11:36, ~00:14:57 [chart]
- Opening range = 09:30-10:00. [new] ~00:11:01
- When the ORG is very large, or the prior day had a very large range, the first 30-45 min are choppy (runs both sides); a run often starts near 11:00, and 13:30-16:00 is the better window. He sacrifices the AM session on such days. [new] ~00:13:27-00:14:31
- Don't trade the first minutes after 9:30 chasing a big move; retail shorts' buy stops sit just above the post-open intraday high and get run. ~00:09:41-00:10:05 [chart]
- To short after a bullish breaker held, the breaker must first be broken and then retested as resistance; an FVG alone above is not enough. [new] ~00:49:43 [chart]
- Better short context: wait for the buy-side run into the premium FVG, then judge the reaction, not a short straight from 9:30. [new] ~00:48:41
- Breaker entry: price leaves the breaker, comes back into it, bodies respect it (wicks may poke through). ~00:57:56, ~00:09:04 [chart]
- Consecutive down-close candles before the up move are treated together as the order block; their opening price is the sensitive level. [new] ~01:03:02
- Mitigation block = low, high, higher low; price returning to it supports (bullish) if the narrative is bullish. Opposite of breaker (low, high, lower low). [new] ~01:03:50 [chart]
- Scalp target: limit at the high of the nearest swing (buy stops) once price leaves the PD array; ~5 handles from discount to external range liquidity. ~01:04:56 [chart]
- Wicks do the damage; bodies tell the narrative (1 tick through an FVG/candle high is fine). ~01:02:36 [chart]
- Use separate chart templates (e.g. NDOG/NWOG only) to keep charts clean; he keeps levels as numbers on paper. ~00:52:46
