## Summary
Defines the breaker. Price runs stops beyond an old swing (a false break / turtle soup), then reverses hard through the opposite swing point formed between the two extremes. The extreme candle at that swing point becomes the breaker. Trapped traders mitigate there on the retrace, so trade it with the new direction. Unlike the mitigation block (cc04_004), a breaker needs the stop run.

## Rules
- Bullish breaker sequence: short-term low L1, swing high H, lower low L2 that runs sell stops below L1 (false break / turtle soup long), then a move above H = market structure shift. Buy when price returns to H ~0:37-2:31 [chart]
- Bearish breaker sequence: high H1, swing low L, higher high H2 that runs buy stops above H1, then a break below L. Sell when price returns to L ~2:45-5:04 [chart]
- Bearish breaker zone = the down-close candle (or "bearish range") at the most recent swing low before the old high was taken (slide) ~3:09-3:32 [chart] [new]
- Bullish breaker zone = the up-close candle (or bullish range) at the most recent swing high before the old low was taken (slide) ~5:04-5:26 [chart] [new]
- Pick the extreme candle: the highest up candle in the swing high (not an earlier, lower one). Use its entire range ~9:16-9:28 [chart] [conflict] mentorship_rules.md line 24 says to use the candle body, not the wick, for the breaker break.
- Confirmation: fast repricing after the stop run (range expansion through the in-between swing). Once structure breaks, treat every retracement as a new entry in the new direction, especially the return to the breaker ~4:24-4:59, ~6:28-7:14 [new]
- After entry, expect range expansion in the new direction to higher objectives ~2:07, ~6:19 (no specific target given)
- Ideal setup: bearish breakers in major-to-intermediate downtrends, bullish breakers in uptrends (slide) [chart] [new]
- On the first return, price may "flirt" with the old low as resistance. Wait for the real displacement through the swing high before treating it as a breaker ~1:08-1:23

## Definitions
- Breaker block (slide text, paraphrased): the bearish breaker is a bearish range or down-close candle at the most recent swing low before an old high is violated. Buyers of that low see it broken and look to mitigate when price returns. The bullish breaker is the mirror (up-close candle at the swing high before an old low is violated) ~3:09, ~5:04 [chart]
- He calls the breaker "this form of mitigation", so it is mitigation after a stop run ~0:20-0:28

## Seen only on the charts
- In the slides, the bearish M has the second high ABOVE the first (stop run) and the bullish W has the second low BELOW the first. This is the visual difference from the mitigation-block M (lower high).
