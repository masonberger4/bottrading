Recorded April 2017 (lesson 7 of the Month 08 day-trading model) / forex (EURUSD-type examples), London open kill zone / slides with small 15m-style chart illustrations, prices not legible / 31 min.

## Summary
A rules checklist for London-session day trades, in mirrored buy and sell versions: when to trade (bias, days, overnight range size, time), where to enter (nine setup types), where to put the stop (fixed rule per setup), and where to take profit (a scale-out list by level and time of day). All levels are in pips and tied to the CBDR/Asian range, so stops and sizes are FX-only, but the structure (bias filter, Mon-Wed, sell-stop raid, first retrace into OB, time-of-day scale-outs) maps onto the bot.

## Rules
Bias and reference ranges, slide at ~06:20 [chart]
- Highest importance: daily and/or 4h direction (~00:34) [chart]
- Bullish: use the previous day's low-to-high range and the previous day's NY-session low-to-high range for retracement entries; use the previous day's low for a sell-stop raid to accumulate longs; aim from HTF discount to premium PD arrays. Bearish: mirror (previous day high-to-low, buy-stop raid of previous day's high) (~00:40-01:34) [chart] [new]
- Also measure the London session high/low, and the range from London's extreme to the opposite extreme just before the NY open; the next move (or next day's retracement) often sits inside that range (~02:12-04:39) [new]
- Raids of the previous day's high/low usually come at the end of a multi-day move into a premium/discount PD array: buy stops run, short the next day (mirror for longs) (~04:57-05:39) [new]
- Bias confirmation on daily/4h: bouncing off bullish OBs, rejecting old lows, filling gaps below then rallying, and short-term premium arrays being violated (~05:43-06:21) [new]

When to buy (short is the mirror), slides at ~07:40 and ~21:40 [chart]
- Ideally in a seasonally bullish period (not required); current or new quarter expected bullish (~06:31-06:45) [chart] [new]
- After the daily has reacted positively at a discount PD array; with a clear, unobstructed path to the opposing premium array. If the target isn't easy to see, it's not high probability (~06:45-07:13) [chart] [new]
- Ideal days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday (~07:17, ~21:02) [chart] [new]
- CBDR ideally < 40 pips; demand an Asian range of 20 pips or less before the Frankfurt open (~01:00 NY) (~07:24, ~21:15) [chart] [new] [conflict: lesson 6 used CBDR > 50 / Asian > 40 as the avoid thresholds; here the ideal thresholds are tighter]
- Buy 02:00-04:00 NY seeking the low of the day (short: seek the high) (~07:39) [chart] [new]
- Buy 1-2 standard deviations of the CBDR and/or Asian range coupled with a discount PD array (~07:48) [chart] [new]
- Execute on the 15m and/or 5m chart (~07:58) [chart]

Where to buy (nine setups), slide at ~13:40 [chart]
1. Under the Asian range low, plus 5 pips for spread (~08:10) [new]
2. FVG below a short-term low from the previous day's NY session (the sell stops there get run in London) (~08:16) [new]
3. Bullish OB below a short-term low, previous day or intraday (~08:56) [new]
4. If very bullish: 1 SD of CBDR/Asian range overlapping any discount PD array in the London kill zone (~09:21) [new]
5. Inside the "protraction lower" from 00:00 to 02:00 NY (the Judas swing) at a PD array (~09:39) [new]
6. A liquidity void being filled that completes under a short-term low (~10:01) [new]
7. If price rallies straight up from 00:00 with no drop, then in 02:00-04:00 buy the first retracement into a 15m or 5m bullish OB (~10:28-11:02) [new]
8. 1-2 SD of the Asian range overlapping a discount PD array (~11:05) [new]
9. If a short-term low is taken out twice with no upside protraction first, buy the second run as a turtle soup (~11:28) [new]
- Every London session will show at least one of these when the market is bullish and respecting a daily discount array (~12:56-13:05)

Stops (buy; short is mirrored above the high), slides at ~16:20 and ~25:20 [chart]
- Don't rush moving the initial stop; no breakeven until price has made at least 40-50% of the daily range (ADR), because a double pass can stop you out (~13:57-14:26) [chart] [new]
- CBDR overlap with PD array: 30 pips beyond entry (~14:29) [chart]
- Run under/over the Asian range: 40 pips beyond entry (~14:39) [chart]
- Any sell-stop/buy-stop raid: 30 pips beyond the low/entry (~14:59) [chart]
- First retracement into an OB: 10 pips beyond the low/high of the day (~15:22) [chart]
- Second return for stops (turtle soup): 30 pips beyond the low/high of the day (~15:32) [chart]
- Any other setup: Asian range low minus 50% of the 5-day ADR (shorts: Asian range high plus 50% of ADR) (~16:01, ~25:09) [chart] [new]
(All pip values FX-only; the [new] tag applies to the scheme as a whole.)

Profit taking (buy; mirror for shorts), slides at ~20:20 and ~27:40 [chart]
- Always take something off at 20-30 pips gain (~16:32) [chart] [new]
- Then scale out every 2 SD of the Asian range or CBDR (~16:40) [chart] [new]
- At the previous day's high +5 to 15 pips (shorts: previous day's low -5 to 15), in case of a turtle soup reversal (~17:03) [chart] [new]
- At 50% (equilibrium) of the 60m range you're trading inside (~17:13) [chart] [new]
- Have 60-80% off by the 5-day ADR projection, always; leave a small runner (~17:25-18:23) [chart] [new]
- Above the previous week's high / previous month's high: take something off (~18:27-18:36) [chart] [new]
- Time of day: a portion at or just before 05:00 NY; at the short-term high made before the 07:00 NY open (NY may reverse; spoken for shorts as a retracement into the 08:20 CME open); and during the 10:00-11:00 NY leg while still trending (~18:41-19:37, ~26:18-26:51) [chart] [new]
- Ideally each scale-out level coincides with a premium PD array (discount for shorts) (~19:41) [chart]
- Not all levels apply to every trade; use whichever are present (~19:58, ~28:37)
- Also valid as profit levels: fib projections 1.27, 1.68 (likely 1.618), and the 100% measured move (~27:39) [new]

## Definitions
- Protraction lower/higher: the 00:00-02:00 NY Judas move against the bias (~09:39, ~22:51).
- Turtle soup (here): a short-term low/high taken out a second time with no prior opposite protraction (~11:28).
- Frankfurt open: about 01:00 NY (~21:19).
- Previous-day NY session range: the NY session's highest high and lowest low (~02:00).
