## Definitions
- FVG classes: BISI ("buy-side imbalance, sell-side inefficiency"; it lacks sell-side delivery) and SIBI, the mirror. Paint-roller analogy: price returns to cover the spots it missed ~29:00-32:30
- Liquidity void: a real gap with no wick/body overlap between candles. It is measured high to low with CE in the middle, like an FVG ~1:58:00
- Inversion FVG: an old FVG that price has passed through, now used from the other side (weekly IFVG as resistance) ~40:00, ~1:08:30
- Order block = a "change in the state of delivery", not orders on a DOM ~1:12:00, ~1:26:30
- Mitigation block: low, high, then a higher low (no lower low), then the break. It differs from a breaker, which needs a lower low ~2:52:00
- Rejection block: the lowest down-close (bodies) of a swing high; it is the first draw below ~1:30:30
- Power of three works on any timeframe. The NY-open run up into resistance = a Judas swing ~2:36:00
- Trigger swing: the swing used to anchor SD projections ~2:22:40
