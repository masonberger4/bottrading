## Summary
Currency mega-trades sit between swing and position trades. Ingredients: quarterly shifts, seasonal tendencies, a supporting dollar index, and relative strength among the seven majors measured on currency futures (or an MT4 overlay). Pair the strongest currency against the weakest via a cross (CAD/JPY, AUD/JPY in June-July 2017).

## Rules
- Quarterly shifts: about every 3 months markets tend to form an intermediate-term turning point; it gives timing, not direction (~0:46-1:13). Long-term only.
- Seasonal tendencies support but never override technicals/institutional order flow (~1:45-3:13).
- A currency mega-trade must be supported by the dollar index direction or it does not qualify (~3:43-4:36). Forex-only.
- Relative strength definition (applies generally): a strong market holds higher lows, breaks old highs and premium arrays easily, finds support at discount arrays, has its big expansion candles in the trend direction and shallow retracements. Weak = mirror (~7:12-8:15, ~12:41-13:34, ~14:06) [chart]. Consistent with existing SMT rules; [new] as an explicit checklist for choosing the stronger of ES/NQ (our application).
- When two closely correlated markets are both strong, keep only the stronger and drop the other (NZD dropped for AUD) (~17:21). Same logic as trading the stronger of ES/NQ.
- Overlay method: put several pairs on one line chart (invert USD-base pairs like USD/JPY, USD/CAD, USD/CHF) and look for SMT divergence at highs/lows (~8:15-9:48) [chart].
- Narrow to one or two currencies; trade via futures, options on futures, or the cross pairing strong vs weak (~13:34, ~17:52-19:19).

## Definitions
- Mega-trade (FX): longer than a swing trade, shorter than a position trade; fast, "in a hurry" moves (~0:46, ~21:24).
- Majors list: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD, USD/CHF, USD/CAD, USD/JPY (~4:36).
