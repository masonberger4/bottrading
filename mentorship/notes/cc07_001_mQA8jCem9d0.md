# cc07_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 07 - Short Term Trading Using Monthly & Weekly Ranges (mQA8jCem9d0)
Recorded for the March 2017 month of the 2016-17 mentorship (slide deck, no live trade). Market:
forex (USDJPY example). Timeframes: monthly, weekly, daily, 4h, 1h (execution). Duration ~45 min.
Mostly a higher-timeframe framing lesson for "one shot one kill" (a one-week trade); the
index-futures value is the weekly-range template (Mon-Wed setup, Mon-Wed high/low break).

## Summary
Short-term trading = holding a few days to one week, trading toward the current/next week's
probable range. Direction comes from a monthly PD array; the target is the opposing PD array
one timeframe lower (monthly premium -> weekly discount, or weekly premium -> daily discount).
Entries come on the 1h chart at weekly/daily/4h PD arrays inside a kill zone, Mon-Wed. A break
of the Mon-Wed high (bullish) or low (bearish) later in the week confirms expansion to the target.

## Rules
- Short-term trade duration: a few days to one week; frame with monthly and weekly charts,
  trade in the direction of the present or next week's probable range ~01:14 [new]
- Do not force trades; setups must be obvious, and they occur every week ~02:04 [new]
- PD array matrix (premium, top to bottom): old high/low, rejection block, bearish OB, FVG,
  liquidity void, bearish breaker, bearish mitigation block; discount mirrors it. Once one side's
  arrays have been traded to / held price, expect the opposite side to be sought next ~06:05 [chart] [new]
- Core pairing: from the active higher-TF PD array, target the opposing PD array one timeframe
  lower (monthly premium -> nearest weekly discount array; monthly discount -> weekly premium)
  ~09:29-10:15 [chart] [new]
- If no OB/breaker/void/FVG exists in the target direction (e.g. a run of all down candles), use
  a rejection block or an old high/low as the target ~11:25 [new]
- Scale out along the way but leave a last piece for the full range objective; the runner often
  earns as much as taking everything at the first target ~08:54, ~11:52 [new] (consistent with Ep 03/04 partials)
- HTF sequence: search/determine PD arrays on monthly, weekly, daily, 4h; execute on the 1h
  ~13:43, ~16:37 [chart] [new]
- For a bearish monthly frame, watch the monthly/weekly/daily/4h discount arrays break one after
  another until the weekly discount target is reached (mirror for bulls) ~14:31-15:00 [new]
- If you missed the monthly array (price already left it), trade off weekly, daily or 4h arrays
  in the same direction, still targeting the weekly opposing array ~17:28 [new]
- 4h-originated setups produce fewer trades than weekly/monthly ones ~18:36, ~22:23 [new]
- "One shot one kill" setup proper: weekly premium array -> daily discount target (mirror for
  longs), short/buy from weekly/daily/4h arrays, executed on 1h ~22:35-23:51 [chart] [new]
- Bullish weekly template: impulse up, then retracement into a discount PD array during Mon-Wed,
  inside a kill zone; profit comes from the expansion swing to the premium target ~27:49-30:12 [chart] [new]
- Retracement can happen any time Mon-Wed (Sunday rally then Monday dip, or a shallow one-day
  retrace) ~28:40 [new]
- Supporting (not required) filters: seasonality, interest-rate movement (bonds moving, not
  stagnant), COT commercials, intermarket/SMT ~27:00 [chart] [new]
- Bearish template mirrors: decline, retrace into premium Mon-Wed, expansion to lower lows
  ~32:52-33:56 [chart]
- Kill zones mentioned: London open and NY open (his preference); Asian 18:00-21:00 NY time for
  AUD/NZD/JPY, avoid after ~22:00 [forex-only] ~30:15-31:06 [new]
- Note each monthly and weekly candle's high/low and whether price is in premium or discount; ~4
  weeks per month, ~5 days per week. Monthly range frames weekly trades; weekly range frames daily
  trades ~34:05-35:54 [chart] [new]
- Weekly candles should keep expanding in the expected monthly direction; that is the confirmation
  the monthly read is right ~35:56 [new]
- Weekly lows (bullish) tend to form Mon-Wed ~42:34 [chart] [new]
- Key last-slide rule: in a bullish market, if the highest high of Mon(-Sun open)-Wed is traded
  through later in the week (Thu/Fri), expect aggressive expansion toward the weekly/monthly premium
  target. Bearish: break of the Mon-Wed low -> aggressive sell-off toward the discount target. It
  may not finish that week; keep the bias into next week ~42:44-44:39 [chart] [new]

## Definitions
- Short-term trading: a holding period of one week or a few days ~01:14
- Point of origin: the higher-TF PD array the move starts from (monthly or weekly) ~28:01 [chart]
- Impulse swing / retracement / expansion swing: the three legs of the weekly template; the
  fractal repeats inside each leg (slide counts 8 then 16 long setups in one 6-month fractal) ~24:34-26:31 [chart]
- A month's candle range vs "the monthly range" traded: he distinguishes one candle's range from
  the span between the monthly discount and premium arrays ~38:06-39:10 [chart]
- Rejection block (as listed): old candle bodies with long wicks beyond them ~21:33
