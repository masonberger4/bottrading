## Rules
- Order block levels: for a bearish OB (last up-close candle), use its opening price when the lower wick is short and stubby. When the wick is long, use the candle low and the CE (consequent encroachment, 50%) of that wick. A lower timeframe can refine the level inside the wick ~26:10 [new]
- An OB does not have to engulf anything. It is the last candle before the rotation away from a key level ~25:23 [new]
- Mean threshold of the weekly OB = 50% of the candle from low to high. DXY weekly MT was 104.540 ~51:40 [chart]
- A PD array (premium/discount array) is three specific levels (high, CE, low), not a zone ~30:04
- A daily candle travelling back and forth inside a weekly FVG does not make it a balanced price range. The higher timeframe must close its candle bodies across the gap ~40:26 [new]
- Price goes up for only two reasons: buy stops or an inefficiency above. Down is the mirror (sell stops or an inefficiency below) ~41:29
- A close above a FVG you expected to be bearish is not a short. It often means the gap will become an inversion FVG, i.e. support ~47:00 [new]
- After a market structure shift (MSS) up, the market is in a "buy program". Frame the dealing range from the past high to the past low and look only for the first PD array at or below equilibrium for longs (mirror for shorts). Do not fade internal highs ~45:25, ~52:52 [chart]
- Use the fib only for premium/discount, never for entries. Levels: 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, plus projections -0.5, -1, -1.5, -2. Setups form around the 25/50/75 levels ~1:34:00, ~2:06:50 [chart] [new]
- Implied dealing range: from the swing low up to the expected draw (target). Its 25/50/75 levels are drawn before price gets there ~1:16:20 [chart] [new]
- Measuring gap: a FVG that forms at the 50% of the dealing range and is not fully closed. Project the move by copying the range from the root swing low to the FVG high and placing it above the gap ~1:22:05 [chart] (refines Ep03)
- Breakaway gap: the first FVG leaving a consolidation after an MSS. Trade it, and trust that price will draw to the next PD array ~1:20:51
- Immediate rebalance: price comes back into the gap the next day (or the day after), then runs. Seeing one confirms the direction ~1:23:38, ~2:19:00 [new]
- Bonds as macro filter: while ZB is in a range, every market consolidates. Wait for bonds to close outside the range. Bonds down means dollar up, foreign currencies down, and equity rallies held back (slow creep higher with small retracements) ~1:05:42, ~1:56:56 [chart] [new]
- FX confirmation: an EURUSD or GBPUSD short needs a matching DXY long setup at the same time (DXY in a discount FVG while EUR is in a premium FVG). USDJPY and USDCHF follow DXY ~1:32:17 [new]
- Choose the pair with one clean, obvious gap (EUR over GBP, which had several small gaps) ~1:49:01 (similar to Ep03's SMT pick)
- Not every PD array shows up in every price run. Trade the one the market gives you ~1:47:10 [new]
- PD arrays can be reused while the narrative holds. A breaker hit several times stays valid ~2:11:51 [chart] [new]
- IFVG signature: an inversion FVG with a normal FVG just below it inside the dealing range means that lower FVG should stay at least partly open. Trigger: a candle body closes at the FVG's high or CE. Entry is the institutional order flow entry drill (to that level or just below). Then expect a fast run to buy-side liquidity ~2:15:00-2:18:36 [chart] [new]
- Relative equal highs that sit under an untraded higher-timeframe PD array are a partial, not a reversal ~2:19:50
- When the target is still unfilled into Thursday or Friday afternoon, it is likely left for Monday or Tuesday. Weekend gap risk: if the open gaps far in your favour, take the profit ~2:20:09, ~2:32:47
- When the higher-timeframe targets are met, go neutral and stay out ("sidelines is a position") ~2:33:05, ~2:46:30 (matches Ep08 neutral bias)
- Midpoint (CE) of a weekly gap is a fine target. The far edge is the "ringer" and is not required ~2:30:15
- The NWOG (new week opening gap) works as a magnet; price need not fill it exactly ~2:28:00 [chart]
- NFP: new traders never trade it, and nobody trades the hour of the release. Experienced traders wait out the initial volatility, then trade toward the daily draw ~2:49:35 (consistent with Ep04)
- Risk after a loss: halve the size, aim to win back 50% of the previous loss, then step risk back up ~2:26:40 [new]
- Fake bull flag: a sharp rally plus consolidation lures breakout buyers. The algorithm runs just above the flag high into a premium FVG, then sells ~1:41:34

## Definitions
- **Inversion FVG (IFVG):** a FVG that price trades through and closes beyond, then uses from the other side as support (or resistance) ~19:42, ~38:34
- **Liquidity void (refined):** the part of a volume imbalance between a candle's close and the next open where no trade happened at all. It is the "sweet spot" inside the volume imbalance ~1:55:43
- **Buy/sell program:** the state after an MSS. The algorithm buys at every short-term discount PD array until it reaches the draw ~45:29, ~2:34:44
- **Multiplier:** the PD array used to get into a trade (OB, FVG, IFVG, institutional order flow entry drill, OTE (optimal trade entry), propulsion block). A multiplier is not a model ~13:18
- **Market profile (his sense):** a regime label, consolidating or trending, inferred from bonds. Not volume profile ~1:52:56
- **Grading a price swing:** dividing the dealing range into 25% steps to see where setups will form ~49:47
