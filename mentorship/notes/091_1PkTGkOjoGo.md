# 091 ICT Mentorship 2023 - September 08, 2023 Review & ICT Ma Deuce Model (1PkTGkOjoGo)
Recorded Fri Sep 8 2023 (weekend review); trade example is NQ Thu Sep 7 2023 (8:30 jobless-claims
release). Markets: DXY, EURUSD, GBPUSD (daily bias only), ES and NQ Sep-23 futures. Timeframes:
daily, 1h, 15m, 1m. Duration 29 min. Ma Deuce part is ~14:13-28:50.

## Summary
Daily "BISI" (a single big up-close daily candle = buy-side imbalance, sell-side inefficiency) on ES
and NQ frames the week; while price sits inside that daily range ICT trades nothing (both sides of
the narrative are valid). The "Ma Deuce" model (named after the M2 machine gun) is his way of using an
**inversion FVG**: in a premium of a daily BISI that he expects to be re-delivered lower, a bullish
1m FVG that price breaks down through becomes resistance; sell its retest after a time gate (08:00),
ahead of 8:30 news, targeting the BISI's CE, its lower 25% quadrant and an hourly BISI low.

## Rules
- Daily BISI = range of one big up-close daily candle with no sell-side delivery: high = the high of
  the candle after it, low = the low of the candle before it (blue lines); CE = midpoint. It must be
  re-delivered downward (sell-side delivery), not necessarily filled to its low ~4:09-4:40, ~16:42 [chart] [new]
- While price trades inside the daily BISI range (both bull and bear cases valid) do not trade that
  index; wait until price leaves above its high or below its low ~6:12-6:36, ~9:00 [new]
- Conflicted higher-TF narrative forces 1m/5m scalping only: one entry, take a winner and stop for
  the session ~10:44-11:08, ~14:00 [new] (consistent with Ep 02 "scalp only" rule)
- Grade a gap/inefficiency into quarters: CE (50%) and the lower 25% quadrant are draws; bodies
  respected both, wicks went through ~12:30-13:06 [chart] [new]
- Premium/discount for the model = the daily BISI range itself: above its CE is premium (sell there
  if bearish), below is discount ~17:29-17:46 [chart] [new]
- Ma Deuce (bearish): (1) higher-TF expectation that a daily BISI is re-delivered lower; (2) price
  in the premium half; (3) lower-TF market structure breaks lower through a bullish 1m FVG
  (it "inverts"); (4) time: do not short the first break; wait until 08:00, then sell when price
  trades back up into the inverted FVG; (5) expect the 8:30 release to deliver the move ~19:02-19:56, ~21:59-22:51 [chart] [new]
- The inverted FVG is reused: the second rejection off the same candle's levels is also valid ~20:00-20:09 [chart] [new]
- Second entry: a new small bearish FVG formed after the drop, retested just before 8:30 ~26:31-26:41 [chart] [new]
- Targets/partials: first partial at daily BISI CE, second at the lower 25% quadrant, then the
  hourly BISI low ~10 handles below; the BISI low is best case and need not be reached; "take
  profits, don't be greedy" ~23:31-23:55, ~26:47 [new]
- Mirror (bullish): a daily SIBI expected to be re-delivered higher; a bearish FVG after a low that
  price breaks up through becomes support ~24:24-24:43 [new]
- An FVG is an inefficiency, not lower-TF support/resistance; he looks "through" the candles to one
  specific candle's levels, not a supply/demand zone ~14:27-14:57, ~19:33-19:48 [new]
- If you are bullish and price goes through a bullish FVG and comes back above it, it is a
  "reclaimed discount array" ~20:11-20:22 [new]
