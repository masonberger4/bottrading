## Summary
Continues the AUDUSD case study (daily bullish order block near 0.7512, turtle soup under an old low). The point is
money management: frame entries on a lower timeframe (5m) inside a higher-timeframe level so the stop is tiny, take half
off at 3R, and let the rest run to higher-timeframe liquidity pools. Then a weekly-compounding calculator shows how a
small fixed % risk and a modest weekly pip goal compound to ~10%+ per month.

## Rules
- Risk is reduced by refining the entry timeframe: a daily level can be traded with a 60m setup, or a 15m, or a 5m one; the lower the TF, the smaller the stop, the bigger the R multiple. The higher-TF level supplies the direction and targets ~0:34-2:02 [chart] [new]
- Trade only at levels with "institutional sponsorship": monthly, weekly or daily PD arrays (here a daily bullish OB); indicators and supply/demand are irrelevant ~9:43-10:18 [new]
- Position plan: risk 2% per trade (once proficient), take half (1%) off at 3:1, let the second half run for the higher-TF liquidity pools ~2:57-3:33, ~12:28-12:41 [chart] [conflict] (Ep 04/07 take the bulk, e.g. 10 of 12 / 9 of 14, at the first target; Ep 07 risk ~1-1.3%)
- The 3R first target may sit below the first (5m) buy-stop pool; the stops do not need to be run for the partial ~3:03-3:12 [chart] [new]
- Runner targets step up through liquidity pools: 5m buy stops, then 15m, then 60m; at each pool consider taking another piece because consolidation or reversal is possible ~3:36-5:05 [chart] [new]
- Always pay yourself a partial when available; all-or-nothing exits are rejected ~12:45, ~14:34-15:37 [new]
- Do not expect the whole projected range; capturing about half of it is a good outcome ~7:14-7:48, ~10:21-11:50 [new]
- Stop-sweep grades: price runs old highs by about 10 or 20 pips (forex units) ~6:40-6:47 [chart] [new, forex-only units]
- A wider stop (20 instead of 10 pips, e.g. the 60m setup) is acceptable; it still pays with a 30-pip partial and a 50-pip runner ~6:24-6:37, ~15:41-16:26 [new]
- Aim for one good setup per week ("one shot one kill"); a weekly goal may need a second or third trade, but do not force it ~12:03, ~13:42-14:02 [new; matches Ep 03 "1 good setup per week"]

## Definitions
- R multiple: profit in units of initial risk ("nine dollars for every one dollar risk") ~4:12
- Liquidity pool: resting buy stops above specific highs (per timeframe: 5m, 15m, 60m pools graded by timeframe) ~0:52, ~3:45
- Turtle soup long: sweep of sell stops under an old low into a higher-TF bullish OB (named on chart only) [chart]
- Forex Projected Goals Calculator: inputs base equity, weekly pip goal, % risk per trade, pips per stop loss. Value per pip = equity x risk% / stop pips; weekly profit = value per pip x weekly pip goal; compounds weekly ~8:00-17:40 [chart]

## Open questions
- "10 per month compounded over the year is over 300%" (~17:23): 1.1^12 is ~214%; his number matches the calculator's 14-week figures, not 12 months. Treat the figures as illustrative.
- Should the bot take 50% at 3R (this video) or the bulk at the first liquidity target (Ep 04/07)? Needs a choice or a backtest of both.
- 3R target vs first liquidity pool: if the pool is closer than 3R, which wins? He only shows 3R below the pool.
- Runner exit rule at each higher pool ("take a portion off") is discretionary: how much, and when to leave the rest?
- Chart time zone of the MT4 5m axis is not given, so the entry time is not usable as a kill-zone rule.
