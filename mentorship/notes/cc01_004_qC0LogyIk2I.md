## Summary
Context, not entry signals: after an impulsive up-leg, wait for a confirmed swing high, then wait for price to retrace to equilibrium (50% of the leg) or, better, into discount (below 50%, ideally the 62-79% "OTE" band). Only there hunt lower-timeframe longs (order block, turtle soup, breaker, OTE). Discount should not last; a quick explosive rally is expected, aimed at buy stops above old/equal highs. He uses the fib only as a ruler, not as magic.

## Rules
- Measure each impulsive (displacement) up-leg from its swing low to its high; equilibrium = 50% of that leg = "fair value" ~10:26 [chart] [new as a general framework; Ep 08 has the sell-side mirror]
- Do nothing until the high is confirmed: a swing high (higher candle with a lower candle each side) plus a following candle that trades lower than the swing-high candle ("fourth candle"). Only then start waiting for the retrace ~9:44, ~12:11, ~21:56 [new]
- Equilibrium = permitted to buy (neutral-to-bullish), but the best buys are below 50% (discount). Nothing above 50% is a buy ~18:12, ~28:37 [new]
- Highest-probability long zone: 62% to 79% retracement (OTE; 70.5% is the "sweet spot"); 62% is the minimum depth he wants ~23:59, ~29:16, ~41:26, ~43:30 [chart] [new]
- Discount must not last: if bullish, price should leave the 50-79% zone quickly and explosively; lingering is a warning ~16:22, ~28:49 [new]
- Invalidation/stop: the leg's own swing low; price must not go below it, and the stop sits below it ~27:23-28:00 [new]
- Re-anchor: if price makes a new high and the leg's low was not violated, keep the fib on the same low and stretch it to the new high ("parent" swing, even if built from several sub-legs) ~13:55, ~21:33, ~35:57 [chart] [new]
- If the retrace never reaches 50%, there is no trade; do not chase ~32:52, ~48:26 [new]
- A break below an old low inside discount, in a bullish context, is a stop raid -> expect turtle soup (false break) and buy it rather than treat it as failure ~32:12, ~37:41, ~45:21 [new for the long side; Ep 08 has the short-side turtle soup]
- If price blows through OTE and you are stopped, look for the next old low it sweeps and buy after that sweep ~45:21, ~53:45 [new]
- Profit taking: exit when price runs above an old (short-term) high; it need not be the most important high ~45:44-46:45 [new]
- Bearish order block above (bottom/open of the last up-close candle before a drop) is a profit target; exit slightly before the exact level ~36:31, ~49:21 [chart] [new as target; partially matches Ep 08 "exit before the level"]
- After a discount bounce, a return to equilibrium is consolidation; the next move is expansion to liquidity above old/equal highs ~50:11, ~51:49 [new]
- Stop runs above equal highs typically reach 10-20 pips beyond them (intraday, forex units) ~51:04 [chart] [new, forex-only units]
- Bullish OB in discount: consecutive down-close candles at the low; the block starts at the top (open) of the first one; drill into 4h/60m/15m/5m for entries ~34:29-35:04 [matches Ep 03]
- Framing is daily, ~one high-odds setup per week; same logic shown to hold on H1 ~37:06, ~47:49 [chart]
- Forex-only: ignore the MT4 Sunday candle when counting candles ~14:19

## Definitions
- Impulsive price swing = displacement: strong one-directional move by large money ~6:13
- Equilibrium: midpoint (50%) of the price leg / range = fair market value ~10:26
- Discount: anything below equilibrium of a bullish leg ~16:09
- OTE (optimal trade entry): 62%-79% retracement, 70.5% sweet spot ~23:59, ~29:29
- Swing high: one candle with a lower high on each side ~11:56
- Turtle soup: false breakout below an old low that runs sell stops ~32:25
- Engineered liquidity: buy stops that build above old highs during a retracement ~46:45
