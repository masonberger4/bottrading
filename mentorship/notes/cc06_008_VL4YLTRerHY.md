# cc06_008 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 06 - The Million Dollar Swing Setup (VL4YLTRerHY)
Lesson 8 of the February 2017 swing-trading module (~0:08) / Gold futures (GC) and spot gold, with DXY, COT, open interest and a commodity sample / monthly, weekly, daily (4h named for stops and PD arrays) / 38m. About 19 min of checklist slides, then one gold long from Dec 2016 to Feb 2017.

## Summary
ICT's full swing checklist, run in order, with each stage a yes/no gate: seasonal tendency, then major market analysis (trending markets), then intermarket analysis (COT commercials, DXY SMT, commodities, open interest; this stage is written for FX/metals), then top-down PD arrays plus the IPDA 20/40/60-day ranges, then the entry type (stop or limit order depending on the PD array), then fixed management rules. Any "no" means no swing trade: drop to short-term or day trades and wait. The example: gold bought off a monthly bullish OB (1142) aiming for weekly PD arrays at 1255-1260, with the stop runs expected at equilibrium.

## Rules
- Gate 1, seasonal: a seasonal tendency to buy (or sell) must be in effect now or forming soon. If not, check another market. If no market has one, no swing trades ~2:53-3:57 [chart] [new]
- Gate 2, major market: rates or the dollar index must be trending (group A), AND commodities or stocks must be trending (group B). One per group is enough. If not, trade short-term or day trades only ~4:20-5:53 [chart] [new]
- Gate 3, COT (FX/metals only): take the commercials' net position over the last 12 months, high to low, and halve it. For longs, commercials must sit in the upper half or be reducing shorts, even while still net short. For shorts, they must be selling or net short ~5:58-8:10, ~10:31-11:05 [new]
- Gate 4, DXY: for longs on FX/metals, the dollar index must look bearish, or show SMT divergence against the traded market. For shorts, DXY must look bullish ~8:13-9:01, ~11:07 [new]
- Gate 5, commodities: for longs, commodities should break old highs and hold old lows. For shorts, they should break old lows and fail at old highs ~9:04-9:45, ~11:31 [new]
- Gate 6, open interest: for longs, a drop of 10-15% or more (commercial short covering). For shorts, a rise of 10-15% or more (commercial short selling) ~9:49-10:19, ~11:58 [new]
- Top-down: note 9-18 months of history. Mark every monthly/weekly/daily/4h PD array and copy them all to the 4h chart ~12:24-12:35 [chart] [new]
- IPDA data ranges: daily lookbacks of 20, 40 and 60 days. If every PD array in the 20-day range has been traded to, move out to 40, then 60. Look for places where the ranges and PD arrays line up ~12:40-13:08 [chart] [new]
- Bullish: frame the entry with monthly/weekly/daily discount arrays. Bearish: use premium arrays. Monthly and weekly arrays give the best odds ~13:17-13:49 [chart] [new]
- Entry order type depends on the PD array ~14:20-14:45, ~15:39-16:02 [chart] [new]:
  - Breaker or mitigation block: buy on a stop (short: sell on a stop)
  - Liquidity void or FVG: buy/sell on a stop
  - Order block, rejection block, or beyond an old low/high: buy/sell on a limit
- Management (long; mirror for shorts) ~16:20-17:41 [chart] [new]:
  - Target: the opposite monthly/weekly/daily premium array, plus any fib extension that lines up with it
  - Leave the stop where it is until price has covered 1/3 of the distance to the objective
  - At 1/4 of the objective, take 20-30% off
  - Move the stop to breakeven only after the first partial ("never before")
  - At 50% of the objective, add the partial size back after a short-term dip
  - Close everything at the objective (or at 75-80% of it) and trail the stop under the most recent 4h short-term low
  - Expect a run on sell stops at or just above 50% of the range. Hold through it and don't trail tightly
  - If stopped out and the reasons still hold, re-enter at a new MWD discount array
- Range quadrants: the swing from the entry PD array to the target has an equilibrium (50%). Once price trades through equilibrium, expect one stop run (a retracement) against the position. The second leg then gets one more stop run, on a pullback after it takes out the high made around the midpoint. After that, expect a quick run to the objective with no further stop run ~26:02-27:24, ~28:36-29:31, ~32:21-33:06 [chart] [new]
- Projection: on the daily, pull a fib from the high to the low of the retracement swing that forms after equilibrium. Use the 1.27 and 1.62 extensions and look for them to line up with weekly/monthly PD arrays ~28:08-28:31 [chart] [new]
- Use the PD array level as the anchor, not the actual extreme. ICT measured from 1142 (monthly OB high), not the ~1123-1130 actual low ~24:56-25:11 [chart] [new]
- Swing setups appear roughly every 4-6 weeks, and the seasonal filter caps how often you can trade ~33:24-34:05 [new]

## Definitions
- Million dollar swing setup: the six-stage checklist (seasonal, major market, intermarket, top-down, setup, management). It is a process, not a promise of profit ~0:55 [chart]
- Commercial "buying" condition: inside the upper half of their own 12-month net-position range, regardless of the sign of the net position ~7:05-7:51
- IPDA data ranges: 20/40/60 trading days back from today on the daily chart ~12:40
- Equilibrium: 50% of the intended swing range (here, the PD array to the objective). Each half can be split again (fractal quadrants) ~26:33-26:56
