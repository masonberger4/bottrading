## Rules
- Immediate rebalance: a large displacement candle, then the next candle trades straight back to touch the opposing candle's edge (wick to wick, no gap left). ICT treats it as one of the strongest signatures. With a bias, enter right away with a very tight stop. An FVG is a deferred rebalance that can stay open for days to years. ~3:46-4:40, ~1:03:50-1:04:20 [new]
- Sell program: while price keeps breaking lower, treat every up-close candle as resistance. Order flow stays bearish until price trades above up-close candles. On 15m, broken up-close candles should act as resistance. ~14:56-15:16, ~50:15 [new]
- After a higher-timeframe target is reached midweek following a large multi-week move, expect consolidation and/or a retracement, not a reversal. Bank partials, avoid heavy or max-leverage new shorts in deep discount, and expect sharp intraday reversals that run short-term buy stops. ~15:51-16:25, ~28:52-29:28, ~49:15 [new]
- Balanced price range: a range price traded back and forth through (overlapping candles) is "efficiently delivered". On re-entry it takes a large move to dig through it, so expect stalling around its midpoint/low. ~11:00-12:30, ~13:05-13:20, ~25:16-26:00 [chart]
- When a volume imbalance sits next to an FVG, extend the FVG box to include the VI (the zone runs from candle 1 low to candle 3 high plus the body gap). ~26:00, ~27:47-28:05 [chart] [new]
- Weekly framework: mark one premium draw and one discount draw. For the rest of the week, watch what price does at specific times of day for signs of a reach toward one of them. ~33:57-35:17 [chart] [new]
- Contingent plan (ES, Thu Sep 28): if price stays tight overnight and does not break the day's low, wait for the 09:30-10:00 opening range to rally into short-term buy-side (the 15m relative equal highs). Then look for an MSS lower, short any FVG, and target the low that formed before the equal highs were swept. ~35:36-36:30, ~51:56-52:33 [chart] [new]
- Holding runners: when holding several contracts, take all off but one, move its stop into profit, and put a limit order at the higher-timeframe level plus a couple of handles. Grow targets gradually (10 -> 15 -> 20 handles). ~37:51-38:21, ~41:04-41:18 [new] [conflict] with Ep 08, which exits 0.5-1 point before an FVG. Here the target sits beyond the level, so this may be a liquidity-pool vs FVG difference.
- A level is not support/resistance: the target is the liquidity beyond the level, so expect price to plow through it. ~1:10:02-1:10:29 [new]
- Selectivity: about 3 trades per week, only the best 4h/1h setups that lead to a weekly/daily draw on liquidity. ~47:36-47:59 [new]

## Definitions
- Immediate rebalance vs FVG (deferred rebalance). ~4:06-4:40
- Efficiently delivered price / balanced price range: multiple overlapping candles with the same high/low area traded back and forth. ~11:12-11:30
- "Redelivered, not rebalanced": price returns into an efficiently delivered range without an inefficiency to fill. ~12:29
- Sell program: a series of breaks lower where each up-close candle holds as resistance. ~14:56
- Sick sister: one correlated index reaches its target early; the other is expected to follow, not to diverge. ~26:30-27:20, ~55:07
- SMT = "smart money technique/tool", reading cracks in correlation. ~55:21
- Consequent encroachment of a daily wick = half of the wick below the body. ~1:00:27
