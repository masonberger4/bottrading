# cc05_018 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 05 - Position Trade Management (okN5p701T7Y)
"Lesson eight" of the January 2017 mentorship (~0:15) / USDJPY (forex) / weekly and daily (MT4) / 25m. Two slides (bullish and bearish checklists), then two USDJPY examples (short Jan 2016, long Oct-Nov 2016). No intraday content.

## Summary
Position (multi-month) trading checklist plus a stop-management rule built on the IPDA 20/40-day look-backs: after entry, trail the stop beyond the extreme of the last 40 trading days, and tighten to the last 20 trading days once price has covered enough of the expected weekly/monthly range. Risk at most 1% per trade, and never rush to breakeven. Directly usable as the stop logic for the parked swing bot.

## Rules
- Checklist (bull; mirror for bear): 1) expect a seasonal tendency, 2) intermarket confirmation, 3) interest-rate yield direction confirms, 4) monthly + weekly PD arrays give the objective, 5) expect a quarterly shift / intermediate swing, 6) daily PD arrays frame the setup, 7) choose stop or limit entry, 8) trail stop beyond the 40-day extreme ~0:27-5:57 [chart] [new]
- Intermarket: stocks, rates, commodities, currencies should agree; 3 of 4 agreeing is enough ~2:11-2:46 [new] (FX framing; for ES/NQ the bot would need bonds/DXY/commodities data)
- Quarterly shift: a new intermediate swing every 3-4 months; it lasts 2-3, at most 4 months. Aim for the middle of the move, not the exact high/low ~3:32-3:58, ~10:23-10:34 [new]
- Daily PD arrays for entry: OBs, liquidity voids, FVGs, rejection blocks, old highs/lows, mitigation blocks, breakers. Map every weekly/monthly PD array along the path in advance; they act as "speed bumps" or "rocket fuel" ~4:03-4:13, ~9:51-10:22 [new]
- Entry order: a limit can miss the fill; a stop order (buy on strength / sell on weakness) nearly always fills but makes the stop distance larger ~4:39-5:13, ~11:28-12:03 [new]
- Risk no more than 1% of the account per position; small allocation, long holding time ~5:13-5:32 [new]
- Initial and trailing stop: below the lowest low of the last 40 trading days (long) / above the highest high of the last 40 trading days (short). Recompute every trading day; it only moves in the trade's favor ~5:52-6:25, ~12:05-12:12, ~21:21-21:49 [new] [chart]
- Tighten to a 20-trading-day look-back late in the move (see the conflict in Open questions: slides say 3/4 of the expected range, the long example switches at 50%) ~7:48-8:05, ~13:59-15:09, ~24:22-25:19 [new] [chart]
- Measure "progress" with a fib from the entry swing extreme to the weekly/monthly objective (0 = range low, 100 = objective; equilibrium = 50%) ~16:22-16:47, ~24:09 [chart] [new]
- Do not move to breakeven early on long-term trades; give the market room ~13:13-13:24 [new] [conflict] with the intraday rule (Ep 03: stop to breakeven after buy-side is run)
- Reason for tightening: near 3/4 of the move a deep (OTE-like, up to ~79%) retracement can become the real reversal ~14:15-14:38 [new]
- The 40-day stop works because a trending market seeks the opposite 40/60-day liquidity, not its own 40-day extreme ~6:00-6:21, ~25:16-25:33 [new]

## Trade examples
1. USDJPY short (daily, read from chart). Weekly range: high ~125.9 (Jun 2015) to low ~99 (Jun 2016); weekly bearish OB (red) ~118.6-120.0, weekly bullish OB (blue) ~100.8-101.5 ~16:04-16:47 [chart]. After a daily structure break, price rallied in late Jan 2016 into the last up-close candle (bearish OB): open 121.69, high 121.72 ~17:30-18:04. Entry: sell limit above that candle's close or sell stop below it ~18:12-18:24. Stop above the 40-day high, about 250 pips -> ~260 pips with buffer ~18:27-19:07. He counted 8R (8 x 260 pips) to the objective; just short of 9R ~19:29-20:55 [chart]. Stop stayed above the 40-day high through the deep spring 2016 retracement (grey box ~111.5) ~21:52-22:08 [chart]. Objective hit at the weekly bullish OB (~Jun 2016).
2. USDJPY long (daily). Weekly bullish OB ~100.8-101.5 (blue), target the weekly bearish OB ~118.6-120 (red) ~22:23-22:32 [chart]. Bought on limits below the close of daily candles before the Nov 2016 election; the election whipsaw wick (~101.2) stayed above the 40-day low ~22:43-23:09 [chart]. 40-day low quoted as "109.19" in the captions; on the chart the lows are ~101.2, so likely 101.19 (caption error) ~23:32. Fib from range low to the bearish OB: equilibrium ~108.9; once price traded through it he switched to the 20-day low (red line ~111.2) ~24:09-25:02 [chart]. Objective (bearish OB) hit mid-Dec 2016.

## Definitions
- PDA / PD array: premium/discount array = OBs and other institutional reference points ~2:53-3:13
- Quarterly shift / intermediate-term swing: the 3-4 month directional leg the position trade aims to catch ~3:32, ~10:23
- "Range expected": distance from entry area to the weekly/monthly PD-array objective, used to grade progress ~7:15-7:37

## Seen only on the charts
- The short entry candle sat at about the 79% / OTE level of the weekly range (fib levels 62%, OTE, 79% drawn on the weekly) ~16:40 [chart]
- Grey boxes mark the 40-day high/low used as the stop level at each stage ~17:00, ~23:20 [chart]
- Price did not quite reach the far edge of the objective in either example; the near edge of the weekly OB was enough
