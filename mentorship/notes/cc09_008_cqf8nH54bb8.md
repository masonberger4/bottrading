## Summary
ICT's daily routine is a fixed checklist (on-screen slide ~41:20, again ~54:20): calendar -> kill-zone news -> reference-market order flow and PD arrays inside the IPDA 20/40/60-day ranges -> premium/discount -> the traded market's daily, 4h, 60m PD arrays -> opening prices (0 GMT, midnight NY) -> standard deviation projections (CBDR, Asian range, flout) and ADR -> 15m/5m PD arrays that converge with them -> intraday scenario templates by day of week. Then you build "if A then buy here, if fails then sell to there" plans.

## The routine, step by step (slide, ~41:20 [chart])
1. Determine the economic calendar impact for today: list high and medium impact events; can be done after the prior session closes (~0:27-1:05). [new]
2. Narrow focus to kill-zone news releases (high or medium): the market with a release inside London (02-05) or NY open kill zone is the one to trade that day; the release time is when manipulation (the low of day on a bullish day) is expected (~1:05-2:45). Example: 03:00 Spanish flash CPI -> EURUSD, expect manipulation at 03:00. [new]
3. Determine daily institutional order flow of USDX: are bullish OBs (down-close) or bearish OBs (up-close) being respected as price moves away; are lows or highs being broken (~16:10-16:47). [new as a step]
4. Locate USDX daily PD arrays inside the IPDA data ranges (~5:40-16:00).
5. Consider if USDX is moving from premium or discount of the 20-day range (~12:42-13:59).
6. Using the news due out, focus on that one market intraday (~2:31).
7. Determine daily institutional order flow of the pair (~22:54: EURUSD daily bullish).
8. Locate the pair's daily PD arrays in IPDA ranges (~18:00-20:56).
9. Confirm the pair is moving from an opposing PD array (in premium -> expect a retrace to discount arrays before buys) (~23:03-23:31).
10. Locate the pair's 4h PD arrays (~22:31-29:00).
11. Locate the pair's 60m PD arrays (~30:19-32:47).
12. Note the 0 GMT opening price and consider the daily range.
13. At midnight NY calculate STDV levels: CBDR, Asian range, flout standard deviations (~33:03-33:20, ~40:37).
14. Note the midnight NY opening price.
15. Look for 15m/5m PD arrays that converge with the STDV levels (~46:19-46:39).
16. Consider the intraday scenarios (templates) that could unfold.
17. ADR high and low can help confirm the STDV levels (~40:49-41:13).

## Rules
- IPDA data ranges: count yesterday's daily candle as day 1 and go back 20, 40, 60 trading days (skip Sunday candles). They roll forward one day every day (~6:03-6:58). [new]
- Day trading and scalping work inside the 20-day range; only if all PD arrays inside it have been traded through, use the 40-day range (~8:47-9:21). [new]
- Premium/discount = where price sits in the 20-day high-low range; also check where it sits in the 40/60-day ranges for a macro view (~12:46-13:40). [new]
- Quarterly shift: every ~3 months expect a possible shift in market structure lasting 2-3 months; not guaranteed (~7:46-8:15). [new]
- PD array priority: when stacking levels above price, take the nearest first; a breaker takes precedence over everything else. Breaker level uses the down candle's body low, not the wick (~9:59-10:24). [new]
- FVG anchor: start the FVG at the last up-close candle's low (bearish case), not a down-close candle (~12:19-12:35). [new]
- An OB whose mean threshold has already been traded on both sides is ignored for MT (~14:37-14:45). [new]
- A down candle whose high is taken out by the next candle, which makes a higher high, is a bullish "propulsion" OB; use its open as the level (~14:47-15:41). [new]
- Keep charts clean: mark only levels near price; add the rest as price approaches (~17:39-17:45, ~21:37).
- Trade with daily institutional order flow; counter-trend day trades are allowed only from a range extreme (discount of the 20-day range after an old low is swept) (~16:57-17:20). [new]
- Invalidation of a 4h bearish OB: trading through its mean threshold voids near-term bearishness; the prior down candles become the bullish OB (~26:37-27:30). [new]
- Bullish day entry: midnight NY 15m open, then a small decline below it into a discount PD array (15m) that converges with CBDR/Asian/flout STDVs; buy there. ICT prefers midnight NY over 0 GMT to avoid overtrading (~33:25-34:07, ~46:07-46:39). [conflict-ish: matches Ep 04 midnight-open rule, adds STDV confluence]
- FX classic day: open declines ~1/3 of the daily range (e.g. ~33 pips of 100) before the low (~46:50-47:04). [forex; index analog untested]
- Targets: STDV projections and the ADR high/low that converge with an opposing premium array on 15m/60m/4h/daily (~47:07-47:25). [new]
- ADR below 60 pips -> a large-range day is due; if ADR is reached before or at the NY open, keep a runner, the move can run past 10:30-11:00 into 13:00-14:00 (~47:30-48:02). [new, FX units]
- Scenario rule: if your buy at the planned level is stopped, the stop-out is the signal; flip to selling the next day: wait for a rally above the 0 GMT / midnight open into a premium array, short on 15m or 5m, target the next lower daily PD array (~36:23-37:24, ~54:35-54:58). [new]
- Bullish order flow -> buy Mon/Tue/Wed; bearish -> sell Mon/Tue/Wed. Avoid buying Friday after a strong Thursday (profit taking). After a Monday holiday, Tuesday acts like Monday and Wednesday is the likely reversal day (~41:53-42:28, ~55:24-55:35, ~57:14-57:33). [new]
- Do not trade every day; one leg of a weekly template is enough (~43:07-43:22, ~45:12-45:22).
- Use the routine daily to chip away at a weekly objective in pieces (~51:45-52:02).

## Definitions
- IPDA data ranges: 20/40/60 trading-day lookbacks from yesterday (~5:26-6:14).
- Institutional order flow: which OBs are respected as price leaves them (~16:10).
- Propulsion candle/block: see Rules (~15:05).
- Flout: CBDR plus Asian range combined (central bank "daily range") (~33:11) [forex].
