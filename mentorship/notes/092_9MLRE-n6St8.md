## Rules
- 15m is the first chart: mark buy-side (above short-term highs / relative equal highs), sell-side, and imbalances; then drop to 5m, 1m, sub-minute. ~2:30
- Silver Bullet windows (NY time): 03:00-04:00 (London), 10:00-11:00, 14:00-15:00 and 15:00-16:00. "Price run really begins" at 03:00; before that price waits at the premium array. ~20:04-21:05 [new: 03:00-04:00 London and 14:00-15:00 windows]
- Silver Bullet entry = the small sub-minute FVG that forms when price, in sell-side delivery, retraces into a higher-TF PD array (here 5m breaker + inversion FVG). Use 30s or lower charts; keep 1m and 5m open alongside. ~48:52, ~51:07, ~57:21 [chart] [new]
- In sell-side delivery, up-close candles (bearish OB) and inversion FVGs should act as resistance; that is where shorts are added. Mirror for longs. ~42:32, ~57:41 [new]
- Inversion FVG: an FVG that price trades through (here a 5m BISI on the far side of a bearish breaker); extend it forward, it flips to resistance. ~22:26, ~36:49 [chart] [new]
- Balanced price range + FVG: a SIBI with a single opposite candle beside it (island-like) is stronger; he would size up to 15-20 contracts on it vs max ~10 on a plain FVG. ~9:44-10:27 [new]
- Bearish: the upper half of each new SIBI should stay open; price should react at its 25% or 50% level and drop "aggressively". Bullish: lower half of a BISI stays open. ~59:47, ~1:08:09, ~1:09:46 [chart] [new]
- If price trades into the upper part of the SIBI and meanders instead of rejecting hard, close the trade (take what profit there is). ~1:04:38-1:05:42 [new]
- Time stop: once price touches the far edge of that SIBI, it has 2 minutes to move back in your favor out of the range, else take half or all off. ~1:11:05 [new]
- Pyramiding only in premium: frame the implied dealing range (swing high -> sell-side target, not yet delivered); add shorts only on FVG/OB formed at or above its 50%. Arrays below EQ are observation levels only, no new or added positions. ~1:15:24-1:17:49 [chart] [new]
- Standard deviation projections: anchor on the manipulation leg (the run above the old high back to the start of the drop); targets are -2, -2.5, -3 SD, preferred where they converge with a PD array beyond the obvious sell-side. ~1:21:26-1:26:31 [chart] [conflict-ish: Ep 06 anchors London low to high; here high to low of the London run]
- Partials: default is a partial at the obvious sell-side liquidity; hold the full position (no partials) only when price goes through that low with "speed and distance". Higher-volatility conditions -> more partials. ~1:28:54-1:30:30 [new]
- Bias held only while bodies respect the PD array: bodies not closing through the CE of the 15m SIBI = bearish; once price gets above the CE, the bearish case is gone. ~1:51:41, ~2:10:25 [chart]
- Opening range gap (RTH): previous 16:15 close (Friday 16:15 on Mondays) to the 09:30 open. Levels: high, 75%, 50%, 25%, low. Track in a template, dim once filled, still useful for ~60 days. ~1:55:54-2:00:36 [new]
- NWOG: keep the last ~5 weeks on the chart. ~1:57:26 [new]
- Order block = the opening price of the uppermost of consecutive up-close candles after buy stops were taken (a "change in state of delivery"); has nothing to do with engulfing. ~2:02:31 [chart]
- Immediate rebalance: after an impulse candle the next candle trades back into the prior candle's range at once. Expansion should come on that candle or the next; if not, expect consolidation/reversal, so exit. ~1:41:14-1:41:58 [new]

## Definitions
- Price spooling: price running one way toward liquidity or an inefficiency, not back and forth. ~30:09
- Buy-side / sell-side delivery: price moving up / down (offering), vs buy-side / sell-side liquidity (stops). ~30:53
- Mohawk: a small wick past the expected level. ~9:00
- Implied dealing range: from the swing high to the target not yet delivered. ~1:16:00
- Deferred rebalance (FVG, revisited later) vs immediate rebalance. ~1:40:48
- Full pull: holding the whole position to the target, no partials. ~1:29:40
- Fib settings: 0, 1, 0.5, 0.25 (quadrants); swaps 0.25 for 0.2 for the "TGIF" weekly retracement. ~1:07:24
