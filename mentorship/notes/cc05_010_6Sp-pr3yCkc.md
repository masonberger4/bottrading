## Summary
Seasonal tendency charts (40-year and 15-year composites of a futures market) are a "roadmap" for which quarter of the year a market tends to rally or fall. Use them only as confluence with an existing HTF bias and the quarterly shift, never alone. When the forex pair quotes USD first (USDCAD), invert the futures seasonal: CAD futures weak Sep to near Christmas = USDCAD bullish. Crude oil's March-June seasonal rally is shown as a related market (Canada's main export). Bearish seasonals and all currency seasonals are deferred to lesson 4.2.

## Rules
- Seasonals are not a panacea; past tendency does not guarantee future moves; use only as one more confluence item ~1:10-1:47 [new]
- Only take a seasonal in the direction the market is already predisposed to (HTF order flow / structure). In years where the weekly trend opposed it (USDCAD 2009, 2010) the bullish seasonal failed ~9:21-9:59 [chart] [new]
- Couple seasonals with the quarterly shift: a large HTF move every 3-4 months; seasonals hint whether that move should be up or down ~11:35-12:24 [new]
- Futures-to-forex inversion: for USD-first pairs (USDCAD, USDJPY, USDCHF style), a bearish futures seasonal = bullish pair seasonal, and vice versa ~6:17-7:47, ~11:04-11:35 [chart] [new] (forex-only)
- CAD futures: bullish mid-March to May/June; bearish September to just before Christmas -> USDCAD bullish in that window ~5:19-5:35, ~7:07-7:47, ~12:49 [chart] (forex-only)
- Crude oil: bullish seasonal mid-Feb/March into May-June (to ~July 1), held even in the 2014-2016 bear market ~14:00-16:26 [chart] [new]
- Focus on the markets/quarters with the strongest seasonal; do not trade every seasonal in many markets (his early mistake) ~17:04-17:44 [new]

## Definitions
- Seasonal tendency chart: composite of each year's price action over 40 years (blue, 1976-2015) and 15 years (red), plotted by calendar month on a 0-100 scale, with vertical lines at contract delivery months; currency futures deliver March, June, September, December ~0:42-2:35, ~3:48 [chart]
- Quarterly shift: an HTF move roughly every 3-4 months (referenced from cc05_003) ~11:48
