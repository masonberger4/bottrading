# cc08_005 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 08 - Intraday Profiles (eU8WcUftUtY)
April 2017 Core Content, lesson 5 of the day trading model. Market: forex (London session); schematic slides plus unlabeled FX example charts (15m/intraday). Duration ~21 min.

## Summary
Four intraday templates for trading the London session, two per direction: "London normal protraction" (Judas swing starts right after midnight NY and runs into 02:00) and "London delayed protraction" (no midnight Judas; the counter-move starts at or just after 02:00 and is a retracement into an intraday PD array). Bias comes from the daily chart first; the profile only decides when and where to enter. If neither template appears, no trade.

## Rules
- Bias first: decide bullish/bearish from the daily chart (respecting support/resistance, which liquidity it seeks); then only the two profiles for that direction are allowed ~19:29-20:17 [new]
- Session windows on the slides: CBDR 14:00-20:00 NY, Asian range 20:00-00:00 NY, then London open, NY open, London close (LC), noon NY end line ~1:53-2:00 [chart] [new]
- Normal protraction filters (both directions): CBDR < 40 pips AND Asian range 20-30 pips ~1:25-3:12 [chart] [new]
- Exception: CBDR > 40 is acceptable if the Asian range then tightens to a 20-30 pip consolidation; if CBDR is wide and Asia is not tight, skip London ~4:14-5:46 [new]
- Sell, normal: price rallies immediately after 00:00 NY into 02:00 NY (a small, modest dip right after midnight is allowed). No rally 00:00-02:00 -> not this profile ~2:16-3:24, ~5:50-6:31 [chart] [new]
- By 01:00-01:30 price should have made a higher high than the Asian range / CBDR; entry can come as early as 01:00-01:30 ~6:11-6:44 [new]
- Judas swing size = 1-2 SD of the CBDR; HOD (sell) typically between 02:00 and 04:00 NY ~3:28-3:34, ~7:09-7:22 [chart] [new]
- Buy, normal: mirror. Decline right after 00:00 NY into 02:00 (LOD can form just ahead of 02:00); consistent lower prices past 01:00-01:30 confirm; Judas 1-2 SD below CBDR = projected low ~11:31-13:51 [chart] [new]
- Delayed protraction (either direction): CBDR may or may not be favorable; Asian range small helps but is not required ~8:16-8:34, ~14:40-15:18 [chart] [new]
- Sell, delayed: price just erodes lower after midnight (no rally). Draw a vertical line at 02:00 NY on the 15m chart; wait for a rally at or shortly after 02:00 that does NOT make a new high; short at an intraday premium PD array (bearish OB, FVG fill, liquidity void) ~8:41-10:47 [chart] [new]
- Buy, delayed: mirror; after 02:00 NY a retracement lower into an intraday discount PD array (OTE long, bullish OB, FVG/liquidity void below price) ~15:21-17:10 [chart] [new]
- The delayed Judas should occur between 02:00 and 03:00 NY ~21:21-21:29 [new]
- Watch from 00:00 to at least 02:30-03:00 NY; that window decides the London trade ~19:05-19:24 [new]
- Template outcome: LOD/HOD in London, continuation through NY, HOD (buy) / LOD (sell) around London close, then consolidation into the daily close ~7:44-7:57, ~18:00-18:10 [chart]
- If neither profile appears, accept missing a trend day (even a 90-150 pip day) ~20:26-20:48 [new]

## Definitions
- Asian range: 20:00 to 00:00 NY ~1:53 [new]
- London normal protraction: Judas swing that starts immediately after 00:00 NY and runs into 02:00 ~2:41 [new]
- London delayed protraction: Judas/retracement that starts at or shortly after 02:00 NY, staying inside the day's existing range (no new extreme) ~8:51-9:26 [new]
- Float building: CBDR and Asian consolidations let orders build above and below price; wide ranges mean the float wasn't allowed to build ~4:36-4:45, ~12:45-12:49 [new]
