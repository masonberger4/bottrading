# cc03_004 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 03 - The Next Setup - Anticipatory Skill Development (9kabTfUEVKg)
Recorded Nov 2016 (4th of 8 November teachings) / forex only (USDCAD, USDJPY, NZDUSD) / monthly, weekly, daily (hourly mentioned) / ~15 min.

## Summary
A top-down "where is the next setup" routine: on the monthly chart, frame a range between the most recent down-close candle and the most recent up-close candle that sits beyond it. Whichever of the two price has violated becomes the active order block; wait for price to return to it and trade toward the opposite candle. Then drill the same levels down to weekly, daily and hourly to refine entry and risk. All examples are FX; the method itself is timeframe/instrument-agnostic and could feed index-futures HTF bias.

## Rules
- Mark the open, high, low and close of each of the last 3 monthly candles and carry them to lower timeframes as reference levels ~1:46-2:35 [new]
- Monthly range = most recent down-close candle plus the nearest prior up-close candle whose low is above that down candle's high (bullish case); mirror for bearish ~2:46-3:44, ~8:15 [new] [chart]
- Price trading above a down candle's high "activates" it as a bullish order block; wait for price to come back to it, then expect a move up toward the last up candle (the opposing monthly bearish OB) ~4:50, ~8:22 [new]
- Mirror: price breaking an up candle's low activates it as a bearish OB; sell the return to it, target the last down candle / lows below ~12:03, ~14:21 [new]
- Bullish OB buy zone: at or below the down candle's open/body ("buyer at 103.26 or less" for USDJPY) ~9:52 [chart]
- Use the larger-bodied down candle of a cluster as the start of the order block; its mean threshold (50%) should not be challenged even on news spikes (US election) ~10:30-10:52 [chart] (consistent with Ep 02 mean threshold)
- Multiple down candles in a row can together form one bullish OB once price trades through them ~4:46 [new]
- Lower timeframes (weekly, daily, hourly) are only for refining entry and risk inside the monthly framework ~13:48 [new]
- Partial/cover target: before the swing low, at the cluster of candle bodies/closes (e.g. NZDUSD bodies 0.7005 vs. objective 0.6983-0.6985) ~12:22-12:35 [chart] (consistent with Ep 08 "exit before the exact level")

## Definitions
- Monthly "range": span from most recent down candle to most recent opposing up candle (or vice versa) ~14:00.
- Activation: an OB is only valid after price trades through the opposite extreme of that candle (above a down candle's high, below an up candle's low) ~14:13-14:30.
