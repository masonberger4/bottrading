## Summary
A liquidity pool is the cluster of resting stops beyond an old high or old low. Use higher-TF bias to decide which side gets raided. When bullish, place a buy limit at or just under an old low so you buy the sell stops as they trigger. Take profit at the opposite pool: buy stops above old or equal highs. When bearish, do the mirror: sell above old highs.

## Rules
- Bias first (higher-TF work). If bullish, wait for price to trade below an old low; if bearish, wait for it to trade above an old high. Do not buy breakouts or bull flags ~2:42-3:12, ~7:25-8:48 [new as stated, consistent with turtle soup Ep 08]
- Find the pools by role play: if I were long, where is my protective sell stop, and if short, my buy stop? Old highs/lows and equal highs/lows; no order book or DOM needed ~4:43-7:25
- Entry (bullish): before price reaches the low, place a buy limit at or just below the recent low ~9:45 [chart] [new]
- Risk (forex, 15m/30m): expect a sweep of 10-20 pips below the old low. The ideal entry is under the low, not above it, with a 30-50 pip stop. Entering above the low is fear of missing out ~10:38-11:22 [chart] [new]
- Invalidation: if price runs more than about 25 pips below the low, it is probably a real decline, not a stop run ~11:22-11:34 [new]
- Targets: the opposite pool. Buy stops above old highs or relative equal highs; also a bearish OB, an FVG or a liquidity void. Take partials at layered buy-stop levels ~6:42-6:54, ~12:00-12:28, ~18:51-19:05 [chart]
- Daily levels feed intraday entries. Take the previous day's low (he also mentions sweeping below daily candle bodies) and put it on the 15m chart as the raid level ~12:57-13:43 [chart] [new]
- A news day (e.g. an interest-rate announcement) is an expected raid day ~13:43-13:55 [chart]
- Friday after a trending week: expect profit taking and chop. Run the nearby buy stops, then the sell stops under relative equal lows; don't expect a new high ~17:59-18:25 [chart] [new]

## Definitions
- Liquidity: open interest of buyers and sellers, as orders resting at or near specific price levels ~0:38-0:54
- Liquidity pool: a collection of resting orders (stops of trapped traders plus breakout entries) above an old high (buy stops) or below an old low (sell stops) ~4:11-4:27
- Run on a bullish liquidity pool (slide): the low under current price holds trailed sell stops of longs plus breakout-short sell stops. Validation: price trades below the low, the stops become market sells, and smart money buys them ~9:01-9:59 [chart]
- Market efficiency paradigm: buy sell stops below lows, sell to buy stops above highs, like a market maker ~12:28-12:44
