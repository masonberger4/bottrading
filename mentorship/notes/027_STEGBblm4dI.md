## Rules
- Propulsion block: a down-close candle trading into an older order block. On a retrace, price
  may touch it but must not go below its midpoint. A break of that midpoint = expect the next PD
  array lower (FVG / volume imbalance) [new] ~11:31-12:57, ~14:59 [chart]
- Volume imbalances are "flexible": price may chop through them and reuse them as S/R. FVGs are
  held strictly; a broken FVG only counts again after price leaves and returns (reclaimed) [new] ~22:09-22:56
- Overshoot allowance: a small PD array may be overshot up to the low of the adjacent candle;
  "seamless delivery" [new] ~16:13-16:37
- Many volume imbalances close together = "spotty/porous" price: back-and-forth, hard to place a
  stop, low-trust trading [new] ~27:38-29:06
- Long stop must sit below any inefficiency under the entry, not just under the FVG
  (example ~4089.25) [new] ~30:20-30:36
- Confirmation when long into a bearish OB overhead: price should tear quickly into the body and
  through its mean threshold (50%). Stalling or bouncing off it = consolidation or reversal risk.
  Same test for a bearish FVG's CE [new] ~31:02-32:59, ~35:02-37:20 [chart]
- Want the next candle to "rip" immediately; small hesitant candles are a warning [new] ~33:57-34:37
- Exit rule: once three PD arrays break against the trade, close it or take some off [new] ~38:26-38:37
- After a partial at buy-side, the stop can go to +1 point; keep a runner with a limit near the
  next high, since the 15:45-15:50 macro can extend [new] ~40:10-40:58
- Annotate each new high and take a partial there ("running down equity"); after a higher high
  the last swing low must hold ~43:36-44:13
- Fund stops are layered up to ~10 handles above old highs, so late-day runs overshoot old
  intraday highs [new] ~44:20-44:49
- Market-on-close macro: if the liquidity pool is taken before 15:50, expect a small flurry,
  then a pullback into the range. An up day rarely closes on its high unless it trends [new] ~50:40-52:02
- Close the trade before 16:00; don't hold past the close [new] ~58:22-58:54
- After a run on the highs, a re-broken FVG means "probably done for the day", so close [new] ~57:44-58:12
- Fed speakers can make price wild; the usual rules may not apply on those days [new] ~25:43-26:12
- On MES, if volume imbalances don't print, read them on the ES chart [new] ~23:02-23:36
