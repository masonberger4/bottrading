# cc02_007 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 02 - Market Maker Trap False Flag (cRbPS3uxkj4)
Recorded October 2016 ("October 2016 - ICT Teaching 7 of 8"), 2016-17 Private Mentorship. Market: AUDUSD (forex). Timeframes: daily, 4h, 1h, 15m, 5m. Duration ~29 min.

## Summary
A bull flag that forms in a higher-timeframe premium/distribution area (or a bear flag in discount/accumulation) is a trap: retail expects the measured-move continuation, smart money reverses it. Use top-down context (daily premium/discount, higher-TF order blocks, body-based old highs/lows); when the "flag" breaks the wrong way, or runs a short-term high/low and fails (turtle soup), trade the reversal from the last opposing candle.

## Rules
- Do not trade flag continuation patterns by themselves; check the daily (at least 4h) for premium/discount first. ~08:47-09:05 [new]
- Bull flag in a mature uptrend or in a higher-TF premium/distribution zone = suspect; expect reversal instead of the measured move. Mirror: bear flag in discount/accumulation. ~01:52-02:24, ~03:38-04:17 [new]
- Premium test: fib from the daily swing high to the swing low before the retracement; price above equilibrium = premium (distribution). ~12:05-12:51 [chart] (consistent with premium/discount in the rules file)
- Use candle BODIES, not wicks, for the old highs/lows that the flag "clears"; heaviest volume is in the bodies. ~10:58-11:05, ~21:07-22:03 [chart] [new]
- Short trigger (false bull flag): price pokes above the short-term high (turtle soup) and rolls over; sell the first return to the bearish order block (last up candle before the drop, entry at its open). Stop above the flag's high wick. ~16:42-17:27 [chart] (matches turtle soup plan in the rules file)
- Long trigger (false bear flag): do not buy the first rally; wait for a swing high to form and be broken, then buy the return to the last down candle; stop below the flag's low. ~22:28-23:10, ~25:40-26:03 [chart] (consistent with MSS + OB entry)
- First target: the liquidity void the false flag created; then the opposing OBs, equal highs/lows (liquidity pools), old highs/lows. ~17:36-17:46, ~23:16-24:10 [chart]
- In the up/down candles that start a move, the sensitive level for shorts is from the bodies down through their low wicks. ~16:13-16:21 [new]
- Equilibrium of a short run of candles: measure the two up candles' range body-low to body-high; 50% = the reaction level (here also the bottom of the last 5m up candle). ~15:20-15:40 [chart] [new]
- Typical false flag: rally, consolidation (sideways or drifting against the trend), a slightly higher short-term high, then collapse; mirror for bear flags. This is turtle soup. ~27:20-29:49 [chart]
- Think sentiment: look for what retail chart-pattern traders will expect, and position against it at institutional levels. ~18:41-18:58, ~26:12-26:37 [new]

## Definitions
- False flag: flag-shaped consolidation after a sudden move that reverses instead of continuing; a market-maker trap. ~00:47, ~01:42 [new]
- Bull/bear flag: impulse (flagpole), small sideways or counter-sloped consolidation, second equal leg (measured move). ~04:44-06:28
- Premium/distribution (discount/accumulation): upper (lower) half of the higher-TF swing range. ~12:22-12:51 [chart]
- IPDA: interbank price delivery algorithm; fills liquidity voids it left. ~15:16
