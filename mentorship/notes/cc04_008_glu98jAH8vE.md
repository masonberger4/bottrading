# cc04_008 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 04 - ICT Propulsion Block (glu98jAH8vE)
2016-17 Core Content, slide "December 2016 - ICT Teaching 3.5 of 8" (Reinforcing Order Block Theory). Market: forex (MT4 charts, 15m, 16-19 Dec 2016; pair not legible). Duration ~7:50. Concept transfers directly to index futures.

## Summary
A propulsion block is a down-close candle (bullish case) that trades down into an earlier bullish order block and then becomes the new, higher support. On a later return price should touch its high (or at most its mean threshold) and leave immediately. Losing its mean threshold means the trade is wrong: tight stop, fast feedback.

## Rules
- Bullish propulsion block: in an up context, a new down-close candle whose range trades down into a previous down-close candle / bullish OB. That new candle is the propulsion block and acts as support for higher prices ~0:30, ~1:02 [chart] [new]
- Slide: ideal setup is in a major-to-intermediate-term uptrend ~1:00 [chart] [new]
- On a return to it, expect price to hit its high (maybe 1-2 pips below) and then leave at once. Allowed depth is its mean threshold. Price should not trade through the MT ~1:24-2:00 [new]
- MT here = half of the candle's body ("middle of the range of that candle's body") ~1:27 [new] [conflict: Ep 02 measures OB mean threshold high-to-low with wicks; this video says body; the 15m example cuts "the candle in half", which looks like the full range ~6:20]
- Invalidation: if the propulsion candle loses its MT, the long is probably wrong. Use an "ultra tight" stop beyond the MT, stand aside, or look to reverse ~5:15-5:40 [new]
- Bearish mirror: an up-close candle that trades up into a prior bearish OB (last up candle) is a bearish propulsion block. Sell on a return to its MT, or later on a return up to the propulsion candle's low, but only after price has broken below that low ~5:45-7:15 [chart] [new]
- Consecutive same-color candles count as one OB (three down candles = one bearish leg = one bullish OB). The OB is confirmed when price trades through the highest of those down candles ~2:39-3:48 [chart] (consistent with Ep 08 multi-candle OB)

## Definitions
- Propulsion block/candle: a candle that already traded into an OB of the same type, so it is predisposed to move in the bias direction. It is "highly sensitive": returns should barely reach it ~1:02-2:10
- Mean threshold: 50% of the candle ("body" in the speech, full candle in the chart drawing) ~1:27, ~6:24
