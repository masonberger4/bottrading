## Definitions
- SIBI = down-close candle FVG; BISI = up-close candle FVG ~11:02
- Volume imbalance: gap between one candle's close and the next one's open with no body overlapping ~13:01
- Rejection block (as used): the lowest close in a run of down candles before the turn; the wick below is measured to its CE ~31:01, ~34:00 [chart] (fills the gap noted in Ep 03)
- Relative equal highs: highs close but not equal -> buy-side above ~38:00
- Low vs high resistance liquidity run: a symmetric market runs fast; a laggard gets there slowly with many stops ~42:01, ~1:50:00
- Fulcrum point: the low (when bearish) whose break should release a move equal to the prior run ~1:29:01, ~1:34:04
- Mental capital: stress spent deciding in bad conditions, separate from money ~1:20:01
