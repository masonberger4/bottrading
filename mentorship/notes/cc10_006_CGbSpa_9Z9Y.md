# cc10_006 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Bond Trading - Basics & Opening Range Concept (CGbSpa_9Z9Y)
June 2017 Core Content (bond lesson 1). Market: 30-year T-bond futures ZBU17 (Sep 2017), June 2017 sessions. Timeframes: 15m (examples), 30m (overview), with volume. ~18 min.

## Summary
Defines the T-bond "true day" (08:00-15:00 NY) and the 08:00-09:00 opening range (OR), which tends to set the day's high or low and is where liquidity builds for the stock-market-open raid at 09:30. Trades: stop run beyond the OR then reverse, or a bullish OB inside the OR; volume divergence on the post-OR extreme flags a false move.

## Rules
- Bond analysis session = New York, 08:20 to 15:00 NY ~00:01:02 [chart] [new]
- Highest volume 08:00-09:30 NY ~00:03:22 [chart] [new]
- True day for bonds = 08:00-15:00 NY [chart] [new] [conflict: index rules use 08:30/09:30 opens and midnight true open; this is bond-specific] ~00:03:22
- Opening range = high and low of 08:00-09:00 NY ~00:03:46 [chart] [new]
- OR 08:00-09:00 tends to create the bond high or low of the day; it can be a run on stops or a fair value setup (OB, fill into a liquidity void / FVG) ~00:03:46 [chart] [new]
- The OR is where liquidity pools build for the stock-market-open (09:30) raid ~00:04:02 [chart] [new]
- Setup A (stop run & reverse): after 09:00, price trades beyond the OR low, makes a lower low on lower volume than the OR bars (volume divergence) -> reversal; OR low/high then acts as support on the retest ~00:04:49-00:06:32 [chart] [new]
- Setup B (OB in the OR): last down-close candle(s) inside the OR = bullish OB; buy the retest (first or second test both valid) ~00:06:54-00:09:29 [chart] [new]
- Exit/fade cue: a new high made on declining volume vs the earlier push (volume divergence) = weak move, "volume precedes price" ~00:07:25-00:08:20, ~00:09:29-00:10:17 [chart] [new]
- Defining the OR: like the Asian range, use bodies but incorporate wicks, and incorporate previous highs/lows just left of 08:00-09:00 (e.g. overnight equal highs a tick above the OR high) ~00:10:34-00:11:18 [chart] [new]
- Even using the highest high within the OR, price still respects the level later ~00:06:19 [chart]
- Signal timing: prefers signals at 08:20 (CME open) or after, ideally 08:00-08:30; can form from 08:00 or slightly before ~00:11:33-00:12:06 [new]
- News: stay out of bonds ahead of FOMC / rate decisions / NFP; market gaps to liquidity, usually against you ~00:13:07-00:14:04 [consistent with FOMC/NFP rules]
- Expectations: small daily range; 5-8 ticks intraday is fine, 16 ticks ($500) a good day, 32 ticks ($1000) a large-range day ~00:15:02-00:15:33 [new]

## Trade examples
- ZBU17 15m, ex 1 [chart ~00:05:00-00:06:20]: overnight selloff into 08:00; OR ~153-21 to ~153-27 (body box; highest high ~153-28 blue line). ~09:30-09:45 lower low ~153-19 on low volume = stop run & reverse; rally back above OR high, OR high held as support ~11:00-12:00, high of day ~154-10 near 13:30.
- ZBU17 15m, ex 2 [chart ~00:07:20-00:08:00]: OR ~153-26 to ~154-00; bullish OB (last down candles in OR) ~153-29/154-00; rally to 154-21 by ~11:00 with falling volume (divergence), then sold back into the OR and consolidated.
- ZBU17 15m, ex 3 [chart ~00:09:20-00:11:20]: OR ~153-29 to 154-21 (tall 08:00 bar; OR high includes wick and ~154-19 overnight equal highs); bullish OB 154-00 to 154-02 tested twice (~09:00-09:30 and ~12:30); rally; 154-26 high in the evening on light volume ran above the OR high, failed, closed into the FVG ~154-10.

## Definitions
- ZB contract: CBOT, months H/M/U/Z, symbol format ZB+code+YY (ZBU17); tick = 1/32 = $31.25; 32 ticks = 1 handle = $1000 ~00:01:19-00:02:42 [chart]. Prices quoted 154-21 = 154 21/32.
- Volume divergence: new price extreme without a higher volume bar ~00:06:03
