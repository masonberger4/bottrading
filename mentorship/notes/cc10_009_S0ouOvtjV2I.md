## Summary
Trending (large-range) days come after a small-range day or a series of shrinking daily ranges, when price sits at a daily discount/premium array and high/medium-impact US news is due at 08:30. The trigger is a bond-complex divergence (SMT) at the 08:00-08:30 NY lows; then rates move and other markets are allowed to expand.

## Rules
- Trending-day recipe: (1) previous day or several days of small/contracting daily ranges, (2) price recently traded into a daily discount array (bullish) or premium array (bearish), (3) high/medium-impact US reports at 08:30 = volatility injection. Expect expansion away from that array ~1:17-2:46 [chart] [new]
- Direction comes from the daily PD array matrix and institutional order flow; movement is liquidity-seeking ~1:28-1:35 [chart] [new]
- Overnight action (trending or ranging) is not a precursor for NY ~0:57 [chart]
- FOMC days: the AM session may be traded if the recipe is present; never be in the afternoon (14:00 release) ~3:48-4:46 [chart] (matches Ep 03; [new] that the AM is explicitly allowed)
- Trigger (bullish): compare lows of 30-year bond, 10-year note (and 5-year) in the 08:00-08:30 NY window; one makes a lower low while the other does not = smart-money accumulation, expect expansion up ~4:55-6:31, ~8:13-8:30, ~14:04-14:19 [chart] [new]
- Smart money entry shows as displacement (large, obvious moves, not small ones) ~6:18-6:28 [new]
- Do not trade every day; cherry-pick days where all criteria line up, since daily forcing whittles away the big winning days ~10:45-11:15 [new]
- Bonds up = rates down = dollar weaker; bonds down = rates up = dollar stronger ~14:28-14:54, ~19:32-19:48 (FX-only consequence)
- HTF analysis of bonds on daily, 4h and 2h charts ~8:00-8:08 [new]
