# cc07_008 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 07 - One Shot One Kill Model (LBlK6JB0QS0)
Date discussed: week of Mon 27 - Fri 31 Mar 2017 / market: EURUSD, with DXY and EURGBP as intermarket checks (forex only) / timeframes: weekly, daily, 4h, 1h, 15m / duration ~37m.
Lesson 8 of the 2016-17 short-term module (last of the March content). OSOK = one weekly-range trade per week: pick the high (or low) of the week and hold for the week's projected range.

## Summary
OSOK is a top-down weekly trade. Directional premise from macro inputs (quarterly shift, HTF PD arrays in the 20/40/60-day IPDA ranges, rates, seasonals, COT commercial hedging, intermarket), then a weekly profile (e.g. Monday high of week), then an intraday fib projection to pick the exact weekly extreme, then the opposing PD array (a liquidity void) as the weekly target. Example: sold EURUSD ~1.0908 Monday (actual high 1.0909), target 1.0650 (Friday low 1.0655 on his feed).

## Rules (the model, in his order)
Prerequisites (slide) ~0:27-2:14 [chart]
- Know the macro/January material; IPDA data ranges (last 20, 40, 60 trading days) and PD array matrix applied to time and price; position and swing model concepts; power of three applied to weekly candles; intraday (day-trading) concepts; kill zones for entries; seasonals; COT and commercial hedging [new]
- Day-of-week: the weekly high or low forms Monday-Wednesday about 70% of the time (slide says 70%; caption reads "76") ~1:35 [new][chart]
Trade procedure (slide, 13 steps) ~6:20-10:10 [chart]
1. Determine the current or potential next quarterly shift.
2. Identify HTF PD arrays inside the IPDA data ranges.
3. Check interest-rate differentials / market profile of rates (bonds trending = room to move; rates consolidating = currency will struggle to move) [forex-only].
4. Scout seasonals; only use them when the long-term and short-term seasonal lines agree in direction ~13:31-13:47.
5. Swing analysis HTF down to 60m: classify impulse / retracement / expansion swings to choose which swings to measure ~7:24-7:55.
6. Anticipate the weekly profile (bearish: Monday high of week, Tuesday high of week, Wednesday reversal).
7. Prepare for the market-maker manipulation that goes with that profile (weekly Judas swing).
8. Mark premium and discount ranges.
9. Wait for volatility: enter when the market is quiet (small ranges) so a large range can follow ~8:49-9:10 [new]
10. COT and open interest to confirm smart money ~9:14.
11. Frame a low-resistance liquidity run between opposing PD arrays: sell at a premium array, paired with a discount array as the target ~9:26-9:43 [new]
12. Fib levels must converge with the opposing PD array, blended with time and price ~9:47-9:57.
13. Confirm with intermarket analysis (DXY, crosses) ~10:02.
- Seasonals or COT alone never trigger a trade; technicals must line up ~22:06-22:41 [new]
- Entry timing: do not try to sell the exact weekly high; sell a small piece Monday, add if Tuesday/Wednesday fails to make a higher high, so a later higher high does not leave a full position underwater ~29:33-30:07 [new]
- Commit to the weekly idea "come hell or high water" until it no longer makes sense; a midweek reversal (lesson 7) is the exception ~33:56-34:09 [new]
- Projecting the weekly high: on Monday, after London makes a high and retraces, fib the London high to the retracement low before the NY rally; the extension gave the exact weekly high ~30:11-31:06 [new][chart]
- Price rounding: projected levels are rounded to the nearest 5 or 0 (98.92 -> 98.95) and he shades the call ~1 pip inside; a target is given +5 pips of tolerance ~11:27-11:29, ~23:41-23:44 [new]
- Weekly target: the anticipated range = projected high to the discount PD array (liquidity void); its 50% = equilibrium for the week's premium/discount ~32:12-32:26 [chart]

## Definitions
- One Shot One Kill (OSOK): one trade per week aimed at the full weekly range, entered near the projected weekly high/low ~0:27, ~29:03.
- COT hedging program: commercials-only net position, re-zeroed using the highest high and lowest low of the last 12 months (midpoint = new zero line); below it = bearish (red), above = bullish (green) ~14:39-17:57 [chart]
- Low-resistance liquidity run: price path from a premium array to a paired discount array with no opposing arrays between ~9:26.
- Weekly Judas swing: the false Monday rally that builds the premium smart money sells into ~31:31-31:51.
