## Rules
- Bullish breaker = low (A), high (B), lower low; the lower low is a stop run. Use only the A-to-B leg; the B-to-lower-low part is "manipulation / time distortion" and is ignored (~34:59-35:47, ~44:15-45:16) [chart].
- No need for price to break back above B and retest: if bias is "on side", buy inside the A-B leg / on the sell-side run before the high breaks; break-and-retest is just one (conservative) way (~37:56-38:30, ~44:36-45:00, ~47:21-47:46, ~1:08:31) [conflict].
- Target ("bread and butter"): project the A-B range from B: B + 1x(B-A) for longs (mirror: B - 1x(A-B) for shorts) (~37:31-37:48, ~42:56-43:07) [new][chart].
- Further SD multiples (-2 ... -7.5) of a breaker leg are targets when they coincide with a higher-TF liquidity level / imbalance; confluence of several legs' levels = high-confidence exit (~1:02:49-1:05:05) [new][chart].
- Best breakers engage two liquidity pools: the short-term low/high (A) and a higher-TF pool reached by the lower low; ideal when price nears the HTF pool, bounces (enticing longs), then runs it (~34:44-35:05, ~40:50-41:40) [new].
- Breaker = order block: grade the zone 0/25/50/75/100; mean threshold is the key level. For a fill at MT, price must print one tick beyond it (MT 4484.50 needs 4484.25) because of the spread (~56:01-57:10) [new][chart].
- Fine-tune on 1m: the last up-close candle before the drop to the lower low, inside the 5m breaker range (~55:10-55:46) [chart].
- Time of day overrides shape: a high-low-higher-high (or low-high-lower-low) outside the right time is time distortion, not a breaker (~6:24-6:52) [new].
- Breaker pyramiding (when entering early in the leg): 1 contract, add 3, then 6 on a good run into the breaker; his standard ES model is 6 then 3 then 1 (~36:21-36:58) [conflict] (Ep 04: largest first, never larger adds).

## Definitions
- A-to-B price leg: breaker leg from the first swing (A) to the opposite swing (B), excluding the stop-run leg (~35:00).
- Mohawk: wicks poking past a level while bodies hold it (~51:43).
- Breakers are order blocks (~56:17).
