Lesson 6 of the February 2017 mentorship (~0:13) / EURUSD (forex) 2008-2012 / monthly frame, 4h execution / 30m. Two bullet slides (risk, reward) for ~20 min, then one worked EURUSD short and one homework short.

## Summary
Swing trades are framed only at monthly/weekly PD arrays, and the target is the next opposite monthly/weekly PD array, so the range is large. Entry is timed on the 4h chart with the January position-trading entry patterns, so the stop is small and R multiples of 5-10+ are normal. Minimum 3:1. Keep leverage low (~3:1 notional) and risk ~1.5% per trade. Expect only one or two swings every 4-6 weeks (~6 good ones a year).

## Rules
- Frame swing trades only at monthly and/or weekly levels; both the entry level and the profit objective come from monthly/weekly PD arrays ~0:58-1:07, ~3:24-3:31, ~25:11-25:18 [chart] [new]
- Premium arrays to sell (hunt them in PD-array-matrix order, from the bottom up): bearish OB, bearish liquidity void, OTE, bearish FVG, false break above an old high, rejection block (sell above the bodies of long-wick candles), old highs/lows. Discount arrays to buy, from the top down: mitigation block, bullish breaker, bullish OB, void below, old low/high, rejection block (buy below the bodies) ~1:07-2:42 [chart] [new]
- Target = the first opposite-side PD array on the monthly chart in the matrix order (for a short, the first discount array below, which may be an old high) ~2:54-3:22, ~21:50-22:31 [chart] [new]
- Execution timeframe is the 4h; nothing below the 4h is needed for swings, and setups often appear around the daily close so they can be framed the night before ~4:57-5:20, ~11:12-11:16 [new]
- Entry technique: reuse the January position-trading entries (buy/sell limit at the level, or buy stop/sell stop) on the 4h ~5:59-6:31 [new]
- Minimum 3:1 reward to risk; at monthly/weekly levels 5:1 is common, 10:1 not unusual, 12-15:1 possible ~7:03-7:22, ~11:44-11:52 [chart] [new]
- At 3:1 a 30% hit rate is profitable ~7:28-7:51 [new]
- Leverage: control it, don't max it. Example: ~3:1 notional (a $10k account trades 3 forex minis); futures are ~10:1 by default ~8:21-9:00, ~15:32-15:45 [chart] [new]
- Risk ~1.5% of equity per swing trade; with a 10R winner that is ~15% per trade. Slide example: 1.5% risk, 3:1 leverage, ~50-pip stops ~17:03-17:27, ~18:01-18:16 [chart] [new]
- Frequency: about 1-2 swings per 4-6 weeks; ~6 choice 10R swings a year can double equity. Don't force one; skip a setup that doesn't look right ~14:25-14:50, ~18:48-19:06, ~20:05-20:20 [chart] [new]
- Institutions layer orders just above/below a higher-timeframe level, not at one price, so lower-TF reactions near the level are expected ~10:17-11:00 [new]

## Trade examples
1. EURUSD short, May 2011 (~20:46-25:30) [chart]
   - Monthly frame: the bearish OB is the last up-close candle of Oct/Nov 2009 (high ~1.5145). Its mean threshold is about 1.4865 (dashed line) and the OB line is about 1.47. Price came back up to them in Mar-Apr 2011 [chart].
   - Target: the first discount PD array below is the old monthly high of Oct/Nov 2010 (blue line), quoted as 1.4280 ~22:31-24:01 [chart].
   - 4h execution (Apr-May 2011): the spike to ~1.4940 on 4 May forms a 4h bearish OB (last up candle). Its open coincides with the monthly MT ~1.4865. Short there, stop 1 pip above that candle's high = 70 pips ~23:18-23:48 [chart].
   - Result: price dropped to ~1.4280 by about 9 May, a 585-pip reward (~8:1, ~12.5% at 1.5% risk) ~24:04-24:58 [chart].
2. Homework short (~25:30-28:24) [chart]: open of the last up-close monthly candle before the down move (Mar 2011), rounded to 1.4155 (he says "bullish order block" but means this up candle). Find a 4h entry there with the January techniques.

## Definitions
- R multiple: reward divided by the risk on the trade (5R = $5 made per $1 risked) ~11:19-11:37
- Bearish OB (monthly): last up-close candle before the down move, with its mean threshold (50%) ~21:08-21:18, ~23:01-23:14 [chart]
