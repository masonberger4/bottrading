## Definitions
- Opening range: 09:30-10:00 always ~00:27:30 [new]
- Opening range gap: gap between the prior RTH close and the 09:30 open; mark it on the ETH chart ~00:28:30 [new]
- New day opening gap: 17:00 close to 18:00 open ~00:29:00 [new]
- New week opening gap: Friday close to Sunday's first open; divide into four quadrants (low, 25%, 50% CE, 75%, high) ~00:29:00, ~01:19:00 [new]. Example NWOG: Jan 27 close 4084 / Jan 29 open 4077, 60m chart ~01:32:00 [chart]
- Volume imbalance: bodies of consecutive candles (can be more than two) neither overlap nor meet; prefers a later body to trade across it ~00:22:30 [refined]
- High resistance liquidity run: price still reaches the target but goes up, retraces a lot, consolidates, repeats; "no hurry" to the opposing PD array. Low resistance: gets there in 2-3 candles with speed and little drawdown. Resistance means delivery speed, not a level ~00:40:00, ~01:21:00 [new]
