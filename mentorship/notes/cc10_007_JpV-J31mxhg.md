# cc10_007 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Bond Trading - Split Session Rules (JpV-J31mxhg)
June 2017 Core Content (bond lesson 2). Market: ZBU17 T-bond futures, same three June 2017 days as cc10_006. Timeframe: 15m with volume. ~18 min.

## Summary
Splits the bond day into London (02:00-05:00, reference only), NY AM (08:00-12:00) and NY PM (12:00-15:00). AM has the most volume and usually makes the day's swing; PM continues, reverses or consolidates. Trending days are one-sided across both; consolidation days oppose or one session goes quiet. If the AM delivered most of the range, skip the PM.

## Rules
- Sessions (NY time): London overnight 02:00-05:00, NY AM 08:00-12:00, NY PM 12:00-15:00 ~00:01:53, ~00:06:22 [chart] [new] [conflict: index rules use AM 08:30-11:00 and PM 14:00-16:00]
- London session is used only to mark stops, liquidity voids, FVGs and PD arrays; trade the NY session ~00:03:09-00:03:41, ~00:06:36-00:07:02 [new]
- Largest volume 08:00-09:30, before equities open; AM session generally forms the main swing of the true day ~00:03:41-00:04:16 [new]
- PM session: continuation, reversal, or consolidation ~00:04:16 [chart]
- ICT's own bond/ES/currency futures "kill zone": 08:20-11:00; PM trading was optional ~00:04:35-00:05:05 [new]
- AM session can end early at ~11:00 (London close; European traders square up), then NY lunch 11:00-13:00 ~00:07:17-00:08:14 [new; extends lunch notes]
- Trending day: whole day one-sided, e.g. 08:00-08:20 makes the low and price trends to 15:00 ~00:05:05-00:05:32 [chart] [new]
- A day can consolidate in AM and trend in PM (e.g. 14:00 FOMC), and the move can carry into the next session as a "runner" ~00:05:32-00:06:04 [new]
- Consolidation day: AM and PM go opposite ways, or one session swings and the other is quiet ~00:06:04 [chart] [new]
- Whatever happens in the AM is usually seen in less time in the PM; if the AM used much of the average daily range, PM is abbreviated ~00:08:34-00:09:05 [new]
- If the AM has fulfilled the day's range, go flat before NY lunch and skip the PM ~00:09:05-00:09:19 [new]
- Large opening range -> look for retracement entries (OB, FVG) rather than breakouts ~00:13:32 [new]

## Trade examples
- Ex 1 [chart ~00:11:00]: London 02:00-05:00 rally into an FVG then sell-off into 08:00; OR; equities open runs the OR low (Judas swing, low ~153-19 around 09:30-09:45); AM rally into 11:00; retrace into noon to OR level ~153-27; PM rally to high ~154-10 before 14:00; consolidation into 15:00. Called power of three in split-session terms ~00:09:36-00:11:10.
- Ex 2 [chart ~00:12:20]: London rally to ~154-06; AM trades below London lows (failure swing), bullish OB ~153-29, expansion to ~154-21 by ~11:30; reversal at noon, PM sells to ~153-28: AM buy and PM sell both tradeable ~00:11:26-00:12:29.
- Ex 3 [chart ~00:14:20-00:16:00]: London high ~154-19 at 02:00; 07:00 rally clears equal highs ~154-19, then 08:00-09:00 drops through London and 06:00-07:00 lows (~154-03) to ~153-28, low of day by 09:00. Bullish OB / turtle soup at 154-02.

## Definitions
- New York AM trend / AM session: 08:00-12:00 (may end ~11:00) ~00:08:14
- New York PM session / afternoon trend: 12:00-15:00 ~00:08:14
- Runner: a session move that carries into the next session(s) ~00:05:48
