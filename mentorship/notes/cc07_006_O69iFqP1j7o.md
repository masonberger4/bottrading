# cc07_006 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 07 - Short Term Trading Low Resistance Liquidity Runs Part 2 (O69iFqP1j7o)
2016-17 Core Content (slides). Market: GBPUSD, Mar 2017 [forex example; weekly-range ideas carry over to index futures]. Timeframes: daily, 4h (main), 1h. Duration ~25 min. Continues cc07_005 (same 60-day graded range).

## Summary
Low resistance liquidity runs in a trending leg: price is fractal, so a leg inside the big daily consolidation trends. On the 4h chart with monthly/weekly/daily PD arrays and the graded range levels (halves, quadrants, eighths), each week runs from a discount array to a premium array. In a bullish week the low forms Monday-Wednesday; 30-50% of the weekly range is done by Wednesday's London close; Thursday/Friday add little.

## Rules
- Same frame as part 1: 20/40/60-day IPDA look-back, PD arrays, trade from one PD array to the next, ideally discount -> premium ~0:19-1:02
- Price is fractal: a consolidation on one timeframe can be a trend on another ~1:26 [new]
- A trending leg starting in deep discount of the larger range wants to reach a premium level; displacement up = institutions building longs that need a premium array to unload into ~2:12-3:38 [new]
- One shot one kill is framed on the 4h chart with monthly/weekly/daily PD arrays plus the range grades; 4h PD arrays optional ~3:48-4:25, ~9:04 [new]
- A discount PD array that forms at or near a grade (quadrant) level gives high-probability moves away, toward the opposing level ~5:44-6:19 [new]
- You don't need the week's low; one logical setup with a realistic objective is enough ~6:54-7:08
- Bullish week = weekly power of three: open near the low, initial move lower makes the week's low, close near the high ~8:28-8:50 [new] (daily version is in Ep 04)
- The equilibrium (50%) of the big 60-day range is itself a high-probability support/turn level ~9:29-10:00 [chart] [new]
- Grade levels act like magnets / "mile markers"; when price trades straight through a level, expect it to reach the next grade; rarely stops in between ~11:35, ~13:53-14:20 [new]
- Weekly prep before Sunday open: load 4h with PD arrays, note where price can reach (up if in discount, down if in premium) within that week ~16:09-16:36 [new]
- Do not trade one shot one kill below the 1h chart; under 60m you are day trading/scalping. 1h adds breakers/mitigation blocks and a second entry chance ~18:30-20:23 [new]
- Bullish trending week: expect the low of the week on Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday (best days) ~20:45-21:33 [chart] [new]
- Typically 30-50% of the weekly range is completed Monday-Wednesday (by Wednesday London close) ~23:35-23:48, ~25:02 [chart] [new]
- If the Mon-Wed move was missed, don't chase a big Thursday/Friday move; Thursday may follow through a little, Friday retraces or closes flat ~24:29-25:33 [new]
- Mirror everything for bearish weeks (premium -> discount; high of week Mon-Wed implied) ~21:01

## Trade examples
- GBPUSD week of 13 Mar 2017 (4h/1h): low forms Tuesday 14 Mar into a daily FVG in deep discount (also near weekly bullish OB mean threshold); Wednesday 15 Mar retrace into FVG/breaker, then explosive rally; Wednesday makes a high just above Monday's, breaks it; Thursday dips into the FVG and a mitigation block from Wednesday, impulses higher; Friday small Judas swing down to equilibrium, then up into the close. Week ran up into a daily bearish OB. ~6:22-7:00, ~19:06, ~21:34-22:23 [chart]
- GBPUSD week of 20 Mar 2017: Monday low at the big range's equilibrium (also daily rejection block / old bullish OB of prior week); Tuesday retouch of that low; Wednesday long off a bullish breaker made at Monday's high; mild Thursday, flat Friday. Closed into a February daily FVG; reached ~half of the premium half of the range. ~10:47-11:04, ~19:19-19:39, ~22:27-23:25 [chart]
- Pip targets (FX): start with 30-50 pips/week, graduate to 50-75, 75-100; he prefers 50-75, not more than 100 ~7:10-7:24 [forex only]
