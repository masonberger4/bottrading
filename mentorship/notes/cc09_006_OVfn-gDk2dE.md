# cc09_006 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 09 - Bread & Butter Buy Setups (OVfn-gDk2dE)
Lesson 6 of the May 2017 "ICT Amplified Day Trading & Scalping" month / FX (pips; examples look like AUDUSD/GBPUSD/USDCAD 5m, symbols not clearly legible), with one aside on S&P futures / 5m / 32 min. Slide lecture; three chart examples, no live trade.

## Summary
These are bullish scalps (15-30 pips, 1:1 R:R, at most 1-2 hours) taken in kill zones on the 5m chart, when HTF institutional order flow is bullish. IPDA delivers buys in one of two ways: offset accumulation (a stop run under an old low) or re-accumulation (a retrace into a discount array, i.e. OTE/FVG). Each session has a rule set: London open, New York open (8:20 CME Judas), London close (fade the day's high at 10:30-13:00 after the 5-day ADR is exceeded) and Asia (0 GMT open). The 5-day ADR high is the main target.

## Rules
- The bias filter is HTF institutional order flow (monthly/weekly/daily/4h): up-close candles keep getting broken, down-close candles hold as support, and lows get run then snap back higher. No trend lines or moving averages ~08:17-10:09 [new]
- Price engine model 1, offset accumulation: price is repriced under an old low, sell stops trigger, then it seeks a short-term premium array. Anticipate this at key intraday lows ~00:54-02:47 [chart] [new]
- Price engine model 2, re-accumulation: a retrace into a discount array (FVG / OTE) squeezes longs with tight stops, often right after a sell-stop raid; then expansion to a short-term premium array ~01:18, ~02:56-03:48 [new]
- Scalp parameters: duration 1-2 h or less (2 h max); 15-30 pips; timing chart 5m; about 2-3 setups per day and 1 per session; R:R about 1:1; risk 0.5-1% per trade, never more than 1% ~03:54-05:53 [chart] [new]. The 0.5-1% cap is a [conflict] with Ep 07's ~1-1.3% risk.
- All scalps only inside ICT kill zones ~06:28 [chart]. Avoid London lunch (05:00-07:00 NY) and the period after the NY open, around 10:00 and later ~06:46-06:57, ~16:36-16:51 [new]
- Order types: market orders to enter scalps, limit orders for exits ~07:06-07:18 [new]. This is a [conflict] with our "limit at FVG top" reading of Ep 01.
- Bullish day template: the open (midnight NY / 0 GMT) is at or near the day's low, with possibly a small dip below it. London open makes the first leg, about 40-60% of the daily range before 05:00. Retrace or consolidation 05:00-07:00. NY continues up to about 10:00. The day's high forms 10:00-noon, at or above the 5-day ADR projected high. Price usually closes off the high ~07:51-12:06 [chart]. The midnight-open part is already in the rules (Ep 04); the timings and ADR are [new].
- On explosive days 10:00, 11:00, noon or 13:00 do not cap price. It can rally into the 15:00 bond close ~12:51-13:01 [new]
- London open scalp (bullish): expect London to make the low of the day. Fade the drop off the midnight NY candle, or buy the 5m retrace into a discount array after the Judas swing, through 05:00 NY ~13:14-17:30 [chart]
- NY scalp (bullish): if London made the daily low, NY continues higher unless a HTF premium array was hit or the ADR was reached ~18:28-18:40 [chart]. Long at a discount array (bullish OB, FVG, liquidity void) in the NY kill zone. ICT says "think OTE" ~18:45-19:07. Use the 8:20 CME open: the drop after 8:20 is the NY Judas swing to fade ~19:07-19:21, ~20:37-20:46 [chart] [new]
- NY targets: the 5-day ADR high, then the next HTF premium array on 4h/60m ~19:23-19:31 [chart] [new]
- If the ADR high is reached before 10:00: take 80% off and leave a runner, because the ADR can be doubled ~19:33-20:12 [chart] [new]
- London close scalp (short off the high of a bullish day). All conditions must hold: NY and London moved the same way; the 5-day ADR high has been reached; time is at least 10:30. The window is 10:30-13:00 ~23:06-23:25 [chart]. This is a [conflict] with the rules' 10:00-12:00 London close kill zone. Ideally the range is at least 125% of the 5-day ADR ~23:28 [chart].
  - Entry: 5m failure swing at the high (a high, a pullback, a rally back into it), entered at the bearish OB, the last up candle ~23:41-23:55 [chart]
  - Stop: 10 pips above the day's high ~23:58 [chart]
  - Target: a 20-30% retracement of the day's total range, ideally 1:1, at most 20 pips. Take the 15-20 pips and stop for the day ~24:01, ~25:22-25:46 [chart] [new]
- Fib tool: anchor the daily low (or London low) to the 5-day ADR high before price gets there. On up-close days the close usually lands between the 0.20 and 0.30 retracement levels ~26:10-27:06 [chart] [new]
- Asia (0 GMT = 20:00 NY in May): long at or just under the 0 GMT open for 15-20 pips; take a full exit with no runner and expect no second leg. ICT considers Asia and the London close low-value. He uses London close only on reversal-profile days ~27:29-30:22 [chart]. Forex-only.
- S&P aside: the AM trend ends at about 10:30-11:00, lunch is 11:00-13:00, and the PM trend runs from after 13:00 to the 16:00 close. London close behaviour in FX matches the S&P lunch retrace ~22:14-23:01 [new]

## Definitions
- Offset accumulation: run below an old low to fill buy orders against triggered sell stops and breakout sellers ~01:59
- Re-accumulation: a retrace into a fair-value discount array to add longs in a bullish market ~03:01
- Open float: the existing orders above and below the current price ~02:12
- CME open: 08:20 NY, when commodity futures open; the reference for the NY Judas swing ~19:09
- Protraction: the Judas phase at each session open ~20:33
