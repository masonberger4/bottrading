# cc10_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Commitment Of Traders (9H4iaaQXV5Y)
Recorded around June 5 2017 (June 2017 content, commodity lesson 1). Market: Japanese yen futures (J6M17, CME) as the example; method applies to any futures contract including ES. Timeframes: daily and weekly charts with the barchart.com net traders position indicator. Duration 34m. No intraday trades.

## Summary
How ICT reads the CFTC Commitment of Traders (COT) report. Only the commercials' net position matters. The zero line gives the long-term buy or sell program; the 6- and 12-month range of the commercial net position, split at its midpoint, reveals shorter hedging programs that can run against the zero-line program. Combine with institutional order flow and PD arrays for higher-timeframe directional bias. Not an intraday entry tool; at most a weekly/daily bias filter for index futures.

## Rules
- Source: CFTC weekly report, futures-only short format (ignore options), commercial long minus commercial short = net; positive = net long, negative = net short (~1:04-2:51). [chart] [new]
- Chart: daily price with the net traders position line chart, at least one year displayed; commercials red, large specs green, small specs blue (~3:11-3:56). [chart] [new]
- Track only commercials; large specs are roughly the mirror of commercials, small specs are the uninformed crowd (~9:49-10:02). [new]
- Net long/short alone is not a signal; that naive reading is why people dismiss COT (~2:56, ~12:15-12:29). [new]
- Above zero line = long-term buy program; below = sell program (~8:48, ~12:37-12:52). [chart] [new]
- Hedging programs: take the highest and lowest commercial net reading over the last 6 months and last 12 months; the midpoint of that range is a new basis line. Readings above it (green "nodules") = commercial buying even if the net is below zero; below it = selling (~20:39-23:09, ~25:47-26:02). [chart] [new]
- Hedging-program nodules coincided with the largest price advances (Jan, mid-March, late May 2017 on JY) (~26:02-26:13). [chart]
- Price levels where nodules form become key future PD arrays; note them on charts (~30:33-30:47). [new]
- Both directions are tradable whether commercials are above or below zero (~31:57-32:03). [chart] [new]
- Longs: bullish institutional order flow + discount PD arrays + net long readings within the 12/6-month range. Shorts: bearish order flow + premium PD arrays + net short readings in the range (~32:10-32:39). [chart] [new]
- Best conditions: zero-line program, hedging range and order flow all agree, plus PD array confluence (~32:42-32:48, ~29:45-29:56). [chart] [new]
- Extremes: a 4-year, 2-year or 12-month extreme in commercial net position usually marks a long-term trend reversal and overrides short-term hedging signals (~28:43-29:08). [new]
- Institutional order flow check (used to judge the program): bullish if down-close candles (bullish OBs) support price, sell-side runs get rejected, and premium arrays get broken through; mirror for bearish (~14:19-15:31, ~18:36-18:57, ~24:44-25:00). (Consistent with the existing OB/premium-discount rules.)
- Quarterly shifts: market structure shifts about every 3-4 months and then trends 2.5-3 months; the 6-month commercial range helps find them (~20:51-21:16). [new]
- Look-back intervals he scanned: 6 months, 12 months, 2, 3 and 4 years (~22:21-22:35).

## Definitions
- Commercials: producers/users of the commodity (for currencies: banks, lenders) who hedge to lock in prices (~4:57-6:20).
- Buy/sell program: long-term commercial net above/below zero (~7:49-8:51).
- Hedging program: shorter buying/selling inside the larger program, seen as readings above/below the midpoint of the 6/12-month commercial range (~8:53, ~19:07).
- Nodule: a stretch where the commercial net plots above (buying) the adjusted basis line (~22:44). [chart]
