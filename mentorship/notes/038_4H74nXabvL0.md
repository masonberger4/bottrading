## Definitions
- New Week Opening Gap (NWOG): the range between the prior Friday's close (settlement) and the Sunday open. It acts as a fair value range where buyers and sellers get delivered. Label each by its Sunday date. Older NWOGs stay relevant ~27:22-27:42, ~34:29-35:19. (Fills the definition missing from Ep 05.) On the chart each NWOG is drawn as two lines with a dashed midpoint.
- NDOG: the gap between one day's close and the next day's open. The opening range gap is yesterday's close to today's 09:30 open ~41:58-42:25. [new]
- Rejection block on DXY: the closing price of a candle plus the CE of its wick ~4:15.
- Analyst / trader / gambler: the rulebook (analyst) must keep the trader from impulsive "gambler" entries ~10:25-11:58.
