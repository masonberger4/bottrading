Date discussed: GBPUSD, 5-9 Sep 2016 (2016-17 Private Mentorship, Month 01; not the 2023 Mentorship) / market: forex (GBPUSD only) / timeframe shown: M15 (MT4, FOREX.com-style demo server on GMT) / duration ~12:10.

## Summary
Price should be read as a chain of impulse price swings (high to low to high). "Market protraction" is a time-sensitive impulse swing: a small move right after a fixed clock time that runs counter to the real direction of that session, meant to trap traders / grab liquidity (the Judas swing). Three such times per 24 h: 0 GMT (8 pm NY), midnight NY, 7 am NY. He then blends the swings with fib premium/equilibrium to frame sells.

## Rules
- Think of price as alternating impulse swings; the smaller swings carry the manipulation / market-maker moves. ~00:01:52-00:02:18 [chart] [new]
- Protraction = impulse swing tied to a time of day; three protraction windows per 24 h: 0 GMT (= 8 pm NY in summer), midnight NY (London), 7:00 am NY (New York). ~00:03:13-00:03:27, ~00:11:25-00:11:59 [new for the 0 GMT / 20:00 window; midnight Judas is in rules ~line 90-91]
- 0 GMT raid: a single small move away from the 0 GMT line, then reversal. He treats Asia as low-influence; this is the only thing watched there. Forex-oriented. ~00:03:27-00:04:01 [chart] [new]
- London: the first move after midnight NY is the fake (Judas) leg; trade against it. E.g. rally after midnight in a bearish context = false rally to sell into. ~00:04:22-00:04:46, ~00:06:29-00:06:50 [chart]
- New York: after 7:00 am NY, if London moved lower, expect a retracement higher (protraction) that reaches for liquidity (often around the London high), then expansion lower; mirror for bullish. Must happen after 07:00 (stated three times). ~00:04:46-00:07:10 [new: 07:00 start of NY Judas; rules only list 7-9 kill zone and 08:30/09:30 opens]
- Counter-direction rule: if the post-time-mark move goes up, think the opposite (down), and vice versa, provided it runs against the prevailing swing direction. ~00:05:53-00:06:20 [new]
- Combine with fib on the last impulse swing: sell when the retrace goes above equilibrium into premium (62% and up, OTE band on chart), target sell-side below the swing low and older lows. ~00:08:59-00:09:33 [chart] (consistent with rules line 143)
- Targets: equal lows and old lows from previous days act as the draw (stops accumulate there). ~00:10:24-00:10:30, ~00:11:18 [chart]

## Definitions
- Impulse price swing: one leg from a swing high to a swing low or vice versa. ~00:00:55
- Market protraction: an impulse price swing sensitive to time of day, counter to the session's real move, designed to manipulate / reach for liquidity; same idea as the Judas swing. ~00:03:13, ~00:06:44
- Equilibrium / premium: 50% of the swing; above it = premium (sell zone). ~00:09:06
