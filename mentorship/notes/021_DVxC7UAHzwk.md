## Definitions
- SIBI is spelled out as "sell-side imbalance, buy-side inefficiency": a candle that delivered
  only downside ~00:12:01-00:12:25.
- Repricing: price touches the gap's edge. Rebalancing: price trades fully through the gap
  ~00:12:33.
- Mean threshold: 50% of an order block, breaker or mitigation block. Consequent encroachment:
  50% of any gap, inefficiency or wick ~00:21:06-00:21:28.
- Priming: price behavior that coaxes traders into trusting a level as resistance or support
  ~00:25:37-00:25:55.
- PD array: premium/discount array, the recurring price elements used to anticipate moves
  ~00:20:43.
