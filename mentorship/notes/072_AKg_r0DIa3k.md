## Rules
- Holiday weeks: the low-volume holiday day distorts the week; expect cleaner delivery late in the week (Thu/Fri), against the usual "find the move before Wednesday of NFP week" rule ~3:36-4:36 [new]
- Weekly power of three: look for the high of the week to form Thursday above the Monday midnight open, then a Judas run through buy-side and a drop through Friday ~4:40-7:16 [chart]
- After a stop run, a rally should be capped at the CE of the high-to-close of the run candle's wick (DXY ~103.421) ~5:27-5:46 [chart] [new]
- Immediate rebalance: the candle after displacement trades straight back to the displacement candle's low/high with no FVG left; signals an urgent, fast delivery toward the next liquidity ~6:02-6:56 [new]
- A higher-TF PD array may not look like a gap on lower TFs; transpose HTF arrays onto lower charts ~1:48-2:03
- Order blocks can be read on non-standard timeframes (2h, 4m, 3m, 30s, 15s); toggle through them ~10:45-11:58 [new]
- Inversion FVG: price trades through an FVG and then uses it as support (or resistance); he wants it not traded through at its low or its CE ~8:31-8:37, ~13:44-14:03 [new]
- Daily close below 4400 on ES would end his "don't pick a top" stance; losing the daily down-close OB points to the BISI near 4300 ~16:56-17:41 [new]
- Opening range gap (RTH): between prior day settlement and the 09:30 open; treat it like a weekly volume imbalance ~20:55-21:24 [new]
- Keep the gap on the chart into the future even after price trades through it; the algorithm refers back to its low, CE and high, plus 25/50/75% quarters ~24:56-25:45 [new]
- If only partly delivered, the real remaining inefficiency is between the partial high and the next open ~23:11-23:44 [new]
- A breaker inside the gap range often stops the full gap closure: expect a reaction at the breaker's down-close candle low rather than the prior close ~31:38-32:13 [new]
- An old liquidity pool acts like an inefficiency and is often revisited from the other side, but only with a reason (not plain resistance-turned-support) ~36:02-36:39 [new]
- Silver bullet windows: 10:00-11:00 and 14:00-15:00 ET; in consolidation, wait for displacement, then trade the FVG; minimum 4-5 handles ~35:11-35:57 [chart]

## Definitions
- Immediate rebalance: next candle returns to the displacement candle's extreme, leaving no FVG ~6:36
- Opening range gap: RTH gap from prior settlement to the 09:30 open; levels are low/CE/high plus quarters ~21:05, ~25:15
- Inversion FVG (IFVG): an FVG price closed through, now used as support/resistance from the other side ~8:31
- Market maker sell model phases: accumulation, reaccumulation, reversal (low-risk sell), distribution, redistribution, sell-off into sell-side ~30:28-30:46
- Breaker (here): high, low, higher high; bullish version from swing low, high, lower low (relative equal lows) ~12:48, ~30:51
