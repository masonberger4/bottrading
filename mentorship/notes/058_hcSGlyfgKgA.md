## Rules
- Weekly OB draw: the opening price of the up-close (bearish OB) or down-close (bullish OB) weekly candle, extended right, is the draw on liquidity ~4:36, ~8:16 [chart] [new]
- Bullish order flow stays intact only while price holds above the mean threshold (fib 50% high-to-low) of the last daily bullish OB; a break through it = definitive failure. Carry the level into next week if untouched ~6:01-7:41 [new]
- Bearish order flow: up-close candles should act as resistance (reject), down-close candles get run through ~12:00 [new]
- Classic S/R (old low becomes ceiling) only works where an FVG sits at that level; use the FVG to qualify which old high/low will hold ~13:26-14:37, ~53:36 [chart] [new]
- When the target pool is missed, at least expect a stab into the CE (50%) of the most recent large wick on the 4h ~17:13-17:50 [new]
- An FVG need not reprice to its far edge when (a) the lower TF already shows several layers of back-and-forth price inside/near it ("layering"), or (b) it contains a balanced price range (one single pass each way). Then expect reaction at CE or the near edge only ~21:40-28:07, ~29:24-30:02 [new]
- Order block = "change in the state of delivery": a 15m OB (e.g. low of two consecutive up-close candles) sitting near an FVG's CE stops price there; the higher-TF OB is the parent, the 5m/1m are subordinate ~32:02-34:39 [chart] [new]
- Narrative from the higher TF (15m), signatures/execution on 5m/1m ~33:48 [chart]
- MSS validity: break the high (or low) of the parent price swing that delivered the liquidity sweep, not just any nearby minor swing (minor ones "can fail") ~41:15-41:31, ~45:57 [chart] (consistent with Ep 08)
- Fib a swing using bodies, not wicks ("bodies tell the story") ~41:54 [new]
- Silver Bullet (AM): entry must be taken 10:00-11:00 ET; the FVG may form before 10:00; entry at 09:57 or 11:01 is outside the rules ~44:37-45:23 [chart] [new]
- Silver Bullet (PM): window 14:00-15:00 ET ~55:21 [chart] [new]
- Silver Bullet range filter: FX needs ~15 pips to the target (expect to take ~10); ES needs 10 handles of room from entry to the draw. If not, it is not a classic SB (scalp only) ~37:16-37:30, ~47:52, ~54:23-55:20 [chart] [new]
- Pyramid allowed if the add is still not in premium (for longs) of the overall swing to the target; no adds when too rich ~43:12-43:41 [new]
- Entry in an FVG by limit order; first partial at the nearer liquidity, runner to the next pool / relative equal lows ~48:13-48:20 [new]
- ES bias: bearish until price gets back above the New Week Opening Gap low ~53:46 [chart] [new]

## Definitions
- Inversion FVG: an FVG price has passed through twice, now treated as support/resistance from the other side ~4:51, ~45:48
- Balanced price range: range between an up-close and down-close candle where price made one single pass each way ~7:13, ~29:24
- Layering: repeated back-and-forth bars near an FVG edge seen on lower TFs; reduces need for a full fill ~27:30
- Change in the state of delivery: the OB level where price stops and reverses ~30:57
- Consequent encroachment of a wick: 50% of the candle's tail (close/body to low) ~17:20
- Evolving Silver Bullet: setup that re-forms at a lower FVG when the first doesn't fill ~46:43
