# cc07_003 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 07 - Short Term Trading Market Maker Manipulation Templates (hbE8S5FSdtQ)
Core Content 2016-17, Month 07 lesson 3 / no live market date (slide-based theory, diagrams only) / all diagrams drawn as a 60-minute chart, Monday-Friday / ~40 min. Forex framing (targets in pips, "pick a pair"); the weekly templates themselves are market-agnostic.

## Summary
Blends lesson 2's weekly profiles with "how the market maker manipulates" each week: 12 templates (bullish/bearish pairs) saying which weekday forms the high or low of the week, what higher-timeframe (HTF) array it runs into, and how to frame the weekly target. Common target rule: the weekly objective is a PD array on a timeframe one step lower than the entry array, overlapping a 1.27 / 1.68 fib extension or a 100% symmetrical swing. Purpose: frame one "one shot one kill" trade per week; if no template fits, day trade, scalp or do nothing.

## Rules
- Bias first from HTF (taught in January); then only consider templates of that direction ~00:38:02 [new]
- Target timeframe = one step below the entry array's timeframe: monthly entry -> weekly/daily/4h target; weekly -> daily/4h; daily -> 4h ~00:02:40, ~00:07:01, ~00:08:00 [new]
- Target must be a premium (longs) / discount (shorts) PD array that overlaps a 1.27 or 1.68 fib extension, or a 100% measured move ("perfect symmetrical price swing"), of the early-week swing ~00:03:01, ~00:16:01, ~00:28:01 [new]
- Fib anchoring (midweek templates): swing from the intra-week extreme (fulcrum point) to the Tuesday/Wednesday retracement extreme; project 1.27/1.68 beyond the fulcrum. Can compute without a fib tool: range x 1.27 or x 1.68 added/subtracted ~00:27:01-00:31:00 [chart] [new]
- Tuesday/Wednesday low (bullish) or high (bearish) of week is formed in the London or New York session drive into the HTF array ~00:02:01 [chart] [new]
- High of week (bullish week) expected by Thursday NY session, can spill into Friday; hold the trade until Thursday/Friday ~00:02:01, ~00:13:02 [new]
- Anticipation cue: if Monday fails to reach the known HTF discount (premium) array, odds favor Tuesday driving into it [chart] ~00:02:20 [new]
- Third swing grade (halfway between equilibrium and terminus) rarely shows a stop run, only a short pause ~00:05:02-00:06:00 [new]
- FOMC (2pm) or 8:30 employment-driven Thursday turtle soup: very small size, wait for the knee-jerk, limit order below the Monday/Tuesday low ~00:24:00 [new]; agrees with "do not trade the FOMC release" only loosely [conflict: Ep 03 says stand aside at FOMC; here a tiny-size limit entry is allowed]
- Seek & Destroy weeks (neutral, consolidation Mon-Thu with shallow raids both ways): sit out; only watch for the run to a daily/weekly PD array, then expect a reversal there ~00:33:00-00:35:00 [chart] [new]
- Exact high/low of week not required; once the weekly range and direction are defined, a later-week entry is still the one-shot trade ~00:39:02 [new]
- Weekly objective ~50-75 pips (forex-only) ~00:39:02

## Definitions
Templates (profile / manipulation / target), from slides and words:
- Classic Tuesday Low of Week (bullish): hovers above HTF discount array Monday, drops into it Tuesday (old M/W/D low liquidity pool, or retest of old M/W/D high as support, or bullish OB / swing-grade level); high forms Thursday, small Friday pullback ~00:01:53 [chart]
- Classic Tuesday High of Week (bearish): mirror; old M/W/D high buy stops, or retest of old low as resistance, or bearish OB ~00:07:01 [chart]
- Wednesday Low of Week (bullish): short-term low Monday, up Tuesday, drop Wednesday into HTF discount; Mon/Tue may also be down days; rally into Friday. Also his "reflection pattern" frame ~00:13:02 [chart]
- Wednesday High of Week (bearish): mirror; classic Wednesday stop run of a short-term high ~00:18:00 [chart]
- Consolidation Thursday Reversal (bullish): Mon-Wed range, Thursday runs the intra-week low (false break, turtle soup) on a 2pm FOMC or 8:30 employment release, then runs the week's buy stops ~00:22:01 [chart]; bearish mirror ~00:25:01 [chart]
- Consolidation Midweek Rally (bullish): rally Mon, retrace Tue-Wed into a discount FVG / void / bullish OB, high or medium impact news Wednesday (London or NY), expand through the intra-week high into Friday ~00:26:01 [chart]; Midweek Decline = bearish mirror ~00:29:01 [chart]
- Seek & Destroy Bullish/Bearish Friday (neutral, low probability): consolidation Mon-Thu, Thursday news raids one side, then a run past all intra-week highs (lows) to a daily/weekly PD array ~00:31:00, ~00:34:01 [chart]
- Wednesday Weekly Reversal (bullish): consolidate Mon-Tue, news drives price below an old low / retail support into HTF discount, market "discounts" the news and reverses strongly; bearish mirror at old high / retail resistance ~00:36:00 [chart]
- Swing projection fulcrum point: the intra-week extreme from which the retracement starts; anchor for projections ~00:27:01
- Swing grades: 1st grade, 2nd = equilibrium, 3rd = halfway between equilibrium and terminus, 4th = terminus (final target) ~00:17:00
- One shot one kill: one setup per week that pays the weekly objective ~00:39:02
