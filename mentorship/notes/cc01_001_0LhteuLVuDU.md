# cc01_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 1 - Elements Of A Trade Setup (0LhteuLVuDU)
Recorded September 2016 (slide footer "September 2016 - ICT Teaching 1 of 8"), 2016-17 Private Mentorship. Market: forex (GBPUSD 30m, USDJPY daily, USDCHF 4h). Duration ~20 min. Conceptual framework, no live trade.

## Summary
Every trade needs (A) a context: price is always in one of four states (expansion, retracement, reversal, consolidation), and (B) an institutional reference point paired with that state: expansion -> order block, retracement -> FVG / liquidity void, reversal -> liquidity pool / stop run, consolidation -> equilibrium. Identify the current state, pick the matching tool, wait for the condition. One mastered pairing is enough to be consistent.

## Rules
- Classify the market into exactly one of four states before trading: expansion, retracement, reversal, consolidation. ~02:05-02:37, ~10:16 [chart] [new]
- State cycle: moves start from consolidation -> expansion (impulse); after expansion either consolidation again, retracement, or reversal; reversal is followed by a retracement, then possibly consolidation. ~07:48-08:26 [chart] [new]
- Consolidation = no trade; wait for the expansion out of it. ~04:29-04:35, ~09:50 [new]
- Wait for the first expansion out of consolidation: its direction reveals the intended repricing (the side with more resting orders). If it ran too far, wait for the retracement or next consolidation. ~09:30-10:05 [new]
- Expansion -> bullish order block: after a fast move up away from equilibrium, do not chase; buy when price returns to the down candle just before the up move, at or near the consolidation's equilibrium. ~10:28-12:33 [chart] [new]
- Retracement -> liquidity void / FVG: a fast run leaves a range price was not delivered efficiently through; expect price to return inside it and fill it, then resume the original direction. Do not chase. ~14:00-15:36 [chart] (consistent with FVG/void definitions in the rules file)
- Reversal -> liquidity pools: reversals happen after stops just above an old high or just below an old low are run; expect a significant move the other way. ~16:02-16:40 [chart] (consistent with turtle soup / buy-side, sell-side liquidity)
- Consolidation -> equilibrium: range defined by candle BODIES, not wicks; equilibrium = exact 50% of that range; after the expansion out, expect a return to equilibrium, then expansion again. ~17:20-18:09 [chart] [new]
- Equilibrium check: the midpoint should be a level price repeatedly touches from above and below inside the range. ~11:50-12:01 [new]
- Expect a couple of setups per week from one condition on one market; not every day. ~19:44-19:56 [new]

## Definitions
- Expansion: price moves quickly away from a level of equilibrium; shows the market makers' intended repricing. ~10:28 [chart]
- Retracement: price moves back inside the recently created range, repricing to levels not efficiently traded. ~13:04, ~14:03
- Reversal: price moves opposite to its current direction after running stops. ~15:48-16:10
- Consolidation: price stays in a clear range with no willingness to move significantly; orders build on both sides. ~17:20 [chart]
- Equilibrium: exact halfway point of the consolidation range. ~17:46 [chart]
- Bullish order block (this video): the down candle right before the up move out of consolidation. ~12:15
- Liquidity void: price range skipped by a fast move ("pockets" not delivered at every price). ~14:58
- Market efficiency paradigm: smart money (liquidity provider) vs speculative uninformed money (willing/unwilling liquidity). ~03:50 [chart]
