# cc08_002 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 08 - Defining The Daily Range (_2nUKLAD9ig)
Date discussed: Monday 3 Apr 2017 (example day) / market: GBPUSD 15m (forex; time windows apply to all markets) / timeframes: 15m / duration ~10m.
Lesson 2 of the April 2017 day-trading module. He calls this the definitive reference for all session/kill-zone times; times are New York local clock, daylight saving included.

## Summary
Throw away the platform's GMT daily dividers and frame the day on New York time as the algorithm (IPDA) does. Defines Asian range, London / New York / London-close kill zones, the IPDA "true day" (midnight to 15:00), London lunch and the 08:20 CME open, then shows one GBPUSD day where the highs and lows formed at these window edges.

## Rules
- Ignore retail/MT4 daily separators (GMT); use NY-time references only ~0:31-1:54 [chart]
- Asian range: 20:00-00:00 NY ~2:31-2:45 (spoken start garbled in captions; slide reads 8:00 pm) [new][chart]
- London kill zone: 01:00-05:00 NY ~3:07-3:21 [conflict: rules file Ep 06 says 02:00-05:00; he says the free-tutorial 2-3-4 am times are not the ones he uses ~8:30-8:43][chart]
- New York kill zone: 07:00-10:00 NY ~4:01-4:17 [conflict: rules file Ep 06 says 07:00-09:00 for FX]
- London close kill zone: 10:00-12:00 NY ~4:32-4:46 [chart] (matches Ep 06)
- IPDA true day: 00:00-15:00 NY; the true day close is 15:00 (bonds close, rate influence over); he calls it "New York close" ~5:15-5:38, ~9:32-9:49 [new][chart]
- 14:00-15:00 contains FOMC / minutes reactions; the bulk of that move is done by 15:00 ~9:54-10:22 [new]
- 08:20 NY = CME open; a reference time where short-term lows/highs form ~7:22-7:43 [new][chart]
- London lunch (between London KZ end 05:00 and NY KZ start 07:00): expect quiet or continuation of the London move, no major new move ~7:48-8:02 [new]
- Daily OHLC is framed by the true day: open at midnight, close at 15:00 ~10:22-10:30 [new]

## Trade examples
- No trade. GBPUSD Monday 3 Apr 2017, 15m [chart 6:20, 7:20]:
  - Midnight open; small dip, then rally above the Asian range resistance; high of day at the start of the London KZ (~01:00).
  - A lower high near 05:00 at the end of the London KZ; selloff.
  - NY KZ: short-term high inside the window; the window low at 08:20 CME open, then a rally (lower high).
  - Decline into London close; low of day in the 10:00-12:00 window, then consolidation into the 15:00 true-day close.
  - Read as power of three: Asian accumulation, London manipulation high, distribution down through London close.

## Definitions
- Retail 24-hour trading day: MT4 GMT-based daily dividers (Ctrl+Y); not used ~0:31 [chart].
- IPDA true day: interbank 24-hour cycle referenced 00:00-15:00 NY ~5:15.
- CME open: 08:20 NY (details promised later) ~7:22.
- London lunch: gap between London and NY kill zones ~7:48.
