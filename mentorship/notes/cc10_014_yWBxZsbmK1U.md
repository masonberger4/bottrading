# cc10_014 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Index Futures - Index Trade Setups (yWBxZsbmK1U)
June 2017 lesson ("ICT Index Trading Concepts, lesson 5"). Market: ES (traded), NQ and YM (SMT legs). Schematic diagrams for the six projected-range profiles from cc10_013, plus one real example (ES/NQ/YM 10-minute line charts, Fri June 23 2017). 21 min.

## Summary
Turns the six projected ranges into trade setups. In each session the entry is triggered by an **index SMT divergence** (ES vs NQ vs YM) at the session's swing low/high, which forms at a PD array; you then "rely on time of day" and hold toward a 15m-60m PD array target at the session's natural end (10:30-11:00 for AM, 2:00 / 3:00 bond close / 4:00 for PM). The HTF (4h/daily) nesting of that 15-60m array decides whether a reversal happens. No explicit stop rules are given.

## Rules
- AM setup: index SMT on the lows (bullish profiles) or highs (bearish profiles), compared from the London session into the 9:30 open. Enter ES at that swing. ~00:01:15, ~00:05:21 [chart] [new]
- AM hold: hold for time of day, minimum 10:30, ideally toward 11:00 (London close); expect consolidation/pause after 11:00 into noon lunch. ~00:01:32, ~00:05:36, ~00:11:37 [chart] [new]
- PM setup: compare the lunch-hour lows/highs and the swing formed right after 1:00 pm across ES/NQ/YM; SMT there = PM entry. Any of the three can be the divergent one (it need not be ES). ~00:02:19-00:03:01, ~00:06:19 [chart] [new]
- A short-term high (or low) almost always forms right after lunch; on a two-session down close day start hunting the ES short-term high from 1:00 pm (sell into an FVG, bearish OB, liquidity void, or a run above the lunch high). ~00:06:33-00:07:16 [new]
- PM hold: do not take quick handles; hold at least until the 3:00 bond close, ideally to 4:00. Daily range expands toward the close, so holding longer captures more of it. ~00:03:01-00:04:15, ~00:07:16-00:07:32 [new] [conflict: mentorship_rules.md says if the target is hit by 15:00 be done, last hour retraces]
- Targets: AM and PM session extremes are usually a 15m or 60m PD array (premium array for longs, discount array for shorts). ~00:04:15-00:04:48 [chart] [new]
- PM can deliver in two stages: falls short of the 15/60m array, retraces, then a last-hour push reaches it. Hold for that second leg. ~00:04:48, ~00:08:06 [chart] [new]
- **AM rally, PM reversal setup:** buy ES on AM SMT on lows, hold to ~11:00. If the AM high stops below a 15/60m premium array, anticipate a post-1:00 rally up into that 15/60m array, then sell it on SMT on highs. The reversal needs the 15/60m premium array nested in a 4h/daily premium array. Afterwards: if the AM discount array was HTF, expect a bounce/resume higher; if not, expect continuation down through the intraday low. ~00:08:42-00:11:04 [chart] [new]
- **AM decline, PM reversal setup:** sell on AM SMT on highs, hold to 10:30-11:00, cover into lunch. If the AM low formed above a 15/60m discount array, expect a lower low after 1:00 into that array (run on the daily/lunch low), then buy on SMT on lows; nested 15/60m + 4h/daily discount array = reversal confirmation. Hold to 3:00. ~00:11:22-00:13:17 [chart] [new]
- **Consolidation AM decline, PM rally setup:** sell on AM SMT on highs, hold to 10:30-11:00; after lunch buy on SMT on lows. Do NOT hold into the close: the PM high is early (around 2:00) and price drifts back to the day's equilibrium (middle of range). ~00:13:17-00:16:07 [chart] [new]
  - PM low runs only the lunch low if the AM low was at a nested HTF array; otherwise it runs the AM intraday low. ~00:15:06 [new]
- **Consolidation AM rally, PM decline setup:** buy on AM SMT on lows, hold to 10:30-11:00; after 1:00 sell on SMT on highs, hold to ~2:00 minimum, target short-term lows. PM runs only the lunch high if the AM high was at a 4h/daily premium array; otherwise it runs the intraday high up into a 4h/daily array, then declines. ~00:16:07-00:18:11 [chart] [new]
- 15m/60m arrays are not HTF by themselves; the catalyst for a session reversal is a 15/60m array nested inside a 4h/daily array. ~00:10:48 [new]

## Definitions
- Rely on time of day: hold a position until the session's typical end time rather than taking quick profits. ~00:03:01
- Nested PD array: a 15m/60m PD array located inside a 4h/daily PD array. ~00:10:48
- Day's equilibrium: middle of the day's range; consolidation days return there into the close. ~00:15:49
