## Summary
ICT's personal intraday checklist in the exact order he applies it: day of week -> IPDA true day -> kill zones -> CBDR SDs -> Asian range SDs -> Flout SDs -> intraday profile -> PD array key levels -> 5-day ADR projections, all after monthly/weekly/daily bias. Then the only two setups he trades: internal-range OTE/FVG (pattern 1) and external-range turtle soup (pattern 2), with a breaker re-entry/pyramid as the contingency (pattern 3). Gold example: long 1278, stop 1274, at a 1H FVG + OTE below swept lows.

## Rules
Checklist order (slides at ~3:20-4:20 [chart])
1. Monthly/weekly/daily analysis is already folded into the 4H view; bias comes from HTF ~1:46 [chart]
2. Day of week first. HTF bearish -> look for shorts Mon/Tue/Wed; bullish -> longs Mon-Wed. If the Mon-Wed move does not show up, switch to Thu/Fri templates and the economic calendar ~6:21-7:15 [new]
3. Drop from 4H to whichever lower TF actually shows FVGs (60/30/15/5m); he prefers 4H -> 30m or 15m, refine on 5m; setups must confirm on 15m minimum, 5m is best; dislikes 1m ~4:20-6:15 [new] [conflict: 2023 rules execute on 1m/5m]
4. IPDA true day: take setups only inside true-day hours; bulk of daily volume 03:00-10:00 NY. Be positioned before that window or in its first half; after the NY open accept smaller objectives ~7:19-7:47 [chart] [new]
5. Kill zones: HTF bullish -> set up in London open aiming for the low of day; bearish -> high of day. Failed in London -> reposition/new entry in NY open ~7:47-8:23 [chart]
6. Close the bulk or all intraday positions starting 10:00-11:00 NY (profit taking / London close begins) ~8:25 [chart] [new]
7. CBDR: bullish -> long entries at -1/-2/-3 SD of the CBDR; bearish -> shorts at +1/+2/+3 SD. Never trade an SD alone: require a 15-60m discount (long) / premium (short) PD array overlapping it (FVG, OB, old high for a turtle soup) ~8:41-10:20 [chart] [new]
8. Asian range: bullish -> long below Asian high, preferably below Asian low; bearish -> short above Asian low, preferably above Asian high. Bullish: expect the Asian high to be retested as support (entry or add), often into NY open; bearish mirror with the Asian low ~10:22-12:18 [chart] [new]
9. Asian range SDs overlapping CBDR SDs + a PD array = likely high/low of day ~12:21-12:43 [chart] [new]
10. Flout = CBDR + Asian combined window, highest high to lowest low (wicks or bodies). One SD = HALF that range, projected from the midpoint; keep adding levels as the day extends. Entries at Flout SD + CBDR/AR SD + 60-15m PD array confluence ~12:56-15:40 [chart] [new]
11. Intraday profile: bearish -> HOD in London; bullish -> LOD in London. If the 4H has NOT yet reached a discount array (bearish) / premium array (bullish), expect NY open to continue London's direction; if NY trades into a 4H premium/discount array, expect a NY reversal ~15:43-16:53 [chart] [new]
12. Choose the dealing range (internal vs external range liquidity); calibrate PD arrays to the nearest .10/.05: premium arrays above price rounded DOWN, discount arrays below price rounded UP ~17:04-17:25 [chart] [new]
13. ADR: 5-day average daily range gives the day's likely extreme. Bullish target = ADR high; if broken, fib the ADR range for 1.27 and 1.62 extensions, valid only when overlapping a 15/60m premium array (mirror bearish) ~17:29-18:45 [chart] [new]
14. All projections together usually land within ~10 pips of the HOD/LOD, so exit ~10 pips early; never hold for the last few pips ~18:54-21:00 [new] (index scaling unknown)

Setups (~26:26-47:00 [chart])
15. Only trade if one of these is clearly visible; otherwise no trade ~37:20-37:34
16. Pattern 1 (internal range / OTE, his "favorite"): HTF bias + bounce off HTF PD array; impulse leaves FVG near the swing; a short-term low forms after equal/higher highs; price retraces into the FVG and under that short-term low (sell stops run); buy there. Four confluences: OTE 62/70.5/79 + FVG + swept short-term low + bullish OB. Without the intermediate short-term low it is lower probability. Target external range liquidity ~26:28-29:30 [chart]
17. Pattern 2 (external range / turtle soup): price stops short of the anticipated HTF discount array (false low), then later drops into it and raids the sell stops under that false low: buy there; do not buy the early bounce ~29:35-33:20 [chart]
18. Pattern 3 (contingency): missed pattern 2 -> wait for the rally through the short-term high; it becomes a bullish breaker; buy the retest of the breaker or pyramid there (example: 20 lots at the soup low, add 10 at the breaker). Do not expect a deeper OTE retrace below the breaker after the stop run ~33:23-36:30 [chart] [new]
19. Patterns are fractal: spot on a higher TF, then look for the same pattern on the lower TF; the retrace into the breaker is often an LTF OTE/FVG ~34:40-35:35
20. On every timeframe, first locate the breakers ~50:13 [new]
