## Summary
ICT's personal daily-to-4H checklist that turns the monthly/weekly bias into a daily bias. Nine boxes in fixed order [chart, ~8:40]: Commercial Hedging -> Open Interest -> Institutional Order Flow -> Weekly Profile -> Intermarket Analysis (SMT) -> Market Structure -> PD Array Matrix -> Key Price Levels -> Daily Bias Defined. The finished daily bias and levels are then transposed to the 4H chart. A bias is not a daily trade: you still wait for price to reach the right PD array at the right time of day.

## Rules (the step-by-step process)
0. Carry the monthly and weekly analysis into the daily; ideally the daily confirms it ~5:12. If monthly/weekly are neutral or conflicted, run the process from the daily alone ~13:16. [new]
1. Commercial hedging (COT): take the last 12 months of commercial hedgers' net position (commercials only, not large speculators). Midpoint = (highest + lowest)/2 is his own zero line. Above the midpoint = bullish, below = bearish. If the 12-month range is too narrow to read, use 6 months. Use a rolling window from today ~4:25-5:08, ~9:26-10:31 [chart]. Bearish COT -> focus on premium arrays; bullish -> discount arrays (he misspeaks this mapping at ~10:31). [new]
2. Open interest (only from the daily down): bullish signal if OI falls about 15% or more while price is at a higher-timeframe discount array; bearish signal if OI rises about 15% or more while price is at an HTF premium array. Anything in between: ignore OI entirely ~10:42-11:37 [chart]. [new]
3. Institutional order flow on the daily (the most important step): monthly/weekly bearish -> daily should find resistance at up-close daily candles (bearish OBs) and break through down-close daily candles. Bullish mirror: support at down-close daily candles, break through up-close daily candles ~11:41-12:15 [chart]. Without sponsorship, treat setups as low probability ~5:56. If you skip HTF, at minimum start every analysis on the daily ~12:43. [new]
4. Weekly profile: pick bullish or bearish weekly profile scenarios that match the bias; the economic calendar (which day, London or NY session the drivers fall in) is the only forecasting tool; do it on the weekend ~13:16-18:15 [chart]. [new]
   - Bulk of the weekly range forms Tuesday to Thursday ~13:57 [chart].
   - Bullish template: low Monday or Tuesday, rally through late Tuesday, Wednesday and first half of Thursday to the weekly high; Friday retraces ~14:03.
   - Bearish template: Tuesday makes the high of the week, Thursday NY open makes the low; Friday/Monday consolidation ~14:19.
   - He lets Monday go: entry on Tuesday off Monday's low (OTE), or Wednesday off Tuesday's low; target is the Tuesday-to-Thursday NY open leg ~15:40-16:09.
   - After Monday his read is about 60% accurate; about 70% after Tuesday ~18:26.
5. Weekly opening prices: track both the Sunday open and the Monday 00:00 New York price (open of the midnight 1H candle). Bullish: want price below one or both opens, into a discount array. Bearish: want price above at least one, into a premium array ~19:03-21:26 [chart: "Weekly Open for Power of 3"]. [new; extends the midnight-open rule at the daily level to the week]
6. Intermarket / SMT, daily into 4H: bullish structure should be supported by positively correlated markets and opposed by negatively correlated ones (bullish pound = weak dollar) [chart ~21:40]. SMT: a dollar higher high with no matching cable lower low, or cable lower low with a failed dollar higher high, = sell-stop run, turtle soup long ~21:30-22:10. [new]
7. Market structure: classify recent highs/lows; scan HTF discount arrays for market maker buy models and premium arrays for sell models (these stage several lower-TF entries) [chart ~22:40]. Use daily breakers above all; trade the leg between a daily bullish breaker and the next bearish breaker, without trying to catch the extreme ~22:14-23:20. [new]
   - Within a daily bearish leg: Power of 3 day = open, rally (Judas) in London, sell, expand down into NY, reversal at London close. Bullish mirror ~23:11-23:40.
8. PD array matrix: choose the price range to analyse, split it into premium/discount, mark only the arrays that actually exist (FVG, void, mitigation block, etc.), on the daily then the 4H ~23:53-24:31 [chart].
9. Key price levels (calibration): round each PD array to the nearest .10 or ._5 level. Premium arrays above price round DOWN, discount arrays below price round UP ~24:36-25:02 [chart]. [new]
10. Daily bias defined: buyer or seller. Only execute when price reaches a discount array (bullish) or premium array (bearish) at a specific time of day, ideally with a calendar-driven manipulation ~25:07-26:24. [new]

## Definitions
- COT midpoint: the half of the 12-month high-low range of commercial net positions, used as a custom zero line ~5:02.
- Daily bias: the direction you want to trade, given the full checklist; it is not a daily trade ~25:10.
- Calibration: rounding PD arrays toward current price to round numbers [chart ~25:00].
- Weekly opening price: Sunday open and/or Monday 00:00 NY 1H open ~21:17.
