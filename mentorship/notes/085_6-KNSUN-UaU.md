## Summary
Short 5 ES into a 1m BISI below, judged himself "offside" when a down-close candle's opening price was respected, closed the short and reversed long. Built a long by pyramiding toward buy-side liquidity (the Friday high, also framed as the 20-30% "TGIF" retracement of the week's range), got stopped in small profit after trailing the stop too tight (on purpose, to teach his son), re-entered at the CE of an inversion FVG and exited 18 of 20 at the target. Key lessons: how to recognise being offside, and never trail a stop into a fresh FVG right after a market structure shift.

## Rules
- Offside test (bearish): after a short, price should smash straight through any down-close candle (bullish OB) below. If bodies respect that candle's opening price (or its wick CE) and trade back above it, the short is wrong; close it, don't wait for the stop ~11:18-13:52 [chart] [new]
- Reverse immediately when offside: he closed the short at a small loss and went long the same size in the same minute ~14:09 [chart] [new] [conflict: Ep 01 says never reverse after an AM loss; here it is a same-setup flip, not a loss-then-revenge trade]
- Never add to a losing short "for a better price"; closing means the idea is wrong ~13:52 [new]
- Bearish order flow means down-close candles get run through; bullish means up-close candles get run through. No DOM/VWAP/volume profile needed ~10:27 [new]
- Long stop: below the candle bodies and below the CE (50%) of the low wick that defended the level ~14:31, ~27:37 [chart]
- A clean one-down-candle, one-up-candle repricing through a BISI makes it a balanced price range; price need not return below it, so the stop can go under it ~16:32-16:51 [new]
- After the level has been retested ~3 times and immediately left, raise the stop to the down-close candle's opening price; a return there means lower prices ~17:46 [new]
- NEVER trail a stop into (or just under) an FVG created right after a market structure shift; price tends to come back and rebalance it. Keep the stop under the older defended level ~19:59-23:02, ~28:55 [new]
- Trailing too soon is the main error; his best trades keep the stop far away ~26:46-27:04 [new]
- Re-entry after a stop-out: as long as the target is untouched and structure intact, re-buy at the CE of the inversion FVG; allow price to trade ~1 tick below the zone/wick CE ("coloring outside the lines" is normal) ~27:21-28:08 [chart] [new]
- The FVG inside the displacement leg after a buy-side run is where to accumulate longs ~28:38-28:55 [new]
- Add on a retrace to a volume imbalance just above the IFVG (discount PD array) ~30:08-31:03 [chart]
- Exit: limit order for most of the size at the target, keep a couple of runners, roll the stop up only after price clears the wick CE/threshold ~31:26-31:52 [chart]
- Instrument choice: trade the index that failed to make a lower low (relatively stronger) for longs; ES did, NQ didn't ~30:28-30:46 [new for long side; SMT in Ep 03]
- Risk-on confirmation: EUR/USD rising (DXY down) supported the long ~23:46 [chart]
- Friday ("TGIF"): after a bearish week, expect a retracement of 20-30% of the week's range, reaching for the day's buy-side ~8:02, ~17:10 [new]
- A down-close candle only counts as a bullish OB if the narrative is bullish ~40:02 [new]
- A "full pull" = entry to target with no partials; one entry with a small stop is valid instead of pyramiding ~22:24, ~34:41 [new]

## Definitions
- Offside / on side: in a trade on the wrong / right side of the delivery ~6:11.
- TGIF: Friday retracement of 20-30% of the weekly range (high-low so far) ~8:23, ~17:30.
- Inversion FVG (IFVG): an FVG price traded through that now acts from the other side; labeled "IFVG" on the chart ~15:28 [chart].
- Full pull: entry to final target, no partials ~22:44.
- Balanced price range: FVG repriced by one clean down and one clean up candle ~16:51, ~39:20.
