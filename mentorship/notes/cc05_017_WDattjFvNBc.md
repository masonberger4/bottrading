# cc05_017 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 05 - Limit Order Entry Techniques For Long Term Traders (WDattjFvNBc)
Lesson "7.2" of the 2017 mentorship. Market: USDJPY (forex). Timeframes: weekly for the draw, daily for execution. Duration ~11 min. Example: Sep-Dec 2016 rally from ~100 to ~118.7.

## Summary
Companion to the stop-entry lesson (cc05_016). Same higher-TF filter and same closed counter-trend daily candle, but the order is a limit at that candle's CLOSE: buy limit below a down-close candle's close (buy deeper in discount), sell limit above an up-close candle's close. Best once a market structure break has already started the trend, and only when combined with a daily PD array, not on any candle.

## Rules
- Bias filter: monthly and/or weekly suggest institutional order flow will seek a PD array above (long) / below (short) daily price. ~0:29-0:37, ~3:02-3:11 [chart] [new]
- Setup candle: the daily must close down (long) / up (short); invalid while still forming. ~0:39-0:48, ~3:11-3:20 [chart] [new]
- Entry: buy limit at the down candle's close; sell limit at the up candle's close. Filled the next day when price trades beyond that close. ~0:50-1:10, ~3:22-3:44 [chart] [new]
- Context: best when the market has already shown a market structure break in the intended direction and the move is underway; you do not need the turning point. ~1:25-2:02 [new]
- Do not take every counter candle. Combine with a daily PD array: bearish OB, a gap traded into, a filled void, a run above a recent high, an old low retested as resistance (mirror for longs). He says this applies to the stop entries too. ~4:23-5:10 [new]
- Higher TF pulling one way + daily retracing the other way to a short-term premium (for shorts) or discount (for longs) = the low-risk, high-probability condition. ~5:23-5:48
- Selling above the up candle's close captures the last surge; intraday this is what the Judas swing looks like (London makes the high, then the sell-off starts a long move). ~5:50-6:12
- Repeat on every new qualifying down candle until the weekly PD array target is reached. ~6:33-6:50, ~8:55-9:22
- Expect quick feedback: a correct limit entry usually shows profit almost immediately. ~2:45-2:55 [new]

## Trade examples
- USDJPY daily Sep-Dec 2016 (frame ~6:40): checkmarks under six down-close candles in the Nov-Dec rally; each next day traded below the close and filled the buy limit, followed by a quick rise. ~6:24-6:50 [chart]
- Weekly context (frame ~7:20): red bearish OB ~118.9-120.1 (premium draw); blue bullish OB ~100.6-101.2 at the discount near 100. ~7:06-7:31 [chart]
- Daily with distances to the weekly bearish OB (frame ~9:20): Sep 2016 fill inside the blue weekly bullish OB (~100.5) = 1800 pips to target; then ~Nov 17 = 980; then 785, 600, 500, 360 pips for later down candles. Pip numbers are to the OB, not realized exits. ~7:52-9:22 [chart]
- The 980-pip entry sat just below the equilibrium of the range; entries above equilibrium still had several hundred pips. ~10:16-10:26

## Definitions
- Limit-at-close vs stop-at-open: the stop entry (cc05_016) waits for price to trade back through the open (confirmation); the limit entry buys below the close (deeper discount, no confirmation). Both use the same closed opposite-color daily candle. ~0:22-0:29 [new]

## Seen only on the charts
- Fill lines on the frames are drawn from each down candle's close to the next candle; several fills come one or two candles later, not always the next day.
- Red vertical lines run from each entry up to the weekly OB, used only to measure distance.
