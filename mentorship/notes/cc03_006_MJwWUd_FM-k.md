# cc03_006 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 03 - Macro Economic To Micro Technical (MJwWUd_FM-k)
Recorded Nov 2016 (6th of 8 November teachings) / Dollar Index futures (DXZ16), 30-yr T-Bond futures (ZBZ16), 10-yr T-Note futures (ZNZ16), FX pairs / daily charts only / ~19 min.

## Summary
A 3-6 month macro bias tool: read interest-rate direction from the bond market (30-yr bond, 10-yr note) instead of fundamental reports. Falling bond futures = rising rates = stronger dollar. Apply SMT divergence (from cc03_005) between bonds and the Dollar Index, and between the 10-yr and 30-yr, to spot the roughly quarterly trend shift; then trade lower timeframes in that direction. Mostly forex application; the bond-vs-other-market SMT idea is the part that could carry over to index futures as an HTF bias filter.

## Rules
- Use daily charts only for this analysis; it is a 3-6 month outlook, not a day-trading tool ~0:50-1:03, ~1:58 [new]
- Expect a "quarterly shift" every 3-4 months: the prevailing trend reverses, or consolidates for an extended period then resumes ~2:29-3:05 [new]
- Bond futures price is inverse to interest rates: bonds rally = rates fall; bonds drop = rates rise ~5:31-5:46 [new]
- Rising rates (bonds falling) -> funds pour into the dollar -> Dollar Index up, foreign currencies down; USD-first pairs (USDCAD, USDCHF, USDJPY) up ~5:22-5:29, ~9:06-9:16, ~14:53-14:59 [new]
- Bond/USDX SMT: Dollar Index fails to make a lower low while bonds make a higher high (late June 2016) -> underlying dollar strength, start of the next quarterly leg ~6:27-7:26 [chart] [new]
- 10-yr vs 30-yr SMT: one makes a higher high and the other a lower high -> expect both to decline (rates up) and the dollar to rally ~11:10-11:53 [chart] [new]
- When the Dollar Index makes a lower low that bonds do not confirm with a higher high, the dollar move is manipulation (election-night spike) and the rate-driven trend resumes ~16:12-16:38 [chart] [new]
- Use HTF order blocks on both: dollar pulling back into a down candle (bullish OB) while bonds rally into an up candle (bearish OB) -> expect dollar up / bonds down ~7:49-8:06 [consistent with OB rules]
- Breakouts above old highs in pairs against the macro bias are false moves (stop runs) ~17:11-17:58 (same idea as cc03_005)

- DXZ16 vs ZBZ16 daily, Jun-Nov 2016: bonds peaked early July (~176 on the chart) while USDX held a higher low late June (~93.5-94) [chart]. Bonds consolidated to mid-August, then fell; USDX rallied from ~94.5 (early Sep) to ~101.3 (mid-Nov) [chart]. Bonds ended near ~153 [chart].
- ZNZ16 vs ZBZ16, Sep 2016: 10-yr higher high vs 30-yr lower high in the last week of Sep into Oct, then both declined; USDX lows in the first and last weeks of Sep led rallies ~11:29-12:31 [chart, Barchart side by side].
- Confirmed across pairs (dates only, no prices called): USDCHF, USDCAD, USDJPY rallied from first/last week of Sep lows; EURUSD, GBPUSD, NZDUSD, AUDUSD sold off from highs in those weeks ~12:41-15:22. ## Definitions
- Interest-rate divergence: SMT applied between bond futures and the Dollar Index, or between 10-yr and 30-yr futures ~6:53-7:09, ~10:46.
- Quarterly shift: a change in trend or condition roughly every 3-4 months ~2:33.
- Long-term (his usage): 3-6 months ~2:23.
