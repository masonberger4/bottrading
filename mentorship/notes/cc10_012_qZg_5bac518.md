June 2017 lesson ("ICT Index Trading Concepts, lesson 3"). Markets: ES (ESU17, ESM17), YM (YMM17), NQ (NQM17), NQU17; 15m barchart.com charts in Central time (ICT converts verbally). Slide deck, no live trade. 13 min.

## Summary
Defines the NY PM session (1:00-4:00 pm) and the "PM trend": one afternoon swing, usually starting around 2:00 pm, either continuing the AM trend or reversing into the close, often launched from an order block formed in the AM session or at lunch and aimed at a rejection block / old high. Lunch (noon-1 pm) is a consolidation/retracement. Index SMT for the PM uses the noon-3:00 pm window. Last hour (3-4 pm, after the 3 pm bond close) tends to print the opposite end of the day's range from the AM extreme.

## Definitions
- PM trend / afternoon swing: the one swing between 1:00 and 4:00 pm. ~00:01:28
- NY lunch hour: quiet consolidation/retracement period, nominally noon-1:00. ~00:02:42
- Bond close: 3:00 pm NY; reference time for the PM extreme. ~00:12:08
