## Rules
- Two high-impact news drivers in the same session (e.g. 08:30 and 10:00): expect a two-stage delivery. The first release extends the move already in play (London, prior day); the second can reverse it or squeeze it further. Price chops between them and runs both sides. Do not trade ahead of the second release ~50:59, ~68:46-71:30, ~1:40:56 [new]
- Friday after a week whose targets were all met: do not trade; protect the week's profit. He also did not trade Thursday once targets were hit ~7:05, ~27:23, ~49:12 [new]
- Mondays are definite trading days in NFP week and in weeks with an FOMC announcement later (Wed/Thu) ~33:00-33:25 [new]
- Thursday often makes the opposite end of the weekly range, especially after higher-timeframe targets are met. Expect Thu/Fri retracement, but do not call the bottom ~8:24, ~23:56 [new]
- Wick CE as a continuation filter: with a draw above and a large up wick, if the next candle closes above the wick's 50% (CE), expect follow-through to the draw. It can wick below CE, but a close below means the idea needs revisiting. Works on any timeframe, mirrored for shorts ~38:13-40:12, ~46:57-47:17 [chart] [new]
- Ultra-short scalp version: buy at the wick CE, take profit with a limit ~0.5 pt below the nearby high ~40:24-40:48 [chart] [new]
- Immediate rebalance: a run where consecutive candles leave no fair value gap (one candle's high equals a later candle's low exactly) signals sudden, fast delivery toward the next draw. Can be used as an entry point ~44:54-45:34, ~1:36:10, ~1:36:40 [chart] [new]
- Balanced price range: price trades easily TO a BPR from above, but rarely THROUGH it in one pass; expect several probes, each deeper ~22:07-23:50 [new] (refines Ep 01's BPR definition)
- Daily wick CE: a daily close below a wick's CE means a deeper retracement is likely ~6:45-7:01 [new]
- Exit a short taken at an immediate rebalance if price retraces back above the range's equilibrium; fast return to 50% means it can run to the other side ~1:37:18-1:37:49 [new]
- Uncertain mid-trade (setup no longer looks one-sided): cut size in half immediately; if trading the minimum size, close it and keep watching ~1:02:17-1:04:22 [new]
- Only trade conditions that are "one-sided"; 50/50 chop is a no-trade ~1:35:38-1:35:53 [new]
- Time distortion: tiny-timeframe consolidations (sub-5m) before news or after the 16:00 close; the algorithm is not seeking liquidity, so do not read direction in them ~42:05-43:12 [new]
- Early entry before or at the 09:30 open is skipped on a two-driver day ~1:26:48 [new]
- A day with no daily FVG nearby: the only daily inefficiency in range is the magnet into news ~5:01 [chart]

## Definitions
- Immediate rebalance: no gap between candles in a run; the high of one candle equals the low of a later one ~44:54
- Volume imbalance (restated): no overlap between two consecutive candle bodies; wicks may link them ~1:29:27-1:29:50
- Mean threshold of a body: half of a candle body (not called an inefficiency) ~49:02
- Time distortion: price held in a range, with no displacement allowed, before news or a session close ~42:09
- Market makers: the central banks originate price; firms called market makers are dealers ~22:28
- Three PD arrays at one level (DXY): a bearish OB containing an FVG and a volume imbalance ~5:51-6:12
