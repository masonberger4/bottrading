## Definitions
- Fulcrum point: the swing high (or low) that price must trade through before you project SD levels ~03:03:40
- Terminus / low hanging fruit: a realistic first objective, not the end of the move ~03:51:20
- Balanced price range (BPR): price passes one way and then back the other in a single pass each (1-2 large candles). It is measured from the higher of the two candles' lows to the lower of their highs, extended right; its midpoint is "equilibrium". It is also the overlap of a bearish and a bullish FVG, like an island reversal. It is a PD array that can sit anywhere in premium/discount (outside the PD array matrix hierarchy), like "Event Horizon" ~04:21:20-04:37:40 [new]
- Breakaway gap: an FVG leaving a BPR or consolidation at a discount/premium, expected not to close ~04:37:00
- Six Sisters: ES moves in sympathy to catch up with the leading NQ ~04:58:20 [new]
- 15m chart = "bellwether" timeframe ~05:01:40
- Seasonal tendency: a time of year in which a market usually moves one way. If it fails to appear, that is a strong signal in the other direction ~04:41:00
