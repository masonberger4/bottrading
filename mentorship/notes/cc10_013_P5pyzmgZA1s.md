June 2017 lesson ("ICT Index Trading Concepts, lesson 4"). Market: ES (S&P E-mini). Schematic diagrams only (AM session green, Lunch white, PM session blue, with "First Hour" and "Last Hour" lines). 17 min.

## Summary
ICT's day-profile templates for ES ("projected range"): he says "eight" but the slides show six. Each profile pairs a higher-timeframe (daily/4h) order-flow state with how the AM trend, lunch and PM trend should unfold, and which liquidity the PM targets (lunch high/low vs the intraday high/low). The deciding filter between "lunch extreme" and "intraday extreme" is whether the AM swing started from (or reached) a 4h/daily PD array. Intraday objectives = stops (liquidity).

## Rules
- Choose the profile from daily + 4h institutional order flow and where price sits relative to HTF premium/discount PD arrays. ~00:01:21, ~00:04:28 [chart] [new]
- **1. Two-session up close** (daily/4h bullish, mid-swing, no 4h/daily premium array reached yet): AM returns to a discount array then rallies; lunch consolidates with shallow retracements; PM runs the lunch-hour lows (sell stops) OR drops into a fair-value discount array, then rallies into the close (close near HOD). ~00:01:21-00:01:56, ~00:05:27 [chart] [new]
  - Often a measured move: PM leg about equal to the AM leg. ~00:02:27 [new]
  - On a strong catalyst (e.g. surprise news) the trend can run straight through lunch with little consolidation. ~00:02:13 [new]
- **2. Two-session down close** (mirror; bearish, no 4h/daily discount array reached yet): AM returns to a premium array then declines; lunch consolidates; PM runs the lunch-hour highs (buy stops) OR rises into a fair-value premium array, then declines into the close. ~00:03:08-00:04:12 [chart] [new]
  - High probability until price reaches a 4h/daily/weekly discount array. ~00:05:10 [new]
- **3. AM rally, PM reversal** (bullish but just under a 4h/daily premium PD array): AM returns to discount then rallies into the HTF premium array; lunch consolidates; PM runs the lunch-hour highs (can be a lower high than the AM high = failure swing) OR runs the intraday high, then reverses into the close. ~00:05:56-00:07:46 [chart] [new]
  - PM can resume higher if the AM discount array was itself a 4h/daily (or higher) discount array (it gets re-tested and holds). If the AM launch came from only a 15m/60m array, expect price to trade through it lower. ~00:07:46-00:08:50 [chart] [new]
- **4. AM decline, PM reversal** (bearish but just above a 4h/daily discount PD array): AM returns to premium then declines; lunch consolidates; PM runs the lunch-hour lows OR the intraday low, then reverses up into the close. PM can resume lower if the AM premium array was an HTF (4h/daily/weekly/monthly) premium array; otherwise expect continuation up. ~00:08:50-00:11:01 [chart] [new]
- **5. Consolidation: AM rally, PM decline** (order flow neutral/unclear, no high/medium-impact news after 10:00): AM returns to discount then rallies, OR after the first hour expands up from equilibrium to run London buy stops (or intraday highs); lunch consolidates; PM runs the lunch-hour highs then reaches for the day's sell stops, OR runs the intraday high then reaches for intraday/London sell stops. PM may also just consolidate into the close. ~00:11:01-00:14:05 [chart] [new]
  - Which high the PM runs: if the AM high already hit an HTF premium array (1h or 4h), PM only runs the lunch high; if not, PM runs the intraday high. ~00:13:09-00:13:52 [new]
- **6. Consolidation: AM decline, PM rally** (mirror): AM returns to premium then declines, OR after the first hour expands down from equilibrium to run London sell stops; PM runs the lunch lows then reaches for the day's buy stops, OR runs the intraday low then reaches for intraday/London buy stops. If the AM low hit an HTF discount array, PM only runs the lunch low; else the intraday low. ~00:14:05-00:15:43 [chart] [new]
- Consolidation profiles are back-and-forth tug-of-war; do not expect them when there is strong directional bias. NFP days are a "seek and destroy" exception. ~00:15:43-00:16:23 [new]
- Lunch is consolidation with shallow retracements on all profiles except the two trending ones, where lunch can be active. ~00:16:39 [new]
- Intraday objectives are stops: indices on intraday basis hunt liquidity (lunch highs/lows, intraday highs/lows, London highs/lows). ~00:17:18 [new]

## Definitions
- Projected range: the expected shape of the day's range (AM trend, lunch, PM trend) chosen before the session from HTF order flow. ~00:00:35
- Failure swing: PM high that runs the lunch high but stays below the AM high, then reverses. ~00:07:32
- HTF PD array here = 4h, daily, weekly (monthly mentioned); 15m/60m arrays are intraday, not HTF. ~00:08:19
- Fair value discount/premium array: an FVG/PD array in the lower/upper part of the range. ~00:01:35

## Seen only on the charts
