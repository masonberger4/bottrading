# cc01_002 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 1 - How Market Makers Condition The Market (XwYYWBttWro)
Recorded September 2016 (teaching 2 of 8, 2016-17 Private Mentorship). Market: forex (EUR/USD "fiber", GBP/USD "cable", EUR/GBP mentioned); no live chart shown, slides only. Timeframes: daily range and weekly range as templates. Duration ~25 min.

## Summary
Conceptual lecture. First half: the "market efficiency paradigm" (smart money / liquidity provider vs. uninformed speculative money; price is driven by an algorithm, not retail supply/demand). Second half: the price delivery cycle (consolidation -> expansion -> retracement or reversal) and how it maps onto the times of the trading day and the trading week. No trades, prices or entries.

## Rules
- Price delivery has four states: consolidation, expansion, retracement, reversal. Allowed transitions: consolidation -> expansion only; expansion -> retracement or reversal. Never consolidation -> retracement, consolidation -> reversal, or consolidation -> expansion -> consolidation (as stated) ~00:17:26-00:19:10, ~00:20:33, ~00:24:25 [chart] [new]
- After a consolidation, expect an impulsive expansion leg; the job is to judge its direction from the higher-timeframe premise ~00:17:52-00:18:09, ~00:21:43 [new]
- Retracement after expansion returns to the order block the expansion left behind, then price continues in the expansion's direction ~00:18:12-00:19:05 [new]
- During consolidation, market makers let orders build above and below the range (liquidity for the expansion) ~00:17:31 [new]
- Manipulation (Judas swing) is always tied to a news driver, at the time of the move or just before it ~00:15:16 [new]
- Daily template (buy-day version, NY time): Asian range consolidates; just after midnight a false drop (Judas) makes the low; London open reversal runs stops and makes the low of the day; expansion up into ~05:00; consolidation 05:00-08:00; retracement 08:00-08:30 (news); then reversal or another expansion running into 10:00-11:00; London close reversal; consolidation rest of day ~00:13:51-00:16:47, ~00:21:04 [chart] [new]. Sell day is the mirror. Mostly forex framing (Asia/London); for index futures the transferable parts are midnight Judas, 08:00-08:30 retrace, and a 10:00-11:00 reversal window.
- London close reversal window stated as 10:00-11:00 here; mentorship_rules.md (Ep 06) has 10:00-12:00 ~00:16:36 [conflict, minor]
- Weekly template: Sunday open consolidation, expansion Monday, reversal Monday or Tuesday (the weekly low/high), expansion, midweek consolidation, then reversal or retracement ~00:24:04-00:24:25 [new]
- The cycle applies on any timeframe; daily and weekly are just the easiest to study ~00:23:49 [new]
- Delivery sequences are time- and day-sensitive and intermarket-related (one market held while a correlated one runs) ~00:19:23-00:19:46 [new]; the EUR/GBP vs. EUR/USD/GBP/USD example is forex-only, the index analog would be the ES/NQ SMT comparison already in the rules file.
