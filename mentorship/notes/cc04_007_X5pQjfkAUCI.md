# cc04_007 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 04 - Reclaimed ICT Orderblock (X5pQjfkAUCI)
2016-17 Private Mentorship, December 2016, "ICT Teaching 3.4 of 8" (slide title "Reinforcing Orderblock Theory [Reclaimed Block]"). Market: forex only (AUDUSD H1 23 Nov-6 Dec 2016; AUDUSD H4 late Aug-mid Oct 2016). Duration ~11 min. Frames viewed: 4.

## Summary
Reclaimed order blocks inside the market maker buy and sell models. In a MM buy model, each down candle on the way down (sell side of curve) that is followed by a small bounce shows early hedged buying by market makers. After the low, on the buy side of the curve, price returns to those same candles and they act as support for new longs. The levels mirror each other across the low. The sell model is the mirror.

## Rules
- MM buy model = a price swing down into a higher-TF support (old high/low, bullish OB, filled void, FVG), then a swing back up. The left leg is the "sell side of the curve", the right leg the "buy side of the curve" ~0:50-1:46, ~3:52-4:03 [chart] [new]
- On the sell side of the curve, mark every down candle that is followed by a small bounce (minor displacement). Each one is a bullish OB where smart money scaled in early ~2:37-3:46 [new]
- Do NOT buy those bounces on the way down; the hedging entries get stopped out. They are only reference levels until the low is in ~3:09-3:35 [new]
- After the low forms and price starts making higher highs (buy side of curve), buy each retrace into one of those old down candles: the reclaimed block ~4:11-4:58 [chart] [new]
- The buy-side entries mirror the sell-side levels (slide: matched horizontal lines at equal heights on both legs) ~4:52-5:00 [chart] [new]
- MM sell model mirror: during the rally (buy side of curve), mark each up candle followed by a small decline. After the high, sell each retrace into those up candles ~6:36-8:35 [chart] [new]
- Ideal setup (slide): bullish reclaimed blocks in major-to-intermediate uptrends, bearish in downtrends [chart]
- Use the model even if you missed the move into the extreme: once the swing has happened, start mapping levels ~1:26-1:39

## Trade examples
- AUDUSD H1, 24 Nov-6 Dec 2016 ~5:22-6:26 [chart]: a decline from about 24 Nov into a low around 29 Nov. A down candle just before a small bounce (~27-28 Nov, ~0.7220 [chart; approx.]) is marked with a horizontal line. After the low, price rallied, returned to that line around 30 Nov-1 Dec, held, and rallied. A second, higher down candle was hit "to the pip" and price moved higher. No entry/stop/size.
- AUDUSD H4, Sep 2016 ~8:58-10:13 [chart]: a rally into a high around 20-21 Sep. Three up candles on the way up that were followed by small declines are marked with lines. After the high, price returned to each line and sold off. One up candle was first traded through during the rally, then acted as resistance after the climax high. No prices legible.

## Definitions
- Bullish reclaimed block (slide): a candle that was previously used to buy price, where a short-term bounce confirmed minor displacement. On the buy side of the curve these old blocks become reclaimed longs ~5:00-5:20 [chart] [new]
- Bearish reclaimed block (slide): a candle previously used to sell, where a short-term decline confirmed minor displacement. On the sell side of the curve these become reclaimed shorts ~8:37-8:55 [chart] [new]
- Sell/buy side of curve: the left and right legs of the MM model ~1:39-1:53, ~4:11-4:16 [new]
- "Minor displacement" here = any small bounce/decline after the candle. No size is given.

## Seen only on the charts
- The slide draws three matched pairs of lines: each sell-side level has a buy-side twin at the same price.
- On AUDUSD H1 the line is drawn from the down candle's body area (around its open/high), extended right to the reaction.
