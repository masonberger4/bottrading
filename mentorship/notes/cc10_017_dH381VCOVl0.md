# cc10_017 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Stock Trading - Building Sell Watchlists (dH381VCOVl0)
Recorded June 2017 (stock week, lesson 3). Market: Dow 30 stocks vs the Dow; daily line charts of Nasdaq/Dow/S&P (2015-2016); weekly stock charts Sep 2013-Jul 2016. Duration ~13 min.

## Summary
The mirror of the buy watchlist. The market must be poised to decline, the month seasonally bearish (main window May to Aug 1), and the stock must make a lower high while the Dow makes a higher high. Weekly order flow must already be bearish. 2015 examples: AXP, CAT, CVX, XOM, WMT.

## Rules
- Filter 1: the market must be poised to decline. Filter 2: in bearish months, pick lower-high stocks ~1:40-1:59 [chart] [new]
- January and May-July are ideal short swing months; main focus is the May high through July (to Aug 1) ~1:52, ~3:00-3:22 [chart] [new]
- Stocks already trending lower on the weekly are ideal; weak stocks show obvious bearish structure ~2:01-2:21 [chart]
- Avoid "safe" stocks (VZ, GE, KO) ~2:09
- Premium arrays with index SMT reveal distribution; leaders sold by institutions fail to rally while the three indices rally, until one index fails to post a higher high ~2:21-2:58 [chart] [new]
- Index-level distribution, May-Jul 2015: Nasdaq made a higher high, Dow and S&P lower highs ("classic fingerprint") ~3:22-3:45 [chart] [new]
- The seasonal alone is not enough: when weekly order flow is bullish (2014), May-Aug produced only small dips and no SMT, so no trade ~4:30-5:09, ~5:52-6:21 [chart] [new]
- SMT must be obvious; if arguable, there is no divergence ~4:47-5:01 [new]
- Prefer names that rally into a premium first (Judas-like) to sell; a steady drift lower with no rally attempt is a weaker pick (WMT discarded) ~9:16-10:01 [new]
- Use put options for the short picks ~10:01

## Trade examples (hindsight, weekly charts, no stops/sizes)
- AXP 2015: Dow higher highs into May while AXP made lower highs; fell ~$80 to near $50 ~5:09-5:52 [chart] (chart shows ~94 high Jan 2015, ~52 low early 2016)
- CAT 2015: Dow higher high vs CAT lower high; ~$90 to ~$55 ~6:21-6:51
- CVX 2015: lower highs vs Dow higher highs; ~$110 to ~$70, selling accelerated in the seasonal window ~7:04-7:52
- XOM 2015: ~$90 to ~$66 ~7:52-8:40
- WMT 2015: lower high vs Dow, ~$80-90 to ~$56, but discarded for no rally to sell ~8:40-10:01 [chart]

## Definitions
- Distribution fingerprint: one index makes the higher high while the others fail ~3:45
- Bearish institutional order flow (weekly): falling structure with lower highs ~4:13

## Seen only on the charts
- Red shaded windows run May to early Aug in both 2014 and 2015; red lines link the Dow's higher high with the stock's lower high.
- On the 2015 daily line charts, after the May-Jul SMT, all three indices fell sharply in late August 2015 (outside the shaded window), with a second low in Jan-Feb 2016.

## Open questions
- The window is "May to Aug 1", but the decline in the 2015 example came in late August. How long should a seasonal short bias be held after the window?
- "Market poised to decline" is not defined; presumably a weekly bearish structure plus index SMT at a premium.
- SPY/QQQ use: in May-Jul (and Jan, late Sep), if QQQ/SPY/DIA show an obvious failed higher high at a premium array while weekly structure is bearish, run a short-swing bias. In a bullish weekly trend, treat the seasonal as consolidation only.
