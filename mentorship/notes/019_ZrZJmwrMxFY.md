## Rules
- NWOG = Friday's close to Sunday's open. You only see it on a chart that includes electronic (overnight) hours; with RTH-only data the gap disappears. Use both RTH and ETH views to find gaps. ~4:31-4:53, ~16:08-16:27 [new] [chart]
- NWOG partial-fill logic: if price has already run back to Friday's close, traded both buy side and sell side inside the gap, then rejected at its CE, it does not need to return to Friday's close. The draw becomes the only inefficiency left, the small FVG at the gap's lower edge. ~16:36-17:35 [new] [chart]
- Soft bias = a bias from technicals only (no 8:30 news, no volatility injection). On soft-bias days, wait until close to the 9:30 equities open before acting. ~7:57-8:15 [new] (no-news wait is in Ep 05; the "soft bias" label is new)
- Bullish MSS does not need the relative equal lows swept: price took a nearby low, then broke a short-term high and left an FVG. That was enough because the higher-TF draw (NWOG) was already set. ~8:31-9:34 [new] [chart]
- A higher-TF FVG in the way of the bias is a "speed bump, not a brick wall". Once price closes above a bullish-path 5m FVG, its levels should act as support. ~7:30-7:36, ~11:06-11:18 [chart]
- Read bodies, not wicks: wicks may reach the far edge of an FVG but bodies should stop at CE or the edge. Retests can match a prior candle's open/high/close to the tick. ~14:55-15:13 [chart]
- Propulsion block: a down-close candle trading into an older, lower order block. Expect at most half of it to be retraced. ~10:03-10:28 [new]
- Bearish flip: once price closes below the 5m FVG it acts as resistance. Expect the next return to stop between its low and its CE. ~19:21-19:49 [new] [chart]
- PM timing: the afternoon move is usually expected from 13:20-13:30, 14:00 being "more favorable". A 13:15 push was early for him. ~20:40-20:56 [new] (adds to the Ep 01 PM window)
- A balanced price range (FVG on both sides) blocks price, so the next leg goes the other way to sell-side below relative equal lows and the low where the AM move started (fund sell stops sit there). ~20:56-22:47 [chart]
- Workflow: first decide the draw on liquidity, then use only one of two parameters (long or short), then read the tape. ~13:57-14:13

## Definitions
- Liquidity void: one candle's high equals its close, the next candle's low equals its open, and no price overlaps between them (a true gap). ~12:40-13:02 [chart] (refines Ep 01)
- Volume imbalance: the two candles' bodies do not touch, but their wicks overlap. ~13:02-13:15 (same as Ep 03)
- Buy-side imbalance sell-side inefficiency (BISI): fast up-move; the expected pullback is to its CE. ~13:20-13:35
- Breaker (bullish): low, high, lower low. The breaker is the up-close candle in the swing high between the low and the lower low; MT = its midpoint. ~11:20-11:41
- Propulsion block: see Rules ~10:06.
- Daily levels: the 4104.25 target = 50% fib between the 4090.75 old high and the CE (~4117.50) of the Dec 13 daily candle. Further levels are 25/50/75% steps and a terminus beyond that. ~2:15-3:17 [chart]
- Daily OB = Jan 25 candle high 4040.50, also the low of the daily FVG (Wed high to Fri Jan 27 low). ~5:36-6:04 [chart]
