- Stage 2: if you can frame a reason for the opposite direction (a buy setup when you want shorts), skip the trade (~27:33-28:27, ~31:03, ~44:02) [new]

## Definitions
- Volume imbalance: separation between the bodies of two consecutive candles, even if a wick connects them (~7:01-7:16) (matches Ep 03)
- BISI on an up-close weekly candle = buy-side imbalance sell-side inefficiency, which needs a sell-side delivery to reprice it (~4:22-4:41)
- Low-resistance liquidity run: a trade where nothing argues for the other side; price moves fast and spends little time near entry (~31:22-31:59) [new]
- Decoupling: correlated markets stop moving together (~25:17-25:24)
