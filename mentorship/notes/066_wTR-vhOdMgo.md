# 066 ICT Mentorship 2023 - T.G.I.F. Setup (wTR-vhOdMgo)
Date discussed: Friday June 16, 2023 (recorded Monday June 19, the Juneteenth holiday). Market: NQ (continuous on 1M/1W, NQU2023 on 1D and below). Timeframes shown: 1M, 1W, 1D, 1h, 15m, 5m, 1m. Duration ~50 min.

## Summary
TGIF ("Thank God It's Friday") is a day-specific model: on Friday, after a week that expanded strongly in one direction into a higher-TF PD array, price retraces back into the weekly range, to 20-30% of it measured from the extreme. It is a draw on liquidity / target framework, not an entry pattern; any of his entry models can be used. Example: NQ week of Jun 12, 2023, short at 15357 during the 14:00 silver bullet, covered at 15280 below sell-side, 15 contracts.

## Rules
- TGIF happens only on Friday. The pattern is a retracement into the current weekly range, the distribution leg of a weekly power of three ~0:00:47-0:01:12 [new]
- Precondition: the week has trended (consecutive bullish weeks closing near their highs) AND price has reached a higher-TF premium array (here a monthly bearish FVG). If no higher-TF array has been reached, the week can keep running and close on its high, so no TGIF ~0:01:38, ~0:15:33 [new] [chart]
- Weekly range = lowest low of the week (here the Sunday 18:00 open candle) to the highest high (formed Friday). TGIF target zone = 20% to 30% of that range measured back from the high: high - 0.2*range and high - 0.3*range ~0:13:42, ~0:17:04, ~0:24:35 [new] [chart]
- 20-30% is the "sweet spot"; 25% is the midpoint. A retrace to 40%+ belongs to reversal/market-top talk, outside this model. Going beyond 30% can signal a longer-term reversal ~0:18:04, ~0:25:34 [new]
- Timing the high: assume the high of the week is in by about 13:30 Friday. Scenario A: Judas swing at the 09:30 open runs buy-side and makes the high. Scenario B: no Judas swing, price keeps climbing and the high forms at lunch / 13:30-14:00 ~0:15:10, ~0:24:58, ~0:46:41 [new]
- More likely in the PM session, especially if the AM continued with the trend; can also form in the AM ~0:17:13 [new]
- Entries: any PD array model; he names a turtle soup at the buy-side run (hard for new traders), the "2022 model" (MSS with displacement, then FVG retrace), a bearish OB, breaker, 1h SIBI gradients, and the PM silver bullet ~0:31:09, ~0:36:30-0:38:47 [chart]
- Bearish order block = last up-close candle before the displacement; its opening price is the change in state of delivery ~0:28:47 [chart] (consistent with Ep 02)
- Lunch hour: a lunch macro runs stops (rally to clear trailed buy stops); small "Mohawk" wicks outside the FVG are allowed as long as bodies stay inside it ~0:34:14, ~0:39:15-0:40:37 [new] [chart]
- PM silver bullet: entries must be taken between 14:00 and 15:00 ET (index futures), in an FVG formed in that hour ~0:42:23, ~0:44:31 [new] [chart]
- Time matters: even after 20% is hit the target can extend until the 16:00 close; the model is bounded by time ~0:43:15-0:44:28 [new]
- Optional exit grid: partial at 20%, another at the upper gradient, most off at 25% (midpoint of 20-30), then trail the stop to the most recent swing high and aim for the next gradient ~0:47:41-0:48:21 [new]. In his own trade he skipped this and targeted sell-side liquidity below the last-hour low (~0:45:42)
- In the last hour, a rally into short-term relative equal highs while staying inside the 20-30% zone is a stop run, not a reason to exit ~0:49:04 [chart]
- Reversible: bearish week -> Friday retrace up to 20-30% from the weekly low ~0:10:35 [new]

## Definitions
- TGIF: Friday draw on liquidity to 20-30% of the weekly range measured from the week's extreme, after a trending week reaches a higher-TF array ~0:30:37
- Weekly power of three: open; manipulation below the open (bullish week) to accumulate; expansion to the weekly high; distribution off the high into the Friday close ~0:07:58-0:09:41
- Time distortion: candles bouncing inside a defined range (e.g. lunch) are noise until the range's low or high is taken; details reserved for his books ~0:31:58
- Rejection block: the highest up-close body before a high; running it does the same work as running liquidity; price need not take the wick ~0:39:31-0:41:03
- "Mohawk": a small wick outside an FVG that doesn't invalidate it ~0:34:32
