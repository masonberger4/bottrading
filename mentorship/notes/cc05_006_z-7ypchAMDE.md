Markets: 10-year T-Note futures (ZN) and US Dollar Index (DX) daily charts plus 15/31-year seasonal tendency charts; aimed at forex position trading. Timeframes: seasonal (yearly), daily. Duration ~17 min. Mostly forex/macro context; little direct index-futures content.

## Summary
Treasury note prices move inverse to yields; rising yields help the dollar, falling yields hurt it. 10-year notes seasonally top in Jan-Feb, bottom late May-early July, rally into a Nov-Dec high. When T-notes follow their seasonal AND the dollar moves the opposite way, expect a long directional trend (position trades). When notes and dollar move the same direction, expect a large consolidation: trade stop raids of prior highs/lows and short-term/day trades only.

## Rules
- Regime filter: T-notes trending with their seasonal and DXY moving inversely -> trending environment, favor long-term directional trades. ~11:19-11:47, ~15:50-16:33 [new] (forex/position context)
- Regime filter: T-notes and DXY moving in tandem -> large consolidation / indecision; expect previous highs and lows to be violated and price back to the middle of the range; focus on stop raids and short-term/day trades, not position trades. ~08:43-09:00, ~10:10-10:57, ~16:08-16:23 [new]
- Seasonal: 10Y notes form a high around Jan-Feb, decline to a low in the last week of May to first week of July, then rally into the Nov-Dec high. Bearish first half, bullish second half (in price; yields inverted). ~00:44-01:03, ~03:08-03:38 [chart] [new]
- If the June-July seasonal buy in notes fails to appear, trade the bearish side instead, keyed off where notes seasonally form highs (e.g. November high). ~16:36-17:18 [new]
- Inverse relation: T-note futures price down = yields up = dollar supported; T-note price up = yields down = dollar weak. ~01:44-02:56 [new] (inter-market; not stated for equity indices)
- Long-term funds seek yield; capital flows to the highest return. ~02:17-02:29 [new]
