# cc06_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 06 - Ideal Swings Conditions For Any Market (0juYnbKays0)
February 2017, swing trading lesson 1 (~0:18) / forex: EURUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY / monthly, weekly, daily (4h named for execution) / 20m. Slides plus static chart slides; no trade executed. The NZDUSD daily is homework reviewed in a later live session.

## Summary
Defines swing trading (intermediate term, 2+ week holds) and the market-selection filter: trade only markets whose monthly and weekly charts are trending or have already broken out of a consolidation, and trade only in that higher-timeframe direction. Setups are found on monthly/weekly/daily and executed on the 4-hour chart. Buying opportunities in a trend appear as retracements into discount every few months.

## Rules
- Swing trade = intermediate-term trade held 2 weeks or longer, long in bullish conditions, short in bearish ~0:31-0:54 [chart] [new]
- Goal: ride the displacement caused by large entities entering a market; FX objectives of 200-500 pips per trade ~1:03-1:26 [new] (index equivalent not given)
- Timeframes: find setups on monthly + weekly + daily, execute on the 4-hour chart ~4:49-5:00 [new]
- Market selection: no favorite or "standard" swing market; the big movers rotate, and a new opportunity forms roughly every 3 months. Re-screen instead of assuming last quarter's mover repeats ~1:35-2:14 [chart] [new]
- Classify each market's monthly and weekly profile: consolidation, trending (expansion + retracement), or reversal ~2:21-3:01 [chart] [new]
- Filter out: lethargic markets with little or no movement over the last 3 months; markets confined to an obvious (large or small) trading range on monthly/weekly ~2:36-2:41, ~3:23-3:27, ~5:29-6:02 [chart] [new]
- Filter in: monthly/weekly trending (successive higher highs and higher lows for longs, mirror for shorts) or price that has already left a consolidation; expect it to run to the next monthly/weekly PD array ~3:42-4:05, ~6:03-6:19, ~13:06-13:29 [chart] [new]
- Direction = monthly/weekly direction only. Don't pick tops or bottoms; the existing long-term trend is more likely to continue than reverse. If the HTF trend has shown for a month, expect at least another month ~6:51-7:45 [chart] [new]
- Take every daily/4h buy signal when monthly/weekly are bullish, even if you have doubts; accept losses ~8:26-8:40 [new]
- Two consecutive failed with-trend signals (stopped out twice) = warning that an intermediate-term shift is near and the trend may be tired ~8:51-9:18 [new]
- Entry location for longs: price must retrace to a discount and to a PD array, with sell stops below (range, liquidity) as references ~17:36-18:03 [new]
- Swing trading here is directional only: no range trading, no turtle soups inside a consolidation ~19:07-19:24 [new]

## Definitions
- Swing trading: trading predictable price moves with high consistency, intermediate term, holds of 2 weeks or more ~0:31-0:54
- Market profiles: consolidation, trending (expansion and retracement), reversal; markets move from one to the next on every timeframe ~2:21-3:01
- Large flows: trending monthly/weekly charts show big institutional participation ~3:10-4:43

## Seen only on the charts
