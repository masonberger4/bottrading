# cc09_004 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 09 - Trading In Consolidations (g7jchu4g31c)
May 2017 mentorship ("ICT Amplified Day Trading & Scalping", lesson 4). Generic (any market); the charts are schematic line diagrams, with no real price data. Timeframes named: daily/4H for bias, 1H/15m for the consolidation. Duration ~21 min.

## Summary
Inside a lower-timeframe consolidation, trade only in the direction of the daily/4H order flow. Fade any expansion away from equilibrium (the range midpoint) that runs a short-term high/low against that bias, or any break of the range's outer high/low against that bias. First target: back to equilibrium. The opposite range extreme (and the liquidity just beyond it) is an optional further target.

## Rules
- Bias comes from the daily and/or 4H order flow (IPA, PD matrix: respecting a bullish OB, or reaching for a premium PD array). The 4H is the "last line of defense"; ideally both agree ~0:00:37, ~0:19:05 [new]
- Look for consolidations on a lower timeframe (1H or 15m) for day trades ~0:01:15 [new]
- Breaking out of the consolidation, price usually goes in the daily/4H direction ~0:05:07
- Daily/4H bearish: a rally above the consolidation's old high is a buy-stop run, so sell above the old high (not at it) ~0:05:43, ~0:13:30 [chart]
- Daily/4H bullish: a drop below the consolidation's old low is a sell-stop run, so buy under the old low ~0:05:31, ~0:12:08 [chart]
- Inside-range version, bearish bias: price expands up from equilibrium and breaks a short-term high just above equilibrium. Sell there and aim for the old low that forms range support, then the sell stops below it ~0:07:57, ~0:15:37 [chart] [new]
- Inside-range version, bullish bias: price expands down from equilibrium and breaks a short-term low below equilibrium. Buy there and aim for the range high and the buy stops above it ~0:08:57, ~0:17:57 [chart] [new]
- Target after an outer-extreme sweep: take the move back to equilibrium. Do not count on reaching the opposite end of the range ~0:12:29 [new]
- Price in a consolidation gravitates back to equilibrium. Even if it turns at equilibrium, that leg is a tradable move ~0:12:47
- The longer the consolidation, the more resting orders (open float) build above and below it ~0:03:59
- In day trading, the swept short-term low (long) is often the Asian-session low or the previous day's low; mirror for shorts ~0:19:20 [new]
- Retail places stops just beyond the retest of a range high/low. Wait for that pocket to be run instead of entering at the retest ~0:11:32, ~0:13:27 [chart]

## Definitions
- Order flow subordination: lower-TF price action follows the daily/4H order flow. Breaks against it on the lower TF are stop runs, not structure breaks ~0:00:39, ~0:06:20
- Equilibrium: the midpoint of the consolidation (the "fair value" line, orange on the slides) ~0:06:58, ~0:12:52
- Open float: the stop and entry orders stacked just above and below a consolidation (breakout entries, trailed stops, buy-stop/sell-stop entries) ~0:03:31, ~0:04:41
- Smart money vs retail rules (slide): retail buys the previous low and sells the previous high; smart money buys under the old low and sells above the old high. Retail chases expansions from equilibrium; smart money fades them ~0:02:54, ~0:06:01 [chart]

## Seen only on the charts
