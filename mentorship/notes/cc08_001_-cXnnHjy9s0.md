# cc08_001 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 08 - Essentials To ICT Daytrading (-cXnnHjy9s0)
Recorded April 2017 (lesson 1 of the Month 08 day-trading model). Market: forex (GBPUSD daily, hourly, weekly examples, late Mar to early Apr 2017). Timeframes: weekly, daily, 60m. 46 min. Slide lecture plus three cable case studies; no live trade.

## Summary
Sets out the frame for ICT day trading: aim for 65-70% of a daily range close to the 5-day ADR, trade in the direction of the expected weekly candle, use the 20/40/60-day IPDA lookback daily PD arrays, and filter by time of day and day of week. The core new tool is the **Sunday (weekly) opening price filter**: in a bearish week sell every day while price is below the week's open; in a bullish week buy while above it, until price hits an opposing daily PD array.

## Rules
- Day-trade target: capture about 65-70% of the daily range; leave the extreme high/low on the table. ~1:53 [new]
- Expected daily range is about the last 5 days' average daily range (ADR); some days can reach 2x ADR or more. ~2:13 [chart] (slide) [new]
- On average there are two setups per day (London and New York). Do not take many day trades per 24h; quality over count. ~1:30, ~5:08 [chart] [new]
- Trade in the direction of the weekly candle you forecast; more HTF support raises odds. ~2:47-3:06 [chart] [new]
- Foundation: IPDA data ranges (look back 20, 40, 60 trading days) plus PD arrays, mainly on the daily chart. Weekly/monthly are not required for day trades. ~6:37, ~7:41-8:04 [new]
- FOMC days and NFP days: no setup, sit out (demo only). ~6:37 (consistent with Ep 03/04; here it is all of NFP day and FOMC day, stated flat)
- Flexible in time, strict in price: price must reach the level, inside a time window; exit before the exact opposing PD array. ~10:28-11:09
- London open kill zone: 01:00-05:00 NY (the high/low of day usually forms 02:00-04:00). ~11:51-12:27 [new] [conflict]: Ep 06 has 02:00-05:00; ICT widens it to 01:00 to cover DST shifts.
- New York open is the easiest session. Skip New York if London has already made about 80% of the 5-day ADR (he also says if ADR is met or exceeded). ~13:41-14:19 [new]
- London close: banking profits; 14:00-15:00 hour, bond market close 15:00 = end of NY session, the daily range is set by then; usually done by noon. ~15:32-16:25 [new] [conflict]: Ep 01 trades the 15:00-16:00 last hour on indices.
- London lunch 05:00-07:00 NY: retracement or consolidation; take partial profits on London trades at or before 05:00. ~17:10-18:52 [new] (forex-leaning)
- Asian open 20:00 NY: small setups only; FX-only (JPY/AUD/NZD can form the daily high/low there). ~16:25 [new, forex-only]
- New York setup (bullish week): after a London low, buy a retracement into the range from London's low up to the high made before 08:20/08:30; 08:20 = CME open. ~42:38-43:00 [new]
- Day of week (slide 22:40): Sunday opt out; Monday small range typically; Tuesday good; Wednesday ideal; Thursday ideal but can reverse (weekly range often capped by Thursday's NY session); Friday small range, unless the weekly PD-array objective is still unmet, then a surprise expansion is possible. ~18:52-22:58 [chart] [new]
- Monday big range into a daily premium PD array -> likely high of week (mirror for discount -> low of week). ~19:49-20:43 [new]
- Tuesday's London open forms the low of the week in about 77% of bullish weeks (he says ~70% for highs in bearish weeks). ~20:43-21:10 [new]
- Sunday opening price filter: note the week's opening price (Sunday open, or Monday open if no Sunday data) and extend it on the 60m chart through Thursday. ~22:58-24:03 [chart] [new]
  - Bearish weekly bias: expect price to trade above the open early in the week (Judas swing), then sell short every day while price is below the open. ~26:04-27:30 [chart]
  - Bullish weekly bias: expect price to dip below the open early in the week, then buy every day while price is above it (London low, NY continuation). ~28:09-29:03 [chart]
  - Valid only until a HTF daily PD array against the trade is reached (a discount array for shorts, premium for longs); then expect an intra-week reversal. ~26:56-27:49 [chart]
  - Thursday crossing back through the weekly open against the bias = likely weekly reversal; trade the new direction Friday or from Monday next week. ~24:20-26:04 [new]
- Weekly candles with big ranges open near one extreme and close near the other (power of three); expect the open-to-close expansion. ~30:01-31:48 [chart]
- Don't rush a Monday London setup, unless the Sunday-into-Monday range is already big; then the weekly high/low likely forms Monday. ~32:55-33:49 [new]
- Price below the weekly open is not enough to sell; you also need the PD array context. ~34:21-34:42, ~38:16-39:11
- No open-and-decline in London on a bullish day -> no London trade; a later dip in New York can still be bought. ~44:40-44:57 [new]

## Trade examples (case studies, no entries/stops given)
1. GBPUSD, week of ~3-7 Apr 2017: price rallied into a daily rejection block (premium) above the Sunday open, then sold off. Tue-Wed held a FVG near 1.2430; Thursday made a slightly higher high and was rejected; NFP Friday sold off. His short targets 1.2420, 1.2375, 1.2310; low of week 1.2365, about 10 pips below the old-low discount array. ~35:07-37:31 [chart]
2. Prior week (~27-31 Mar): Monday spiked up, Tuesday London fell through the Sunday open. Wednesday hit a daily FVG plus daily discount bullish order block, so no shorts despite being below the open; Wednesday became low of week (Wednesday-low profile). Price went back above the open and later closed Tuesday's liquidity void near 1.2550. ~37:54-41:23 [chart]
3. Week of ~20-24 Mar: dropped into the daily bullish-OB discount array Monday/Tuesday, then Tuesday traded above the Sunday open -> buy every day (London open-and-decline, NY retracement). Friday quiet, weekly range capped Thursday. Following week: rallied into old/equal highs (premium) near 1.2590, failure swing, high of week Tuesday. ~41:23-44:19 [chart]

## Definitions
- Daily range opportunity: the move inside one daily candle; ADR = average of the last 5 days' ranges. ~2:13
- Weekly range framework / Sunday opening price filter: see Rules. ~22:58
- Intra-week reversal: price crosses back through the week's opening price on Thursday against the weekly bias. ~24:57
- London lunch: 05:00-07:00 NY. ~17:30
- CME open: 08:20 NY. ~42:59
