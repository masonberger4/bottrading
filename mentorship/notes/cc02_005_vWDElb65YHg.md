# cc02_005 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 02 - How To Mitigate Losing Trades Effectively (vWDElb65YHg)
Recorded October 2016 (slide footer "October 2016 - ICT Teaching 5 of 8"), 2016-17 Private Mentorship. Market: unlabeled schematic chart (same forex sample as cc02_004, no prices/times). Duration ~17 min. Risk-management lesson built on one bullish order block long.

## Summary
After a stop-out on a still-valid idea, re-enter on the next order block with HALF the previous risk and a wider stop below the order block. Because setups are framed at 2-3R+, the half-size re-entry recovers the earlier loss at 2R; new traders should then exit flat, experienced ones lock the recovered equity with a trailing stop and target 3R. Never increase size after a loss.

## Rules
- A stop-out does not void the idea if the setup has not "completely unraveled"; re-evaluate and re-enter the same trade idea. ~03:18-03:58 [new]
- Re-entry trigger: a new down candle (new bullish OB) forms, then price trades above that down candle; that authorizes the next return into it as a buy. ~04:02-04:19 [chart] [new]
- Re-entry entry: at the top/open of the new down candle (OB); stop below the low of that order block (wider than the first, tight mean-threshold stop). ~04:49-06:32 [chart] [new]
- Re-entry size: half the risk % of the losing trade (2% loss -> 1%; 1% loss -> 0.5%). After two consecutive losses keep halving (2% -> 1% -> 0.5%). ~05:20-05:44, ~14:02-14:18 [new]
- Never raise size/leverage to recover a loss; a loss may be the start of a 10-trade losing streak. ~15:44-17:13 [new] (consistent with "after a loss on the day, no max size" in the rules file)
- Recovery at 2R: with half risk, 2R on the re-entry repays the full original loss. New traders: close the trade there and go flat. ~07:05-07:58, ~10:22-10:40 [chart] [new]
- Late in the week (Thu/Fri) or late in the session: if the market lets you get back to flat, take it and do not carry a net loss into the weekend. ~08:05-08:33 [new]
- Experienced traders: once 2R is reached, trail the stop to the level where the original loss is fully recovered (never give back below the pre-drawdown equity high); take profits around 3R. ~09:29-10:22, ~11:13-11:50 [chart] [new]
- Recovery does not require the target (old highs / buy stops) to be reached; 2R came before price traded above the old highs. ~08:55-09:14 [chart]
- Stop just below the mean threshold of the first OB is acceptable but is the "too tight" stop that got hit in the scenario. ~01:34-01:56, ~02:51-03:05 [chart] (consistent with mean-threshold rule in the rules file)

## Definitions
- Mitigate (a loss): earn back the percentage lost, restoring the equity high before the drawdown. ~07:40, ~10:00
- R multiples (R1, R2, R3): profit equal to 1, 2, 3 times the risk distance from entry to stop. ~07:07 [chart]
- Secondary bullish order block: the down candle in the retracement that price rallies away from, used as the first entry. ~01:08 [chart]

## Open questions
- Halving rule: when does size return to normal (after the loss is recovered, after a new equity high, next week)?
- "Not completely unraveled": no objective test; a bot could require the original draw/target and higher-timeframe bias to still be intact and the swing low that started the move not broken on a close.
- Is the re-entry limited to the same session/day as the first loss? Rules file says a losing AM means no reversal; re-entering in the SAME direction is not addressed there.
- Exact stop on re-entry: below the new OB candle low, or below the sweep low (they coincide in this example).
- Forex percentages only; no handle/tick equivalents.
