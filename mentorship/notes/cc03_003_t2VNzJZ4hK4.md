## Summary
Institutional sponsorship = big players defending a price swing that has a high chance of unfolding. You confirm it with a checklist (HTF displacement, retrace to discount / sell-side run, short-term buy-side liquidity above as target, time of day), then watch for immediate, dynamic reaction at the OB. On the intraday chart, sponsorship shows as price dipping below the NY midnight open into a bullish OB (London or NY) and being bought every day until the HTF buy-side target is reached.

## Rules
- Long setup checklist (slide): (1) HTF price displacement: reversal, expansion or return to fair value; (2) intermediate-term imbalance: move to discount or sell-side liquidity run; (3) short-term buy liquidity above the market as the exit target; (4) time of day: London open low of day or NY session low ~0:49-1:56 [chart] [new]
- Short setup is the mirror: premium / buy-side run, sell liquidity below as the cover target, London open high or NY high ~3:36 [chart] (slide lists only 3 items for shorts; time of day is said aloud) [new]
- Trading windows named: London open, NY session, London close, sometimes Asia ~3:11
- The sponsorship level is the origin of the HTF displacement leg: the last down candle (OB), using its body, not the wick ~12:53-13:18 [new]
- Sponsorship must show as an immediate, dynamic reaction from the level. If the entry is lethargic / stalls, cut size in half or exit and re-enter later ~13:18-14:35 [new]
- Exits: scale at each layer of buy stops (short-term highs, then older highs); after a level is taken, do not exit the whole trade, look for reversal patterns or use indicator divergence; expect the next layer ~25:06-26:11 [new]
- Once a daily old high is broken (bullish structure shift), do not wait for price to return to the origin OB; sponsorship should keep it from going back there ~21:03-23:28 [new]
- Grade the swing in 4 stages: origin, first grade (quarter), equilibrium (50%), terminus (target). Sponsorship should hold price above the logical stage levels; a stall before equilibrium is normal ~22:00-22:42, ~26:38 [new]
- Daily bias via midnight NY open (blue lines): when bullish, ignore trading above the open; buy dips below it into a bullish OB ~31:25-32:33 [chart] (consistent with Ep 04 power of three)
- Entry: a down candle near/below the day's midnight open that is later traded through on the upside becomes a bullish OB; buy the return to it. London or NY session both valid ~33:22 [chart]
- Entry offset: add about 5 pips to the OB level for the limit (forex) ~32:55, ~34:06 [new; forex-specific size]
- At equilibrium, skip the small OBs and wait for a fresh OB to form before buying the move away ~33:44-34:06 [new]
- The OB may be from the previous day's (or up to ~3 sessions earlier) London/NY session; old bullish OBs get re-bought ~33:44, ~39:54 [new]
- Old highs: price stalls / consolidates under them (retail shorts), then runs through their buy stops; a retrace after that usually goes back to the prior capitalized OB, not a top ~36:38-38:21 [chart] [new]
- Many 15m down candles together form one larger HTF order block ~38:47

## Definitions
- Institutional sponsorship: willingness of large institutions to defend a price swing likely to unfold; they fund the side you anticipate ~5:01-5:23
- HTF price displacement: a dynamic daily move only possible with large participants (the "elephant in a kiddie pool" analogy) ~12:04, ~15:17
- Liquidity pool: sell stops clustering below an old low (longs protecting positions) ~6:17
- Buy-side liquidity: buy stops above old highs / relatively equal highs, plus a liquidity void above ~10:24
- Power of three: on a bullish day, buy near or below the open and exit near the close ~35:19 (already in rules)
- Terminus: the final objective of a price swing ~22:21
