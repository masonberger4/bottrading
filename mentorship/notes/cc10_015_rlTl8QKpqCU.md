# cc10_015 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Stock Trading - Seasonals & Monthly Swings (rlTl8QKpqCU)
Recorded June 2017 (stock-trading week, lesson 1). Market: Dow / S&P / Nasdaq (E-mini DJIA 20-year seasonal 1997-2016; 120-minute futures charts of NQ, ES, YM for Feb-May 2017). Duration ~21 min.

## Summary
Calendar seasonality for US stock indices, used as a higher-timeframe bias filter. The year splits into a bullish high-magnitude first part, a low-magnitude May-October range, and a strong bullish Q4. A month-by-month bias table follows, then 2017 examples where index SMT (one of NQ/ES/YM failing to make the new low/high) timed the swing inside each seasonal month.

## Rules
- Three parts of the year: Jan-Apr high magnitude, directional, generally bullish; May-Oct "low magnitude period", range-bound; Q4 (Oct-Dec) bullish and strongest/fastest (holidays, year-end spending) ~2:03-3:30 [chart] [new]
- May-Oct: expect consolidation; use less leverage, fewer options trades, less aggression ~3:44-4:39 [chart] [new]
- Focus active trading on Oct to year-end and Feb to May ~4:46 [new]
- Monthly bias table (Dow seasonals, said to mirror S&P 500) ~5:04-6:20 [chart] [new]:
  Jan bearish; Feb bullish; Mar consolidation; Apr bullish; May bearish; Jun consolidation ending bearish; Jul bullish into the mid-year high; Aug consolidation; Sep 1st half bullish, 2nd half bearish; Oct bullish (makes the Q4 low, possibly in late Sep, then rallies hard); Nov bullish; Dec bullish (Santa Claus rally).
- Low-probability months: March, June, August ~8:10 [new]
- Bias confidence: 20-, 15- and 5-year seasonal averages agree, which he takes as consistency, not certainty ~6:33-7:44 [chart]
- Use the table for S&P day trading as well as stock swings ~7:59-8:05 [new]. Bullish months = best long swing windows; bearish months (Jan, May, late Sep) can become "supercharged" short months when the broader trend is down ~9:10-10:10 [new]
- Timing inside the month: index SMT; in a bullish month look for one index refusing to make the lower low the others make (accumulation) ~11:27-11:54 [chart]. Mirror for bearish months at highs ~14:52.
- Intramonth detail: Mar weak 2nd-3rd week then rallies into month end ~12:40; Apr soft early then rallies aggressively mid-to-late month ~14:21; May sells off into about the 3rd week, small rally into month end ~15:10 [chart] [new]
- Seasonal months will sometimes fail; the underlying trend decides ~8:43-8:55

## Definitions
- Low magnitude period: May-October, range-bound seasonal window ~3:42
- Index SMT: divergence among NQ, ES and YM at a swing low/high ~11:52
