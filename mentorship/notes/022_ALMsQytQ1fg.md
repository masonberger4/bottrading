## Summary
A no-trade lesson. Pre-market, he reviews the overnight 2022-model short that ran from buy-side
(~4133) to sell-side (4113) before 09:30. He uses it to show how to pick the right FVG, where the
stop goes and when to stop adding. Because the draw was delivered before the open, he expects
09:30 to chop and sits out. He narrates the chop as a "high resistance liquidity run", with
hypothetical longs that get stopped out. At the end he shows the 10:50-11:10 "lunch macro" run
to buy-side.

## Rules
- DXY is the risk barometer. Dollar up means risk-off: ES, FX and gold struggle. Dollar down
  means risk-on. All of his analysis starts with DXY ~00:03:50-00:05:17, ~01:18:45-01:19:20.
  [new] (Ep 06 only had DXY vs EURUSD)
- An FVG has three levels: high, CE and low. Price trading down into a BISI most likely reaches
  the top or the CE. The far edge is the least likely objective ~00:09:09-00:09:56. [new]
- An order block needs an FVG after it. "Last up-close candle" alone is not enough ~00:13:12-00:13:25,
  ~00:56:19. [new] (refines the OB definition)
- NWOG: on the 15m chart, from Friday's close to Sunday's open, extended through Friday. In a
  non-trending week it is a strong magnet. Its high, low and midpoint act as support and
  resistance ~00:26:52-00:29:52 [chart]. [new] (Ep 05 left the definition out)
- 2022 model, short: buy-side is taken, then displacement breaks a short-term low (the MSS). Look
  inside the displacement leg (high to the low that broke) for an FVG at or above the leg's 50%
  level. Don't sell the breakout candle ~00:38:38-00:39:16, ~00:42:43-00:47:56 [chart].
- Pyramiding threshold: add to shorts only while price is in premium. Once price trades through
  the midpoint of the discount half of the leg (the 25% level), stop adding and just take partials
  and trail ~00:49:26-00:52:23, ~01:12:03-01:12:15 [chart]. [new]
- Choosing among FVGs: prefer the one that lines up with a volume imbalance and a bearish OB,
  extending the gap "through the candles" ~00:55:00-00:57:19. With no VI, use the first FVG
  above equilibrium ~01:03:08-01:03:20. For a big pyramid, he may instead halve the run-up leg
  just before the drop and use FVGs above that midpoint ~01:03:28-01:04:20. [new]

- If the session's draw is delivered before 09:30, expect a choppy 09:30 open and trade the PM

## Definitions
- Mean threshold is 50% of a candle or order block. Consequent encroachment is 50% of a gap,
  inefficiency or wick ~00:58:39-00:58:55, ~01:14:11.
- Volume imbalance: gap between one candle's close and the next one's open, with only the wicks
  overlapping ~00:54:26-00:55:17.
- Premium/discount: above or below 50% of the active price leg ~00:48:15-00:49:34.
- High resistance liquidity run: slow, overlapping delivery, deep retracements and frequent
  failure. Low resistance run: quick, wide moves in your favor soon after entry ~01:48:52-01:50:02,
  ~02:19:50-02:20:01.
- Fulcrum point: the swing used to anchor the fib for a 1 SD projection ~02:56:41.
