# cc10_016 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 10 - Stock Trading - Building Buy Watchlists (rcO8-nb3S0Q)
Recorded June 2017 (stock week, lesson 2). Market: Dow 30 stocks vs the Dow index; daily charts Dec 2016-Jun 2017 and weekly charts 2012-2017. Duration ~21 min.

## Summary
A screen for long swing stocks: the market must be poised to rally, the month must be seasonally bullish, and the stock must hold a higher low while the index makes a lower low (stock-vs-index SMT). Then keep names whose weekly chart sits just under a clean old high (a "low resistance liquidity run" to a breakout). Example: Jan 2017 lows, held Feb-May 2017; six names from the Dow 30.

## Rules
- Filter 1: the overall market must be technically poised to rally ~1:39 [chart] [new]
- Filter 2: in bullish months, pick stocks that make a higher low while the index makes a lower low ~2:01, ~4:15 [chart] [new]
- Feb-May and Oct-Jan are ideal long swing windows ~2:11 [chart] [new]
- Most index stocks rise with the market; stocks already trending up on the weekly are ideal ~2:38-2:57 [chart]
- Avoid "safe" low-beta names (VZ, GE, KO; he also skips MSFT, INTC) ~3:06, ~11:13
- Discount arrays plus index SMT highlight stocks under accumulation in bullish months ~3:06 [chart]
- Leadership stocks bought by institutions refuse to drop while the three indices fall, until one index fails to make a lower low. That failure signals the market trend change ~3:31-4:06 [chart] [new]
- Narrow to 2-4 names; pick by affordable call options and by where price is historically ~3:24, ~11:55 [chart]
- Drop a stock that is too extended from weekly/daily structure. Prefer one sitting just below a clean old high so it can break out (low resistance liquidity run); one far below its old high goes lethargic or reverses ~12:04-13:07 [chart] [new]
- Quarterly sales/profit growth helps keep a name on the list ~13:07 [chart]
- Entry context: an FVG/liquidity void left by a January gap-up and refilled in early February acted as the discount array (BA, HD) ~7:10, ~9:02 [new]
- On weekly charts, down-close candles holding as support (institutional order flow) mark a strong uptrend ~14:14

## Definitions
- Low resistance liquidity run: expected run through an old high/low where structure already qualifies ~12:14 [new]
- Stock-vs-index SMT: overlay the index (barchart "$DOWI" comparison, left axis) on a stock; the stock not making the index's lower low = accumulation ~9:48
