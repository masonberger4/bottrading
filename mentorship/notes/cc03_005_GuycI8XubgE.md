## Summary
"Institutional market structure" = comparing each swing of an asset against its correlated or inversely correlated partner to see what smart money is accumulating or distributing. If both confirm (symmetry), the trend continues and reversal setups should be avoided; a run beyond a short-term high/low against the trend is just a stop run. If one fails to confirm (SMT divergence), stalk reversals: the asset that made the new extreme is being distributed (stop run), the one that refused is being accumulated. Forex-specific in examples, but the logic is the same SMT used for ES vs NQ in the rules.

## Rules
- Compare every price swing of the traded asset with its correlated (or inversely correlated) partner; symmetry confirms the swing ~1:11, ~1:24 [new]
- Inverse pair (USDX vs foreign currency), symmetrical: USDX lower low with foreign higher high (or USDX higher high with foreign lower low) confirms trend; do not stalk reversals ~2:43-3:08, ~4:19-4:44 [chart] [new]
- In a symmetrical (confirmed) trend, a counter-move beyond a short-term high/low is a stop run to pair orders; expect trend resumption, i.e. anticipate the turtle soup before it happens ~3:13-4:12, ~4:44-5:09 [new]
- Non-symmetrical = SMT: USDX makes a lower low but foreign fails to make a higher high (or USDX fails a higher high while foreign makes a lower low) -> trend likely not to continue; stalking reversal patterns is high probability ~5:19-5:45, ~6:15-7:11 [chart] [new as a stated filter; SMT itself is in Ep 03/04]
- The asset that refuses to make the new extreme is being accumulated (bought aggressively, they won't wait for lower prices); the one that breaks the old high is being distributed into buy stops ~10:18-11:01, ~13:09-13:30
- Every apparent breakout above an old high must be cross-checked against the correlated asset; unconfirmed breakouts are false (retail "sucker play") ~13:57-14:17
- Once a daily SMT gives the bias, trade only in that direction: short-term traders sell every 60-min or 4-hour bearish OB; day traders sell above the NY midnight opening price ~19:41-19:58 [consistent with Ep 04/08 midnight-open rules]
- After accumulation, every retracement should be followed by another expansion in the bias direction ~15:06-15:13

## Definitions
- Institutional market structure: analysis of correlated assets in relation to inversely correlated assets, to find what smart money accumulates or distributes ~0:42-0:58 [chart].
- SMT: "smart money tool / technique"; a divergence between closely or inversely correlated assets ~2:16-2:29.
- Symmetrical vs non-symmetrical market conditions: whether the paired swings confirm each other ~2:43, ~5:14.
- Failure swing: the partner asset's failure to make the matching extreme ~6:09, ~13:54.
