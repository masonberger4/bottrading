## Summary
A pep talk explaining why the month covered bonds, currencies, commodities and stocks: big one-sided moves happen when all four asset classes move in sync (risk-on or risk-off); when they decouple, smart money is waiting and moves are small and choppy, so size down or stand aside. No setups or levels.

## Rules
- Before trading, check the four asset classes (bonds, currencies, commodities, stocks) periodically and rate the environment risk-on or risk-off ~4:13, ~17:12 [new]
- Risk-on: bonds fall (yields rise; he also says rates decline, inconsistent), stocks rally, foreign currencies rally, dollar falls. Risk-off: bonds rally, stocks fall, foreign currencies fall, dollar rallies ~2:37, ~6:47 [new]
- All four in harmony -> expect large, fast, one-sided moves and hold trades longer; decoupled/erratic -> moves lack magnitude, expect consolidation, trade less ~4:33, ~13:29, ~25:03 [new]
- Count how many classes are behaving "as they should": 2 of 4 = uncertain; a third joining = exit from uncertainty, smart money starting to commit ~20:54 [new]
- Check each class against its seasonal tendency; if they are not behaving seasonally, that is decoupling ~25:03 [new]
- Stand aside: before a widely followed US holiday (that day was the Friday before the Tuesday July 4 holiday) he saw consolidation across all four classes and did not trade ~15:02, ~23:29 [new]
- Once the weekly objective (called low) is met, do not trade just because the target hit; that does not imply reversal ~15:47 [new] (related to Ep 08 "done for the week" remark, which our rules mark as personal)
- If smart money is not leaving clear tracks, keep hands in pockets ~22:21 [new]
