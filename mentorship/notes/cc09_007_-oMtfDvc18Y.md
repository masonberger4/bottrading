Recorded for the May 2017 mentorship (lesson 7 of "Amplified Day Trading & Scalping"). Market: forex (EURUSD/AUDUSD-style 5m charts, pips). Timeframes: 5m execution, 4h/60m for targets. Duration 27m. Slide lecture; mirror of the "Bread & Butter Buy Setups" lesson.

## Summary
Bearish counterpart of the bread-and-butter scalps: two sell-program models (offset distribution, redistribution), the bearish daily-range template by session, a scalp per kill zone (London open, NY open, London close, Asia), and how to use the 5-day average daily range (ADR) as an objective and exit filter. Mostly forex; the session timing, ADR exit buffer and 08:20 CME open reference carry over to index futures.

## Rules
- Scalps only inside kill zones (highest volume: London open, NY open); not outside them (~4:00). [chart]
- Realistic objectives (slide): hold 1-2 h or less, 15-30 pips, 5m timing chart, 2-3 setups/day, about 1 per session, R:R typically 1:1, risk 0.5-1% per trade (~3:20). [chart] [new]
- Bearish day template: the open (midnight NY / 0 GMT) sits at or near the day's high; a small rally above the open is allowed; London posts the first leg down, then price waits for NY (~4:15-5:01). [chart] (Matches Ep 04 power of three.)
- London lunch: a small retracement 05:00-07:00 NY before the NY open; a short can be taken there once price hits a premium array (~5:13, ~6:57). [chart] [new]
- NY open continues the London move until the 5-day ADR low is reached; then a retracement off the low forms 10:00-12:00 (~5:37-6:12). [new]
- London open short: scalp from the open or just below it before 01:00 NY; the London Judas swing higher is the easier entry; if missed, short a 5m retracement and hold to 05:00 NY (~6:35-7:18). [chart] FX-only timing.
- NY session short: once London posted the daily high, expect NY to continue lower unless a HTF discount array has been hit or ADR low reached. Use the 08:20 NY CME open to anticipate the NY Judas swing to fade; short at intraday premium arrays (~7:58-8:28). [chart] [new] (08:20 is the FX-futures open; for ES the 08:30/09:30 opens apply.)
- NY targets: 5-day ADR low and the next HTF discount array on 4h/60m (~8:32). [chart] [new]
- If ADR low is reached before 10:00: take 80% off, leave a small runner for range expansion (~8:42). [chart] [new]
- London close reversal (counter-trend scalp): only if London and NY moved in tandem, ADR low reached, time is 10:30-13:00 NY; ideally price exceeded ADR; enter on a 5m failure swing at the low plus a bullish order block; stop 10 pips under the day's low; target 20-30% of the day's range, ideally 1:1 with stop no more than 20 pips (~9:25-10:29). [chart] [new]
- Asian open short: at or just above the 0 GMT (19:00/20:00 NY) open, aim 15-20 pips, full exit, no second leg expected (~11:11-11:57). [chart] FX-only.
- ADR does not have to fill; it can fall short or be exceeded (~12:17). [chart]
- ADR can be half the real range: in a long-term trend when an intermediate swing starts (ADR under 60 pips) or when a swing completes into a HTF array on high-impact news (capitulation) (~14:02-15:31). [chart] [new]
- ADR not filled by NY open but filled in London close = ideal (more range left) (~15:36). [chart]
- If ADR fills at or before NY open and high-impact news comes after the 09:30 equities open, ADR will likely be exceeded (not necessarily doubled) (~18:32-19:06). [chart] [new]
- Exit buffer: take the bulk (or all) off a fixed 15 pips before the ADR high/low, because data feeds differ (~16:11-18:27). [new] For ES a point equivalent would need choosing.
- Scalping model: 5m micro-setups framed on HTF charts; HTF PD arrays draw price, LTF PD arrays time the entry; use scalps to fill slow periods or join a move whose best entry passed (~20:28-21:19). [chart]
- Best days Mon-Wed; London and NY kill zones; London close and Asia give little volatility (~22:37-22:56). [new]
- Expect a large-range day after contraction (small-range days) (~21:22, ~22:17).

## Definitions
- Offset distribution: IPDA reprices above an old high to trigger buy stops (shorts' stops, breakout buyers), offsetting short holders and inducing buyers at premium; unfolds quickly at key intraday highs (~0:41-2:09). [chart]
- Redistribution: IPDA reprices higher into a fair value array (FVG/OB) to give smart money premium short entries, often after a recent buy stop raid; pains shorts and induces buyers (~2:20-3:05). [chart]
- 5-day ADR: average of the last 5 daily ranges, plotted as ADR high/low from the day's levels (~12:17). [chart]
- London lunch: 05:00-07:00 NY (~5:15).
