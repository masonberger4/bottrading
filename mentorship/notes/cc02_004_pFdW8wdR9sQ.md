# cc02_004 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 02 - No Fear Of Losing (pFdW8wdR9sQ)
Recorded Oct 2016 (slide footer "October 2016 - ICT Teaching 4 of 8"), 2016-17 Private Mentorship, not the 2023 series. Market: unlabeled forex chart (pips). Timeframe not shown. Duration ~20 min. Mostly risk/expectancy math and psychology; one schematic setup slide.

## Summary
Losses are a cost of business; fear of losing causes hesitation and "trade paralysis". Profitability comes from framing trades at 3:1 or better (ideally 5:1) reward-to-risk with small fixed risk (1%, up to 2% max), so even 30-50% accuracy is profitable. One schematic bullish order block long illustrates framing a 20-pip stop against a target 20 pips above an old high.

## Rules
- Only take setups that can be framed at >= 3:1 reward-to-risk; 3:1 is the "initial foundation", 5:1 or more is the goal. ~01:51, ~02:00 [chart] [new]
- Risk per trade: 1% default ("half the industry standard"); 2% is the stated maximum; 0.5% is acceptable. ~10:40, ~14:30, ~15:42 [new] (rules file has ~1-1.3% / ~2.1% from 2023 live trades; consistent, but the 2% cap is new)
- Position size = (account x risk %) / stop distance; he uses $5,000 x 1% = $50 over a 20-pip stop to get dollars per pip. ~18:56 (he says "25 stop" and phrases the division backwards; correct is $50 / 20 pips = $2.50/pip) [new]
- Target ~50% accuracy at 5:1 with 1% risk; never require higher than 50%. ~19:31-19:55 [new]
- Low trade frequency is fine: about 10 trades per month in the examples; you need not trade every month (some months he avoids). ~14:58, ~17:07, ~17:22 [new] (which months is not stated here)
- Bullish order block long: price returns to the down candle before the prior rally; entry inside the retracement on a "secondary" bullish OB; that OB candle's mean threshold (50% of the candle) must not be violated on a closing basis. ~02:39-03:36 [chart] (closing-basis wording matches the existing "bodies should respect it")
- The bullish OB's high-to-open range defines the fair value / "most probable support". ~02:57 [chart] [new wording; existing file anchors OB on the down-close candle high or open]
- Target: an old high plus a 20-pip run beyond it ("20 pip sweep on old high"). ~03:55 [chart] [new; forex-specific distance]

## Definitions
- Bullish order block: down candle before the prior rally, marking earlier institutional buying. ~02:42 [chart]
- Mean threshold: midpoint of the OB down candle; invalid if closed beyond. ~03:28
- Fair value gap (this video's older use): the OB high-to-open zone as "most probable support"; not the 3-candle FVG. ~02:57 [chart] [conflict in terminology with the 2023 FVG definition]
- Reward-to-risk multiple (R): dollars targeted per dollar risked. ~05:01
