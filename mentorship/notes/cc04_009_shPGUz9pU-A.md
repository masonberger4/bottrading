# cc04_009 ICT Mentorship Core Content - Month 04 - ICT Vacuum Block (shPGUz9pU-A)
2016-17 Core Content, slide "December 2016 - ICT Teaching 3.6 of 8" (Reinforcing Order Block Theory). Diagram only, no live chart. Duration ~14:40. Applies to futures session-open gaps and news gaps.

## Summary
A vacuum block is a price gap made by a volatility event (NFP, FOMC, rate news, a geopolitical shock, or a futures session open). No trades happen inside it, but you treat it like a candle with a high, low and mean threshold. In a bullish case price usually comes back to fill it. It either stops at a bullish OB inside the gap and leaves a smaller FVG, or it fills completely and then must not come back below the pre-gap close.

## Rules
- Vacuum block = gap between the close of the last candle before the event and the open of the first candle after it. Treat it as a candle: high, low, MT (50%) ~3:58-6:51 [chart] [new]
- In futures, a session open can create one (e.g. the daily open gap). Volatility events such as NFP, FOMC or interest-rate news can too ~0:47-1:58 [new]
- Context for a bullish one: price gaps up from a discount (after a retracement) while you expect higher prices. A gap up after days or weeks of rally may instead be an exhaustion gap (capitulation) ~2:38-3:45 [new]
- Default expectation: "high probability" that price comes back into the gap. He gives no percentage ~4:34-4:58 [new]
- Partial fill: if a bullish OB sits inside the gap, price may stop there and bounce. That leaves part of the gap open as an FVG. OB = the higher of the two consecutive down candles before the gap. Entry is at that OB; the stop goes below the lowest down candle, and that distance defines the risk ~7:03-7:54 [chart] [new]
- Full fill: price reaches the close of the last up candle before the gap. That is "perfect delivery". Buy there with the stop below the lowest low. This gives a tighter, defined risk, so more leverage at the same % equity ~10:05-11:13 [chart] [new]
- Time-of-day filter: a gap at or near the 08:30 news or the early NY open is likely to fill fully. A gap at 10:00-11:00 or later in the afternoon is more likely to stay partly open that day and fill on a later day ~8:25-10:05 [new]
- After a full fill and a rally, price should cut cleanly up through the low of the first gap-up candle without stalling there. If it hesitates, that candle may act as a bearish OB ~11:31-11:53 [new]
- After a full fill, price should not trade back below the pre-gap up candle's close. If it starts back down, take partials; if it keeps correcting, close the whole trade ~12:29, ~14:01-14:26 [new]
- Taking out the gap candle's high after the fill confirms continuation ~12:42 [new]
- Bearish: the exact mirror. Gap down, wait for up candles to fill it, then short ~14:26 [new]

## Definitions
- Vacuum block: a gap from a "vacuum of liquidity" during an event; no trades between the prior close and the next open ~0:47, ~3:58 [new]
- Vacuum block (summary): "nothing more than a breakaway gap" ~12:55
- If the gap stays open while bullish, call it a breakaway gap. Any part left open is an FVG to target on a later day ~13:09-13:45 (consistent with Ep 01/02 breakaway gap)
- Exhaustion gap: a gap at the end of a long trend, showing capitulation ~2:48 [new]
