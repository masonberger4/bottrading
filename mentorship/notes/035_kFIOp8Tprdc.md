## Rules
- Weekly bias first: before the week, judge whether the weekly candle expands up or down toward a visible objective; then trade only that direction (bearish week = filter out longs) ~16:53, ~57:06 [new]
- Use DXY as the risk barometer: DXY rallying = risk off, sell indices and foreign currencies; DXY hitting its target = take partials on those shorts ~4:44, ~23:56 [new]
- Volume imbalance needs bodies that do not overlap at all (not even equal); a candle body that later trades through it "fills" it. Expect price to lay a body inside an unfilled VI ~5:50, ~20:42 [chart]
- Wicks are gaps: the 50% of a large daily wick (its CE) is a target/reaction level ~24:54, ~56:43 [chart] [new]
- Rejection block = an old swing high's candle close (bearish use): when price trades to and through that close, wait for displacement (a 1m/5m short-term swing low pierced, no close needed) plus an FVG above, then short. If bias is the other way it is only a target ~20:15, ~23:05 [new]
- Projection: fib on a short-term swing low-to-high; a -1.5 SD level that lands at a higher-timeframe level marks a likely intraday high ~38:15 [chart]
- Lunch + Friday + higher-TF objective almost reached + DXY SMT -> expect consolidation or retrace, not continuation ~39:24, ~40:15
- Breaker refinement: if price closes below the swing low (not just wicks), treat that area as a breaker and expect the retrace to stop there rather than to the FVG higher up ~42:44 [new]
- In a bearish program, rallies only go to (a) short-term highs for buy stops or (b) an inefficiency (FVG, wick CE); sell those ~56:00, ~59:10
- IOFED entry: enter as soon as price trades a little into the FVG and pyramid there; he rarely gets CE fills so does not wait for them ~53:42
- "Dress your charts": keep higher-TF key levels on the 1-15m chart ~1:00:21 [chart]
- FVG quadrants: split a daily FVG into 0/25/50/75/100%. For shorts, best entries are between the low and the midpoint; price trading above the midpoint means weaker bearishness ~1:04:12 [chart] [new]
- Price that fails to reach a level signals strong momentum in the opposite direction (to be taught later) ~1:05:45
- SMT: indices make a higher high into the level while DXY fails to make a lower low (or NQ vs YM disagree) = smart money entering; target the opposite liquidity ~1:06:43, ~1:09:15 [chart]
