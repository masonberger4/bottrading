## Summary
Open float made mechanical: on any date, look back 20, 40 and 60 trading days and note the
highest high and lowest low of each window (buy stops above, sell stops below); also frame the
next 20/40/60 days forward. The 120-day window (60 back + 60 forward) is the "large fund"
range. Which side keeps getting raided gives institutional order flow (bias); a switch in which
side is raided signals a quarterly shift. Futures open interest dropping sharply into a
support sweep is shown as confirmation of a low.

## Rules
- On date D, compute high/low of the last 20, 40 and 60 trading days: near-term (20),
  short-term (40), intermediate-term (60) liquidity pools; buy stops above each high, sell
  stops below each low (~1:56-2:49, ~4:41-6:06, ~26:38-27:00) [chart] [new].
- Re-anchor monthly (1st of each month) and roll forward (~9:28, ~16:11) [chart: red vertical
  line at each month start] [new].
- Forward window: within the next 20/40/60 trading days expect a new range high/low to form; the
  next near-term move usually runs the 20-day pool, the easiest target, useful for day trades
  and scalps (~5:07-5:40, ~26:38-26:48) [new].
- Bias [new]: if buy stops (20/40/60-day highs) keep getting taken and sell stops rarely,
  institutional order flow is bullish; do not short. Mirror for bearish; focus on shorts until
  an obvious change (~8:51-9:26, ~24:01-24:27).
- Quarterly shift signal: while above the 60-day range and running highs, the first runs on
  sell stops suggest a shift; below the 60-day low (deep discount/oversold), runs on buy stops
  suggest a shift (~16:53-17:24) [new].
- Near the 60-day low, even a counter bounce can be large (150-200 pips on the daily) before
  continuation; avoid fading blindly (~24:29-24:50).
- Turtle soup basis: 20-day breakouts (Turtle system) are often false; the false break above a
  20-day high / below a 20-day low is the edge (~6:45-8:28) [new: 20-day link].
- Daily stop runs: candle bodies mark the level; wicks run past the bodies to take stops, then
  reject (~13:35-14:23, ~18:22-18:44) [chart].
- Open interest confirmation (futures) [new]: a sharp OI drop (~15% is enough) while price falls
  into support and sweeps an old low suggests smart money is not short and a rally follows
  (~20:40-22:55) [chart].
- Markets usually move range to range; one-sided parabolic moves are the exception (~26:18).
- Can execute purely from the daily chart once the shift is confirmed (~27:15).

## Definitions
- Open float (refined): the 120-trading-day window, 60 back + 60 forward, whose highest high and
  lowest low are the large-fund buy/sell stop pools (~2:32-2:47, ~25:15-25:41) [new].
- Near-term / short-term / intermediate-term open float = high/low of the last 20 / 40 / 60
  trading days (~26:48-27:00) [new].
- IPDA data ranges: the slide name for these 20/40/60-day ranges (not said aloud) [chart].
- Look-back = where actual (hard) stops are; cast-forward = where the new range will form
  (~25:43-26:07).
