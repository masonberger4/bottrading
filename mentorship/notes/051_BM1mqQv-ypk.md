## Summary
Follow-up to Part 1 (039). Adds: the Sunday 18:00 opening price alone is extended through the
week; NWOG high/low labels depend on gap direction; regime read (sharp move away = trending,
repeated returns or clustered NWOGs = range); the "halfway between two NWOGs" continuation
rule; and a 60-day lookback (replacing "4-5 NWOGs" for experienced users). No entry model; the
NWOG is a magnet/target to blend with other PD arrays. Worked example: NFP Fri May 5 2023.

## Rules
- Sunday 18:00 ET opening price (first tick of the week) is a level of its own: extend it through the whole current week; the full NWOG range is projected forward many weeks ~3:01-3:40 [new]
- NWOG high/low labelling: if Friday close > Sunday open, Friday close = NWOG high and Sunday open = NWOG low; reversed for a gap up ~5:38-6:10 [chart]
- "Current NWOG" = the NWOG of the week being traded (or the week about to start); all earlier ones are "old" NWOGs ~10:55-11:08 [new]
- NWOGs act as magnets ("fair value"): price may slice through them, or reach one, consolidate in it, then expand to the next ~1:19-1:40 [chart]
- Trending signature: price leaves the current NWOG sharply and does not come back -> expect a trending week with larger moves; one return to the NWOG as resistance before continuing is allowed ~9:25-10:33 [chart]
- Consolidation signature: price keeps returning to the current NWOG, or several NWOGs sit close together -> expect range-bound, scalp-sized targets; highs/lows get run and price is pulled back into the middle ~11:10-13:30 [chart] [new]
- Halfway rule: when price moves from one NWOG toward the next, if it reaches 50% of the space between them it will probably reach the next NWOG ~16:51-17:05 [chart] [new]
- Fib for that 50% is drawn from the near edge of one NWOG to the near edge of the next (e.g. 4089.25 -> 4118.75 = 4104.00) [chart] [new]
- NWOGs can be "mile markers" (intermediate) or the final target ~16:40 [new]
- NWOGs alone are not a trade signal; combine with liquidity (old highs/lows), FVGs and time ~13:53-14:14 [new]
- If price is consolidating, look back for the old NWOG that runs through that cluster; it explains the range ~17:37-17:54 [new]
- Lookback: keep every NWOG formed in the last 60 days (IPDA 60-day lookback) as the maximum; at least 5 for new students ~18:00-19:03 [chart] [conflict: Part 1 said 4-5 with no hard upper limit; 60 days is now the stated max]
- Old PD arrays (FVGs included) stay valid for 60 days even after being filled ~19:34-19:58 [new]
- Keep a separate chart template labelled for NWOGs ~13:43 [new]

## Definitions
- Current NWOG: the gap between the current week's Sunday open and the prior Friday close ~11:00
- Mile marker: an intermediate NWOG price reaches on the way to the next one ~16:42
- IPDA data range: 60-day lookback / 60-day cast forward (Core Content month 5) ~18:21
