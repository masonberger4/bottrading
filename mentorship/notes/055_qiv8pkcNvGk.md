# 055 ICT Mentorship 2023 - ES Market Review May 17, 2023 (qiv8pkcNvGk)
Date discussed: Wed May 17 2023 (recorded that evening). Market: ES June 2023 (ESM2023). Timeframes shown: weekly, daily, 1h, 15m, 5m, 3m, 1m (ETH and RTH). Duration ~28 min.

## Summary
Top-down review of a "classic buy day": weekly/daily discount FVG holds, the 9:30 open drops (Judas swing) to fill the opening range gap at the daily FVG's CE, a 10:00-11:00 Silver Bullet long forms, and the day's high prints at the top of the daily premium FVG. Introduces FVG quadrant (25/50/75%) levels, the RTH opening range gap and its projection, and refines "balanced price range". Second half is journaling/backtesting advice.

## Rules
- Weekly candle reference: when a down-close candle has a short, stubby wick relative to its range, use its opening price instead of its high as the level ~1:44 [new]
- Draw on liquidity: weekly volume imbalance and relative equal highs inside it (4244 marked); buy-side above daily highs (4206.25 on 1h) ~0:30, ~3:15 [chart]
- Stacked unrun highs mean liquidity is building; the lows that were bought get offset by later runs to that buy-side ~4:12 [new]
- FVG gradient levels: plot a fib on any FVG/gap high-to-low with 0, 25, 50 (CE), 75, 100; bodies respect these, and they are not "quarters theory" ~4:50, ~8:10 [chart] [new]
- Efficient repricing of an FVG is a pass through it in both directions (up then down, or down then up) ~7:20 [new]
- Balanced price range (refined): an FVG traded through once has only been "repriced"; it becomes balanced only after price returns to its other edge, fails to go beyond it, and then leaves the range ~15:05-15:45 [chart] [conflict: rules file calls it balanced after passing both ways and says price should not re-enter; here it acts as a trading range first]
- Silver Bullet (AM): 10:00-11:00 ET; after a sell-side run at the open and a market structure shift, buy the retrace into the FVG formed in that window; require ~10 handles of room to the draw, take profit at 5 handles ~11:44-14:30 [chart] [new]
- PM Silver Bullet mentioned in passing: structure shift, retrace into FVG, run to sell-side ~12:35 [new] (window not stated here)
- Top-down drill: 15m -> 5m -> 1m (3m used here) until the imbalance/model appears ~11:45
- Opening range gap (ORG): previous RTH close (16:15 settlement shown on RTH chart) to the 9:30 open; view on RTH session to see it ~16:48-19:05 [chart] [new]
  - Bullish + gap up: expect price to trade down and fill the gap, then move higher ~17:50 [new]
  - Bearish + gap up: rally/fill can be a false run, then break lower ~18:05 [new]
  - Consolidation after a weekly objective is met: gap fills and price ranges inside it all day ~18:20 [new]
  - After a full repricing of the gap, project its size (low to high) up in multiples; 2 SD reached near the day's high ~19:30 [chart] [new]
- 9:30 open volatility usually seeks to reprice to the previous session's close ~20:50 [new]
- Once price meaningfully moves back above the 9:30 opening price after the Judas swing, expect the run to the premium target ~21:00
- New Day Opening Gap is an additional confluence at the low ~22:00 [new] (defined in an earlier video)
- Price only moves to reach an inefficiency or run stops; otherwise it consolidates ~23:05
- Trade one market (or two closely correlated) ~27:35
